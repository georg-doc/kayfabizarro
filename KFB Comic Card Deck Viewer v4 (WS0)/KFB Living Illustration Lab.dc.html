Field notes

Each treatment judged against the single criterion from the brief: does it still read as a printed watercolor card? All are auto-derived from the flat card bitmap — no hand-authored masks, depth maps or layers. Round 2: intensities boosted well past round 1, D + E added from the Perplexity notes, and deck + card are switchable via Tweaks — test against the dirtier decks, not just glossy Utopia.

A · ATMOSPHERE — passes

Safest of the three. Dust and mist sit on top of the paint, so the illustration itself is never resampled — printed quality is preserved by construction. Risk is kitsch, not uncanniness: too many particles and it becomes a snow globe. Sparse is right. Works on any card regardless of subject.

B · DEPTH — passes with a fence

Most alive under the pointer, and the most dangerous: the dark-is-near luminance heuristic is wrong on some compositions, and past ~6px of drift the feathered layer edge reveals itself as a halo — the exact "puppet" failure the brief bans. At the amplitudes used here it reads as the card sitting in shallow air. A real monocular depth model (offline, per deck) would remove the fence.

C · SHIMMER — passes, best mood fit

Strongest where a bright focal region exists (Glossy Horizon's dome): the highlight mask finds it automatically and the iridescent wash lands exactly there. On cards without one it degrades to almost nothing — correct behavior, but it means C is subject-dependent, not universal.

D · BOIL — new · the KFB-native one

Squigglevision: three feTurbulence/feDisplacementMap filters with different seeds, swapped stepped (~7fps) so the ink line "boils" like hand-drawn animation. Uniquely suited to the punky, dirty KFB line work — it makes the drawing look drawn, not processed. Nearly free at runtime (one filter swap per step, no per-frame canvas redraw). Displacement is the whole image, not just outlines — for outline-only wobble the line art would need separation (Parking Lot).

E · KEN BURNS — new · chapter openers only

Slow zoom + pan of the whole card. Deliberately breaks the "stable frame" rule from the brief — the frame itself moves — so it is wrong for cards in play, right for covers, chapter starts and idle/attract screens. Cheap and reliable everywhere.

RECOMMENDATION

Revised after round 2: D (boil) + A (dust) layered is the KFB-native default — the boiling line matches the punky hand-drawn register of the normal decks far better than shimmer, which stays a mood accent for glossy subjects. Keep B behind the pointer only; E for covers and chapter starts.

Substrate: canvas 2D + SVG filters is sufficient — five panels animate simultaneously without WebGL; the boil treatment costs almost nothing. Revisit WebGL only if flow-maps (water, fabric) become a requirement.

INTEGRATION SEAM

Engine consumes exactly what getCardCanvas() returns; Mood is parsed from the existing artworkPrompt. Proposed: mount(el, { living: true }) — a third render mode beside Cards/Pages; the viewer hands the cropped region to KFBLiving.attach() and everything else (slicing, i18n, themes, prefs) stays untouched.
