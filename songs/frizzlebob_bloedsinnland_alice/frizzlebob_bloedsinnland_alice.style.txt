STYLE A
Psychedelic jazz-funk and dry cabaret swing; analog drums, rubbery electric bass, Wurlitzer, muted trumpet, bass clarinet, tremolo guitar and brushed percussion; loose offbeat bounce at 118 BPM; solo alto voice, clipped deadpan diction and agile pauses; warm tape saturation, sparse fills, nimble and faintly uneasy.

STYLE B
Chamber ska meets crooked music-hall swing; upright bass, acoustic drum kit, clarinet, accordion, violin, vibraphone and muted trombone; skanking shuffle at 112 BPM; solo low male voice with restrained irony, crisp consonants, and brisk rhythmic stops; woody close-miked production, bright and peculiar.
