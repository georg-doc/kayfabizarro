## Purpose and audience
Ancient Egyptian deities performing as a wrestling roster, complete with heels, crowd reactions and an unreliable booking committee. Kayfabe is a contract between performers and believers, not a lecture about a tabletop ruleset. Comic-seminar and card-table listeners, playful rough humour. 52 canonical numbered cards; four on each inferred printed page.

## Narrative arc
The show begins before creation, then moves from a fractured divine family into judgment, wild personal claims, authority tools, fate, the underworld and a final round of mythic callbacks. Cards perform themselves; the narrator exists only to open/close the show. No borrowed Pharaoh Script dark-deck conspiracy arc. Power is not sung as a gameplay instruction.
