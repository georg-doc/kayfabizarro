# Trust & Betrayal · Writer's Room wr4 · V-372/V-373

## Purpose and audience
A cartoon card deck of betrayal archetypes, traitor-wrestling and trust games. Each trope performs itself instead of reading out its gameplay effects; the humor comes from incompatible claims made with complete certainty. Designed for performative card-table and social-deduction audiences, with the treacherous alliance surviving as the main joke.

## Narrative arc
A knowingly treacherous handshake opens the performance. The alliance expands into surveillance, succession, family, romance and ideological flip-flops, then lays traps and invokes game-theory justifications. It sells friends and scapegoats, unmasks itself in the final verse, and accepts an ambiguous bill for the damage. Card 30 is a title-only printed-content discrepancy per prior WS0 dossier; its poetic claim is explicitly editorial, not certified card text.
