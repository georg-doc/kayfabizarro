Clay / craft texture donors 01

Free or low-friction clay, plaster, sculpting-artifact, fabric and paper/cardboard texture sources for KFB DIY diorama terrain, props and characters. Nothing on this page is production-approved merely because it is downloadable or seamless.

Open catalogKFB StageKFB Hub

License split

A · preferred

Poly Haven, TextureCan and CGBookcase are treated here as CC0 donor pools. These are the cleanest candidates for later KFB repository/runtime admission after per-asset QA.

B · look-dev only

EmaceArt and BlenQube are useful actual-clay/plasticine references, but their raw files should not be mirrored into public KFB output until their redistribution terms are handled explicitly.

Owner boundary

K2 clay-material/relief/toolmix, Clay Asset Studio, Blender and Asset Librarian keep their current roles. This page is research, not a new material owner.

KFB usage split

Characters

Quiet matte base + weak micro-roughness + separate low-strength fingerprints/tool masks.

Terrain / buildings

Broader plaster/tool donors are useful, while K2 remains the actual KFB clay deformation/material owner.

Props

Use felt, burlap, paper and cardboard as distinct craft families instead of one recolored generic texture.

Boundary / next gate

No raw external texture has been copied into KFB, no donor is human-approved, and no Blender/runtime promotion is claimed. Next: expand the verified CC0 pool for cork, craft foam, rough wood/MDF, more paper/fabric and sculpting-imperfection donors, then map coverage back to the Clay Asset Studio queue.
