What stays separate

Implementation, automated tests, public deployment and Georg's playtest acceptance have separate status. Test configurations do not migrate saves between applications or browser origins. There is no automatic character/vehicle transfer between the comparison apps.

Future Free Drive releases receive permanent version paths. This entry changes its current-release pointer without deleting older published tests. Broken or rejected versions remain labelled rather than being promoted silently.
