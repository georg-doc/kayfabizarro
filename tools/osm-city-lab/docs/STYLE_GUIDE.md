# KFB City Style v0

S1 is intentionally procedural and reversible. Source geometry remains independent of presentation.

## Material classes

- asphalt road;
- warm stone / brick-like massing;
- pale plaster-like massing;
- dark industrial massing;
- small roof family;
- green / water surfaces;
- limited accent colours.

No photo textures in v0.

## Massing

Buildings retain the OSM footprint and derived height. Roof hints may be simplified and are allowed to read as graphic KFB massing rather than architectural reconstruction. Any future landmark asset is an explicit override keyed to OSM identity; the base city must continue to render without it.

## Visual gate

The first human review should inspect top, oblique and street-height cameras for:

- recognizable same-neighbourhood structure;
- road continuity / junction readability;
- gross building-road overlap;
- palette coherence;
- roof scale;
- whether street-height reads as a designed low-poly quarter rather than unstyled GIS extrusion.
