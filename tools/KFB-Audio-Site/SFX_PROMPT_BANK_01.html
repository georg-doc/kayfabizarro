Runtime mix strategy

Weather bedsFree-running seamless loops. Keep rain, wind and room/surface detail separate. Crossfade 3–5 seconds; thunder is a seeded event pool.
CrowdOne sustained low bed + event one-shots (cheer, groan, gasp). Dialogue ducks the bed but should not stop its timeline.
TrafficOne distant city bed + spatial pass-by events. Avoid baking close engines into the bed.
Tyre / frictionSeparate from engine. Crossfade dry/wet/gravel and speed bands from existing Race telemetry; no new physics logic.
VehiclesExisting Racer already has V8 idle/load/rev and steampunk engine WAVs. New prompts are optional quality alternates, not blockers.
Rhythmic texturesUse Suno Sounds with BPM when an ambience intentionally acts like percussion. Start on the next bar; do not force ordinary weather loops onto the music clock.

Intake checklist

Keep original masterNever overwrite a render. Variants become siblings.
Record metadataFilename, tool/model, prompt, Loop/One Shot, duration, BPM/key if relevant, and whether it is P0/P1/P2.
Audition before promotionNew assets are INBOX_ONLY until heard. For loops, explicitly check the seam and stable loudness.
Site handoffLater, drop the selected files into KFB Audio Site Intake and attach the prompt bank ID. Canonical GitHub promotion stays a separate explicit step.
