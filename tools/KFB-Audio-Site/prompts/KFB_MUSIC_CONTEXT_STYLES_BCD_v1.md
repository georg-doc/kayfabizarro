## KFB Base Style B · Downtown Groove Ecology

Instrumental KFB groove ecology: dirty downtown electro-jazz and post-punk funk with Hammond-style organ, Wurlitzer/Rhodes, elastic electric bass, tight live breakbeat drums, hand percussion, tremolo/reverb surf guitar, analog synth dust and occasional brass or found-object hits. Accessible hypnotic pocket first, controlled avant-garde improvisation around it. Dry, tactile, slightly crooked, playful but never cute. Ensemble call-and-response, fresh detail every 8/16 bars, brief holes for dialogue and game SFX. No trailer swells, no glossy EDM drops, no generic lounge jazz. Clean loopable ending.

## Utopia · Style B · Forget Utopia

118 BPM instrumental KFB groove ecology for a polished frictionless future. Start from the Base Style B ensemble language but make the public surface immaculate: glassy Rhodes/Wurlitzer, pristine organ and restrained analog synth, clean electric/sub bass, exact live/electronic drums and precise micro-percussion. Hold a seductive geometrically regular pocket with seamless 8/16-bar transitions. Underneath, reveal tiny phase slips, maintenance clicks, muted detuned organ tones and occasional missing expected accents. Controlled, elegant, faintly uncanny, never ambient. Leave space for dialogue and game SFX; finish on a clean loopable bar.

## Dystopia · Style B · Ignore Dystopia

106 BPM instrumental KFB groove ecology under stress. Overdriven Hammond-style organ, short distorted electric-bass ostinato, clipped live breakbeat, metallic hand percussion, sparse tremolo guitar and narrow analog warning pulses. Build an obsessive repeating groove that gradually constricts: displaced accents, sudden dropouts, hard restarts, unstable organ clusters and overloaded rhythmic loops, while the beat remains physical and readable. Tension comes from repetition and narrowed attention, not horror-score clichés or a wall of industrial noise. Dry, claustrophobic mix with deliberate negative-space holes for dialogue and game SFX. Clean loop point.

## Protopia · Style B · Embrace Protopia

112 BPM instrumental KFB groove ecology as a collective workshop jam. Warm Wurlitzer and organ, woody electric/acoustic bass, loose live drums, hand percussion, found-object clacks, muted guitar and small brass answers. Keep a strong human pocket with audible microtiming; instruments enter and leave like different people contributing useful parts without breaking the groove. Let musical repairs remain visible: reused motifs, grafted rhythm cells, asymmetrical turnarounds that resolve, small call-and-response mutations. Curious, practical, communal and unfinished but coherent; never pastoral or twee. Evolve every 8/16 bars, leave room for dialogue/SFX, clean loopable ending.

## KFB Cozy Style C · Base

82 BPM instrumental KFB cozy groove: warm ambient lounge and easy-listening jazz with deep visceral electric bass, soft Rhodes/Wurlitzer, mellow organ, brushed drums, restrained hand percussion, muted guitar, vibraphone and subtle analog synth haze. Slow, spacious and tactile with a relaxed pocket, slightly crooked harmony and occasional strange little KFB details hiding at the edges. Bass should feel physical and warm, never aggressive. Minimal brass, no dominant horns, no busy solos, no driving drums, no EDM lift. Gentle 8/16-bar evolution, breathing room for dialogue and environmental SFX, clean loopable ending.

Soft glassy Rhodes, warm sub/electric bass, brushed electronic-acoustic drums, muted guitar harmonics, gentle organ and translucent synth pads. Calm, elegant and extremely controlled, with a luxurious easy-listening pocket. Beneath the polished surface, introduce tiny timing slips, almost inaudible maintenance clicks, slightly detuned sustained notes and occasional missing expected accents. Deep physical bass without pressure. Very little brass, no energetic fills, no cinematic swell. Spacious, seductive, subtly uncanny, clean loopable ending.

## Dystopia · Cozy C

72 BPM instrumental KFB late-night lounge inside a failing system. Deep rounded bass, dusty Rhodes, dark mellow organ, sparse brushed drums, soft metallic percussion, distant tremolo guitar and low analog textures. Keep the groove slow, comfortable and almost reassuring while small repetitions gradually feel wrong: one chord lingers too long, a muted click returns, a bass figure subtly loops, an expected beat occasionally disappears. Weirdness through restraint, not horror. No harsh industrial noise, no aggressive brass, no driving beat. Warm, claustrophobic, hypnotic and loopable.

Relaxed human pocket with gentle microtiming and small ensemble call-and-response; instruments casually enter and leave as if people around a room are joining in. ## KFB Conversation Style D · Base

94 BPM instrumental KFB conversation groove for dialogue-heavy scenes. Atmospheric ambient jazz with a soft swung/triplet pulse, deep warm electric bass, Rhodes/Wurlitzer, mellow organ, muted guitar, brushed drums, subtle hand percussion and tiny analog textures. The music should feel conversational: short call-and-response phrases, little musical questions, pauses, restrained answers and playful three-note figures between larger pockets of space. Keep the speech range uncluttered and avoid constant melodic activity. Moodful but discreet, rhythmically alive without driving forward. Minimal brass, no busy solos, no dense cymbals, no dramatic rises. Built to sit naturally under TTS ducking and cartoon triplet dialogue, with clean loopable phrasing.

## Utopia · Conversation D

92 BPM instrumental KFB conversation groove for a polished future dialogue scene. Soft glassy Rhodes, clean deep bass, restrained organ, muted guitar harmonics, delicate electronic-brush percussion and translucent synth atmosphere. Use a subtle swung/triplet pulse with precise little musical replies: neat three-note figures, tiny pauses and orderly call-and-response gestures that leave generous space for voices. Everything sounds beautifully coordinated, but occasional delayed replies, missing accents and microscopic timing errors suggest maintenance beneath the seamless surface. No dominant lead instrument, almost no brass, no driving drums. Elegant, conversational, lightly uncanny and easy to duck beneath TTS.

## Dystopia · Conversation D

88 BPM instrumental KFB conversation groove for tense but understated dialogue. Deep rounded bass, dusty Rhodes, low mellow organ, sparse brushed drums, quiet metallic ticks and distant muted guitar. Maintain a slow triplet/shuffle pulse that feels like a conversation becoming circular: a short phrase asks, another repeats it slightly wrong, silence answers, then the bass quietly returns to the same thought. Atmospheric rather than threatening. Use repetition, missing beats and restrained rhythmic hesitation instead of horror effects. No aggressive percussion, no loud brass, no industrial wall of sound. Plenty of negative space for TTS, subtly uneasy but still comfortable enough for long conversations.

## Protopia · Conversation D

92 BPM instrumental KFB conversation groove as a relaxed communal exchange. Warm woody bass, Wurlitzer, mellow organ, muted guitar, brushed drums, hand percussion, vibraphone and occasional tiny found-object sounds. Soft triplet pulse with human microtiming and gentle musical dialogue: one instrument offers a short phrase, another answers, a third adds a quiet three-note comment, then everyone leaves space. Small motifs may be reused, repaired or slightly changed as the conversation develops. Intimate, curious and socially alive without becoming busy. Minimal horns, no extended solos, no driving beat. Keep the centre clear for TTS and let the groove make cartoon dialogue naturally bounce.
