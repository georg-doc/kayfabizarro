# Decision · OMS fidelity vs. surreal cartoon fun · Georg · 2026-09-27

**Rule (Georg):**
- OMS/OSM supplies only the **rough architecture**.
- The **optimal track flow** and **chill & fun & stunt-race cartoon action** matter more than map data.
- Places must still be **recognisable**, through characteristic elements, the street scene and landmarks.

**Consequences for all track and set-piece work:**
1. OSM footprints and heights are a starting sketch, not a constraint. Geometry may be scaled, bent, stacked and simplified wherever driving flow or stunts gain.
2. Recognition carriers are chosen deliberately per place: 2–4 signature elements (silhouette, colour code, landmark, typical street furniture).
3. Driving numbers (lane width, radii, grades, headroom, jump lengths) are never compromised for fidelity.
4. There are no real brands or exact sign copies. KFB satire replaces them.

**First application:** TD02 "Uni-Center Y-Drift-Tower" (see `KFB_Uni-Center_Tokyo-Drift_v1.md`, Perplexity input, in this folder).
- The recognition carriers are the Y-shape with three wings of different heights, the central tower, the parking podium and the orange/green/yellow wing colour code (the colour code is from Perplexity and not yet verified by Coworker).
