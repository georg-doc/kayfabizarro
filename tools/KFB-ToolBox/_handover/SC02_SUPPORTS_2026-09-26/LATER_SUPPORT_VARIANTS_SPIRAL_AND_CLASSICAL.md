# LATER · Support variants requested by Georg · 2026-09-26

**Status:** noted, not started. Build it on the SC02b family (`sc_support.py`) as additional styles. The profile rules stay: strictly monotonic z, soffit capital band, round footing.

1. **Spiral bulge pylon.** One continuous bulge winds spirally around the cylindrical shaft, from the foot flare up to the capital.
   - Parameters: turns, bulge radius, handedness, phase seed.
   - The bulge must follow the tapered radius and end cleanly in the flares.
2. **Classical Roman/Greek columns.** Fluted shaft with entasis; base (torus/scotia) and capital variants (Doric, Ionic, Corinthian as a simplified cartoon).
   - Principles only; no copied reference meshes.
3. **Pedestals/plinths** as a separate family. Square or round, stepped, also usable without a track above (scenery, podiums, billboard bases).

Checks as in SC02: corridor intrusions, plate contact, no profile folds, no geometry above the plate top, and short-height behaviour (ladder 3–15 m).
