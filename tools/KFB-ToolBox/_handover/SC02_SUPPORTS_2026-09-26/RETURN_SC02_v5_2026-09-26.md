## Defects first

1. **Found and fixed:** on the *banked* fixture route, 20 shaft vertices of the classic supports poked through the bearing plate. The flat ladder could not show this.
   - Cause: the capital disc stayed level while the plate follows the tilted soffit.
   - Fix (concept rule, not a patch): the top band of the shaft (0.75 m under `cap_top`) is sheared by the same soffit function as the plate. The shear is eased in over 0.33 m.
   - Result: 0 pokes in all four styles; the ladder regression is still `all_pass`.
2. **Open (known, not new):** organic styles below ~4 m clear height (trunk at 3 m) still read as "melted". Proposal: pedestal-class supports fall back to the classic profile, or get a dedicated short form. Georg decides.
3. **Open:** the long span 206–240 (keep-out of road B) remains a design decision.
4. **Only a stand-in:** `ground(x,z)` is a stand-in for the single Surface-Adapter height query (WSA D3).
