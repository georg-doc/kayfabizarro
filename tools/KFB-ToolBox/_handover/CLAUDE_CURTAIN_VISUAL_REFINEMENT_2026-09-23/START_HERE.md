## Required visual direction

### Rod / rail

Keep the same functional support role, but make it:
- chunkier;
- simpler;
- rounded;
- toy-like;
- visibly KFB/KayKit-adjacent rather than realistic stage hardware.

Possible language:
- oversized wooden/painted rod;
- chunky end caps;
- simplified brackets;
- broad cartoon proportions.

### Rings / hooks

Current repeated top attachments remain functionally useful.

Redesign visually as:
- larger readable rings/hooks;
- fewer tiny mechanical details;
- exaggerated rounded silhouettes;
- slightly irregular spacing/rotation where physically safe;
- clear attachment relationship to the cloth.

### Side-opening motion

The desired motion is not only "top rail gathers inward/outward".

Create a readable theatre pull:
- left cloth gathers left;
- right cloth gathers right;
- lower/mid cloth sweeps outward;
- tieback region creates a recognisable swag;
- optional cord/tassel can visually explain the gathered state.

The v1 cloth physics remains underneath.

### Tieback / swag

This is already an explicit human-requested Core-v2 refinement.

Target silhouette:
- open centre;
- cloth mass gathered around lower-third / side region;
- theatrical but cartoon;
- not rigidly symmetrical;
- clear enough to read at game-camera distance.

### Crease / line artifacts
