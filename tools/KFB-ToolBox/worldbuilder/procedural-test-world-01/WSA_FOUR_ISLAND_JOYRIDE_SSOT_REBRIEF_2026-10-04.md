# 4 · NEW BINDING WORLD FORM · each island is an excavated Life Tree

Georg decision · 2026-10-04:

> **Each island is conceived as one excavated tree with visible roots. A large Claymation Life Tree stands in the centre.**

This supersedes the visual idea of:
- flat green plate;
- generic floating rock;
- arbitrary underside;
- disconnected terrain slab with decorative tree on top.

## Island morphology

Each island is one coherent **tree-earth organism**.

Read it as if a giant old tree and the earth held by its roots were excavated from the ground and now float as one world object.

Required silhouette:

### Above
- central Life Tree dominates the island skyline;
- trunk emerges from the island's social/semantic centre;
- major surface roots spread outward and visibly organize paths, plazas, terrain ridges and built zones;
- terrain rolls between roots rather than reading as a flat pad;
- roads weave with/around the root system rather than cutting through it arbitrarily.

### Edge
- soil/grass/clay surface rolls organically over the island rim;
- roots may become structural edge forms;
- no generic grey/brown ring separating “top mesh” from “under mesh”;
- no engineering slab silhouette.

### Below
- exposed large roots descend and taper;
- roots fork into smaller roots;
- embedded earth/clay remains caught between them;
- the underside silhouette is asymmetric and gravitational;
- roots point downward and inward/outward naturally;
- no radial cone fan;
- no pasted stalactites;
- no identical copied underside on all islands.

The underside is the **root architecture of that island's Life Tree**, not a generic floating-island generator.

## One organism rule

Tree + roots + island body must read as one composition.

Do not build:
`island mesh + separate tree prop + separate root decoration`

as three visually unrelated systems.

The visual contract is:

`LifeTreeRecipe → trunk / crown / surface roots / exposed roots / island-earth envelope`

R2D terrain/support remains the gameplay terrain owner.
The LifeTree/root body is a presentation/world-form layer aligned to it.
