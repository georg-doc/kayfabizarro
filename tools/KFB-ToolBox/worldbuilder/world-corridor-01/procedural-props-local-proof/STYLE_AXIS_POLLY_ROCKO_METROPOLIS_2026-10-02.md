## User style formula

Georg's previously proposed direction:

**Polly & Her Pals**
meets
**Rocko's Modern Life**
meets
**Fritz Lang — Metropolis & related urban cinema**

This is now a first-class design axis for the procedural environment/building language.

It does **not** replace the proven KFB deformation owners. It tells us how to calibrate and judge them.

## Important method

Do not blend the references into a vague average.

Assign each reference a specific job.

### Axis A · POLLY & HER PALS · graphic / perspective deformation

Primary role:
- surreal perspective;
- architecture/background as active graphic composition;
- cubist / Art-Deco-like shape collision;
- bold plane changes;
- non-naturalistic spatial logic;
- deliberate pattern and rhythm;
- permission for built space to bend around the composition.

For KFB this should inform:
- skewed silhouettes;
- non-parallel facade/ridge relations;
- controlled perspective-like distortions in geometry;
- abstract pattern/rhythm in façade grouping;
- playful disagreement between architectural parts while the object still reads clearly.

Do not reduce Polly to random wobble.
The value is **designed distortion**.

### Axis B · ROCKO'S MODERN LIFE · everyday cartoon world / wonky object language

Primary role:
- ordinary suburban/commercial/utility architecture made characterful;
- crooked boxes and poles;
- asymmetric perspective;
- elastic, off-axis streets and facades;
- strong readable silhouettes;
- mundane world objects pushed into cartoon personality.

For KFB this should inform:
- normal houses, shops, fences, signs, utility props;
- irregular but readable facade rhythm;
- modest per-object deformation;
- one strong silhouette/quirk rather than noise everywhere;
- the “this whole town belongs to the same cartoon” feeling.

This matches earlier KFB notes that already named 1990s Nickelodeon staging and Rocko as visual/staging references.

### Axis C · FRITZ LANG / METROPOLIS · urban hierarchy / monumental massing

Primary role:
- vertical hierarchy;
- stacked city layers;
- towers vs low city fabric;
- bridges, elevated connections and deep streets;
- strong architectural silhouettes;
- monumental urban focal points;
- rhythm of repeated masses interrupted by hero forms.

For KFB this should inform:
- city composition and skyline;
- landmark scale hierarchy;
- tall-building families;
- elevated paths/bridges/track-world interfaces;
- civic/industrial/hero silhouettes;
- district massing.

Do not copy Metropolis surface ornament literally.
Use it primarily as **massing + hierarchy + urban staging**.

## Synthesis rule

The KFB procedural world should not look like any one donor.

A useful shorthand is:

**Polly bends the picture.  
Rocko makes the everyday world cartoon-alive.  
Metropolis gives the city hierarchy and drama.**

Then apply the existing KFB deformation stack:
- Elastic Grotesque Clay V2;
- City Cartoon/Grotesque;
- LandmarkElastic;
- LOOK-TORSION;
- FACADE_RULE v1;
- KayKit/K-Kid source identity;
- approved P0B soft procedural trees/props;
- Hivebound soft-rounded/cozy constraint.

This style axis is not invented from scratch.

Recovered repo evidence already contains parts of it:

Fritz Lang / Metropolis is the new explicit third axis here for urban hierarchy and monumental composition.

## What this means for procedural buildings

Before inventing building families, derive them from real Golden Samples and grade them against this three-axis line.

For each candidate building ask:

### Polly test
- Does the silhouette/perspective feel intentionally designed rather than mechanically perturbed?
- Are facade planes/rhythms allowed to disagree in an expressive way?

### Rocko test
- Does an ordinary house/shop read as a cartoon object with personality?
- Is the wonkiness controlled and readable rather than random?

### Metropolis test
- Does the building contribute to a clear urban hierarchy?
- Is it obviously background fabric, mid-rise rhythm, tower, landmark, bridge/support or hero mass?

### KFB donor test
- Is the candidate still using the proven Elastic/Grotesque/Landmark/Torsion/FACADE rules?
- Does it preserve KayKit/K-Kid or real source identity where a source exists?
- Is the soft/cozy rounded constraint strengthening the KFB grammar rather than replacing it?
