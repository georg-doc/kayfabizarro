## 4 · Open points (carry over, ranked)
1. **Roofs look stripy.** Two probable causes, both in code: (a) `sw-mesh.js › surface()` darkens every second tile row to 0.84 (`stripe`) plus per-quad `jit()` colour jitter, and the row count changes with LOD (6 → 3 → 1), so the stripe frequency jumps on LOD change; (b) shadow acne on low-slope roof faces: `sun.shadow.bias = -0.0004` with no `normalBias`. Try first: set `normalBias ≈ 0.04`, then soften `stripe` (0.84 → 0.93) or replace it with a per-row ridge offset. Compare screenshots at LOD0 and LOD1 before/after.
2. **Shadows jump at times.** `sw-app.js` line ~75 moves sun and target with the focus point every frame without texel snapping. With a 190 m ortho frustum on a 2048 map one texel is ≈ 9.3 cm, so edges swim and jump. Fix: snap the light-space focus to whole shadow texels (standard stable-CSM trick). Second suspect: chunk LOD swaps change the casters (LOD2/3 shells cast different silhouettes). Check whether jumps line up with chunk integration (`F1` shows integrations per frame).
3. **Full re-run pending.** T1–T12 last ran complete on 2026-10-05. T13 (locomotion) passes after its third rework (8 headings, longest distance). A complete T1–T13 run plus bench A–E after the walk sprint has **not** been recorded. Do it first, update `TEST_REPORT.md` and `benchmark.json`.

## 6 · How to rebuild the standalone
1. Regenerate `seedworld/sw-bundle.js`: read the eight modules, rewrite `from './sw-X.js'` to `__SWURL_sw-X.js__` and `new URL('./sw-X.js', import.meta.url)` to `new URL(window.__SWU['sw-X.js'])`, embed as JSON (escape `<`). The DC's `componentDidMount` uses the bundle only when `<meta name="sw-bundle">` is present.
2. Make a copy of the DC with that meta + `<script src="seedworld/sw-bundle.js">` in the helmet and a `__bundler_thumbnail` template, inline it into one HTML file, delete the copy.
