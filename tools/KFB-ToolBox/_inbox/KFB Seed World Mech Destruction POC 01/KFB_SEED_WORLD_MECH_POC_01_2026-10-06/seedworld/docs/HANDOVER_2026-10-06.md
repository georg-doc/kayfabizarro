## Current state (after Georg's review rounds of 2026-10-05/06)
Georg's verdict: "good state reached". The POC runs in `KFB Seed World Mech Destruction POC 01.dc.html` (Claude Design project, **nothing pushed**).

Changed since RETURN.md:
1. **Roads as ribbons** (`sw-mesh.js › roads()`): centre lines in warped local (u,v) space every 2 m, edges offset locally. A segment belongs to the chunk that holds its midpoint, so there is no overlap. The terrain vertex colours no longer carry road or walk colour (`sw-gen.js › ground()`). Layers: walk +0.05 m, road +0.09 m, path +0.07 m, each × (1 + LOD). Known caveat: on hilly field paths at LOD2/3 the terrain can poke through.
2. **WoW-style controls** (`sw-mech.js`): facing (`face`) is separate from the camera (`look`).
   - W/S forward/back (back ×0.6) · A/D turn (turn input smoothed, tinyskies TURN_INPUT_SMOOTH 8) · Q/E strafe · ←/→ turn · ↑/↓ altitude · Shift boost
   - LMB drag = camera only, 360° · RMB drag = steer (face = camera; A/D then strafe) · LMB+RMB = run forward · mouse wheel = zoom
   - Space = fire the selected weapon (a tap gives a 0.14 s burst) · 1 Minigun · 2 Rockets · T rocket mode · F continuous fire · X rocket
   - Pointer lock only while a mouse button is held; the frame fallback uses movementX.
3. **Stuck-input guards** (cause of "turns right forever"):
   - Keys are cleared while `!document.hasFocus()`.
   - Meta key held means clear all keys (macOS swallows keyup while Cmd is down).
   - Mouse buttons are re-read on every mousemove from `e.buttons`.
   - Mouse jumps over 250 px are ignored.
   - No automatic camera follow; the camera tracks the mouse almost directly (slerp 40).
   - Not reproduced in the synthetic test. If it comes back: `__sw.mech.debug()` in the console (look/face/turnCmd/keys/mouse).
4. **Weapon FX** (`sw-fx.js`, new):
   - Effects: camera-facing streak shader (tracers, muzzle cones, rocket exhaust, sparks), dot flashes, ejected brass, muzzle point light, barrel heat glow plus after-fire smoke.
   - Pools: FXCAPS tracers 128, flares 48, sparks 160, dots 160, casings 96, one draw call per family.
   - Minigun 18 rounds/s, spin-up 0.16 s. Shake per round is light and smooth (no per-frame random jitter).
5. **Camera**: rigid in the mech frame (`camOff`), only the boom length is smoothed. The mech no longer slides out of view.
6. Frame time while firing was 8–12 ms (self-measured, preview frame). The 12 checks were **not** re-run after these changes.
