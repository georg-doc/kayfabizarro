02

What made the old presentation cheap

D1 · panels slide, never gather. Every pin moves by the same offset, so the cloth translates and the hem trails diagonally.
D2 · lower quarter never drawn. The render loop stops at row 30 of 40.
D3 · cover is see-through. Opacity 0.85 shows the stage behind the closed curtain (right).
D4 · centre seam. Both panels share one plane.
D6 · no stage. No rail, pelmet or frame; white satin sheen in a plaza HDR.
Consumer result. With no theatre read in the cloth, the Birthday/Golden-Journey flow moved identity and loading onto clay plaques in front of it. The candidate removes the need for them.
