**CURRENT PRODUCT DIRECTION**

The game is a **KFB claymation-cartoon living toy world**.

World history remains visible as layers, scars, repairs, reuse and institutions.

# 3 · KFB TOWN · historical hub

## 3.1 Authored biography

**CURRENT PRODUCT DIRECTION**

Town's existing six-beat biography seed:

1. **Origin** — early Kayfabian settlement; Caveman belongs among the oldest Residents / Genesis-era population.
2. **First use** — practical settlement around island resources, mine and market.
3. **Settlement** — Residents, trades and public life accumulate around the central town/market.

6. **Present day** — a lively layered town whose roads/gates connect to the three satellite future-worlds.

The source explicitly says this biography should drive **layout, props, architecture and Resident context**.

- streets serve market, homes, work and social life;
- onward island connections read as destinations, not dead-end technical sockets;
- a Joyride/race loop may touch or orbit the town but must not cut the living town apart;

# 8 · Relations that must be visible

These relations are recovered from existing KFB product intent and the 2026-09-20 failure lessons.

A world is not "objects at coordinates".

For each island the concept must visually answer:

- **History → terrain:** what made the island physically this way?
- **Function → settlement:** why did people settle here?
- **Resident role → building:** why does this building exist?
- **Daily activity → path:** who actually walks/drives this route and why?
- **Production/resource → market/storage:** what moves through the place?
- **Institution/power → landmark:** who built it, who uses it, what does it communicate?
- **Conflict/tension → scars/backstage:** what contradiction is visible in the environment?
- **World → Track:** why does the road pass through/around these areas?
- **Island → next island:** why is the connector here and what destination does it promise?

If these relations are not readable, the concept is not ready for geometry.
