## Purpose

Persist the first researched pool of free clay / claymation, plaster, sculpting-artifact, fabric and paper/cardboard texture donors for KFB DIY diorama terrain, props and characters.

This catalog does **not** replace the KFB Clay Asset Studio queue, K2 clay-material / relief owners, Blender material work, Asset Librarian or any game runtime. A donor being downloadable, seamless or CC0 does not make it KFB-production-approved.

## License buckets

### A · preferred runtime/repository donors
Use sources whose current published terms are CC0 / public-domain-style and allow redistribution inside KFB outputs.

- **Poly Haven** — CC0 library; preferred for neutral material foundations.
- **TextureCan** — CC0 texture/material library; preferred for craft surfaces and tool-mark donors.
- **CGBookcase** — CC0 textures; useful for fingerprints / micro-detail donors.

### B · look-dev donors only until packaging rights are rechecked
Commercial use may be allowed, but raw asset redistribution is restricted. Keep these isolated in look-dev; do not mirror their raw maps into the public KFB runtime without a license-specific packaging decision.

- **EmaceArt · Free Plasticine PBR Material**
- **BlenQube · Modelling Clay / clay material pack**

## KFB usage split

### Characters
Prefer: quiet matte base + weak micro-roughness + separate low-strength fingerprint/tool masks.  
Avoid: baking large cracks, obvious repeated fingerprints or directional trowel strokes across faces/bodies.

### Terrain / buildings
Prefer: Clay Plaster / Patterned Clay Plaster / rough trowel donors, with K2 relief/toolmix remaining the actual KFB deformation/material owner.

### Props
Use felt, burlap, fiber paper, cardboard and corrugated cardboard as explicit craft-material families rather than recoloring one generic surface.

## Current boundary

- no external texture has been copied into the repository;

- no second material registry is created;
