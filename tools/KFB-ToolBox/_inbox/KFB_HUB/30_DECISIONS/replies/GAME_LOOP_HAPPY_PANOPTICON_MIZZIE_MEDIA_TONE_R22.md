## 1. The tonal correction
**Not primarily dystopian horror.** Georg wants **Brave New World meets Idiocracy**: superficially optimistic entertainment, a warm service society, cheerful procedural absurdity, comforting consumer fantasy, polished news delivery and comically literal bureaucracy. Underneath, the world may suggest **Big Brother, Foucault's Panopticon, technocracy, Dark Enlightenment and surveillance capitalism** as subtle, sometimes contradictory references. These are different historical/philosophical concepts: use them as authoring lenses, not as one undifferentiated doctrine or omniscient political conclusion.

**Creative shorthand:** “Welcome to the happiest institution you are not permitted to leave.”

The primary register is **bright, welcoming, playful, materially comic**, with the unsettling parts revealed through the mismatch between what the system says and what residents do. A shock moment or ominous camera is a *counterpoint* to that surface, not the color palette for the whole game.

## 2. Intro / prison / maze framing (R21 continuation)
- Heavy genuine full-screen physical Curtain remains the start/loading cover, not a clay theatre stage or SVG/CSS imitation. Intro actor/mood selection, if retained, is spare and integrated by the existing owner.
- The amnesiac player wakes in a **pleasantly maintained cell**, rather than a torture-chamber preset. Cheerful lighting, tidy cardboard/plastic decor, excessive courtesy, contradictory access policies, an animated resident and visible actual Card sources.
- The jailed Clown and **`anti_rules_toolkit:1` The Rules Lawyer** are still the first-card *proposal*, not accepted revision of current Golden Journey. The Clown is not the authoritative truth-teller.
- Toy Soldier guard is an absurdly earnest, helpful enforcer of nonsensical service rules; the player may exploit a contradiction, distract him or use a noncombat route. Neither “obedience is wrong” nor “the escape proves enlightenment” should be hard-coded.
- Maze walls/HyperNorm projections may act as fashionable amenities, informational signage, advertisements and public-safety displays. Occasional movement, missed frames and duplicated camera labels create unease without making every surface glitchy. Preserve path/collider solvability and short duration.
- The Open World reveal should be **beautiful, colorful and inviting**, despite a lingering surveillance motif. No narrative claim that player is universally watched at every moment.

## 4. Visual/audio grammar: cheerful first, interference sparingly
**Normal world:** saturated KFB clay/card colors, cozy practical props, service decor, inviting stage tempo, diegetic pop/jingle and readable opening direction.
**Micro-disruption:** a camera tracks too long; a cheerful caption contradicts a visibly locked door; a newsfeed briefly shows incompatible information; a HyperNorm surface slips for a split second into red/black; distant Black-Fluff sky pupils track with curious, comic timing.

Rule of thumb (editorial, not a global shader constant): **one memorable dissonant event is worth more than a corridor full of glitches**. Use existing HyperNorm Rabbit-Hole/Autopilot montage grammar and source-owned Media/Audio, not a new permanent glitch shader or synthetic propaganda feed. Sound cues must remain controlled, captions available, with mute/reduced-motion/no-flash options.

The genuinely useful tension is between **happy presentation** and **lived consequence**. The color itself must not serve as a factual truth or ideological virtue meter; bright propaganda can obscure, an unpleasant reality may help, and a dissident mascot may run an equally commercial pitch.

## 6. Anti-preachiness / productive contrast
**Universe in a Nutshell**, **You choose something, ride the consequences**, **Conflict is King**, **Cognitive dissonance is the resonance space of Kayfabe Bizarro** remain the foundations. A player may laugh at the institution, enjoy its amenities, trust Mizzie, suspect brainwashing, reject the Clown or leave without metaphysical conclusions. NPCs are people with concrete interests, not philosopher-king lectures.

The artistic goal is a playable world full of contradictory claims and interesting actions, not a demonstrative exposé that rewards one sanctioned anti-surveillance belief. Don’t conflate fictional MKULTRA-like atmosphere with factual claims about a present-day hidden system.
