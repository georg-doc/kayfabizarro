## 0 · Short answer
The research was not forgotten in the **values** (J17 day, E50, sun direction, shadow recipe, one light writer, pooled practicals are all in WS1). What got lost were the **principles**. Four WS1 tricks were each asked for or allowed by WS0 itself: rim per actor (L2 Step 1), face fill (V-346 (1)), ground `toneMapped:false` (V-346 (3), V-378), card unlit / "pixels unchanged" (WS0_TO_WS1 "Kartenfläche bleibt toneMapped:false", G3). All four break the rules the earlier research set down: one world light response, no private character light, no bright seam, sunset = warm key against cool fill, torches are accent, material and light kept separate. That is why the result looks pasted-on. KayKit's own approach (as far as we documented it) is exactly that one-environment rule.

## 3 · KayKit's own lighting approach (what our files actually document)
- **Creator statement (S3, S14):** Kay's Godot tutorial covers World Environment + lighting, a day scene, **"turning that same scene into a night-time scene"**, and indoor/dungeon principles as a separate topic. Our audit got **0 frames / 0 numbers** (S14, 10.10.). WSA (S15, 11.10.) later cites one frame (7:20, Ambient Energy 0.75, Godot units, private capture `C_VIDEO_FRAME_CAPTURE.json`, not located). No Godot values may be ported.
- **KayKit promo images, measured by us (S4, S5):** one shadow-casting key from above ("alle Schatten in dieselbe Richtung"); candles/torches are **accent**, not the light source; interiors bright and readable; soft contact shadow under every prop; matte (roughness 0.95, the pack ships 0.45); ACES with exposure 1.15; emissive flame on its own layer; glow via bloom, not a lamp in the mesh; dark violet radial background.
- **KayKit asset convention (S3):** light-bearing parts use a separate `holiday_glow` material meant for engine emission → glow is a material property that the environment switches, not a light per prop.
- **Our lessons from it (S14 A–E):** the environment is scene state, not a character hack; night is a variant of the same scene; indoor is a different problem; check assets under neutral and production light; material and light stay orthogonal.
- **Did WS1 follow it?** Day/night as one scene with one shadow key: **yes**. "Characters consume scene light only": **no** (rim, faceFill, sheen tuned per actor). Torches as accent with a readable ambient layer: **half** (night ambient exists, but stage practicals are missing and the card self-lights). Emissive-material glow that follows night weight: **no** (halo sprites only, billboard without lamps). Matte material: **mostly** (sheen excepted). So the part of KayKit's approach that matters for the FAILs (one environment, nothing private) was not followed. This is not a "forgotten" document. Our own L2 brief overrode it.

## 4 · R2 vs. research and accepted decisions: gaps and contradictions
**R2 gets right (matches research):** one rig for all layers (S1/S8/S13/S14 A); no face self-illumination, no global rim (S7/S11); the card is lit (fixes C); billboard practicals (S26 highway donor); moods as rig settings; measurable grid; story modes only as tint (V-342).

**R2 is missing what our research already said:**
1. **Sheen** as a halo source (clay v8 `sheen 0.18 #fff1e0`, FB untuned). The R2 diagnosis names only Fresnel.
2. **Why gold ≈ day:** R2 says moods change "nur Sonnenwinkel/Schattenlänge", which is not accurate. Gold does change sun colour, −15 % sun and −23 % hemi. The real causes are the E50 midpoint design (S15) and Look B already warming day (#7/#8).
3. **Warm key against cool fill** for gold (S1, S2, S11, S12, S15, S17). R2 sets gold fill "wärmer". That repeats V-050 ("zu orange und zu dunkel") and S9 ("gesättigtes Licht färbt Knete um").
4. **Practicals as accent, not "Hauptquelle":** S4/S5/S1 say night readability comes from a weak ambient layer plus one key, with practicals as islands. R2 makes billboard light the main source at night.
5. **Hemisphere ground = stage `edgePalette`** (V-342/V-355). R2 says "Himmels- und Bodenfarbe der Palette V-305".
6. **One material response / two lighting models** (S11 §6j). R2 is silent on Meshy PBR + metal (V-370 PMREM only for metal), MeshPhysical sheen, additive halos `toneMapped:false`.
7. **Exposure lock while comparing light roles** (S15). R2 changes exposure per mood together with the rig.
8. **Contact/AO** (K1/D1 GTAO, S2, S4 "nächster ehrlicher Schritt SSAO"). R2 has no AO line, but P needs it.
9. **Chin/mouth readability without self-light:** S11 uses a named fill from slightly below ("hebt Kinnpartie"), and the hemi ground bounce from the stage does the same job. R2 removes faceFill and names no replacement.
10. **Night background/silhouette:** frame vs. sky/fog contrast (S4 dark violet radial background; fog = sky bottom at L×0.35). L-1 measures frame vs. card only, not frame vs. background.
11. **Grid gaps vs. accepted grids:** V-355 ≤15 % L/C per layer, G2 <0.1 % clipped figure pixels, G4 ≥2 light islands, 16:9 + 9:16 (V-329) are dropped.
12. **Tone-mapping alternatives in our own sources:** Lab = NeutralToneMapping (S13), ClayBound = AgX (S12), TinySkies = none plus albedo calibration (S11 S9d). R2 fixes ACES without testing why the ground looked better "ohne".

**R2 contradicts accepted decisions (needs explicit amendment, not a silent change):**
- **Tone mapping:** V-346 (3) and **V-378** ("Tonemapping bleibt ACES im Renderer (toneSplit)", 17.10.) and WS0_TO_WS1 17.10. ("sonst bricht `toneSplit`"). V-382 supersedes V-346 only. ## 5 · Top 10 corrections for LIGHT_MODEL_R2
1. **Silhouette:** remove actorRim **and** set figure sheen to measured ≤0.05 (or warm, low sheenColor) in the same step. Prove L-3 with sheen on/off toggles. Keep J17 back light #ffe6d6/0.6 **always on**, unsaturated (no violet shift). *S7, S8 §5, S10, S12, code clay-material.v8:423–424.*
2. **Gold = contrast, not saturation:** sun warm (E50 #ffd3ae or slightly warmer), low (≈16°), **hemi sky/fill cooler than day**, hemi ground from the stage; sky gradient changes in L as well as hue. Do not make fill warmer or darker. Measure L-4 against Look-B day, not Look-A day. *S1, S2, S11, S15 §3, S17, V-050.*
3. **Drop Look B's light-role saturation:** warmth comes from sky tint, stage edgePalette ground bounce and key warmth only; sun stays warm-white at day. *S9 §4, S18, V-174 (4).*
4. **Ambient from the stage:** `hemi.groundColor` = stage `edgePalette` (amphitheater: its palette ground role), intensity carried per mood. This also replaces faceFill for chin/mouth (bounce from below), plus optionally one named low fill row in the rig table with values per mood. *V-342 (1), V-355 (3), S11 pet-lighting.*
5. **One tone-mapping operator for every layer, chosen by test:** ACES vs. Neutral (Lab) vs. AgX (ClayBound) at a locked exposure on the same frame, with the ground L/C deviation vs. "ohne" as the metric. Supersede V-378's toneSplit sentence explicitly. *S10, S12, S13, S15, V-346 (2), V-378.*
6. **Night as KayKit does it:** same scene; moon key + cool hemi give silhouettes readably (S4 night ratios as guidance, not values). Billboard lamps and delivered stage practicals are **islands** (G4 ≥2). Do not raise exposure to compensate.
