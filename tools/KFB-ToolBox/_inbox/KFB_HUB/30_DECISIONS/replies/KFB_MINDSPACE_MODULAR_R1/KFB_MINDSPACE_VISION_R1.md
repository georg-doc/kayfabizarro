## 2 · Product identity: one Mindspace, several entry points

**Working label:** `KFB Mindspace / Creative Resonance Room`. A *portable creative medium* with optional standalone form and KFB world-embedded form.

**Loop:** `Arrive → Select a mood or stay unguided → Settle/Observe → Drift or Explore → optional dialogue/reframe → Create/remember → Leave/return`. No mandatory breath/voice/script steps. The user may enter for only a minute, sit still, work on an actual creative problem, or enjoy a longer personal dream journey. Anything requiring proprietary NATHAL/Silva/Monroe training stays out of the original app.

**Potential session invitations, NOT prescriptions or performance scores:**
- **Still Garden:** quiet forest, pond, lantern/garden light, slow foliage, small touches/ambient details, no voice necessary.
- **Focus Chamber:** uncluttered framing; breathing visual as optional personal aid; sparse sound bed, no „entrained“ EEG status.
- **Open Studio / Rick Rubin creative listening:** open attention to emerging materials; choose and rearrange 2–3 visual cues; allow silence and unconstrained idea generation; only later edit and refine.
- **Dream Voyage:** surreal transitions through imaginary architecture / shifting interiors / memory landscapes; goal is imaginative travel, not “lucid dream achieved.”
- **Vision Field:** rich psychedelic colour, mandala-like refraction, tesseract/caustic/splat illusions; sensory intensity controlled by user, reduced-motion/still variant, no automated rapid-flash.
- **Two Witnesses / Creative Counselor:** optional voices/personas aid attention and alternative interpretation; no claim to decode real thoughts or to conduct NATHAL's Supra-Dialog®.
- **Optional Curious Physicist lens:** playfully inspect the very geometry, interference patterns and shader models producing the space; scientifically described acoustics/optics kept distinct from metaphorical dimensions.

No rigid taxonomy has yet been selected. Each setting is a **recipe for mood, visuals, audio, dialogue cadence and optional activity**, not a separate render system. The same garden could host Stillness, curiosity, creative writing or an absurd KFB encounter. Later existing six D6 modes can colour **stories** if relevant, without transforming this into six initiation ranks.

## 5 · Audio and breath: intent-first, precise but not over-clinical

**Breath-as-option:** Existing Lovable shell is preferred donor. Offer ordinary, customizable breathing guides or silence; current exact breath patterns **must come from verified Lovable source**. Do not import unsupervised holotropic/hyperventilatory programming into a generic mixed-media artifact just because Grof was discussed separately.

**Schumann theme:** Geophysically the Schumann resonance is an electromagnetic standing-wave family in the Earth–ionosphere cavity, excited by lightning, with fundamental around 7.8 Hz (NASA: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/10891). The value is **not a directly audible atmospheric tone**. An optional audio preset where stereo-carrier `f_R-f_L≈7.83 Hz` is therefore a **creative acoustic analogy**, not physical playback of Earth's field or evidence of physiology syncing to the planet. Keep the user's meaningful personal association without scientizing it.

**Binaural/audio:** Two intentionally different carrier tones to L/R headphones can yield perceived binaural beating at their difference; no EEG measurement is inferred. A PLOS ONE 2023 systematic review of 14 EEG studies found inconsistent EEG entrainment effects (5 aligned, 8 contradictory, 1 mixed); the audio remains useful as listening texture/experimental option (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286023).

**Modal autonomy:** breathing visual guides, headphones, background audio, voice and screen imagery can independently be on/off. The visitor can use **just garden**, **just sound**, **just breathing**, or combined imagery/counselor. For a private exploratory prototype avoid regulatory-first complexity, while keeping simple ergonomic basics: consent for microphone/sound, Start/Stop, sensible volume, pause/return, reduced motion.
