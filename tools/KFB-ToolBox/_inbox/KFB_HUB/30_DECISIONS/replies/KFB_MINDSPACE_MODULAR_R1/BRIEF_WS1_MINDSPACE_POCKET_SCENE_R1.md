## Experiments – open alternatives

**W1 · Through a window:** On an ordinary drive/walk path player notices a *caustic or splat-like* sphere/pool/edge containing **visible spatial depth**. First proof can be **drive-by only**, as earlier Gutter/Splat brief explicitly allows. Does it look authored, surprising and truly different from a flat water texture? This is a **visible scenic seam**, with no collision/entrance required.

**W2 · Enter Garden:** Same source-created entrance can open a little Pocket Garden using meshes in the existing R3F world owner. Safe player return and asset lifecycle; treat as bounded instance, not world fragmentation origin. Only collision-bearing meshes carry traversal. Compare: continuous scene vs owner-managed swap, based on actual performance.

**W3 · Seamless dream transition:** Source-backed Gutter / stylized threshold disguises transition (screen wipe/caustic/shader/paper fold), player sees depth and parallax, the interior can be *apparently* larger than exterior using authored scene tricks. Do not demand 4D/tesseract math or a new physics engine.

**W4 · Rare procedural encounter:** The authored Mindspace recipe may be a rare biome/POI/event attached through validated WFC constraints; time/day/weather can vary if owned. No random room spam and no disappearing vital story paths.

**W5 · Variants:** Still Garden, creative sketch-room, crystalline observatory, memory-gutter archive, psychedelic tesseract-like visual environment. Each variant is a different *recipe*, not a different owner or processor architecture.

**Performance test:** actually measure fps/draw calls/GPU/memory, input-to-visual lag, audio interruption and restore, collision, seed reproducibility, off-screen dormancy and asset loading on existing WS1 acceptance hardware. Numbers remain `UNKNOWN` until tested; no fake target FPS in this brief.
