## 0. Design decision for this R2

Six mindspaces are **six curated applications / experiential recipes**, not:
- six separate technical runtimes or websites;
- six Heim dimensions, consciousness grades or stages of initiation;
- one-to-one obligatory mappings of KFB Story Mode faces, Fluff colors, frequencies or claimed EEG bands;
- default psychedelic renders, stock neon particle tunnels, remade KFB branding, or six forced breathing patterns.

**Same reusable shell** can run `screensaver` (automatic slow camera/light), `guided experience` (optional breathing/audio/counselor), `explorable pocket` (later KFB world-hosted scene), `creative intervention` (self-authored image/music/notes). The user may disable audio, breath, voice, camera movement or splat effects independently. A chosen mood and a mode of consumption are **orthogonal**.

**Design thesis:** A user may listen to one soundbed while looking into a garden, later traverse a dreamworld, and later ask a Counselor a question while seeing the same environment transformed. The imagery may be beautiful and mysterious without presuming consciousness scores or paranormal verification.

## 2. Six playable/ambient recipes — content and why they are distinct

### M1 · The Living Garden
**Entry image:** Approaching a detailed moss-lined curved footpath, hearing realistic subtle water and birds; color with natural depth; perhaps a genuinely source-verified garden Splat in the background.  
**Screensaver:** very slow orbit/dolly in the same environment; no compulsory instruction text.  
**Guided:** user selects an actual verified breathing guide from the Lovable donor, or keeps it entirely off.  
**In-world:** an authored grove entry near an existing POI, return always available; no quest gate, no need for harvesting Fluff.  
**Primary proof:** source Splat/mesh garden seen individually and side-by-side against an original H13 composition; clear foreground/background depth.

### M2 · The Creative Atelier
**Entry image:** A painterly workroom with authentic material textures and floating cards/images arranged as a delicate theatrical sculpture.  
**Screensaver:** a handful of authored sequences in which images rearrange without interrupting music.  
**Creative:** user chooses actual assets or a personal safe-to-use upload; prompts invite noticing small differences, not clinical interpretation; can copy a small idea sketch.  
**In-world:** world object points to existing Almanac/Quill when available, never writes new shadow Almanac data.

### M3 · The Mirror Chamber
**Entry image:** Beautiful atmospheric reflective room and an **original** symmetrical image that slowly evolves; strong focus on user's agency.  
**Guided:** Counselor may invite two different interpretations. It neither pretends to see inner images nor pronounces their hidden personality meaning.  
**Sound:** low-amplitude bed reduces automatically during speech, voice optional.  
**In-world:** a resident/Academy device can satirize certainty, but the real user never faces a required occult exam.

### M4 · The Dream Passage
**Entry image:** Distant world visible through a veiled portal; smooth transition into forest/waterfall/sky island.  
**Screensaver:** curated 5–15 min camera loops, authored intro→drift→soft landing; durations editorial starting points, not proven comfort targets.  
**Guided:** counselor offers an optional word/prompt at scene boundaries, not continuous narration.  
**In-world:** portal scene renders as visual consumer of current WS1 owner; collisions only from conventional mesh/physics, not the splats.

### M5 · The Fractal Vision
**Entry image:** Deep, intricate, visually pleasant recursive organic geometry, color as live composition, no preset stock neon or random-effects soup.  
**Screensaver:** non-flashing smooth morph; allows standalone visual pleasure.  
**Tech:** test existing H13 source first. A Splat is for immersive volume; math fractal shape is generated separately, and 4D Gaussian methods should not be assumed to solve editable recursive topology.  
**In-world:** surreal authored pocket; LOD/performance/still fallback necessary.

### M6 · The Threshold Observatory
**Entry image:** A very large contemplative observatory, volumetric light, symbolic patterns, precise readable source labels and no fabricated science.  
**Research/fiction switch:** documentary links for Monroe/Gateway and uncertain DMT-laser assertions versus authored KFB/Demon Lord story motifs, never confused.  
**Screensaver:** unusual star geometry, slow cosmic camera, sparse environmental sound.  
**In-world:** lore-rich room, not a required spiritual level for progression.

## 3. Real Gaussian Splat modes and technical realism

### Render strategy by required property

| Requested visible effect | First proof candidate | Use of real 3DGS? | Practical fallback / caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realistic garden/forest with parallax | source-verified forest/garden captured from multiple views | **Good candidate** | Mesh walkable paths/foliage plus equirectangular sky, if genuine Splat has gaps/artefacts |
| Atmospheric interior/observatory | pinned scanned/authored room | **Good scenic candidate** | conventional geometry + HDR backdrop |
| Deep-looking portal/window | off-screen scenic shell or pre-rendered lightfield + surface refraction | **Optional** | RenderTexture/2.5D/mesh with real parallax; splat is **not** portal logic |
| Edited living fractal geometry / kaleidoscope | H13 authored fraktal/screen effects and WebGL mesh/shader | **Secondary scenic role** | a precomputed 4DGS sequence only after technical source and GPU proof |
| Precise writing/codes/symbols | crisp world-space mesh, canvas text or UI overlay | **No**, need legible semantics | splat ghosting/holes and low resolution may make text unreadable |
| Player movement & touching objects | existing WS1 R3F/Three/Rapier shapes and scene owner | **No physics ownership** | don't infer traversability/collision from rendered splat points |
| Screensaver guided camera | source Splat with controlled camera keyframes + track/choreography | **Very suitable** | cheaper offline rendered video/2.5D if mobile GPU weak |
| Slow visual modulation to music | H13 audiovisual visual grammar; isolate unmodified H13 first | **Splat scene can be background** | texture/render load, sync measured separately |

**Reality check from external research:** Kerbl et al., *3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering*, SIGGRAPH 2023 introduced a fast photorealistic representation (https://repo-sam.inria.fr/fungraph/3d-gaussian-splatting/). Guanjun Wu et al. CVPR 2024 4DGS handles some temporal dynamic scenes, **not automatically locally editable fractal worlds** (https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wu_4D_Gaussian_Splatting_for_Real-Time_Dynamic_Scene_Rendering_CVPR_2024_paper.html).

## 5. Relation to researched consciousness and esoteric models – labels before beliefs

**Method and practice:** NATHAL, Silva and Jung may inspire *imagery and dialogue*, but private/trademarked training sequences are not cloned. Rick Rubin's `The Creative Act` inspires deliberate noticing/open associations without turning his book into a six-step certification protocol.

**Historical/programmatic:** Monroe/Gateway Focus-level vocabulary and audio technology describe real provider programs and phenomenology, NOT tested mechanism guaranteeing astral travel. Do not import copyrighted proprietary Gateway/Hemi-Sync recordings. The Monroe Institute itself notes that Hemi-Sync and Monroe Institute have become separate organizations: https://support.monroeinstitute.org/support/solutions/articles/70000659271-hemi-sync-in-expand-app.

**Speculative science:** Burkhard Heim and dimension metaphors are not verified six-mode physical gateways. The Schumann resonance is an EM atmospheric cavity phenomenon; an `~7.83 Hz` binaural audio *difference* is an acoustic artistic analogy, not playback of the geophysical field (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/10891). PLOS ONE 2023 systematic review reports mixed EEG-entrainment evidence across 14 studies (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286023).

**DMT and laser/code claims:** peer-reviewed DMT human experience/EEG studies exist (e.g. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41468116/ , 2026, 19 participants), **but no peer-reviewed direct support for “DMT laser reveals objective hidden numerical code” was identified in this R2**. Use it at most as a consciously fictional/interpretive visual lead until exact pre-registration, measurement methods and controls are independently established. No psychedelic drug instructions or inference that a website reproduces psychedelic effects.

**Rorschach-like:** user-driven original inkblot visuals are art and conversation prompts, not actual Rorschach test plates or personality assessment.
