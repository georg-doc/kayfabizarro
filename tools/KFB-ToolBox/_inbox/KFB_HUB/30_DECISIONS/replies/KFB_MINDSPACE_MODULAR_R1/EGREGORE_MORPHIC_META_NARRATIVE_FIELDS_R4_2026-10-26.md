## 1 · Desired experience (Georg-originated, separate from proposals)

- Mindscapes as visible experiential **thought-spaces / collective egregores / morphogenetic or morphic fields**: embodied metanarratives that NPCs may encounter, explore, imitate, join, nurture, resist, weaponize, parody or abandon.
- Their spatial presence and apparent size in terrain can change as NPCs and meaningful acts engage their ideas. Content might include **real sourced poetry, literary fragments, Cards, public slogans and propaganda language**, which may influence a Resident's conceptual repertoire and later **Triplet selection** without rewriting the original source.
- Appearance may be planned/procedurally placed Mindscape POIs or an extraordinary **meteorite-like impact** of an Egregore into the terrain, revealing a surreal crashed narrative territory rather than an ordinary building.
- Men in Black, **Hunky & Dory** (user said Hanky, existing character name Hunky), FrizzleBob and other KFB actors can have distinct projects: contain, cultivate, profit, appropriate, study, explore, contest or repair. No automatic villain/good alignment for every field.
- Desired KFB tone: surreal, allegorical, politically satirical and occasionally genuinely lyrical; responsive world with consequences. Avoid generic one-liners, random metaphor soup or identical portals for every idea.

### Two concepts to retain as related but nonidentical

- **Mindscape** = subjective, enterable *experience and scene*; can be solitary, calm, imaginative, or authored even without followers. A Garden does not have to be an Egregore.
- **Egregore** = persistent *collective narrative identity/influence relation*; may manifest as a Sphere, meteor, staged institution, poster ecosystem or invisible social attractor. Not every Egregore must own a walkable Mindscape, and a Mindscape could host two incompatible Egregores.

## 2 · Intellectual sources — interpretation rather than physics contract

- Collective thought-form/egregore as described in esoteric tradition: https://www.llewellyn.com/encyclopedia/term/egregore and https://www.theosophical.org/publications/quest-magazine/egregores-the-occult-entities-that-watch-over-human-destiny . His account describes organizing morphic fields, inherited memory and resonance. It is a **hypothesis, not an experimentally established game-world physics law**; a KFB world may treat it allegorically or internally true according to lore without claiming scientific demonstration.
- Complexity/social contagion has a separate computational basis: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-28958-6 and https://ndg.asc.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Centola-2015-AJS.pdf . Multi-source confirmation and heterogeneous thresholds are useful inspirations for models of adoption/resistance; do not equate social influence mechanisms with paranormal causation.
- Optional conceptual cousins (not new canon): Jung's collective unconscious/archetypes, Dawkins's meme as cultural replicator, Gramsci's hegemony, Debord's spectacle, McLuhan's media ecology and Brecht's visible stage conventions. These are inspiration choices; avoid flattening distinct schools into pseudoscientific synonyms.

## 4 · Assistant design options — an editable, contestable field, not mind control

### 4.1 Possible Egregore lifecycle (story beats, never mandatory rigid ladder)

| Possible state | Visible and social consequence | Possible transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seed / unobserved | Rumor, strange phrase, Card motif, abandoned set | Witness or discovery |
| Falling / planted | Meteor-like drop, authored POI or broadcast tower | Valid safe site in world/terrain |
| Manifest | Small sphere, collage halo, altered prop cluster, localized sound | Participating Residents, curated events |
| Established / contested | Supporters, skeptics and opportunists inhabit overlapping meanings | Reinforcement or counterargument |
| Fissured / co-opted | Rival groups use the same label differently; two visual readings of one object | Reinterpretation or capture |
| Dormant / remembered | Remnant, ruin, echo, poem, archaeological clue | Renewed attention or long absence |
| Transformed / liberated | New meaning, hybrid zone, repair or artistic reinvention | Author-/player-shaped resolution |

Do not enforce growth as one monotonically increasing energy bar. A field can be influential yet spatially small, visible yet not widely believed, large but hollow, or beloved and challenged. Popularity, conviction, geographical reach, evidence, institutional amplification and NPC impact remain separable variables. An egregore can survive as a forgotten relic or be split into incompatible reinterpretations.

### 4.2 Resonance/affiliation is MULTIPLE and reversible

- Residents may **witness**, **quote**, **enact**, **identify**, **doubt**, **satirize**, **oppose**, **exit**, or **appropriate** a field. Mere proximity is *exposure*, not proof of adoption; entering a Mindscape is not automatic endorsement.
- Resident profiles retain identity, motives, occupation, social edges and prior memory. The candidate field yields **context/tags/weight changes** to existing knowledge/Triplet selection; never writes raw catchphrases directly into a canonical NPC pool or rewrites Player belief.
- More attention can increase visibility/reach, but criticism/satire can also amplify visibility while reducing adherence. Model attention ≠ conviction ≠ truth ≠ legitimacy ≠ activity.
- NPCs should vary in threshold, trust, relationship and interests, with a path for partial membership, selective use, independent dissent and deliberate resistance. Avoid all NPCs changing wording simultaneously upon crossing a collider.
- Residents can carry **multiple competing fields**, including poetic versus propagandistic interpretations; use existing relationship/Lean Memory and author-proven Cards for causal, reversible local effects.
- Content from poetry/lyrics/propaganda must have explicit original source ID/license/rights, exact excerpt and interpretive label. Public Domain/CC0 assets preferred when permitted; do not invent or automatically quote copyrighted lyrics in game build. Real propaganda content is clearly an in-world voice/claim, never the World Truth owner.

Card source may be 'THE EGREGORE', but the fictional world's interpretation of its majority-vote POWER must not literally modify original Card records or independent physical/geographic truth.

### 4.4 Influence computation: proposed, simple to benchmark

- Author narrative relevance, neighborhood/social relation graph, repeat encounters, credibility/conviction tags and authored counterpoint events can feed **slow/event-driven** influence summaries per region and Resident. A social-threshold/complex-contagion comparator is preferable to 'N followers = sphere radius'.
- Geographical **presence/influence radius**, visual sphere radius, narrative reach through media and time-persistent memory are four different quantities; World may use smoothed visual masks/LOD driven by the summary **without duplicating physics or propagating on every render frame**.
- Use reproducible seed + version, sparse event log and derived caches; fixed input/event order and profile -> same engagement summary; no per-frame all-pairs NPC graph scan. The actual World scheduling/performance budget and serialization design belong to the receiving owners.
- A local Egregore POI is not a new hard biome covering terrain. WFC/Genii-Loci placement must obey slope, traversal, protected OSM landmarks, Track roads, collision, bridge/river and accessible entry/return. The 'meteorite hit' is VFX and optional terrain-change **request to existing single Surface Truth owner**, not permission to write a crater mesh or terrain heights independently.
- Cheap outer layers: one atmospheric shell/decals/paper silhouettes/media fragments/voice cues; bounded inner detail only when observed/entered, with LOD/culling and hibernation. Actual H13, Splat or globe source shown in isolation before use.

## 7 · Creative connections worth keeping open

1. **Narrative weather:** language forms move through world lighting/audio/props rather than an overlay HUD; an Egregore might first appear as a recurring illustrated motif on actual Paper Pop-up or HyperNormalisation billboards, then a tangible landscape.
2. **Egregore meteorology:** impacts as rare *story events* with a recoverable provenance trail (who placed/recalled/manufactured it), visible ejecta letters/cards/paper panels and bounded cosmetic terrain disturbance. Real crater contact optional and owner-negotiated.
3. **Memetic ecology:** narratives can coexist, hybridize, parasitize or leave ruins, but WFC only arranges allowed local stage/architecture; it cannot establish factual truth or override OSM source identity.
4. **Three witnesses:** different Residents respond to same manifestation using their roles and memories; the player hears three meaningfully different interpretations. This is better than generic 'infect all NPCs' behavior.
5. **Counterfactual archaeological strata:** if interest collapses, posters fade and stage supports remain; Almanac holds the difference between witnessed event, propaganda claim and player interpretation.
6. **Theater of the majority:** physical facades respond to voting and institutional power; the behind-stage view reveals their construction. An Egregore can become a Brechtian world object as per Paper Facade canon, not a second destruction engine.
7. **Field inside field:** a nested Sphere/three-globe visualizes competing narratives, but no requirement for real spherical terrain or 20 concurrently rendered globes.
8. **Protopia small influence:** a minor repair ritual by a handful of Residents may produce durable local improvement without conquering the entire map. Meaningful consequences outrank sphere size.
