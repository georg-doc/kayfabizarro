## 1 · Georg's corrected intention, without reinterpretation
The player wakes **without memory inside a Mindscape / Egregore / morphogenetic-field / filter-bubble sphere**. FrizzleBob the rabbit is the proposed initial playable avatar. The enclosure is a womb-like immersive sphere, not just a tutorial room. It contains excessive **trash, propaganda, fearmongering, manufactured narratives and media turbulence**: the Hypernormalisation Engine as an experiential world. The player has to **move / swim through** it, encounter NPCs and real KFB Cards, discover or traverse adjacent Mindscapes/Egregores and recognize that the apparent total reality is a bounded editorial enclosure. A strange, Truman-Show-like **“NO EXIT” door** on the inside of the sphere provides a visible paradox, but must be designed as an original KFB object, not a recreation of any copyrighted film shot. Eventually the player crosses/leaves the enclosing sphere and reaches the **Gutter Space of the KFB Multiverse**, where cross-Mindscape discovery and later open-world terrain/vehicle freedom emerge.

The strongest one-line pitch:
> **You do not begin outside the simulation to learn how it works. You wake inside an opinion that thinks it is the universe.**

“Without memory” describes FrizzleBob's *subjective first-person ignorance*, NOT a wipe of current NPC history, authoritative world state, or existing Lean Memory/Almanac records.

## 2 · Fundamental gameplay: the player's epistemic escape is a spatial activity
- **Body in a medium.** Swim/float/stride through volumetric-looking propaganda collage; H13's *actual existing imagery and animation* is the source-first possible visual donor. Surface/splats/particles/mesh/physics must be selected by genuine visual performance proof, not assumed.
- **Narrative currents.** Broadcasts and slogans appear as authored directional currents or attention attractors: a repeated headline can literally pull the camera's gaze or make a corridor feel closed. Keep actual camera and movement input with existing owner: use visual/audio/environmental cues or light forces mediated by its API, not involuntary camera hijacks.
- **Filter bubble behavior.** Two witnesses can see or interpret the same genuine Card differently, depending on their source-backed knowledge/Triplet repertoire. **Do not distort the underlying immutable Card identity or invent its wording.** The local scene can distort *framing, repetition, cropping, audio and prominence* so players physically sense selective attention.
- **Frames inside the sphere.** Fragments, Card facets, media membranes, projections and mini-scenes float past; the player can follow a signal into a *neighbor Mindscape* or inspect the seam revealing a second point of view.
- **World replies to curiosity.** Inspecting a claim, following a Resident, touching a Card, refusing a loud call, doubling back, or comparing two accounts changes how the environment visibly reacts. There is no correct ideology quiz and no “skepticism meter”.
- **Gutter ≠ void.** After the exit, the *space between narratives* becomes an actual traversable connective medium with distant spheres, torn comic gutters, Card paths and authentic world entrances. This is not necessarily a safe objective truth; the player has escaped a frame, not achieved omniscience.

## 3 · Womb, broadcast and threshold: three distinct dramaturgical layers
**A. Narrative womb (opening interior):** dark/soft-lit, rounded, thick membrane; heartbeat-like *sound design as an artistic metaphor*, slow respiratory swell, distant broadcast voices. A short offscreen typographic cliché — **“It was a dark and stormy night...”** — unfolds as an authored comic lettering layer. Immediately contrast it with the actual experience: the inside may be stormy with information rather than meteorological rain. Player sees their existing FrizzleBob actor and gradually recognizes boundaries.

**B. Hypernormalisation bloom (playable middle):** contradictory floating media, old Cards, tacky ads, warnings, fake exits, competing NPC guidance, increasingly unstable scale and apparent depth. The density is deliberately staged, not always maximal: **quiet gaps and readable islands** are required so authored content is decipherable. Audio layers recede while someone speaks (existing KFB Audio owner); no blanket alarm noise or automatically looped sensory assault.

**C. “NO EXIT” boundary (turning point):** an impossible door fixed to the inner shell; its frame points toward nowhere, perhaps visible from far away. The door need not be literally locked. Physically approaching, following its contour, looking *behind* it, or noticing a gap in the frame reveals an alternate geometry of escape. Strongest interpretation: **the door is a piece of scenery pretending to be the only boundary, while the comic Gutter starts at the seam around it**. If the door eventually opens, the reveal should be experienced physically, not solved by selecting an approved interpretation.

**Motif:** the enclosing membrane can be simultaneously nurturing, claustrophobic, comedic and dangerous; don't reduce it to a generic neon prison, brainwashing machine or political lecture. The player can encounter beauty, kindness, absurdity and genuine knowledge *inside* it too.

**Important staging correction to prior draft:** The **Mindsphere precedes the Gutter**, not vice versa. A narrow room/panel is **not** the starting premise. ## 6 · Why the mechanic could feel original
1. **Traversal is interpretation made physical:** selecting a focus changes perceived affordances, not the immutable facts of a Card.
2. **Multiple bounded 'totalities':** you can emerge from one sphere and re-enter a second whose sky, gravity, sound and shared assumptions differ, but both inhabit the same wider KFB navigation.
3. **Asymmetric inhabitants:** some Resident characters do not know the shell is a shell; some earn a living keeping it intact; some help FrizzleBob; some legitimately like the place; Hunky/Dory/MIB have source-provenanced faction goals rather than one universal secret script.
4. **Egregore dynamics later:** recurring Cards/phrases and many NPC actions can change the sphere's visible presence as proposal-level social field modeling, while a personal artistic Mindspace may exist without Egregore followers. Do not collapse all six creative Mindspaces into propaganda traps.
5. **Actual Comic Closure:** there is no monologue that stitches the meaning back together after the escape. Player constructs a story from physical gaps and mismatched testimony.
6. **Re-entry with knowledge:** when FrizzleBob returns to a sphere, the content need not be deleted. The player simply recognizes its framing; the first encounter has changed them.

Strong for comparative epistemology, but more portal/camera/state transitions, thus future extension not required for first proof.
