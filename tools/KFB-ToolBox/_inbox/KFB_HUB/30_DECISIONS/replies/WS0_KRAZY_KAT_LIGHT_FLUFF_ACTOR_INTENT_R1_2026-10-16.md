## 1. Design North Star — warmth, not one uniform color
Georg chooses a strongly *Krazy Kat* / George Herriman (1935–44 color Sundays) inspired **warm cozy orange–coral–red scenic atmosphere as the preferred DEFAULT LOOK**. Include clay texture, warm sun, reddish mesas, ochre sand/paper, soft crimson accents, occasional cool/blue/violet contrast and spontaneous surreal panel-to-panel daylight or scenery switches. This is a LOOK DIRECTION and original KFB color composition, not a claim that all Krazy Kat strips have a single orange palette or permission to reuse scanned strip art.
Research refs: https://www.taschen.com/en/books/comics/41426/george-herriman-krazy-kat-the-complete-color-sundays-1935-1944/ ; https://tomlennon.com/krazy-kat-started-brick/ .
**Do not introduce a new palette owner or overwrite V-305:** current V-305 kfb-cartoon-palette.js is the ONE OKLCH color generator; V-331 already explicitly approved fromStoryMode(story-mode of Card,0), Story color on stage ground, mesh albedo 18% tint V-286. Thus warm-default versus accepted per-Card ground-color behavior is a **real WS0 design choice**. Preferred bounded comparison: A = current V-305/V-329 exactly, B = same V-305 role hues + warmer motivated sun, ground bounce, props and sky *within the host* while still retaining approved StoryMode ground for each real Card. If Georg then wants warm ground independently of StoryMode, WS0 must explicitly decide an amendment, not pretend it is already approved.
Time and mood remain different axes: neutral/day, golden/cozy and story-night from V-329 are the current lighting presets; tragic, comic, absurd, heroic, mystical, forbidden are existing story hues/Fluff identity, **not six replacement global world lights**. Retain clean readable printed Card pixels, eyes, mouths, bubbles; avoid plastic gloss and globally graded PDFs.

## 2. Every character has an authored LIGHT IDENTITY, not a shadow-casting light
Georg wants EACH KFB character to be individually stagable in tragic/heroic/mystical/absurd/etc. lighting, even in ToolBox previews and MVP dialogue scenes.
**Proposal: light-intent/profile reference** for each source-owned Actor: key-direction preference, warm/cool fill, rim/outline color, intensity ceiling, optional motivated practical at/near a prop, mode cue, focus/priority, contact-shadow preference and camera readability. A personal scene “light source” can be *perceptual/illustrative* (colored rim shader, light card, baked prop glow or short-lived pooled actor focus), not necessarily an active real PointLight.
Existing V-329 limits are binding unless WS0 revises them: ONE WS1 host owns key/hemi/fill/sky/fog/exposure; sun is ONLY real shadow caster; 4–6 shadowless local PointLights shared by ALL stage practicals; other fixture glows are emissive/fake; face shadowOff, each actor gets proper contact shadow. A per-actor light does NOT override world rig or reserve an expensive lamp each for crowds. Actor focus near camera/talking actor can temporarily allocate one available pooled light, then release; assess frame time rather than assume.
Possible editorial cues, not fixed color tables: tragic = cool relief vs warm edge; comic/absurd = contrasting bright accent; heroic = warm angled key/clear silhouette; mystical = violet halo, local emitter; forbidden = magenta/wine practical, readable face; **all preserve scenic warm base and V-305 story role colors**.

## 4. From world pickup to UI pip — one authored continuous motion
Proposed smallest believable physical/comic sequence: glowing clay Fluff bead bounces into collectible height → actor/player overlaps trusted pickup collider → authoritative Fluff inventory receives event immediately exactly once → a **visual copy** follows a smooth projected trajectory from its real world position using the existing StageOrbitCam into the chosen pip anchor of the existing HUD → a short squash/overshoot/two-bounce settle and subtle slot acknowledgment. The source world bead is not teleported visually into the UI until the pickup is confirmed. Do not create a second inventory owner, camera, rAF or physics system. Offscreen source or interrupted shot = short HUD fade/scale fallback. Reduced-motion = instantaneous accessible update. Rapid batch pickups may aggregate/speed rather than stacking 30 DOM animations. Minimize readability/occlusion; do not cover Card, Bubble or in-flight 3D target arrow.
Visual animation is a consumer of inventory truth (dedupe/replay-safe), not the source of entitlement. Save/reload retains correct inventory without replaying visual pickups or multiplying rewards. Same screen-stage projection works at 16:9 and 9:16; verify color-blind-friendly shapes or text/count assist (color alone cannot carry inventory meaning).

Existing Fluff concept v0.3 proposes small recipes with High/Low mixing, and v0.5 a Witch's cauldron; current GAME_LOOP_R1.md already lists crafting missing real Cards through Fluff and NPC request. Seeded dice/StoryMode roll should choose an enjoyable *presentation/twist*, not arbitrary denial after spending acquired Fluff, absent WS0 acceptance. If combination unknown, no hidden loss. Almanac records source/provenance.

## 7. StoryMode × biome × Card × stage = weighted WORLD IDEA, not a new deterministic source
A biome, NPC, deck or Card zone may *prefer* tragic, comic, absurd, heroic, mystical or forbidden Fluff through weighted authoring. Character personality and profession may influence possible encounters. The V-292/V-305 colors remain fixed identities; local light intentionally follows dramatic role and environment. Story color linked to Card currently drives ground per V-331. Changes to world default palette require explicit WS0 decision. Do not automatically generate the same Fluff on every island, treat High/Low as morality, or equate orange light with Heroic-only resources.
Propose one small comparison before declaring balance/rarity; do not invent exact spawn weights or rates. The “fractal” insight is that one mode can appear as Card, world tint, Resident mannerism, Fluff particle and optional light cue with SAME source identity—not five autonomous data generators.
