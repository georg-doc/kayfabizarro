# KFB Game Loop R15 · Universe in a Nutshell / No Philosopher King
2026-10-29 · GEORG CREATIVE CORRECTION · editorial design only, no runtime or new canonical SSOT.

Owner: existing KFB_HUB/30_DECISIONS/replies, Draft PR #460. Georg/WS0 alone decides acceptance into current Bigger Picture/Global Intent. R15 **clarifies and overrides the *didactic implications* of R14**, while preserving useful care for personhood, sources and character consequences.

## 1 · The precise correction
The goal is **not** to create a subtle machine that teaches players critical thinking, enlightenment, tolerance, empathy or a desired political/philosophical position. Even if no explicit alignment/education score exists, invisible reward weighting, uniformly approving NPC responses, optimistic narrative payoffs, recommended Card chains or "good" unlock paths can covertly impose one lesson.

Georg's *personal artistic-humanistic hope* for curiosity and empathy is legitimate **author motivation**, but is **not** an in-world progression target, victory criterion, behavioral nudge or player-learning KPI. The player's and NPCs' interpretive closure cannot be planned or measured in advance. The poetic “2–4%” metaphor must not turn into an evaluation requirement or hidden influence design.

**North Star reframing:** **KFB · Universe in a Nutshell** is an open satirical transmedia microcosm: characters, institutions, worldviews, contradictory ideas, flaws, virtues, Card claims, media performances and relations coexist. Players can encounter, stage, interrupt, ignore, misunderstand, share, suppress, help, argue or pass on. KFB neither arbitrates which worldview wins nor assumes the explorer has escaped Plato's cave.

## 2 · Do not script liberation
**Plato's cave is a compelling situation, not a moral staircase.** Leaving the cave can provoke welcome, anger, ridicule, rescue attempts, isolation, a return visit or indifference. The model cannot pre-know what human meaning the player assigns. Do not secretly route all paths to emancipation, disillusionment or becoming more reasonable. Nor assume the person staying inside is deficient.

The authentic play question is not “Did the player understand the lesson?” but “What happened when these particular characters, Cards and physical circumstances met?”

## 3 · Model constraints, not moral destinations
Give source-authored NPCs **particular histories, jobs, interests, social ties, anxieties, strengths, knowledge, partial ignorance and situational objectives**. Reuse existing Resident/ChatterBox/Lean Event Memory and canonical Card source owners. Examples of contingent reactions: warn, agree, bargain, attack, help, joke, withhold, retreat, remain silent. They follow character motives and actual event conditions; no universal enlightenment/healing arc. A character is not a teaching proxy.

Represent **physical/social consequences** (a covered poster remains hidden, a damaged wall needs repair, someone remembers a favor or insult). Do not equate cover with bad, reveal with good, healing with ideological liberation or discomfort with growth. Current world owners handle damage and save state; do not add a simulator of a player's actual mind.

Distinguish **source record** (exact Card identity, provenance and direct claims), **in-world character belief**, **narrator performance**, and **player interpretation**. Editorial factual verification protects the integrity of quoted real-world claims; it must not act as a diegetic omniscient judge of all metaphysical, ethical or fictional disputes. False equivalence is also not required: an unsupported empirical assertion does not become a fact by being playably voiced.

## 4 · Freedom requires consequential affordances
Keep **PULL, DON'T GATE**. The player may investigate or ignore; can be collector, Men in Black, Face, Heel, indifferent traveler or unexpectedly collaborative. No compulsory tutorial, ideological faction loyalty, Kayfabulation answer or source-research ritual.

Choices need material response without didactic scoring: changes in contact, visibility, access to particular people, relationships, memories, local reputation, entertainment or physical environment may occur for source-backed reasons. A scenario can become funnier, sadder or unresolved. Real harm within fiction retains its consequences. “No moral judge” is not a magical reset or immunity from other inhabitants' refusal rights.

**Juicy motion and comedy first:** squash/stretch, tangible Goo, cardboard seams, music, expressive Residents and physical destruction/healing are offers for play, not lesson delivery vehicles. A song, a joke, a beautiful place and quiet free roam can stand on their own without an educational objective.

## 5 · Editorial independence and anti-bias review
Absolute neutrality is impossible because authors select the world, rules, cast and framing. Aim for **no preordained interpretation** rather than pretending authorial choices have zero perspective.

Before a future scene is promoted, ask: Would it still work if the player rejects the implied interpretation? Are opposing characters more than straw people? Can an NPC's apparently admirable action have adverse consequences and an antagonist's action have comprehensible reasons? Are the actor, actual source and observed events differentiated? Are any rewards or UI cues secretly steering toward approved beliefs? Does the player have genuinely enjoyable non-conversion paths?

**Review product experience, not belief transformation:** authentic visuals, comprehensible affordances, character-specific plausible reactions, source fidelity, replayability and fun. If user feedback is gathered later, ask about felt coercion/agency and clarity, not whether the player became more tolerant/educated. Do not build subtle manipulation to induce an ideology.
