## 1 · Premise: Cards are unexplained intrusions, not merely loot

**GEORG CREATIVE DIRECTION, NEWLY ARTICULATED:** The KFB Cards are strange, sometimes unsettling anomalies appearing in terrain, sky or physical structures. Each Card asserts a thought, claim or absurd hypothesis; its presence can disturb a managed everyday reality. They are manifestations of "cancelled" or suppressed ideas in the `Cancel This Comic` / Kayfabe performance sense. **Show it → Spin it → Sell it** remains the game/story grammar; a Card source claim is not automatically established world truth.

**Source-backed Genesis frame:** the *Fluff Incident*, Lord Hunky, Lady Dory, Fluff harvesting and Academy already belong to the recovered `Cancel This Planet!` meta-story. Georg now offers **failed terraforming experiments by Hunky → coloured Fluff as human surplus** as an authorial explanation. Mark that specific terraforming causation **NEW GEORG CREATIVE CLARIFICATION / WS0 INTAKE**, not retroactive proof that the 2026-10-08 Recovery or character master already established each detail.

**Deliberately UNRESOLVED:** the actual origin of the Cards. Neither Hunky, the aliens nor the Men in Black necessarily knows it. Do not automatically claim Hunky invented Cards, or that the Fluff Incident mechanically causes every Card anomaly. The Fluff Genesis and the Card mystery are related interpretive strands without a forced single answer.

**Central satirical tension:** governing institutions fear uncontrolled interpretations, cognitive dissonance, conversation between isolated worldviews and what they call "thought-crime contamination." A Men in Black actor may genuinely claim to protect fragile NPCs from ontological shock; a collector may be foolish or persuasive; an official may be partly right about a particular danger. **Player/reader closure remains open.**

## 2 · One Card, four physical manifestations, one canonical source

The actual Card always resolves to **one verified `deckId:cardNumber` and source PDF/registry**. Its appearance changes; its `power/lore` source is never silently rewritten.

| Appearance | In-world perception | Later physical / interaction possibility | Reuse and guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Terrain Card** | A card-like piece or printed stratum protrudes from a hillside/ground, maybe found by digging, walking or blowing off dust | inspect, uncover, collect, or note the source; no universal crater mesh | World Surface Truth / Card presentation owner; terrain mutation only if approved |
| **Sky Card** | An impossible card cutout in the sky or along a journey; a readable distant promise | approach via supported movement, collect or chase clue | Existing Sky/Card visual donor; don't invent new flight physics |
| **Card Building / card quarter** | The world presents a coloured cube/ordinary facade; beneath it lies a canonical Card claim | remove HyperNorm *cover*, inspect hidden Card face, optionally damage built shell with existing Cascade/Destruction owner | `KONZEPT_KARTENVIERTEL_R1.md`, Cascade material/impact proposal; no free universal demolition |
| **MindSphere / Mindscape** | A floating sphere, Card-ground collage room, nested subjective landscape | enter a distinct existing instance/experience if real portal/route owner accepts | `KONZEPTBLATT_HYPERNORM_SCENELET_R2.md` Rabbit-Hole/Autopilot donor; not a mandatory new World scene |

**Important design simplification:** avoid a compulsory gigantic **impact crater**. Card anomaly needs no shared meteorite geometry. The crater-like MindSphere can survive as a **rare authored landmark/donor**, not the standard placement grammar. Terrain Cards, Sky Cards and revealable Card Buildings are sufficient lightweight physicalizations and are already represented in concept sources.

**MindSphere is not discarded:** keep its two user experiences (Rabbit-Hole / Autopilot) as *deep-dive interpretation of some cards*, not force every Card pickup into a dimension-loading portal.

## 3 · HyperNormalisation is a cover state, not a universal shader trick

**Visual/world hypothesis:** An apparent public building, cheerful colour film, corporate facade, Billboard or harmless street material can be an **overlay on a Card-sourced object**. The facade's day-to-day use and underlying Card truth coexist; reveal must not turn every building into a flimsy fake.

- **World object** has stable identity, physical support/contact and any authored Card association.
- **Cover/presentation state** describes which layer is currently shown: ordinary surface, HyperNorm image, censorship patch, torn/peeled/revealed Card art, repaired cover, etc. These are **candidate states**, not a newly decreed production enum.
- **Interaction** may inspect/peel/ask/cleanse or use *authorized* projectile damage and material response, depending on the local object. Shooting is a vivid possible reveal route but should not be the only one. The game should not incentivize wrecking any Resident's ordinary home just to farm Cards.
- **Truth/visibility vs ownership:** Revealing a facade is not itself ownership of the Card or proof of its metaphysical claim. Only a canonical acquisition/provenance event adds a player Almanac Card; NPC knowledge changes only on meaningful exposure.
- **Fluff as secondary physics:** Breaking the cover could release coloured Fluff as an **optional visual/recovery/impact by-product**. It is not the cause or price of the Card and need not be an equally rewarding drop from each facade. Preserve the six-colour/tained distinction and finite loot rules.
- **Existing-owner rule:** HyperNorm/media owner supplies content; current world/Destruction owner owns state/contact; Card Builder/Almanac owns Card image/ID; effect layer shows the transition. Source-loaded art or texture alone does not prove integrated design. Isolate a real source object first.

**Contra generic loot:** A Card building has a *reason* to exist in that place: its associated Deck, institution, nearby Residents, censorship interest and a specific contradiction. Source-first world composition; no Card billboard pasted onto an arbitrary cube as product PASS.

## 4 · The same anomaly supports two playable roles

### Collector / metaphysical detective (FrizzleBob direction)
Find a suspicious building/card, investigate provenance, expose the underlying statement, obtain a canonical Card where eligible, bring it to a Resident with an unfamiliar worldview, observe interpretations, add optional connections in Fractal Almanac. The collector may form a false pattern; the game does not automatically confirm it.

### Containment / Men in Black / institutional role
Detect the same anomaly, classify/screen/cover it, safely store the source in an evidence archive or redirect its spread, possibly to "protect" NPCs from dissonance. A meaningful containment task can have its own playful world response, social memory and even an Almanac record *of having encountered the Card* without forced endorsement or permanent deletion of canonical art. Suppression may motivate an opposing NPC to investigate.

**Not good/evil polarity:** Both roles can propagate a false claim or preserve a useful one; worlds and Residents have actual stakes. Role/mask ≠ belief ≠ condition ≠ concrete action (R7). The choice is local/optional, reversible where authored; no global morality score or binary ending.

## 5 · Connect the Dots without authorial overclosure

**Fractal Almanac suggestion (read model, not another knowledge engine):**
- Canonical Card/Deck nodes (source verified);
- actual encountered NPC/POI/world nodes;
- **source-proven relations:** Card belongs to Deck, Resident has seen Card, particular object concealed Card, etc.;
- **NPC interpretations or official classifications** with speaker/source attribution;
- **player-authored speculative links**, labelled as the player's hypotheses, which can be contradicted, edited or removed without rewriting source truth.

The same Card set can produce divergent player graphs: one traveler draws links between opposing theories, another collects evidence of institutional containment, a third does neither. The game can reward curiosity, unusual source-backed connections or a memorable conversation, not "correct conspiracy score".

**Signal Memory compatibility:** The *sequence* in which the player and NPCs see anomaly Cards, receive them via courier or witness their concealment remains the existing ordered Lean Event Memory path (R5). A reveal, classification, delivery, cover, restoration and first acquisition are **different meaningful event kinds**. Save/reload must not duplicate Card rewards or erase the provenance of a controversial interpretation.

## 6 · One compact KFB scene (paper illustration only)

A Utopia "finished" facade is painted with pleasant hypernormalised light. A maintenance Robot complains that its rear entrance was sealed during the launch keynote. The player inspects a seam or uses a source-approved weapon on a **bounded fragile cover**, and a real Card layer is revealed. A few coloured Fluff balls spring from the broken covering as material reaction; the true reward is the Card and the question it provokes.

- Collector: shows it to the Robot or takes it toward a Protopia Farmer. The Robot responds through its own Card knowledge; the same claim can be praised or doubted.
- Containment role: covers/flags it for the Men in Black. The Robot may be grateful, suspicious or frustrated; a receipt records the event without erasing the source Card.
- MindSphere follow-up: a nearby optional source-backed sphere invites a deeper collage interpretation, not a forced portal.
- End: the world retains the facade/cover state and encountered Card/provenance; the player has not been forced to choose the authoritative metaphysical truth.

The example **does not claim** these exact objects, shader compositions, interactions or character lines are implemented.
