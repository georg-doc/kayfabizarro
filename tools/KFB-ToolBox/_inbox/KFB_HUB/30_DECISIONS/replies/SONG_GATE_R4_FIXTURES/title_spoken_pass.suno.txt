[Spoken Word]

The Fixture Parade, and I am the one who has to march it. Hold your umbrella up high, tonight you are the weather.

I march this street every Sunday and I have never once seen it finish on schedule.

First out of the gate, The Ringmaster cracks the whip at the clock and sells tickets to the delay; everyone pays twice.

Behind him, The Paper Elephant looks enormous on the poster, folds flat when it rains; the crowd applauds.

[Crowd Cheering]

Somebody always has to pay for the noise, and tonight it is the band.

The Brass Accountant counts the trumpets, never the tunes; every note audited, nothing heard.

Which leaves the sweepers. The Last Float arrives after the street is empty and declares the parade a total success.

Keep the umbrella up, folks. The weather was the only act here that showed up on schedule.

Stay Fluffy, Folks!
