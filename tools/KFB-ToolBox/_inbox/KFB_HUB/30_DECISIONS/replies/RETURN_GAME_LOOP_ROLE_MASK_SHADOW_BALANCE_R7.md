## Actual design outcome
- **Character identity** vs **Face/Heel Kayfabe role/mask** vs **temporary infection/taint** vs **performed actions and remembered NPC consequences** are four separate concepts.
- Long term, any suitable real Character or rigged prop (Pencil, Men in Black, Demon Lord) may be playable, subject to valid source/rig/motion/physics acceptance. Current first-run Character Select's narrow roster is NOT silently enlarged.
- Temporary infection's presently documented first player effect remains V-679 brief stumble/control loss; true prolonged opposing-side play is separately deferred. A voluntarily chosen role mask does not automatically confer loyalty or change belief.
- Six colourful Story-Mode Fluff kinds remain; `dark_fluffer` taint is NOT the seventh colour. Dark concealment and colourful release/repair can be two opposing local processes, not moral classifications or a newly created shadow Wallet.
- Player and NPC interpretation stay plural; canonical Cards and provenance cannot be rewritten by a mask. Local social/reputation/world events remain persistent. A visual mask change never erases earlier actions or resident memory.
- `PULL, DON'T GATE`: no forced righteousness, conversion or ideology/reputation meter. Social and world consequences can be meaningful without prescribing the “correct” interpretation.
