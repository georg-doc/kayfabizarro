## Statements to treat as DESIGN rather than finished KFB systems

1. **The Fluff Incident is a Genesis frame.** It is *not settled* whether it caused world/island fragmentation. The export's assertion of a confirmed cosmic rupture exceeds the story sources.
2. **Hunky and Dory's TARDIS-like UFO room network remains a future proposal.** The present named UFO concept first uses a cockpit/dialogue set; don't claim non-Euclidean rooms or an alien garden have been integrated.
3. **KFB D6 Story Mode and Quest Die are different.** For tabletop Freestyle v18.4, Quest Die starts at 1 and is deliberately moved +2/+1/0/−1; reaching 6 triggers a **finale on the next turn**, not automatic immediate input locking, Almanac logging or diorama teardown. Digital adaptation is not an approved second Quest engine.
4. **Names like `OBJ_FLUFF_TREE_01`, `KFB-CARD-DECK-01-#14`, `INCREMENT_HUNKY_RESOURCE_METER`, and `SenseFieldMasks` are illustrative synthetics, not existing asset IDs, events or approved schemas.** Genuine card identity is source-backed `deckId + cardNumber`; real world, card, NPC, Quest and Almanac owners remain authoritative.
5. **Six Heim coordinates ≠ six KFB Story Modes ≠ six creative/inquiry operations ≠ NATHAL's experiential descriptions.** Their historical color-to-coordinate mapping remains explicitly unresolved; don't reconstruct it from analogy.
6. **Markus Gabriel's Fields of Sense are not merely subjective player opinions.** KFB's one-object/multiple-context design is an editorial analogy; different interpretations do not necessarily change physical properties.
7. **Our Two Witnesses practice is an independently authored, voluntary creative exercise**, inspired by public descriptions of NATHAL and other methods. Do **not** claim KFB has implemented the protected `Supra-Dialog®` or reproduced an original NATHAL initiation/training.
8. **Specific E.B.E./olfactory/higher-ethics seminar parameters in the export are SOURCE_UNVERIFIED.** Require exact provider passage and attribution before using those assertions even as claims; do not introduce sensory hardware or coercive psychological actions.
9. **NATHAL's claimed 2024 EEG replication is not independently established by the cited 2024 `Mathematics` article, which is a review.** Separate provider claims, theoretical models, and controlled empirical measurements. No claim about psychic effects is proven by a simulated supply container.
10. **Preserve imagination and satirical magic as fiction.** Real-world validation is separate from in-world claims, theatrical wonder, mythology, and NPC beliefs. A skeptic-only „debunking“ game is not the target; neither is compulsory spiritual training.
11. **Remember the whole Infinite Canvas:** Deck → world/biome → card → POI/encounter → resident worldview → Triplet/dialogue → media → Almanac memory → recombination. This is narrative design grammar, **not** an 8-stage mandatory runtime data pipeline.
12. **`Pull, Don't Gate` remains essential.** One actual source-backed card, one existing encounter, two distinct contextual relationships, a voluntary action and a visible consequence are preferable to a speculative global schema.
