# Blind review · trust_and_betrayal.wr4 (4342 characters)

The reviewer saw only the lyrics and a short description of the deck (WS0, 17.10.2026).

## Scores
- **Track to listen to: 6/10.** The lines are short and punchy, but 64 lines in the same shape get monotonous. Tempo and intensity never change.
- **Stand-up: 4/10.** It reads as a list, not an arc.
  - The opener and the closer frame it well: "knowing exactly where the knives were" and "Nobody in this partnership was innocent".
  - In between there is no escalation and no turn. The narrator never betrays the listener.
  - There are only two callbacks, and the dessert one is set up by a nonsensical line.
- **Deck spirit: 6/10.** The tropes and part of the game theory come through. Kayfabe and wrestling are nearly missing. "Price: YOUR SOUL" and "Trust Me, Bro" are not used.

## Lines that sound like a rulebook (isolated)
- I Surrender, Suckers!: "Now watch the reversal."
- Parental Betrayal: "You liked this level?" (sounds like a video game)
- Lying Finger Cross: "The contract never counted."
- Heel-Face Turn: "Let me change sides." (describes the trope instead of playing it)

## Three biggest weaknesses
1. **Flat structure.** 16 identical quatrains with no build-up. The game-theory cards are scattered when they should build to a climax.
2. **The title's payoff is missing.** "Price: YOUR SOUL. Trust Me, Bro" is the strongest material, and the closer undersells it.
3. **Logic errors.** Prisoner's Dilemma: "If I talk, we're finished. If you talk, we both are." Both halves say the same thing, so the dilemma is lost.

## Edits
1. **False Friend:** "I'd take a bullet for you. Then I'd invoice you for the bullet."
2. **Prisoner's Dilemma:** "If we both stay quiet, we both walk. That's why I'm already talking."
3. **Not Actually His Child:** "He has your eyes. He has the milkman's everything else."
4. **Heel-Face Turn:** "Ten years a villain. Cheer now, the hero merch is already printed." (WS0: card #30 is printed blank. Use the title and the motif of the wolf changing expression and offering a flower, not the registry POWER.)
5. **Kinslaying:** "Blood is thicker than water. That's why it stains."
6. **Parental Betrayal:** "I gave you life. I'd like it back, with interest."
7. **Betrayal by Offspring:** "You taught me everything. Including where you sleep."
8. **Ending:** after "Save me a seat at the next betrayal.", add "Price of admission: your soul. / Trust me, bro.", then "Stay Fluffy!"

Optional: a kayfabe line in the intro, "It's all scripted. The chair shots are real."

## What first-time listeners won't get
- "Stay Fluffy!" has no setup.
- "Rash Equilibrium" needs emphasis so it is heard as a pun on Nash, not a slip.
- Niche references: Starscream, Sixth Ranger, Quisling, Firefly.
- "Face-Heel" will lose non-wrestling fans.
- Obscure images: old captain, milkman's book, monster costume, newspaper, Fido.
- The boundary between card name and line is unclear. A short beat or vocal cue before each name would help.

**Keep:** Board to Death, Chronic Backstabbing Disorder, The Scapegoat, I Can Rule Alone, Bastard Understudy, Sixth Ranger Traitor, You Have Outlived Your Usefulness, The Honey Trap.
