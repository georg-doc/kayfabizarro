# Blind review · pharaoh_piledriver.wr4 (3,883 characters)
The reviewer saw only the lyrics and a short description of the deck (WS0, 17.10.2026).

## Scores
- **Track to listen to: 5/10.** All 52 lines have the same shape and there is no reset. On audio it will sound like a list. The bookend between intro and outro works.
- **Stand-up: 5/10.**
  - The bookend works: "Is the fight fixed?" is answered by "The booking was settled… the applause is still yours."
  - The middle runs in deck order, not comedy order.
  - The callbacks give their payoffs away in advance.
- **Deck spirit: 6/10.** The mythology is rich, but the wrestling is thin: about 8 of 52 lines. Nobody does a piledriver, and there are no mummies.

## Three biggest weaknesses
1. **The wrestling frame is mostly missing.** Set (the chair) and Osiris (the biggest cheer) show what's possible.
2. **The callbacks spoil themselves.** Sekhmet's "Red beer was my weakness" gives away the Red Beer Trick. The salad joke is told twice, once by Horus and once by Lettuce.
3. **The rhythm is monotonous, and some syntax is knotted.** Examples: the Eye of Ra line, "The year refused five birthdays", and "I printed people first" for Khnum.
