# KFB Game Loop R13 · Greater Intent: Decks, Songs, Videos and Performative Worldviews
29.10.2026 · Georg creative direction · HIGH-LEVEL EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE / NO RUNTIME

## Why this note exists
Georg corrects the recent drift into detailed mini-scenario breakdowns, rap-like NPC replies and repeated micro-gameplay mechanisms. Keep the **greater intent** in view. KFB is the *Infinite Canvas of the Tenth Art*: a chill, humorous, provocative, transmedia exploration of human ideas, follies, psychological contradictions, worldviews and interpretive closure, expressed across comics, Cards, decks, music, performances, Residents, videos and a living toy-world. The player does not need to accept the performer's position.

**This note is an addendum, not a new story, R9–R11 substitute, narrator owner, media scheduler, songwriting engine or campaign specification.** R3–R8 and R12 concept outcomes remain intact. R9–R11 from earlier chat are local-only drafts, not assumed GitHub merges.

## One theme, many performances
- **Deck/Card:** source of an authored concept, claim, joke, image and connected ideas. Canonical `deckId:cardNumber` and actual artwork/text stay authoritative; source text is a claim, not automatically world truth.
- **FrizzleBob or another anthropomorphic/psychological performer:** a distinctive fallible *perspective*, not an official answer-machine. Character, human weakness, voice and stakes matter more than fashionable delivery style.
- **Song:** emotionally and rhetorically performs a topic. A song may review, exaggerate, contradict, criticise or celebrate. It is **not required to be rap** or to explain every Card one by one.
- **Video/animated act:** shows a concrete character/scenic situation with deliberate timing, images and satire. It may be entertaining in its own right and may work as an optional first encounter, teaser or entry point into the associated Deck and game location.
- **Open World/Residents:** provide voluntary embodied encounters. Another NPC can admire, sell, fear, dismiss or challenge the very same song/card/video based on their own worldview and accumulated Card knowledge.
- **Fractal Almanac:** preserves actual Cards, provenance and optionally source-backed/counterpoint links across media. Player closure is not settled by a slogan or a generated summary.

**Directional flow, not funnel obligation:** encounter a character/song/video → discover related Deck/Card/world → explore/ask/collect/connect → optionally return to the comic/song/video or share a self-authored link. The game may also be the first entry point. *Pull, don't gate*. No required subscriptions, referrals, virality metrics or compulsory consumption of every media artefact.

## Editorial symmetry and satirical ambivalence
The exact same performance can be celebrated as insight, criticised as propaganda, commodified as a clever business, concealed as an anomalous Card-related phenomenon or contested by another Resident. Both an official advertisement and a slogan promising independent thinking can rely on rhetorical tricks. No system-level political, theological or epistemological endorsement comes from its source character, genre, AI video or visibility state.

**Show It → Spin It → Sell It** remains a *performance grammar* that ends with player/reader interpretation, not an obligation to use a particular "RAPGOD" voice or explain a moral. Sometimes an excellent joke, silence, choreography or an unresolved question carries the idea more effectively than a Triplet.

## Data/production boundaries
Reuse existing Deck Registry, Card provenance, Asset Librarian/Audio, current ChatterBox/narrator, Video/HyperNorm and Playable World owners. A simple **source-verified topic-to-media relation** is enough for future discovery:
`topic/semantic tags ↔ canonical deckId ↔ real song/media asset ref ↔ performer/Resident refs ↔ relevant cardRefs`.
This is a conceptual relation, **not a proposed new authoritative schema**. Material may be many-to-many; do not assume one song per Deck or one video per Card, auto-tagging truth, mass-generating 150 scenes or an unverified cross-media database.

GitHub contains real examples of `kfb.deck-song.v1` metadata with an explicit `deckId` and performance/audio source refs (e.g. `epistemic_sabotage__suno_05`, `kayfabizarro_philosophy__suno_01d`). A historical May 2026 handover mentions **200+ Suno songs** in an external archive, **not a verified current source count or an accepted mapping to all Decks**. Georg's broad thematic coverage is a creative ambition; verify a current catalog and rights/media references before any large-scale mapping.

- No generic videos replacing source-owned cast, actual comic artworks or accepted animation.
- No new production owner, runtime task, Site/Stage/Cloudflare deployment, Hub-router change or Live promotion from a creative discussion.
