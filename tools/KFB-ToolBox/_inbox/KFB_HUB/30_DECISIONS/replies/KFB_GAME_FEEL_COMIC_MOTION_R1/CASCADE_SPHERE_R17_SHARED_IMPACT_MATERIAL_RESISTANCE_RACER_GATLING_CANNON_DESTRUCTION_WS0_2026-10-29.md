## 2. One causal pipeline; distinguish physics facts and gameplay authority
```
Vehicle chassis contact OR accepted projectile HitEvent
   → CONTACT_CAPTURE {hitId,tick,sourceActor,receiverBuilding,cellId,normal,worldPoint,
                      preimpactVelocity, contactSource, weaponMode,contactArea}
   → IMPACT_EVALUATION material profile + event-specific impulse/energy/pressure rules
   → IMPACT_RESOLUTION {BLOCKED|DENTED|CRACKED|BREACHED|SUPPORT_COLLAPSE}
   → Damage/Destruction owner accepts cell state and support consequences exactly once
   → Receiving renderer paints source-mesh zone damage/rim/scorch; authoritative bay collider updated
   → On FIRST accepted breach: spawn ONE pool-bounded card-colored bead wave at aperture
   → Existing MazeGraph/Fluff/Goo rules may accept a distinct ROUTE_CHANGED/FLUFF_CONVERTED event
   → Later REBUILD_ACCEPTED resets material/structural bay collider and bead-source state
```
Not every impact makes a hole. A harsh bounce with intact wall is a valid satisfying event. No novel independent collision authority, no GPU particle feedback masquerading as damage. Damage events keyed to `runId/tick/actorId/receiverId/cellId/contactId`; cooldown for **sustained chassis contact** (same impact cannot damage 60 times per second), while separate Gatling projectile IDs can accumulate valid damage. No guessed collision normals from screen-space effects.

## 3. Material profiles and physically inspired damage rules (tuning, not physics claims)
**Material source roles:** `rough_linen/canvas` (frays, stretches and burns; visually fabric but underlying solid game wall can resist car), `clay/stone` (dents/chips and crack rubble), `wood/paper/cardboard` (folds/tears and lighter fracture), `metal` (rings/dents, high penetration threshold, possible ricochet), `HyperNorm` (shader skin independent of underlying substrate and structure). **Never** assume a HyperNorm color shader physically weakens/strengthens a building unless a *gameplay profile* explicitly says so.
**Two independent descriptors:**
- `lookMaterial`: KFB palette/colorband/linen/K2/surface normal/roughness/mask; rendered only.
- `structuralMaterial`: profile for `impactThreshold`, `penetrationResistance`, `fractureEnergy`, `restitution`, `friction`, `ductility`, `damageAccumulation`, `supportRole` and `repairCost`; chosen by stage authors, **not** inferred blindly from a textile normal map. Do not turn real physical constants into pseudo-science; these are tuned gameplay parameters.

**Impact normal and contact area:** energy and impulse depend on the relative **normal velocity**, not raw speedometer value. Candidate simplified `v_n = max(0,-dot(v_rel, n))`, `E_n≈(1/2) m_eff v_n²` for an inertial crash / `J_n≈m_eff (1+e) v_n` for normal impulse, where `m_eff` depends on collision body masses/inertias and `e` on restitution. These are **inspired scoring features** for designer tuning, not a substitute for actual Rapier solver impulses, off-center torque, deformable car/body energy loss, or shot projectile mass. The active receiver must prefer actual measured/contact telemetry where API allows. `contactArea` splits energy across one/few wall-bays; tip-of-car/edge corner may focus damage, broad bumper may distribute.
**Cannon/Gatling:** may have authored gameplay projectile profiles for `pierce`, `impulse`, `heat`, `area` and `falloff`; do not assume energy from vehicle `m_eff`. A gatling burst increments bounded per-bay wear/crack count from unique actual shots, cannon/rocket damages adjacent cells in a localized cone/sphere, then collapse adjacency may propagate. Must preserve input weapon aim/firing owner and authoritative Combat HitEvent.
**Racer:** derive `speedNormal, incidentAngle, m_eff, wheelContact, contactPoint, colliderId` from existing Vehicle/Rapier. A slow frontal collision → BLOCKED/Bounce and retained collider; fast grazing strike → edge crumble/skid/ricochet, car remains constrained; fast near-normal/strong hit → accept cell breach, remove collider segment only once, transfer/damp vehicle momentum appropriately, revalidate path clearance. **Do not double apply bounce:** physical Rapier solver already resolves contact; cartoon vehicle squash/recovery, suspension, audio/hitstop, camera and readable dust consume the same event as visual feedback. Any additional explicit reverse impulse must be tuning-controlled and physically integrated with same single vehicle controller (never another bespoke movement solution).
**Life-cycle:** intact building is lightweight source GLTF + coarse compound solid collider; on impact "promote" only local 1–4 active buildings to **source-measured segmented bay colliders**, while unchanged source geometry remains recognizable. On breach, remove the exact allowed bay collider and corresponding visible source patch from the runtime presentation *without changing original GLTF disk*, and update Rapier scene queries; hold frame where safe. On repair, restore once only when doorway/aperture is clear of chassis/player and Fluff packets so the vehicle isn't trapped in a rebuilt wall. Persist compact bay state and critical events, not per-particle trajectories.

## 4. Real source GLTF hole technique — avoid runtime arbitrary polygon booleans
**Recommended P0: small authored breakable panels** attached to selected existing KayKit wall sections. For a natural-looking hole, choose a genuinely independent source wall submesh OR mask the exact intersecting source triangles/UV region, including all source geometry that would still occlude the opening; add depth rim/interior underside, darkened/frayed/burned edge and small debris meshes. Collider is a matching simple bay box; break means the source's **selected patch** visually disappears and the bay collider is disabled/removed. If source mesh is monolithic, use an **opaque author-made breach patch/insert** aligned to its original facade, or precomputed intact/breached alternatives. A shader-only "discard" without corresponding triangles and collider removal is **visual damage only**, do not claim a traversable opening.
**Scorch/rim implementation:** edge signed-distance or damage-mask gradient drives `scorch`, fibers, color darkening, AO-like contact shadow in 0–small neighborhood around hole, with source PBR normals/roughness preserved. Do NOT imply actual screen-space SSAO alone can color burned edges—scorch is material albedo and local geometry/rim. Material color layers: `sourcePBR` → `KFB_K2_clay|rough_linen` → `terrain3|HyperNorm mask` → `damage/scorch/singe` → `gooInfection separate`. Damage map anchored in **local-object or source UV coordinates** so moving/elevated panels do not acquire world-space crawl.
**Visual feel:** accepted wall cell crack grows under Gatling, big cannon burst creates a bounded near-round torn opening, heavy race bumper tears out one or several large rectangular/irregular adjacent cells, corner cells affect roof support but NOT automatically entire skyscraper. Source silhouette remains intact until accepted structural break; overt large deformations optional offline later. Inner `cardVault` may show real KFB source card; separate optional `splatInterior` shows a **Gaussian Splat scene** if sourced/accepted, not a static texture assumed 3DGS.

## 5. Balls, rolling, curb, slope and game logic
- Reservoir spawned from accepted original **Card Registry** palette/identity, independent visual fill from **3D Gaussian Splats**. `BREACH_ACCEPTED` triggers one deterministic counted burst budget `nVis`, seed, emitter sector and palette; render instanced colored clay beads from a fixed pool, not a free world full of RigidBody spheres.
- For realistic-looking flow, sample street height/normal from **existing Track Core/World Surface Truth** and author a gutter route with curb edge confinement, inclines, short slopes and drainage; a few near-player beads may have Rapier contacts, most use cheap curve/heightfield gravity+damping. Avoid naive "constant velocity bead follows path uphill" without visible source energy; downhill is easier; source-matched ripple/cascade offers spectacle without thousands of solver contacts.
- Colliding bead → true Fluff/Goo conversion only if **explicit authoritatively accepted** `FLUFF_COLLECTED`/`FLUFF_DELIVERED` or `GOO_CLEANSED` etc, at a bounded exchange and once-only receipt. Do not invent healing by rainbow beads unless WS0 approves.
- Vehicle crossing existing ball wave should skid/bounce only if a **bounded aggregate hazard/collider** is authorized; particles alone are graphical. Material effects can show balls scattering off bumper but do not infer physical oil/traction changes from a color mask.

- **B fast high-normal Racer crash** must break one allowed cell with coordinated source visual hole and matching collider opening, actual momentum loss / bounce/recovery or physically consistent pass-through, and release one seed-controlled ball wave into gutter. The vehicle may pass only after structural permission and collider update. No extra damage from persistent contact.
- **C repeated Gatling hits** must damage one wall cell and eventually expose a smaller breach with unique hit events/accumulation; cannon variant is higher single-shot impulse/flank-area and may detach neighboring cell only where explicitly accepted.
- **D reconstruction** must restore original source material/geometry, local damage map, collider and structural strength, with safe occupancy and correct source packet/Fluff world state.
**Evidence:** side-by-side original PBR/K2 vs linen/colorband vs damaged look; camera street/35°/vehicle bumper/high roof. **Stop conditions:** no glTF source identity lost, no shader-only invisible collider hole, no unmeasured "physically accurate" claim, no unbounded per-bullet mesh boolean, no thousands of Rapier sphere bodies, no second vehicle movement/physics world.
```txt
ImpactEvent {
 eventId, tick, sourceActorId, sourceMode: GATLING|CANNON|RACER,
 buildingId, cellId, contactPoint, contactNormal,
 vRelativeNormal?, contactArea?, projectileId?, 
 sourcePhysicsRef, materialProfileId
}
DamageDecision {
 eventId, cellId, integrityBefore, integrityAfter,
 response: BLOCKED|DENTED|CRACKED|BREACHED|COLLAPSED,
 energySpent?, impulseReturned?, bounceResponseHint?,

```
Existing Combat/Vehicle authority emits actual source contact; existing Destruct/World accepts DamageDecision only once; R3F visual receiver reads current state; actual Physics owner toggles/remove/rebuilds colliders on accepted game update; source Card Registry/Fluff owner owns any reward or healing. The `RACER` mode may be missing in the current POC and the object-level contract remains a WS0 **concept only**, not API-ready or official WS0 schema.
