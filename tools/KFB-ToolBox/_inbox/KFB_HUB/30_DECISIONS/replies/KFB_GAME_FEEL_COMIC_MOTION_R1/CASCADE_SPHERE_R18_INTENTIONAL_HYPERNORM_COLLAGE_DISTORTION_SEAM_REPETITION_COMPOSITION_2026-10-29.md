# R18 · Deliberate HyperNorm Collage Projection on Irregular 3D Assets
**2026-10-29 · conceptual addendum for existing WS0 Draft PR #435 ONLY; no official V-962 edit or new engine/render/client code.**
**Latest Georg correction:** KFB Hypernormalisation is **already a fleeting, fragmented, overlaid, music-video-like COLLAGE**. Its meaningful source-card motifs need NOT remain legible and rectilinear on arbitrary building facades or props. A distorted, skewed, stretched, partially obscured, folded or self-overlapping image on unusual original 3D KayKit/Kenney geometry can be a **desired surreal/dada/Fluxus aesthetic**. Do not burden WSA with forcing seamless, readable whole-card art on all faces. Embrace 3D object surface curvature while suppressing obviously accidental UV seams, stamp-like repetition, flicker and muddy layer overload. Full source card legibility is needed only where a separate **genuine collectible Card Vault / card reward** is revealed.

## 2. Three layers of accepted "surreal distortion"
**A. Object-space collage projection:** let moving image planes, UV fragments, swirling bands and slitscan stretch across organically irregular source geometry, roofs and exposed pipes. Object-space UV or triplanar for ABSTRACT layers can follow normal/shape and avoid crawling while the source model moves. Long captions/faces/symbols may distort on purpose, not necessarily be preserved. The original material and geometry still keep shadows, roughness, normals and silhouette legible.
**B. Layered composite:** one accepted H13/H14/H15 **frame owner** supplies a composited base frame + optionally 1–2 strategic supplemental visual layers (mask, organic grain, low-opacity luma-key, hallucinated source/video streaks or HyperNorm aura). Use source-normalized temporal pattern; do NOT launch 5 separate hidden HTML canvases, 5 new audio contexts, new independent shot RNGs or 6 texture pipelines per building face. Game-owned `worldTime` / accepted audio envelope may drive the *same current source composer* after source API proof. Specific number of layers is a **suggested QA variable**, not a universal rule.
**C. Material substrate:** screen-printed fabric/rough linen, K2 clay, source KayKit PBR and optional terrain3 colorbands exist *beneath/around* the overlay. The 3D mesh gives physical mass, light/shadow, real bevel/cloth weave; the collage remains a show layer, not the complete material. Contact and road/gutter readability win over 100% moving coverage.

## 3. Seam camouflage strategies (allow controlled surrealism)
1. **Deliberate cut seam:** use a credible comic/paper edge, beveled clay joint, card-fold, stitch, architectural trim, facade frame or window break at an actual source UV/material discontinuity; do not pretend every corner must be seamless.
2. **Matched abstract cross-fade at the edge:** for abstract pattern only use object/world-space tri-planar weights or shared local phase/seed; avoid repeating one legible card headline on six surfaces. Avoid a mathematically simple visible gradient band where an authored material transition is intended.
3. **Staggered layer interruption:** one layer changes/masks slightly before the next, with short intentional ink-bleed/grain/occlusion cut or waveform in between. Borrow S4 source design principle of per-role stagger/negative space, not literal Track geometry.
4. **Break repetition with deterministic selection:** H13 already ranks novelty over recent shots, images/text/camera; give neighboring buildings different CARD/BIOME source seeds, cropping orientation, start offset, relative beat phase, layer blend weight and view-dependent update tier, *still driven by one H13 shot scheduler*. No identical tiling or live randomized texture offsets every render frame.
5. **Selective zone mask:** different projection/coverage on rough linen source wall vs original window, door, roof, sculptural prop, clay curb. Source highlights, shadow contact and 3D silhouette remain visible and readable.
6. **Single shared edited source frame:** one source HyperNorm Canvas or render target may feed multiple receiving materials/face transforms; avoid duplicate full-res CanvasTexture per source mesh if not needed. Frame caching/visibility/fps/asset rights remain to verify in receiving host.

## 4. Keep the collage vivid instead of visually muddy
- **Visual hierarchy:** on any one source building, give one visual layer dominant movement, one complementary subdued spatial/color texture and a few occasional accents (flash, glitch or text). "Many layers" means authored temporal exchange and selective occlusion—not all layers full strength all the time.
- **Temporal composition:** stagger dominant layers with H13 song form/beat structure. When action/gameplay becomes visually dense (Gatling impact, wall cell breach, Goo explosion, rolling colored balls), briefly lower the HyperNorm overlay contrast/motion/frequency so the cause/effect remains readable; afterwards restore. This is a rendering feedback policy, **not a new combat event owner**.
- **Spatial calm:** protect Gutter route sightlines, street/sidewalk boundaries, original FB_CANON/player, source windows/entry points and nearby Fluff goal markers. Game camera gets stable landmark silhouettes. All-over moving architecture remains optional stage mode, not automatic setting.
- **Palette and contrast:** a single KFB WORLD palette and approved `terrain3`/Clay roles can constrain disparate footage; keep limited near-neutral quiet regions as rests, avoid all-saturated neon. Full texture colors and shadows are source-dependent; test real palette tone/toneMapping under game light.
- **Accessibility/performance:** support paused/reduced-motion/low-animation modes without removing hit openings or interactive state; expose actual frame update throttling and mobile GPU texture upload/memory, prevent flicker and seizure-prone flashing. No absolute FPS guarantee without device test.
