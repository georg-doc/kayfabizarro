## 0. User idea in one sentence
A maze of **card-sized, different-height cuboids / extruded illustrated blocks**, whose **all outer faces exhibit the existing KFB Hypernormalisation animated collage appearance**, with a **hidden real KFB card** and **source-colored ball-relief/ball-stream fill** inside; FrizzleBob as his **authentic fully rigged 3D FB_CANON** walks/drives/flys through **white comic gutters interpreted as streets**, edged by **Joyride clay kerbs and pavements**. Hits/infection erode shell masks, expose card faces, release colored balls that pour into streets and can later interact with Goo/Black Fluff, while authoritatively changing the existing Cascade MazeGraph, cell destruct/repair and collectible/card mechanics. Potential mapping to **56-card decks** by explicit numbered card identity, not mandatory sequential 56 stage grind.

**Georg's phrasing matters:** This is *not* "FrizzleBob as a 2D standee". Standees and flat pop-ups are optional only for enemy/decoration. The main player, combat rig and later other KFB rigs remain the real 3D originals, same Host R1.2. Do not replace them or their canonical animations with cutouts. "Hypernormalisation" is the actual KFB HyperNorm artistically curated visual language, NOT a generic animated rainbow checker shader. **"Gutter" = the negative white space between comic panels, here a physical walkable street network.**

## 2. Three compatible Card Building appearance options (not mutually exclusive)
**A. HYPERNORM BOX (PREFERRED FIRST PROOF):** cuboid, flat or slightly rounded corners, varying width/depth/height, 2:1 or 16:9 art on roof or one selected hero facade, remainder receives the SAME HyperNorm frame or its palette/texture derivative. The original full card image remains uncropped somewhere (roof/behind shell/inside) with stable card ID and exact card crop. Cube geometry is not assumed to be square; rectangle permits KFB card aspect ratios and a navigable street grid.
**B. CRACKED CARD VAULT:** beneath animated outer shell is a second fixed **full source KFB card** on an inner plane/slot; hit/infection edits an outer damage mask, showing the inner art through a *visible breach*. Card remains a real collectible, visible/accessible only after an accepted in-game exposure/route condition.
**C. COLOR-BALL / CARD ZONE BUILDING:** underlying volume filled with colored instanced spheres or sparse relief particles derived from card palette/height/seed. Opening an accepted wall section spawns or releases a controlled wave flowing onto the Gutter. Can be a surreal cube or open Card Zone scene on top. Later terrain-hybrid, full 3D colored particle diffusion, "walk/swim in balls" or balls that cure Black Goo are optional, separate gameplay mechanic proposals—not automatic P0 behavior.

**Recommended visual:** restrained clay/ink bevel + moving colored HyperNorm imagery; a single integrated palette per scene from accepted KFB Card/World roles, avoid every face behaving as noisy TV. Street material steady enough to show Fluff lanes and player contacts. Source card image readable in at least one hero face or visible internal card; no random image smear masquerading as a collectible.

## 3. Six-face material mapping and the *real* seam problem
- Each block has 6 faces (4 sidewalls, roof, bottom); show 4–5 externally, bottom can have cheap native clay source. Reusing **ONE source output** per building/visual state with face-specific UV transforms or single atlas beats six independent HyperNorm engines. Hero face can be a 16:9 KFB card window; narrow sides can extend texture via controlled abstract collaged bands. Card source crop is not warped to square face merely because geometry is square.
- Two distinct kinds of continuity: **(i) temporal/material continuity** — one time/palette/phase across faces is feasible with shared frame and world/object-space UV; **(ii) pictorial content continuity** — a meaningful illustration crossing box corners without obvious seam is NOT automatic. Tame with frame-based UV atlas, bevel/ink seam, explicitly shared edges, cylindrical/triplanar projection for abstract bands only; test at rotating camera, not claim seamless.
- A physically torn/blown-off wall demands more than `discard` on the shader: opaque shell material damage mask can show hole, but **an actual aperture in collider/interaction requires separate quantized destructible shell cells / segment colliders** updated only on accepted game events. Shell transparency alone may reveal card but must not create invisible impassable glass.
- Animated screen textures: favor one low-resolution shared render/frame owner with per-building palette/card index; cap updates by camera distance/visibility and damage event. Offline frames + shared procedural shader as fallback. HyperNorm original owner/API must be confirmed—no duplicated rAF/AudioContexts, offscreen engine loops, DOM 2D surface per facade, or global renderer.
- **Mask roles**: `hypernormVisible`, `damage/alpha`, `gooInfection`, `cardReveal`, `clayBevel`. Independent channels: Goo darkness/infection is not automatically a hole or a weapon hit. Healing via Fluff event does not "erase" structural damage unless world rules authorize repair. Rendering reads authoritative damage/cure state.

## 4. Card-colour balls: inheritance, motion and game authority
**Color extraction:** each numbered card gets `cardId` and source image; choose a deterministic weighted palette from source card pixel data (or existing Card/World palette); explicit stratified sampling of dominant/mid/accent/neutral rather than one independent random color per ball. Respect real card typography/contrast and CORS. Convert image sRGB→linear where needed so rendered ball shading matches source, not artificially pale. Height profile may come from a source-provided approved depth map; deriving depth from saturated colors alone is heuristic and must be opt-in labelled `INFERRED`.
**Visual body:** a few large low-poly/clay spheres + many smaller instanced particles. On **`SHELL_BREACH_ACCEPTED`**, sample from only the opened source-wall sector; release a wave on the correct side near the street, with initial burst then gravity, gutter flow and obstacles. Each ball has instance seed, source sector and palette index, for replayable visuals.
**Physics tiers:**
- `P0_GUTTER_FLOW` (best initial): logic is **cell graph / sparse physical actor collisions**; most spheres follow a scripted/analytic street curve or heightfield with basic bounce (visual density), no per-sphere Rapier body. Add a tiny bounded subset of actual colliders only for hero action/near-camera contacts.
- `P1_LOCAL_INTERACTIONS`: near-player spheres can be pushed/kicked by the existing player/Mech and splash off a dropped wall piece; spatial hashed neighbor avoidance or lightweight custom simulation only if profiled.
- `P2_GPU_COMPUTE`: only if actual existing Card Relief R2/R3 donor is isolated, renderer/backend tested and desktop/mobile fallback proven; GPU positions alone **do not write authoritative World/Fluff damage or card collection** without sparse readback/event bridge. Do not promise 50k full rigid-body collisions.
**Fluff and Goo semantics:** colored debris beads are initially **visual/physical resources, not automatically six canonical story-mode Fluff units**. Only at an approved conversion/gather/suction gate may a bounded number enter the existing Fluff ledger/flow; rules about curing Black Fluff/Goo remain the existing V-609/V-532 owner. Prevent "every tiny particle cures one Goo" exponential success. Hero card as treasure after real breach/pacification can trigger save/deck acquire once.

## 5. GutterGraph / road/sidewalk composition
- **Topology first**: author a solvable maze graph; treat its edges as street centerlines, distance, width, ramps and junctions. `GutterGraph` is a *view/recipe of the authoritative `cascade-core` nodes and edges*, not a parallel simulation/Pathfinder. Draw 2–3 road widths from actual Gameplay/Player/Vehicle clearance. Panel blocks occupy the cells between roads, never cover required exit path. Variation is panel footprint (2:1 card, square cube, 1:1, long block), height, angle and side art. Do not hard lock every building to 16:9 if that breaks routes.
- **Walk/fly/drive:** player can travel street-level on real host collision; jetpack can reach roofs and card-exposure points; later track/car uses actual Track Core road profile. Road may slope; stairs are mostly player/path affordances and ball VFX, not necessarily a valid full vehicle path (define traversal profiles!). Mark `walkable`, `flyClearance`, `vehicleClearance`, `rollingBallChannel` per edge.
- **Bordsteine:** reuse accepted Joyride `transition-atlas.v1.js` low-poly clay `curbStones` and sidewalk slabs through Track Core configured profile. Physical pedestrian sidewalk and drivable street can use separate receiving material roles. One `terrain3` time/palette owner is an optional selective animated band overlay on road or sidewalk; curb/step remains visibly grounded, stable and not a false collider.
- **City dressing:** few source-pinned streetlamps, signal, stairs, street sign, drains, barriers, Goo patches and unusual KFB artifacts with intentional spacing; props should not mask panel art, lane flow or FB camera. No OSM-data prerequisite for this variant. A real OSM crop can later inform gutter orientation/anchors but shouldn't be artificially forced into initial scene.
- **White Gutter aesthetic:** preserve pale paper/ink negative space as a design gesture; street must still communicate material and clear road curvature, so use dirty chalky white/asphalt and colored borders, with road markings and lighting consistent with KFB CANON_3D. Avoid a giant white unshaded plane.

## 6. Card collection / 56 possible buildings
- A usual 56-card deck gives **up to 56 stable card slots** `cardNo:1..56` per `deckId`; **NOT** 56 mandatory buildings simultaneously in the MVP or fixed player destruction order. Deterministic catalog and draw/seed can distribute a subset into a local Mindscape, leaving remaining in deck/mission pool. If 56 visible, stage/stream/chunk/LOD reuse and instancing required.
- Original card slot identity should never be mistaken for a street grid ID. `panelBlockId` != `deckId/cardNo`. One card can live in two scenes with distinct world placements, but collectible receipt should dedupe by original card identity + mission state/owner.
- **Candidate future mechanics**, optional and explicitly nonbinding: numbered destruction sequence puzzles, conditional dependent collapses, faction "cleanse instead of destroy," deck collection and portal-to-card Mindscape, cardhouses where loose paper/levels fold, Qiva process/server types as card themes, time-warp route resets, defending a Bell Landmark as anti-Goo area effect.
- A hidden source card should be discovered/revealed via interaction, not assumed to spawn just because a surface shader has an alpha hole. Keep card reward legal under existing Player Save and Registry owners.

## 8. Performance / functional tiering proposals (NOT hardware-validated budgets)
| Tier | Card Buildings | HyperNorm | Balls | Collision/state |

| `SAFE` mobile | 4–6 | one hero animated face; distant faces baked/static at native res, throttled update | about 200–600 instanced visible beads | fixed shell sector colliders + few hero ball contacts |
| `STANDARD` | 6–12 | shared dynamic atlas/frame for visible hero faces and slow animated side accents | about 500–2,000 instanced beads | sparse near-player contacts, logical flow on `cascade-core` |
| `STRESS` diagnostic | 12–56 (only if genuinely necessary) | lower-res, distance/occlusion cached textures | 2,000+ visual instances profiled | no full per-particle Rapier by default |
Numbers above are **test candidates**, not claims of 60fps or of any existing WebGPU compute. Real target device/frame budget must be measured in the receiving KFB host; shared 2D-frame upload is potentially expensive, memory-bound if every building has separate texture. In-world card art must survive compressed textures/reduced update without illegibility.
**P0 graceful degradation:** Pause animated HyperNorm on distant faces; blend to static KFB image/first frame, keep MASK/HIT and source card reveals functional. If GPU ball sim fails, substitute seeded CPU curves for display only. No POC depends on 6 separate live 2D renderers, high-end GPU compute or complete physical granular dynamics.
