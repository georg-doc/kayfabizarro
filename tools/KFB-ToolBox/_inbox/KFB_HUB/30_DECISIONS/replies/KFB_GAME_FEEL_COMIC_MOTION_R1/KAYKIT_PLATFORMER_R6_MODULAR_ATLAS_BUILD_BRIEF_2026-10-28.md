- **Composite actuator hierarchy** in `button_base_blue.gltf`: node `button_base_blue` has child `button_blue`, two meshes; `lever_floor_base_blue.gltf`: child `lever_floor_blue` with nonzero local translation (~0.3 in Y); `lever_wall_base_B_green.gltf` similarly has nested movable lever. - **Composite dynamic hazards**: `saw_trap_blue.gltf` includes nested `saw_trap_sawblade_blue`; `cannon_base_blue.gltf` contains nested turret and barrel with transforms. These are potential motion channels, not ready-made gameplay scripts.
- **Conveyor** `conveyor_4x4x1_blue.gltf` declares two image assets `platformer_texture.png`, `threads.png`. Separate UV scrolling of belt material from world/physics flow; a moving texture does not transport Fluff. Its accessor-local X span reaches approx -2.1…+2.1 around a nominal 4 unit tile; author must measure actual assembled contact/seam/overhang rather than hardcoding a 4.0 full-box collider.
- **Ramp** `platform_slope_4x4x4_blue.gltf` mesh-local POSITION accessor bound [-2,0,-2] to [2,4,2]. A valid surface path from low to high edge is unproven; need actual triangle slope/contact/ray probe and local +Y calibration.
