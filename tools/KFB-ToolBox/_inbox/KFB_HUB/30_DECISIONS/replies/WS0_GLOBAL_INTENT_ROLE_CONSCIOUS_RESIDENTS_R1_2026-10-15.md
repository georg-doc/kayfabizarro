## 1. The precise intent, restated
KFB inhabitants (KayKit/KFB Residents and other source-owned characters) are not simply unaware loop-driven NPCs, nor all-wise mouthpieces for the author. They can recognize that they embody **roles**: social masks, jobs, archetypes, relationships and wrestling-style Kayfabe performances. Their tragicomic biography, faults, recurring triggers, competencies, strengths and loyalties make those roles lived and consequential.

A character can perceive a possible exit from a stale role, defect, habitual trigger spiral or self-imposed staging without automatically choosing it. Sometimes they knowingly continue their cosmic assignment, social position or eccentric performance because it gives meaning, pleasure, belonging, continuity or a comic identity. Sometimes they improvise or step outside the expected script. A chosen continuation is not evidence of unconscious programming; a self-aware character can still struggle with a habit and rationalize it. **Self-recognition, practical freedom and habitual action are separate tensions.**

The intended tone is compassionate, sardonic, self-deprecating and unsentimental. Residents may recognize their own failings and the failings of others, but are not forced into therapy talk, generic meta quips, catchphrases, explanatory speeches, or a redemption arc. Their actions, body language, repeated choices and occasional contradictions can communicate more than dialogue.

**Global performance rule:** *The performers may know they are playing; they still have lives inside the play.*

## 2. Distinct intellectual source lenses (analogy, not a claim of theoretical equivalence)
- **Karl Marx / Charaktermaske**: socially/economically situated individuals as personifications of relationships/roles; applied here as an *interpretive lens* for roles, not a claim that Marx wrote about voluntary wrestling performances. Historical source: `Das Kapital` vol. I ch. 2, and conceptual research at https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charaktermaske . Do not conflate imposed socioeconomic structures with a freely chosen theatrical costume.
- **Wrestling Kayfabe / theatrical acting**: a performer keeps an intelligible role going in a social performance even when actor and audience can comprehend the convention. KFB interpretation: role allegiance and deliberate performance can themselves be expressive agency. The Resident is **not** merely an actor's out-of-fiction human avatar; their inside-world personal history remains real for that fiction.
- **Markus Gabriel / fields of sense**: what an object, act, Card, or role is *about* varies across meaningful contexts; the Resident retains continuity while context reveals a different aspect. Interview: https://euppublishingblog.com/2016/09/23/graham-harman-interviews-markus-gabriel/ . Do not trivialize Gabriel's ontology as literal portals or use his concept as a new runtime field without WS0 approval.
- **Aby Warburg / good-neighbour principle**: unexpected proximity/rearrangement creates meaningful associative discovery, rather than pre-assigned categorical order. Institutional source: https://warburg.sas.ac.uk/library-collections/library/about-library . KFB interpretation: new Card/biome/Resident juxtaposition invites players to discover a non-obvious connection. It is **not** a rule that any random mismatch works.
- **Herriman / Sterrett + metatheatre**: the scene, day/night or stage backdrop may change radically without interrupting the character's performance. Alternative settings can activate new social masks and readings. The scenery is a participant in storytelling without becoming the Resident's personality controller.

## 3. Stable person, shifting local mask — conceptual axes, not a data schema
1. **Who this person is**: source-backed identity, body/face, individual biography, strengths, fears/vulnerabilities, competencies, chosen attachments, important relationships, resident-owned accumulated memories.
2. **Who they are performing here**: local profession / institution / faction / social mask / current onstage assignment, what the audience and nearby residents expect.
3. **Why the loop returns**: a specific authored contradiction, learned trigger, reward, unresolved obligation or tragicomic flaw; ordinary repetition is not a diagnostic mental-health claim or trauma label. Only connect a trigger to trauma when the authored story supports that link.
4. **What changes the reading**: a Card, a new field of sense/biome, unexpected Warburgian neighbor, changed stage backdrop/light or changed interaction with a particular person, while the resident remains the same identity.
5. **What the Resident knows**: capacity for self-observation and role literacy; awareness can be explicit, partially revealed, embodied, private or inconsistent. **Knowing one's mask is not automatically knowing the Academy, Fluff genesis, developer UI or metanarrative conspiracy.** Preserve character-specific information boundaries.
6. **What the Resident could do otherwise**: an honest imaginable alternative; not always immediately feasible, safe or consequence-free. A possible change can be glimpsed without forcing actual transformation. They may choose to continue, make one small divergence, change role for a context, or re-enter a habit.
7. **What others remember**: relationships, prior encounters, invitations, disappointments, gratitude, contradictions, chosen actions. Make return visits legibly different using existing Lean Memory and actual event truth, not author-generated autobiographical claims.

Avoid treating these axes as eight new sliders or a second NPC database. They are **authoring questions** that can be routed through existing ResidentProfile/identity, Affect, Activity/Intent, Encounter/Relationship, PerformanceCue, approved ChatterBox/Triplets, world/card references and Lean Memory.

## 4. Social and dramaturgical motion
**A useful recurring microstructure, not a compulsory formula:**
`invitation/encounter → character enacts expected role → contradiction/foil through another Resident or Card → glimpse of a different self-reading → free choice/response (repeat, improvise, refuse, offer, briefly change) → social/world consequence → memory/return opportunity`.

The moment of insight is not synonymous with a confession or a line containing “I know I'm an NPC.” A pause, helpful gesture, deliberately mistimed reaction, stubbornly resumed duty, or one counter-role action may tell the story better. Silence remains a valid response. Avoid endlessly rehearsing the same trigger for every player visit; contexts, relationships and memories offer real variation even if the character's distinctive weakness survives.

**Actor expressivity:** inherited KayKit/KFB rig/face/eye/mouth and Motion/ChatterBox consumer cues (gaze → head → body, tempo, micro-action, spatial position) should carry the mask before any meta-dialogue. The face-driver owner remains exclusive. KFB story and actor performance owners must not be replaced by a new `RoleMaskManager`.

## 5. How meta-stage and surreal set participate
The same Resident can stand before a Card under J17 daylight, move into a Clay dungeon lit by torch practicals, appear beside a deliberately flattened expressionist façade, or find an incompatible toy chess knight treated as an ordinary village official. His identity remains stable, yet social expectations and semantic associations change.

**Key distinction:** the actor can **play along** with a visible theatrical construction. This does not require reacting with stock “the world is fake” jokes every time. Awareness is a character attribute and editorial decision; never a mandatory exposition mechanic. The Card remains a real uncut source object; V-305 one color/palette source, V-286 bounded Meshy tint, same WS1 R3F camera/light host. Stage-/scene effects do not override player agency, authored lore or NPC continuity.

## 6. Worked editorial examples — hypotheses only, not newly accepted character canon
- **Goblin banker / brokerage-mask:** a character whose social role demands counting, hoarding and negotiating encounters a self-reliant cooperative character or a gift Card. They may genuinely help for a moment, then recommit to the bank's ceremonial ritual, finding dignity or comic comfort in the very contradiction. Do not make the banker's acceptance of an alternative a mandatory moral lesson.
- **Lorekeeper / mentor-mask:** an apparently authoritative librarian can be genuinely helpful yet deliberately offer a *neighboring* contradictory Card instead of the one asked for. The player infers the interpretation; the librarian is not the author's ultimate answer.
- **Lord Hunky and Lady Dory:** their Academy/Fluff history gives them distinct attitudes toward cosmic duty and self-deception, but the precise source-authorized personality and scene boundaries must come from WS0 `META_NARRATIVE_R1.md` and the current Hunky/Dory authoring source. No new lore or guaranteed redemption.
- **Krazy-Kat landscape exchange:** backgrounds and lights change while an actor finishes the same task; if the actor neither comments nor resets, the player must supply the closure. A changed context may modify interpretation without changing the authored performer.

These examples illustrate the model; they do not license invented source lines, trauma histories, personally identifiable player profiling or a universal moral scoring system.

## 7. Play loop: repeatability with agency, not compulsive extraction
The global aspiration is a **chill, funny, revisitable, discovery-rich** simulation of Cut&Play: players explore, travel, encounter, listen, negotiate, help, collect/interpret Cards, witness performances, compare other readings and return to changed relationships. Preserve freedom to observe, pass by, decline, refuse the expected answer or take an unexpected path. A character's recurrence is a reward of recognition, not a mandate to grind or click through therapy monologues.

Current WS0 `GAME_LOOP_R1.md` and `WORLD_CONCEPT_R1.md` are **OPEN proposals**; only the confirmed V-288 triplet of reasons to travel → travel fun → encounter at diorama peak is stable. Do not silently canonize a new quest, XP, trauma mechanic, progression gate or universal moral ranking. The earlier Bigger Picture `PULL, DON'T GATE` remains operative.

**Reader/player closure:** no obligation to agree with any Resident or Card. A vivid contradiction plus lived consequence can produce the inference. Kayfabe is neither a claim that all truth is arbitrary nor compulsory relativism. The audience's interpretive freedom is part of the design.

## R1.1 · Georg creative refinement · 2026-10-15 · Joyful fate, self-aware failure, cosmic casting and convivial opponents

**Source class:** Georg's direct Global Intent clarification, not a WS0-assigned V-number, not newly proven live behavior or license to fabricate specific individual Resident backstories. **It sharpens R1:** residents are not merely *capable* of recognizing their role eventually. Across many authored encounters they can already see their own recurring mistakes, quirks, externalizations, projections, relational/communication spirals and role contradictions with startling clarity. They can comment on these with dry, self-deprecating, sometimes savage good humour — while inhabiting the mask with wholehearted seriousness and playfulness rather than cynical detachment.

### A. Ontological frame: chosen, assigned, celebrated
The KFB universe may be experienced as a **cosmic casting**. A figure may imagine, believe, recall or in individual authored lore actually have chosen *this* role for their immortal soul: the recurring Orc, the restless Graveyard skeleton, the toy soldier, the unsuccessful schemer, the obsessive banker, the hopeful farmer. The cosmic job matters to them precisely because their flaws, hardships, compulsions, embarrassment and minor triumphs constitute *their* part of the ensemble.

**Do not assert a universal reincarnation/soul-selection fact about every inhabitant until Georg/WS0 settles that metaphysical rule.** Preserve plural interpretations: willingly selected role, inherited/assigned position, imposed institution, job enjoyed despite cost, or a Resident's personal myth about why they do it. Being capable of stepping out does not imply every character can instantly escape every material condition, war, hierarchy, compulsion or trauma. Role knowledge, agency, circumstance and consent remain distinct.

**Guiding thesis:** *They know the part is absurd. They still play it with their whole heart.* They can be simultaneously conscious of their role and emotionally committed to it. Neither a naive NPC trapped in code nor a smug actor mocking the set.

### B. Two equally true social registers
**In character / on duty:** the Orc hurls himself into another hopeless battle; the undead skeleton haunts its cemetery; a toy soldier embodies the petty, procedural banality of authority; the banker sincerely worships his ledgers. They act in-world and accept real scene stakes and consequences. Combat, fear, solidarity, injury, coercion and responsibility are not fake merely because the performance has Kayfabe form.

**Between performances / off duty:** opponents can share a beer or campfire, repair props, compare defeats, dance together, tell embarrassing stories, acknowledge their pattern and celebrate being cast in the same improbable universe. The Orc may be proud of his spectacular incompetence and the survivor genuinely tired of fighting; the skeleton may happily discuss the miserable logistics of undeath without an NPC “I'm dead LOL” gag. One Resident can recognize another as a fellow performer without agreeing with or excusing their on-duty behavior.

This need **not** look like a clean game-wide ON/OFF switch. A mask may leak into private life; after-hours camaraderie can coexist with resentment and exhaustion. A character might never clock out, might refuse the truce, or might privately be more ruthless than the publicly theatrical version. Author different thresholds of awareness, voluntariness and humour rather than one universal sitcom cast.

### C. Self-awareness is not rehabilitation, nor absolution
Georg's intended **reconciliatory** attitude is toward human imperfection and the comic dignity of bearing one's failures without pomposity. A character can love their own ridiculous predicament and laugh at a trauma-tinted habit; that is their distinctive perspective, **not a claim that harm is harmless or that actual trauma is funny by default**. Do not psycho-diagnose every quirk. Where biography clearly establishes trauma, portray it as authored history, not shorthand to excuse violence or control.

A toy soldier embodying the *banality of evil* can self-knowingly enact routine cruelty as an institutionally produced role. In an off-duty scene, he might also show warmth, humour or loyalty; that tension must **increase**, not erase, the player's ability to see that others were hurt. Compassion for the performer does not automatically absolve the action. Victims and other Residents retain independent perspectives, refusal rights, consequence and memory. There is no required redemptive moral ending and no convenient “only acting” defence.

### D. Cognitive dissonance as Kayfabe's resonance space
Proposed KFB formulation of Georg's existing maxim:
**“Cognitive dissonance is the resonance space of Kayfabe Bizarro.”**
This is a *creative metaphor*, not a clinical definition of cognitive dissonance or a requirement to resolve every contradiction.

The same person can be enemy and convivial neighbour, socially scripted and personally aware, shamefully fallible and sincerely joyful, inhabit a hand-built backdrop and behave as if it is real, play the same failure loop and still see an exit. The audience need not sign up for the character's explanation: the **simultaneous tension** invites Comic Closure and personal interpretation.

The scene must offer a *felt* contradiction — action, reaction, rhythm, scenery, relationship, accumulated memory — not just a speech announcing that contradiction exists. Never force every Resident to say “I know my own failures,” “we are all actors,” “our cosmic assignment,” etc. Generic writerly meta-jokes destroy the very conviction that makes the tension work.

### E. Situational perspective shift: the same character in several semantic fields
Reuse rather than replace the existing identity/Affect/Activity/Relationship/PerformanceCue and existing ChatterBox/Lean Memory axes. For an authored Resident, editorial questions:
1. **Role on stage:** who do others expect me to be, what do I do, and what is my real investment?
2. **Fault loop:** what exactly do I repeat (specific action/trigger and motive), what does it cost, what do I enjoy or secretly defend about it?
3. **Reflective aperture:** which alternate encounter/Card/“good neighbour” makes that loop legible even to me?
4. **Self-deprecation style:** brief sardonic observation, delighted exaggeration, embarrassed pause, or silent unforced gesture — character-specific, with no template catchphrase.
5. **Off-duty relation:** with whom can I lower the mask, laugh, contest a result, repair the set, form a truce or remain hostile?
6. **Agency and consequence:** can I improvise, choose to keep playing, refuse, rest or change a small act? Does my choice leave a believable trace in someone else's relationship or event memory?

These are **authored dramaturgy prompts, not fields to add to every NPC's runtime schema**. A resident can be highly role-aware without knowing the Academy's full story, the player UI or all other Residents' histories.

### F. Small scenic test concept: Orc vs toy soldier, then after hours
**Beat 1, public:** the source-true Orc mob re-enacts a losing skirmish in front of a Card/billboard; a toy soldier conscientiously applies a stupid order. Lighting and set may be exaggerated, but physical consequences/other actors still matter.
**Beat 2, after duty:** same original actors in the same source-backed diorama with one motivated night lamp or campfire; one offhand comment or gesture reveals they each know their own failure spiral. No mass confession, generic “NPC” joke or moral lesson. They can enjoy their parts, yet genuinely disagree about the soldier's actions.
**Beat 3, return:** a new Card/biome or dawn backdrop changes what the player notices; next morning the roles resume with one remembered relationship beat. No automatic permanent conversion, no resetting identity or dialogue/history and no forced reconciliation. Player may join, listen, leave or hold a contrary judgment.

These are **illustrative characters/actions only**, not approved exact cast, encounter, animation, Card mapping, text pool line or gameplay reward.

The experience is repeatable because meetings are interesting, funny and meaningfully revisitable, not because progress is withheld.
- Writing/acting outputs: no canned fourth-wall gag, therapeutic speech, generic self-deprecation, repetitive snark, “secretly good villain” trope, or automatic forgiveness. Let the Resident demonstrate both competence and defects through specific activity, expression, timing and relationships.
