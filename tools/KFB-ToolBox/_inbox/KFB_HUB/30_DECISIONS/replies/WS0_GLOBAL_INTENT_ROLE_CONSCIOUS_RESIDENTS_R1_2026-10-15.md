## 1. The precise intent, restated
KFB inhabitants (KayKit/KFB Residents and other source-owned characters) are not simply unaware loop-driven NPCs, nor all-wise mouthpieces for the author. They can recognize that they embody **roles**: social masks, jobs, archetypes, relationships and wrestling-style Kayfabe performances. Their tragicomic biography, faults, recurring triggers, competencies, strengths and loyalties make those roles lived and consequential.

A character can perceive a possible exit from a stale role, defect, habitual trigger spiral or self-imposed staging without automatically choosing it. Sometimes they knowingly continue their cosmic assignment, social position or eccentric performance because it gives meaning, pleasure, belonging, continuity or a comic identity. Sometimes they improvise or step outside the expected script. A chosen continuation is not evidence of unconscious programming; a self-aware character can still struggle with a habit and rationalize it. **Self-recognition, practical freedom and habitual action are separate tensions.**

The intended tone is compassionate, sardonic, self-deprecating and unsentimental. Residents may recognize their own failings and the failings of others, but are not forced into therapy talk, generic meta quips, catchphrases, explanatory speeches, or a redemption arc. Their actions, body language, repeated choices and occasional contradictions can communicate more than dialogue.

**Global performance rule:** *The performers may know they are playing; they still have lives inside the play.*

## 2. Distinct intellectual source lenses (analogy, not a claim of theoretical equivalence)
- **Karl Marx / Charaktermaske**: socially/economically situated individuals as personifications of relationships/roles; applied here as an *interpretive lens* for roles, not a claim that Marx wrote about voluntary wrestling performances. Historical source: `Das Kapital` vol. I ch. 2, and conceptual research at https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charaktermaske . Do not conflate imposed socioeconomic structures with a freely chosen theatrical costume.
- **Wrestling Kayfabe / theatrical acting**: a performer keeps an intelligible role going in a social performance even when actor and audience can comprehend the convention. KFB interpretation: role allegiance and deliberate performance can themselves be expressive agency. The Resident is **not** merely an actor's out-of-fiction human avatar; their inside-world personal history remains real for that fiction.
- **Markus Gabriel / fields of sense**: what an object, act, Card, or role is *about* varies across meaningful contexts; the Resident retains continuity while context reveals a different aspect. Interview: https://euppublishingblog.com/2016/09/23/graham-harman-interviews-markus-gabriel/ . Do not trivialize Gabriel's ontology as literal portals or use his concept as a new runtime field without WS0 approval.
- **Aby Warburg / good-neighbour principle**: unexpected proximity/rearrangement creates meaningful associative discovery, rather than pre-assigned categorical order. Institutional source: https://warburg.sas.ac.uk/library-collections/library/about-library . KFB interpretation: new Card/biome/Resident juxtaposition invites players to discover a non-obvious connection. It is **not** a rule that any random mismatch works.
- **Herriman / Sterrett + metatheatre**: the scene, day/night or stage backdrop may change radically without interrupting the character's performance. Alternative settings can activate new social masks and readings. The scenery is a participant in storytelling without becoming the Resident's personality controller.

## 3. Stable person, shifting local mask — conceptual axes, not a data schema
1. **Who this person is**: source-backed identity, body/face, individual biography, strengths, fears/vulnerabilities, competencies, chosen attachments, important relationships, resident-owned accumulated memories.
2. **Who they are performing here**: local profession / institution / faction / social mask / current onstage assignment, what the audience and nearby residents expect.
3. **Why the loop returns**: a specific authored contradiction, learned trigger, reward, unresolved obligation or tragicomic flaw; ordinary repetition is not a diagnostic mental-health claim or trauma label. Only connect a trigger to trauma when the authored story supports that link.
4. **What changes the reading**: a Card, a new field of sense/biome, unexpected Warburgian neighbor, changed stage backdrop/light or changed interaction with a particular person, while the resident remains the same identity.
5. **What the Resident knows**: capacity for self-observation and role literacy; awareness can be explicit, partially revealed, embodied, private or inconsistent. **Knowing one's mask is not automatically knowing the Academy, Fluff genesis, developer UI or metanarrative conspiracy.** Preserve character-specific information boundaries.
6. **What the Resident could do otherwise**: an honest imaginable alternative; not always immediately feasible, safe or consequence-free. A possible change can be glimpsed without forcing actual transformation. They may choose to continue, make one small divergence, change role for a context, or re-enter a habit.
7. **What others remember**: relationships, prior encounters, invitations, disappointments, gratitude, contradictions, chosen actions. Make return visits legibly different using existing Lean Memory and actual event truth, not author-generated autobiographical claims.

Avoid treating these axes as eight new sliders or a second NPC database. They are **authoring questions** that can be routed through existing ResidentProfile/identity, Affect, Activity/Intent, Encounter/Relationship, PerformanceCue, approved ChatterBox/Triplets, world/card references and Lean Memory.

## 4. Social and dramaturgical motion
**A useful recurring microstructure, not a compulsory formula:**
`invitation/encounter → character enacts expected role → contradiction/foil through another Resident or Card → glimpse of a different self-reading → free choice/response (repeat, improvise, refuse, offer, briefly change) → social/world consequence → memory/return opportunity`.

The moment of insight is not synonymous with a confession or a line containing “I know I'm an NPC.” A pause, helpful gesture, deliberately mistimed reaction, stubbornly resumed duty, or one counter-role action may tell the story better. Silence remains a valid response. Avoid endlessly rehearsing the same trigger for every player visit; contexts, relationships and memories offer real variation even if the character's distinctive weakness survives.

**Actor expressivity:** inherited KayKit/KFB rig/face/eye/mouth and Motion/ChatterBox consumer cues (gaze → head → body, tempo, micro-action, spatial position) should carry the mask before any meta-dialogue. The face-driver owner remains exclusive. KFB story and actor performance owners must not be replaced by a new `RoleMaskManager`.

## 5. How meta-stage and surreal set participate
The same Resident can stand before a Card under J17 daylight, move into a Clay dungeon lit by torch practicals, appear beside a deliberately flattened expressionist façade, or find an incompatible toy chess knight treated as an ordinary village official. His identity remains stable, yet social expectations and semantic associations change.

**Key distinction:** the actor can **play along** with a visible theatrical construction. This does not require reacting with stock “the world is fake” jokes every time. Awareness is a character attribute and editorial decision; never a mandatory exposition mechanic. The Card remains a real uncut source object; V-305 one color/palette source, V-286 bounded Meshy tint, same WS1 R3F camera/light host. Stage-/scene effects do not override player agency, authored lore or NPC continuity.

## 6. Worked editorial examples — hypotheses only, not newly accepted character canon
- **Goblin banker / brokerage-mask:** a character whose social role demands counting, hoarding and negotiating encounters a self-reliant cooperative character or a gift Card. They may genuinely help for a moment, then recommit to the bank's ceremonial ritual, finding dignity or comic comfort in the very contradiction. Do not make the banker's acceptance of an alternative a mandatory moral lesson.
- **Lorekeeper / mentor-mask:** an apparently authoritative librarian can be genuinely helpful yet deliberately offer a *neighboring* contradictory Card instead of the one asked for. The player infers the interpretation; the librarian is not the author's ultimate answer.
- **Lord Hunky and Lady Dory:** their Academy/Fluff history gives them distinct attitudes toward cosmic duty and self-deception, but the precise source-authorized personality and scene boundaries must come from WS0 `META_NARRATIVE_R1.md` and the current Hunky/Dory authoring source. No new lore or guaranteed redemption.
- **Krazy-Kat landscape exchange:** backgrounds and lights change while an actor finishes the same task; if the actor neither comments nor resets, the player must supply the closure. A changed context may modify interpretation without changing the authored performer.

These examples illustrate the model; they do not license invented source lines, trauma histories, personally identifiable player profiling or a universal moral scoring system.

## 7. Play loop: repeatability with agency, not compulsive extraction
The global aspiration is a **chill, funny, revisitable, discovery-rich** simulation of Cut&Play: players explore, travel, encounter, listen, negotiate, help, collect/interpret Cards, witness performances, compare other readings and return to changed relationships. Preserve freedom to observe, pass by, decline, refuse the expected answer or take an unexpected path. A character's recurrence is a reward of recognition, not a mandate to grind or click through therapy monologues.

Current WS0 `GAME_LOOP_R1.md` and `WORLD_CONCEPT_R1.md` are **OPEN proposals**; only the confirmed V-288 triplet of reasons to travel → travel fun → encounter at diorama peak is stable. Do not silently canonize a new quest, XP, trauma mechanic, progression gate or universal moral ranking. The earlier Bigger Picture `PULL, DON'T GATE` remains operative.

**Reader/player closure:** no obligation to agree with any Resident or Card. A vivid contradiction plus lived consequence can produce the inference. Kayfabe is neither a claim that all truth is arbitrary nor compulsory relativism. The audience's interpretive freedom is part of the design.

## R1.1 · Georg creative refinement · 2026-10-15 · Joyful fate, self-aware failure, cosmic casting and convivial opponents

**Source class:** Georg's direct Global Intent clarification, not a WS0-assigned V-number, not newly proven live behavior or license to fabricate specific individual Resident backstories. **It sharpens R1:** residents are not merely *capable* of recognizing their role eventually. Across many authored encounters they can already see their own recurring mistakes, quirks, externalizations, projections, relational/communication spirals and role contradictions with startling clarity. They can comment on these with dry, self-deprecating, sometimes savage good humour — while inhabiting the mask with wholehearted seriousness and playfulness rather than cynical detachment.

### A. Ontological frame: chosen, assigned, celebrated
The KFB universe may be experienced as a **cosmic casting**. A figure may imagine, believe, recall or in individual authored lore actually have chosen *this* role for their immortal soul: the recurring Orc, the restless Graveyard skeleton, the toy soldier, the unsuccessful schemer, the obsessive banker, the hopeful farmer. The cosmic job matters to them precisely because their flaws, hardships, compulsions, embarrassment and minor triumphs constitute *their* part of the ensemble.

**Do not assert a universal reincarnation/soul-selection fact about every inhabitant until Georg/WS0 settles that metaphysical rule.** Preserve plural interpretations: willingly selected role, inherited/assigned position, imposed institution, job enjoyed despite cost, or a Resident's personal myth about why they do it. Being capable of stepping out does not imply every character can instantly escape every material condition, war, hierarchy, compulsion or trauma. Role knowledge, agency, circumstance and consent remain distinct.

**Guiding thesis:** *They know the part is absurd. They still play it with their whole heart.* They can be simultaneously conscious of their role and emotionally committed to it. Neither a naive NPC trapped in code nor a smug actor mocking the set.

### B. Two equally true social registers
**In character / on duty:** the Orc hurls himself into another hopeless battle; the undead skeleton haunts its cemetery; a toy soldier embodies the petty, procedural banality of authority; the banker sincerely worships his ledgers. They act in-world and accept real scene stakes and consequences. Combat, fear, solidarity, injury, coercion and responsibility are not fake merely because the performance has Kayfabe form.

**Between performances / off duty:** opponents can share a beer or campfire, repair props, compare defeats, dance together, tell embarrassing stories, acknowledge their pattern and celebrate being cast in the same improbable universe. The Orc may be proud of his spectacular incompetence and the survivor genuinely tired of fighting; the skeleton may happily discuss the miserable logistics of undeath without an NPC “I'm dead LOL” gag. One Resident can recognize another as a fellow performer without agreeing with or excusing their on-duty behavior.

This need **not** look like a clean game-wide ON/OFF switch. A mask may leak into private life; after-hours camaraderie can coexist with resentment and exhaustion. A character might never clock out, might refuse the truce, or might privately be more ruthless than the publicly theatrical version. Author different thresholds of awareness, voluntariness and humour rather than one universal sitcom cast.

### C. Self-awareness is not rehabilitation, nor absolution
Georg's intended **reconciliatory** attitude is toward human imperfection and the comic dignity of bearing one's failures without pomposity. A character can love their own ridiculous predicament and laugh at a trauma-tinted habit; that is their distinctive perspective, **not a claim that harm is harmless or that actual trauma is funny by default**. Do not psycho-diagnose every quirk. Where biography clearly establishes trauma, portray it as authored history, not shorthand to excuse violence or control.

A toy soldier embodying the *banality of evil* can self-knowingly enact routine cruelty as an institutionally produced role. In an off-duty scene, he might also show warmth, humour or loyalty; that tension must **increase**, not erase, the player's ability to see that others were hurt. Compassion for the performer does not automatically absolve the action. Victims and other Residents retain independent perspectives, refusal rights, consequence and memory. There is no required redemptive moral ending and no convenient “only acting” defence.

### D. Cognitive dissonance as Kayfabe's resonance space
Proposed KFB formulation of Georg's existing maxim:
**“Cognitive dissonance is the resonance space of Kayfabe Bizarro.”**
This is a *creative metaphor*, not a clinical definition of cognitive dissonance or a requirement to resolve every contradiction.

The same person can be enemy and convivial neighbour, socially scripted and personally aware, shamefully fallible and sincerely joyful, inhabit a hand-built backdrop and behave as if it is real, play the same failure loop and still see an exit. The audience need not sign up for the character's explanation: the **simultaneous tension** invites Comic Closure and personal interpretation.

The scene must offer a *felt* contradiction — action, reaction, rhythm, scenery, relationship, accumulated memory — not just a speech announcing that contradiction exists. Never force every Resident to say “I know my own failures,” “we are all actors,” “our cosmic assignment,” etc. Generic writerly meta-jokes destroy the very conviction that makes the tension work.

### E. Situational perspective shift: the same character in several semantic fields
Reuse rather than replace the existing identity/Affect/Activity/Relationship/PerformanceCue and existing ChatterBox/Lean Memory axes. For an authored Resident, editorial questions:
1. **Role on stage:** who do others expect me to be, what do I do, and what is my real investment?
2. **Fault loop:** what exactly do I repeat (specific action/trigger and motive), what does it cost, what do I enjoy or secretly defend about it?
3. **Reflective aperture:** which alternate encounter/Card/“good neighbour” makes that loop legible even to me?
4. **Self-deprecation style:** brief sardonic observation, delighted exaggeration, embarrassed pause, or silent unforced gesture — character-specific, with no template catchphrase.
5. **Off-duty relation:** with whom can I lower the mask, laugh, contest a result, repair the set, form a truce or remain hostile?
6. **Agency and consequence:** can I improvise, choose to keep playing, refuse, rest or change a small act? Does my choice leave a believable trace in someone else's relationship or event memory?

These are **authored dramaturgy prompts, not fields to add to every NPC's runtime schema**. A resident can be highly role-aware without knowing the Academy's full story, the player UI or all other Residents' histories.

### F. Small scenic test concept: Orc vs toy soldier, then after hours
**Beat 1, public:** the source-true Orc mob re-enacts a losing skirmish in front of a Card/billboard; a toy soldier conscientiously applies a stupid order. Lighting and set may be exaggerated, but physical consequences/other actors still matter.
**Beat 2, after duty:** same original actors in the same source-backed diorama with one motivated night lamp or campfire; one offhand comment or gesture reveals they each know their own failure spiral. No mass confession, generic “NPC” joke or moral lesson. They can enjoy their parts, yet genuinely disagree about the soldier's actions.
**Beat 3, return:** a new Card/biome or dawn backdrop changes what the player notices; next morning the roles resume with one remembered relationship beat. No automatic permanent conversion, no resetting identity or dialogue/history and no forced reconciliation. Player may join, listen, leave or hold a contrary judgment.

These are **illustrative characters/actions only**, not approved exact cast, encounter, animation, Card mapping, text pool line or gameplay reward.

### G. Quality guard / relation to current production
- Preserve **Conviction + Reflectiveness + Joy + Cost** together. Avoid a facile “everyone is friends because it was theatre” retcon. Avoid treating the cast's ritual suffering as a compulsion engine for the player. The experience is repeatable because meetings are interesting, funny and meaningfully revisitable, not because progress is withheld.
- Writing/acting outputs: no canned fourth-wall gag, therapeutic speech, generic self-deprecation, repetitive snark, “secretly good villain” trope, or automatic forgiveness. Let the Resident demonstrate both competence and defects through specific activity, expression, timing and relationships.
- This extends the ongoing WS0 Global Intent and the already filed metatheatre/light intake #404; it does **not** widen the active WS1 MVP into fully simulated psyche, immortality or faction after-hours AI.
- The existing WS0 register remains authority for stage/lighting, actual Card, approved dialogue pool and real KKit cast. Existing WS1 R3F host is the only runtime writer. No new schema, V-number, deployment, new issue or human gate from this addendum.

**Exactly one next gate (unchanged): \`WS0_LIGHTING_AND_METATHEATRE_SLICE_ASSIGNMENT\`.** WS0 may use one role-aware contrast as *narrative reference* when selecting its current two-actor Card-stage pilot; any later resident-life production work requires a separately authorized receiving slice, not this conceptual addendum.

## R1.2 · Georg Global Intent refinement · 2026-10-15 · The Player's Cosmic Theatre / Audience Covenant

**Status: DIRECT GEORG CREATIVE INTENT; editorial/ontological grammar, NOT an approved metaphysics for every character, gameplay retention algorithm, new WS1 owner, added MVP gate, or claim of technical implementation.** This is an *additive clarification* of R1/R1.1 (not a replacement): Kayfabe Residents perform, reflect, fail, reconcile and invent because there is an audience/player with whom they share their universe.

### 1. The constitutive audience: the player is part of the fiction
The “Kayfabe Bizarro Multiverse” may be inhabited by characters who feel or know that **their continued existence as performers is bound up with the player's enjoyment, curiosity and willingness to return/interact with their simulation.** Their cosmic calling is *to make this universe worth inhabiting and experiencing*. The player is therefore not merely an external button-pusher, observer or evaluator, but a constitutive collaborator / guest / co-performer whose curiosity and actions complete the work.

**Interpretive nuance:** this is *diegetic/metanarrative consciousness*, not a literal assertion that the program deletes all NPCs when the player logs off, that their subjective suffering is real, or that an app session must remain open to keep anyone alive. It must **not contradict** the established game metaphor that Residents have a lived life before the player arrives and Lean Memory preserves actual encounters. The fictional awareness of dependence on being played and the within-fiction continuity of offscreen lives should remain a **productive paradox**: the world exists for the player as a work, yet the characters behave as if their world continues when no one watches.

Do not turn this into an unwanted “save me by staying online” demand, emotional blackmail, fear-of-missing-out retention prompt, compulsive engagement mechanism or manipulative pseudo-friendship. The player can always leave, ignore, choose, return or decline without punishment or guilt. Keep current KFB **PULL, DON'T GATE**.

### 2. A mutual invitation, not forced friendliness or obedience
Many inhabitants may **wish to befriend the player**, because meeting a player and having their idiosyncratic role witnessed is meaningful and fun. This is an underlying orientation toward *encounter*, not an all-NPC friendliness score or mandatory pleasant dialogue.

- The antagonist may be an absolutely committed opponent, ambush, taunt, deceive *in their authored role*, and later be pleased that the player enjoyed a fair and memorable contest.
- The Toy Soldier or bureaucratic gatekeeper may sincerely perform power and become a compelling foil; no instant cozy makeover, no claim that coercion has no victims.
- Another Resident may be suspicious, grumpy, distant, contemptuous, shy, mischievous or simply too busy to talk. Some will not want friendship; social difference is essential for dramatic life.
- **Friendship must be earned and contextual** through events, recognition, trust, shared interests, boundaries, help, disagreement and re-encounters—not by automatic flattering assertions, “you're my best friend”, gift farming or generic cozy banter. An enemy can nonetheless be an excellent fellow performer.

**Creative maxim:** *Every character may have a reason to make the player's visit worthwhile. Not every character has a reason to make it comfortable.*

Characters themselves may consciously defend this enjoyable cosmic contract, with an energetic sense of professional pride and theatrical duty, without repeating identical explanatory lore speeches.

### 3. Universe in a Nutshell: living viewpoints rather than a moral flower show
The game is a **chill-and-fun miniature of contradictory human experience**, not a sanitized idyllic amusement park, replacement textbook or compulsory ethical lesson. It should combine visual/haptic comedy, emotional truth, alien viewpoints, competing social roles, awkward encounters, questionable motives, genuine acts of generosity, tragicomic failures and occasional difficult or dark implications.

Cards and encounters can invite players to **play, inhabit, confront, negotiate with or deliver messages on behalf of** ideas they would not endorse. This supports critical distance and Scott-McCloud-style closure through experience, not coercion into accepting any Card claim. Players need not be forced to internalize, declare allegiance to, or agree with controversial/provocative opinions. Context, authorship and consequences remain clear; alternate perspectives are *encounterable*, not automatically presented as equally true or ethically acceptable.

Practical examples (proposals, not approved mission data):
- A courier accepts a message from a Dystopian officer and carries it to a Protopian farmer, encountering two radically different interpretations before choosing their own response.
- A Card performance casts the player as a temporarily complicit participant in a ridiculous institution; another NPC offers a contrasting interpretation, but nobody turns it into a multiple-choice moral exam.
- The “enemy” Orc may explain, briefly and wryly, how seriously he takes another performance of inevitable defeat; the player's participation is real play and may change memory/relations.
- Player agency includes **observing without participating**, declining an errand, helping an adversary, disagreeing with a Card's frame, or simply traveling and laughing at an object-based gag.

The game itself need not explain each paradox. A Resident's body, a wrong-way paper facade, a prop, a performed refusal or the difference between on-duty and after-hours life can make it clear.

### 4. Historical performance vocabulary: precise, complementary sources
**Buster Keaton — physical-space comedy:** stunts, mechanisms, props, perspective, mounting consequences and exact framing make absurd physical situations legible even with restrained/deadpan expression. Use **object → expectation → physically clear violation → delayed reaction / consequence**; preserve spatial clarity and timing. Keaton's films are not “random chaos”: the architectural gag often depends on a precise world that seems to follow its own rules. Source: BFI on *The General* (1926), https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/general-greatest-comedy-all-time .

**Charlie Chaplin — body, social hierarchy and pathos:** the character's smallest impulse or dignified attempt at mastery may trigger escalating slapstick amid poverty, authority or industrial coercion. Emotional vulnerability and funny body-language are simultaneously true; *City Lights*, *The Kid* and *Modern Times* are useful distinct examples. The Tramp's physical persona and clothes communicate a “character mask” without a philosophical monologue. Sources: BFI https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/18e7950b-fa7e-5a5e-a897-a782a40f1c5c/city-lights and https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/4cdccc6d-f143-506d-a2b1-4c34605cb088/modern-times ; https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/charlie-chaplin-promotional-photos .

**Brecht — critical alienation/distance:** make the conventions, constructed scenery or alternative possibilities visible *at meaningful times*, to allow player interpretation and disagreement. This can coexist with immersion via invested characters; it is not a command to break the fourth wall with a joke every minute.

**Ancient Greek dramatic traditions — differentiated sources:** tragedy, comedy, satyr drama and chorus can contribute masks, competing voices, staged ritual, spectatorship and the oscillation between suffering and laughter. Avoid a false claim that “Greek tragicomedy” was one uniform classical genre with the same logic as modern slapstick. This is an editorial montage of distinct traditions, not an exact historical reconstruction.

**Existing KFB theatrical/graphic donors** from prior R1: Caligari expressionist flats, Lang's *Metropolis* miniatures/forced perspective, Lugosi Gothic entrances, Herriman shifting Sunday-panel backgrounds, Sterrett warped comic-space, source-backed pop-up 2.5D props, and card/boardgame framing. These are **construction principles**, not license to substitute generic art or run another camera/light controller.

### 5. The player as actor: a possible interaction dramaturgy
A playful role-offer should never require “accept this ideology” or a mandatory fourth-wall conversation. Candidate beat grammar:
1. **World invites:** encounter with a committed Resident, real KFB Card, playable stage-prop, mission, performative conflict or funny physical premise.
2. **Player chooses a stance:** join the act, carry a message, oppose it, help, experiment, watch, ignore or walk away.
3. **World performs honestly:** one real authorial voice and world consequence, not a moralizing tutorial or a universal “yes, player” NPC.
4. **One contradiction materializes:** another neighbor, shifting light/backdrop, a minor stage malfunction handled in-character, or Keaton-like tangible object gag exposes a second reading.
5. **Humanized continuation:** characters remain dedicated to their roles, can show joy, fatigue, self-awareness or conviviality without erasing real stakes and without playing psychological tricks on the player.
6. **Return value:** a memorable Card, source provenance, earned relationship, new context, altered prop, short performance, social memory or simply the desire to see what happens next—never threat of lost friends if the player leaves.

This is an **editorial lens**, not a six-state quest engine, not a forced consent click-through, not automatic resident awareness of the game's developer or technical lifecycle.

### 6. Concrete staging/proof suggestion — the same existing WS1 duo stage
A source-true two-character KFB Card/Billboard encounter stays on the current accepted stage host. One actor plays a role with total seriousness, another gives a genuinely different reading; the player can stand, walk, decline or observe. In a second shot the physical stage briefly becomes more obviously constructed (a facade's paper rear, spotlight, painted city or backdrop switch). An optional coherent Keaton-like gag uses real collision/prop physics and delayed expression **only with accepted source rigs and scene ownership**. In a follow-up social beat both performers can share a wink, disagreement or invitation, but no forced friendship. Evaluate **player can resist or leave; original Card readable; credible resident actions; no generic meta jokes; light/contact/world continuity** before even considering broad rollout. This is a possible editorial comparison inside the existing Issue #404 pilot, not a separate approved milestone.

### 7. Explicit safeguards / scope
- The “their purpose is to entertain me” fiction **does not** mean Residents are obliged to flatter, forgive harm, be always available, or concede a debate. Their authored dignity, error loops and limits generate the entertainment.
- Avoid harmful design that makes users feel NPCs will die or suffer from ending a play session. Never imply actual AI consciousness or require continuous play.
- Preserve freedom to hold one's own opinions, not a propagandistic forced march or truth relativism. Critical thinking emerges from comparing grounded rival interpretations and living consequences.
- No automatic second NPC runtime, feelings engine, mood database, global narrative camera, extra renderer/audio owner, invented stable lore or premature adoption of legal/asset rights.
- No change to the R1.1 principle that compassion for a performer doesn't absolve institutional cruelty or erase victim memory. Do not trivialize violent/traumatic conditions for humor.
- WS0 remains the sole canon/V-number/priority authority; WS1 remains the actual runtime/scene owner; the pending lighting/metatheatre assignment is still the **single next gate**.

## R1.3 · GEORG CANONICAL CREATIVE CORRECTION · 2026-10-15 · Joyful Being-in-the-World, Vita Activa and Circle of Life

**Priority: EXPLICIT CORRECTION OF THE R1.2 “AUDIENCE-DEPENDENT EXISTENCE” CLAIM, not merely another possible interpretation.** All previous history remains in this additive ledger, but R1.3 overrides the affected R1.2 sentences. **DO NOT RETELL THIS as “NPCs exist only while the player enjoys them”, “their continued existence is tied to the player's mood, return or login”, “the player keeps them alive”, “NPCs are happy because they must please the player”, or any FOMO/game-retention dependency.** Those were an incorrect interpretation of Georg's creative intent.

### A. Correct ontological relation — existence and joy before the audience
The KFB KayKit/KFB residents exist **as persons inside the fiction** with their own biography, role, meaningful work, joy, frustrations, relationships and offscreen/world continuity. They are not contingent on a player's arbitrary choice. The player's visit or return is **a happy occasion to enact, share, contest and celebrate** their own cosmic roles — not the condition of the characters' continued being.

Their delight derives from **being-in-the-world, doing what they do, embracing or humorously reflecting their own sometimes deeply dysfunctional character mask**. An Orc, Evil Mage, Trickster, Graveyard revenant, Toy Soldier, resident builder or socially awkward authority can find joy and a sense of vocation in inhabiting that part with full Kayfabe conviction. They welcome a returning player as a fellow participant in a game they are already energetically playing. The character can challenge, oppose, annoy or even mock the player; a compelling encounter does not mean unconditional friendliness.

**Corrected guiding maxim:** *“The player does not sustain their existence; the player joins their celebration of it.”* (editorial formulation, not a verbatim Georg quotation)
**A second concise line:** *“They were already alive in their world. The player arrives in time for another performance.”* (editorial formulation)

A specific Resident might have a *diegetic belief* about cosmic soul-casting, a destined job or a metaphysical theatre, but there is no universal software-lifecycle threat, no player-sanctioned creation/annihilation, no instant resurrection obligation and no global coercion to keep acting. Existing source-established Academy/Fluff lore remains distinct from what each Resident is authorized to know.

### B. Philosophical alignment with explicit differences
- **Martin Heidegger — Dasein / In-der-Welt-sein / Geworfenheit and Entwurf**: a person finds themselves already in a situation, history and possibility-space not fully chosen and can take a stance toward their own being. Source: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heidegger/ . **Caveat:** Heidegger's thrownness itself is *not* equivalent to cheerfulness, a chosen soul prebirth or an already established KFB reincarnation law. KFB's joyful affirmation is its own creative transformation of the existential framing.
- **Hannah Arendt — Vita activa**: **labor / work / action**, which differ: repetitive activity that sustains life; construction of a shared world; action among plural people through which who one is becomes manifest. Source: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arendt/ . In KFB, the joyful builder/repair crew and recurring work cycle illuminate *work and maintenance*; friendly rivalries, stage participation, surprise and resident relationships illuminate *action*. **Do not reduce Arendt's whole theory to a mechanical build-destroy labor loop**. Plurality and the unpredictable arrival of another participant matter.
- **G. W. F. Hegel — reciprocal recognition / social self-consciousness**: the player, Orc, builder, adversary and community confer social meaning through encounter and recognition, but a resident's ontological right to exist is **not at the mercy of a single player's appreciation**. Source: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hegel/ . This is a selective KFB editorial use, not an assertion that Hegel proposed comic game loops.
- Earlier **Marx/Charaktermasken, Gabriel/Sinnfelder, Warburg/gute Nachbarschaft** still supply distinct roles, contextual meaning and associative encounters, not a blended doctrine. The KFB-specific gain is affirmative agency, committed performance and comic hospitality despite failure.

### C. The Doozers analogy — rebuilding is a joyful contribution
The source analog is Jim Henson's **Fraggle Rock Doozers**: they enthusiastically construct elaborate structures that Fraggles eat, making room and occasion to build anew. This is a *story/design donor*, not an assertion the KFB Freddies are Doozers or a permission to copy character IP. Reference/context: https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Doozers and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraggle_Rock . The symbiosis is **not a threat to exist until somebody destroys something**, and is not an excuse to trap the player in a repetitive extraction loop.

**KFB Circle of Life (proposed native story/scene grammar):**
- A world-state is a **living place**, with authored structure, residents, scars, repair practices, meaningful use and ongoing routines.
- Player or in-world events may damage, dismantle, rearrange or consume structures; not every building must be destructible and an act of aggression is not automatically rewarded or called beneficial.

- A player returning for a Dungeon run may find the same delighted, committed skeleton performing the next haunting; the cycle evokes festive recurring play, with combat and consequences still meaningful in fiction. No universal joy assumption for victims, and not every skeleton needs to enjoy being defeated.
- Rebuilding, tending, staging, fighting, resting and celebrating together form **one convivial circle of life**. The world is active *between* player encounters; the player is a welcome collaborator/visitor rather than its existential life support.

### D. Fluff, laughter, and a deeply humane multiverse

## R1.4 · Georg Creative Intent · 2026-10-15 · The right to lament, self-aware failure and stoic-comic reprise

**Status:** Georg's explicit conceptual refinement of current R1.3 life-affirming cosmology; **no new V-number, runtime gate, mandatory character reaction, or accepted dialogue line**. All earlier Global Intent remains unless explicitly superseded by R1.3. This addition makes *room for actual suffering, complaint and mood changes* inside sincere affection for one's role.

### A. No compulsory happiness — subjective complaint is real
KFB characters are entitled to complain, grumble, lament, feel irritated, resent the weather, blame bad luck, fight their circumstances, temporarily lose motivation, react bitterly to failure, and remain frustrated during an encounter. The emotion is **not a disguised joke or evidence their love of existence is fake**. Resident individuality emerges from being able to hold contradictory feelings in different beats and contexts.

The same Orc may wholeheartedly curse the dungeon run at the instant of defeat and later laugh affectionately about the predictability of the result, prepare for tomorrow's encounter and bring fresh ingenuity to it. A Graveyard skeleton can be weary of its night shift and nonetheless take pride in haunting; a Doozer-like builder may curse the latest fallen wall before enjoying the craftsmanship of repairing it. Some Residents may take longer to recover, decline an encounter, change a habit or remain angry; the whole world is not forced into instant cheerful closure.

**Key creative rule:** *Complaint may be entirely genuine; later affirmation need not invalidate the complaint. The role remains loved without demanding that every minute of it be pleasant.*

### B. Epictetus supplies an especially exact stage metaphor
**Epictetus, Enchiridion / Handbook §17:** the human is an actor in a play whose role and circumstances may be assigned; the performer is responsible for playing the given part well. Direct text/review: https://epictetus-enchiridion.org/en/chapters/enchiridion/17/ ; scholarly overview updated 2025: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epictetus/ .

**Enchiridion §5:** external events and our judgments of them are distinguished; critical reflection can reappraise the same predicament without altering its physical facts. Primary-text translation at Tufts Perseus: https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0237%3Atext%3Denc%3Achapter%3D5 .

The stoic analogy is **not** a mandate to smile through harm, surrender one's agency, attribute real structural injury solely to attitude, or believe all roles were freely chosen. Epictetus distinguishes assigned part from quality of performance; KFB creatively overlays conviction, explicit comic awareness, embodied failure, irony and the occasional fourth-wall awareness. Stoic moral development is neither emotional anesthesia nor a proof that a character never complains. The SEP explicitly notes that Epictetus is not advocating being “unfeeling like a statue” and recognizes meaningful appropriate feelings. KFB's comedy and joyful reprise are **its own authored variation**, not a falsely attributed Stoic doctrine.

### C. Staged rhythm: grief/irritation → different angle → owned laughter → re-entry
A small **editorial beat grammar**, not a four-state gameplay machine:
1. **Actual obstruction / failure:** a setback to what the resident is trying sincerely to achieve. Visible action, stage blocking, costume, role and relationship matter. A genuine exasperated reaction or complaint can land here.
2. **Shift in situation / relationship:** another resident, a different card, off-duty gathering, absurd pop-up facade, a changed light/semantic field or an unexpected visitor exposes an alternative perspective. No requirement that the NPC narrate their private psychology.
3. **Self-aware inflection:** one character-specific glance, dry observation, embarrassed admission, sardonically accurate quip or even an affectionate silent shrug signals recognition of their personal failure pattern. The fourth wall may briefly become perceptible where dramaturgically justified, without a generic “I'm an NPC” gag or repeated cosmic exposition.
4. **Unforced continuation or departure:** the same person chooses to recommit energetically, take a rest, improvise, help another, change a small tactic, protest again or refuse. The event leaves an appropriate consequence in existing world state and social memory. **R1.3 remains authoritative:** their existence, friendship or intrinsic life-joy does not depend on player attention.

This beat grammar creates **character recognition and return value** without mandatory therapeutic resolutions, automatic self-healing, imposed positivity or one predetermined lesson. The player is invited to infer the model for their own setbacks rather than being instructed to emulate a stoic sage.

### D. Satire versus suffering: character-specific balance
- A complaint should identify a **specific authored inconvenience or contradiction**, not filler grumbling. The failure loop must have a real repeated cause, a recognizable skill/weakness and scene consequence.
- Don't ridicule trauma itself. When a resident's biography establishes a trauma or recurring wound, humor can emerge from their authored relationship to their own coping style; it does not excuse cruelty or force them to disclose or laugh at their injuries. Different residents differ in ability and willingness to self-deprecate.
- A “villain on duty / person at campfire” duality remains possible, but a reflective joke after work **does not absolve** violence against others or cancel a survivor's memory. An offended counterpart may refuse to laugh.
- Jokes may be verbal, spatial/visual, physically choreographed in the Keaton/Chaplin tradition, silent, or depend on another character's deadpan reading. Vary the timing and semantic field to avoid repetitive canned lines and uniform fourth-wall breaks.
- Residents can openly know/celebrate their cosmic Kayfabe task **without knowing all of the Academy, Fluff cosmology or developer interface**. Choosing a role, accepting a role, resenting and affirming a role are distinct possibilities.

### E. Example for a future source-accurate performance comparison (not canon)
**Beat A:** a real authored Orc gets defeated by an adventurer and curses the unfairness of how reliably the situation goes against it.
**Beat B:** later, by a campfire, the same Orc watches a builder struggle with another repeatedly repaired stage wall; both notice the comedy in their different kinds of stubbornness.
**Beat C:** one little physical action or dry *approved source-pool* line makes clear that the Orc recognizes its own performance and cares about doing it well, even when it complains.
**Beat D:** next visit, the Orc returns to its role with a new tactic; perhaps the builder has visibly repaired the wall differently. Preserve the real Card, chosen original cast, situation, world/repair and Lean Memory rather than fabricating exact dialogue or fresh actor metadata.

In a real pilot, content must come from current accepted card/dialogue/cast sources; no invented lines, NPC trauma stories or fake Stage screenshot. Two properly timed acts may accomplish the intent; **not all four beats need to be shown or spoken**.
