## Product intent
Use **Plato's cave and a playful wrestling match** as an optional local incident in the KFB open world. Present an authored Card claim, let the player physically cover, expose, challenge or reframe it, and let existing Residents react in character. Cognitive dissonance is the artistic resonance space, not a score or instruction to agree. The game is **Chill & Fun**, inviting curiosity and comedy rather than ideological lectures or a mandatory combat ladder.

**Layering:** canonical Card → visible facade/projection → reversible local Goo/Ink surface state → optional HyperNormalisation montage → character-specific ChatterBox reaction → player Kayfabulation/Almanac connection. Neither the revealed picture nor the projected shadow is automatically objective truth.

## Suggested small optional incident: The Shadow Match
One small authored cave or covered Card-building room, a wall/projection, two source-backed Cards, and one actual NPC. A wrestling-style commentator can be a **ChatterBox presentation** of an already accepted Resident, not a newly invented voice engine.

**Set-up:** A surface displays a confident shadow/play projection of `forget_utopia:1` (*The Glossy Horizon*), an authentic mapped Card. A nearby `embrace_protopia:2` (*The Erasable Line*) offers a counterpoint. The player can enter/leave freely, pick either encounter order and need not fight.

**Three tangible actions, same local owner contract:**
1. **COVER / CONTAIN:** apply a bounded source-approved Ink layer or restore an existing facade; an optional propaganda/comfort montage plays. A Resident may appreciate the quiet or complain about the missing text.
2. **PEEL / EXPOSE:** interact with a seam or a bounded destructible cover, without automatically demolishing the support building. The canonical Card becomes inspectable; small clay/Fluff VFX are secondary.
3. **CONNECT / REFRAME:** choose an existing Kayfabulation relation, such as **BUT**, between the two real Cards. The NPC offers a concrete counter-example or a character-specific joke; the player may accept, dispute or leave without a truth verdict.

**Performative wrestling:** Babyface/Heel are *roles*, not fixed ideologies. The same actor can play promoter, challenger, referee or reluctant bystander. A promo, staged bell, crowd reaction, microphone gesture or poster can make this feel like wrestling without requiring full Combat Arena integration. Optional challenge/mob encounter is never a compulsory Card gate.

**Physical feedback:** card-sized Goo patch contracts/peels; clay facade bends and folds; a short sound pulse and visible NPC gesture signal the outcome. Existing source-owned ink/contact, destruction/build/heal, resident gesture and audio semantics should be reused if validated **in isolation**. No new generic shader, second combat loop, full demolition or invented Card art.

## Minimal world-state / interpretative separation
Keep a real `cardRef`, visible source print, bounded `worldObjectRef`, reversible Ink/cover state, optional montage reference, observed NPC/player encounter receipts and optional Almanac relation events under **existing** owners. A successful cover, exposure or repair does not itself mean the Card has been collected, believed, shared or proven true. NPCs can remember each action while disagreeing about whether it was helpful.

**Do not** turn every philosophical claim into an objective political lesson, every NPC into a lecturer, every dark particle into a seventh currency or every restored wall into a moral victory. Media critique is satirical fiction; any resemblance to actual institutions must be specific, attributable and not presented as a factual universal conspiracy.

## Transmedia and voluntary discovery
The Infinite Canvas applies across **2×2 Comic page → Deck → 3D world/Cave/POI → NPC performance → player Card relation → Almanac**. Give the player a satisfying original Card or funny moment first. Optional links later can open the actual Card/Deck/comic source and encourage sharing a self-authored Card connection or scene with friends; never make referral or viral growth a prerequisite for progression. Private save/event history should not be published by default.
