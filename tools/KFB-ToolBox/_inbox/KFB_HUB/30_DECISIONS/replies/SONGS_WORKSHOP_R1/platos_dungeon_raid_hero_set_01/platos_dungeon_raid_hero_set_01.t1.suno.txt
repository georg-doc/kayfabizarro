The Rebel Rouser. A man with a field becomes a man with a mob, and neither was the goal.

The Cheap Blade, honest in a way a blade for principle is not.

The Reputation raises the rates, pays better than a sword and shows up faster.

The Explosion Herself. The party loves you and fears you; no use for warnings anymore.

The Tracker. A path is a story, and the hunter is its first reader.

The Tavern Champion. Strength remembered loud is strength remembered last.

The Tournament Winner, rumored fixed. A win is a win until someone counts the coins.

The Decorated Veteran. The medal is heavier than the comrade it replaced.

Of the Unstopping Hammer. The peace between the wars is the harder war.

The Wrong Joke. A joke about a beard is the cheapest way to die in this country.

The Herbalist's salve works, a small good that does not need a bigger story.

The Salve-Mistress. A healer's hands learn before her heart catches up.

Of the Mercy That Spreads. You heal the wounded monster; the party will not understand.

The Resurrectionist. To save everyone is to save no one in the order they hoped.

The Apprentice of Frames. The first trick is recognising that everything is a trick.

The Stagecrafter bends what the room agrees to see. Most contracts are stages.

Of the Recognised Match. The match has been fixed since before the start.

The Card-Replacer. The trick was never the trick; the deck was the trick.

The Corpse-Looter takes what others called body and calls it parts.

The First Homunculus. Every parent says hello before realising what they have done.

The Festival Circuit. A song that catches has caught more than the singer.

Of the Quiet Suspicion. A lyric that flatters power was written by someone power flattered first.
