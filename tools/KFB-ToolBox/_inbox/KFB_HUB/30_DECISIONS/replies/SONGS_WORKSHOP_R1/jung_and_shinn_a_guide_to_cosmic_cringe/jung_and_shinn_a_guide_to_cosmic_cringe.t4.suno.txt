[Spoken Word]

Jung and Shinn: a guide to cosmic cringe, where the unconscious meets the power of positive thinking.

Shinn warns against The Old-Fashioned Chat: destructive words, principal topics being lack and sickness.

Jung recommends The Persona: a mask for the collective psyche, a compromise between individual and society.

Shinn spots it in every lobby: The Counterfeit Face. Tried to look rich while feeling poor.

Jung's verdict on The Extrovert: thinking being under repression, relatively infantile, colourless and dry.

Shinn's darkest claim? The Mother-Fear is responsible for many of the diseases and accidents.

Jung blames The Father-Imago: parental power guides the child like a higher controlling fate.

Shinn's cure: say it out loud. The Spoken Word. I now speak and demolish every untrue record in my subconscious mind.

Jung calls the same energy The Libido: sun-energy. God is our own longing to which we pay divine honors.

Ever thrown The Boomerang? Hate, resentment and criticism come back laden with sickness and sorrow.

Did you know The Wish-Fulfilment is a dream replacing reality by archaic surrogates?

Shinn's investment tip: The Magic Purse. Spending money to make money, the alchemical transmutation.

Jung's theory of fire? You won't like it. The Boring Finger: an infantile presexual activity, discovery of fire.

Shinn's ladder story: The Invisible Ladder. Fear attracted the ladder, and fearlessness removed it.

Jung's ladder is The Fire-Stick: friction creating libido, fire-preparation regarded as forbidden, as robbery.

Shinn once met The Hoarder: the woman's arms were gradually becoming paralyzed from holding on to things.

Jung's opposite number: The Introvert ignores external events. Feelings repressed remain undeveloped.

Ever played The Resentment Record? Old bad records in the subconscious mind.

Jung himself confesses to The Cryptomnesia: unconscious plagiarism, not from my brain at all.

Everybody owns The Shadow, the dark twin, repressed and concealed.

Shinn quotes Numbers on The Giant of Doubt: we were in our own sight as grasshoppers.

Jung goes bigger with The Terrible Mother: furious gigantic lizards, Tiamat, our mother.

Shinn's version is smaller: The Army of Aliens, the adverse thoughts of doubt and fear.

Jung's muse is The Anima, ghostly, seductive, projected upon the object.

And good versus evil? Shinn calls that The False Law: Adam and Eve ate of maya, the tree of illusion.

What if you believe both? You get The Split Mind: conflict between the individual psyche and the collective psyche.

Shinn's solution: The Negative Capability. If I have a failure, I baptize it success.

One of Jung's patients saw The Schizophrenic Sun: sun and moon made artificially from oil paper.

Jung draws The Mandala, the circle of sanity, the unity of difference.

Shinn draws The Perfect Square: Health, Wealth, Love and Perfect Self-Expression.

In between, Jung builds The Transcendent Function, a bridge over the abyss.

Shinn would rather float on The Non-Resistant Water: perfectly non-resistant, it can wear away a rock.

Jung would rather swim out of a whale: The Rebirth. New fertility springs.

Shinn calls you The Golden Link: every man is a golden link in the chain of my good.

Jung calls it The Sacrifice of the infantile personality.

Broke? Shinn says buy The Blankets. I'll dig my ditches by buying the blankets! Active faith.

Next, dig The Ditches, filled to over-flowing.

Meanwhile Jung is looking for The Key: 'twill lead thee to the Mothers.

At this point the table yells Blödsinn! A bearded figure speaking nonsense.

Shinn's fix is New-Fashioned Chat: talk about what we want, not what we don't want.

Jung's fix is Glossolalia, rudimentary, a Martian language.

Who keeps the books? Karma: automatically the law of karma is set in action.

Shinn's forecast: The Harvest. Look on the fields, for they are ripe already to harvest.

Jung's forecast: The Earthquake, the end of the hero.

They agree on one word only: Enantiodromia. Everything meets with its opposite.

Shinn calls the ending The Game Completed: this is the square of life.

Jung's ending is Apocalypse: epidemic of madness. Everyone loses.

Shinn gets the last word: The Resurrection. Start new game, memories intact. Whatever man voices, he begins to attract.

The End? Hello? No.

Stay Fluffy, Folks!
