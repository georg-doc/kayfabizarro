STYLE A: Ghanaian highlife at 114 BPM: two interlocking clean electric guitars, bright trumpet and saxophone riffs, bubbling melodic bass, bell pattern, congas, shaker, a ratchet and a squeaky hinge as percussion. One warm male robot voice, slightly worn and creaky, talking while he works, landing each line with a dry smile. Sunny live-room mix, nothing polished.

STYLE B: Cumbia at 104 BPM: accordion lead, guacharaca scraper, rubbery bass, timbales and congas, offbeat piano, a wrench tapping on a pipe. One mellow male voice with a faint vocoder edge, chatty and unhurried. Warm tape mix, room for the voice, a short accordion break before the last section.
