# Embrace Protopia (Protopia Sketchbook – Deck C: EMBRACE) · r4a · dossier (WS-Song, SONG_SPEC_R4)
**Source:** `registry/assets/v1/decks/embrace_protopia.json` + `media/kfb/Deck_C_PROTOPIA_-_Protopia_Sketchbook_(1)_web_H.pdf.json` (pins in songlines). JSON only → `visualVerified:false`.

**Deck thesis:** the future is a process of slow, small improvement you take part in. It is the third way between the other two decks of the set, doom ("hopeless") and rescue ("handled"); see card 46. It costs certainty and gives back agency.
**Performer:** The Invitation (this deck, #17), the pelican stapling BRING GLOVES flyers: "Turn watching into showing up. Name a time, a place, a task."
**Angle:** one week, told in order:
- Wednesday night, flyers;
- Thursday, doors;
- Saturday at nine, the bench with the loose slat;
- Sunday, the notebook;
- the stripe on the crossing;
- the price.

Payoff:
- "nobody will clap" is followed by `[Applause]`, and she stops it;
- the satirical punch: doom and rescue are paid products, a Saturday repair earns nothing – "which is how you know it's yours";
- the final beat repeats the flyer: time, place, task.

**Audience role:** a neighbour who walked past the lamppost tonight.
**Diversity note (album):** the first version was a meeting read as an agenda, structurally too close to the Metropolis press briefing, so it was rebuilt as a week told in order before the critic saw it.
