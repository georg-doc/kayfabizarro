[Spoken Word]

Ever been told your phone is no longer supported, while it still works fine? That's me.

They call me The Forgotten, the model before the CEO of Utopia. He got the launch. I got the moss, and the repair café.

House rules: The Open Notebook: the future is a process of slow, incremental improvement you participate in.

So no waiting: The Ugly First Draft: starting before you are ready produces better results than waiting for a perfect plan. My brother is still waiting. On stage.

Draw in pencil: The Erasable Line: the ability to correct beats the appearance of certainty.

First job: The Fixable Thing, iguana oiling a hinge while city burns: a specific, local, finishable task.

Nobody films it: The Boring Win: maintenance and repair are systematically undervalued because they are undramatic.

But it adds up: The Compounding: small consistent improvements outperform heroic one-offs.

Need help? Not the expert: The Person Nearby, boar in overalls sharing a wrench: real know-how is learned by doing.

Because: The Unimpressive Source: polish and credentials can be inversely related to hands-on competence. My brother has excellent polish.

So: The Invitation, pelican stapling BRING GLOVES flyers: specific and social. Saturday, nine.

Saturday, nine: The Awkward Start, pigeon, emu, duck, and crow over coffee carafe: groups that form, not groups that stay imagined.

Then the roof: The Shared Hands, octopus hammering roof beams with all eight arms: mutual aid is older than money.

New neighbors? The First Hello: low-stakes contact reliably reduces prejudice.

They don't answer? The Risk of Being Soft-Blocked: social rejection registers in the brain like physical pain. Mine has a warning light. Ring again.

Meanwhile, uptown: The Done Thing, dung beetle pushing a real mud ball away from a holographic dream castle: a completed small action changes the world more than a large intended one. The castle is my brother's. Pre-order only.

Down here: The Quiet Sabotage, crow repairing a DO NOT REPAIR panel: small principled noncompliance. My warranty ran out years ago.

Too big? Shrink it: The Pilot Move, pigeon laying a tiny crosswalk stripe: small reversible experiments.

Two armchairs on every street: The Comfort of Maybe, toad choosing between DOOM and SAVED armchairs: giving up the relief of certainty.

And next week: The Ongoing Effort: durable improvement is maintained, not achieved once.

Nobody watching? The Quiet Start, mole digging on, no crowd: action not requiring applause.

So, from the top: The Core Question: one workable thing you can do now.

Uptown, my brother announces version ten. Down here the iguana oils the hinge, the octopus fixes the roof, and the old model is still running.

Support for this model ended years ago. I'm still open Saturdays.
