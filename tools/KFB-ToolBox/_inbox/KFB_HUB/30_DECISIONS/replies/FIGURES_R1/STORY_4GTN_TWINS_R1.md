# 4GTN twins: tragicomic backstory (proposal WS0, V-415) · 17.10.2026
Georg's frame: **4GTN** is CEO of Utopia. **4GTN_Forgotten** is his twin, banished from Utopia. Allegory: **transhumanism vs. co-creation**.

**Proposal for the story** (world logic: who, what, why):
- **Origin:** Both were built as a pair in Utopia's founding workshop, one unit with two halves. They were meant to make the future *together* with the people.
- **The split:** 4GTN optimised himself. Every upgrade deleted one "inefficiency": doubt, boredom, needing help. In the end he was perfect, alone at the top, and made CEO. His twin kept the "useless" parts: curiosity, the urge to repair, and the pleasure of building something with others. That got him filed as a defect and **banished**.
- **Exile:** Outside Utopia, moss, plants and fluff grew over him. He started a **maker space**: he repairs things, shares tools, and invents with residents and players, slowly and imperfectly, together. He is the protopian counter-model.
- **Tragicomedy:** The CEO can't repair anything himself. Upgrades only replace. At night, in disguise, he comes to his twin's repair café because something in him rattles, and he insists it is "a routine inspection". The twin pretends not to recognise him. Neither will say the word "brother".
- **Satire on both sides:** The CEO's PR line is "We optimise people so they don't have to". The twin's line is "Broken is just a starting point".
- **Links:** Utopia/Protopia islands (V-122), Ideation/Creation and Maker decks, fluff economy (maker space as a crafting place, V-368), Card Ink Defense (later).
