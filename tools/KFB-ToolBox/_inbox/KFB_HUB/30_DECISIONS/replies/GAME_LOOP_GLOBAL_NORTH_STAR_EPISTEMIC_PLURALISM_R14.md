## 1 · One proposed mission sentence

**Kayfabe Bizarro is a playful transmedia exploration of human worldviews, contradictions and cultural imagination. Its comics, Cards, characters, songs, videos and interactive worlds invite players to examine unusual perspectives, trace sources, question their own convictions and recognize the people behind competing claims. The game does not prescribe a worldview or award final epistemic victory; readers and players retain interpretive closure.**

**Short form:** *Challenge claims, explore perspectives, keep the people in view.*

Georg's ambition is an open, informed, curious and human dialogue across a broad spectrum of ideologies, opinions, intellectual traditions, absurdities, cultural practices, scientific inquiry, metaphysics and popular entertainment. Satire can puncture authority, conformity and self-deception without turning every opponent into a disposable caricature.

## 2 · Five editorial commitments

### A. Intellectual openness, not evidence-free relativism
Different viewpoints deserve a fair hearing, **not automatically equal factual support**. A Card can be a claim, original quotation, thought experiment, false belief, satire or deliberate provocation. A sourced empirical assertion should remain distinguishable from an opinion or an unverified allegation. Original Card/PDF text, context, provenance and where possible primary material remain inspectable. A hidden Card or censorship overlay is not guaranteed to reveal “the real truth” when removed.

### B. Human dignity, not automatic approval
A figure may entertain a belief the player regards as strange, offensive, irrational or abhorrent. The encounter can reveal the figure's history, fear, aspiration, mistaken evidence or social relationships without asking the player to agree. **Understand a person ≠ endorse a claim ≠ excuse harmful conduct.** Residents and affected others retain their own memories, objections, agency and consequences. Avoid guilt-by-association as a default narrative shortcut, but neither erase actual injury nor turn cruelty into a harmless joke.

### C. Cognitive dissonance as artistic resonance, not a score
Keep the existing maxim **“Cognitive dissonance is the resonance space of Kayfabe Bizarro.”** Friction between two Cards, a staged public story, a character's private life and the player's expectations can spark humor and thought. Neither resolution nor conversion is compulsory. A genuinely grounded NPC may retain an unpopular opinion or change it after new evidence. No “openness XP”, “enlightenment meter”, forced moral quiz or diagnostic claim about player psychology.

### D. Source research as invitation, not homework
The Fractal Almanac and card-to-source links may distinguish: canonical Card identity and original 2×2-page/Deck context; what a character actually said; independently checkable evidence; a second source/counterexample; and a **player-authored hypothesis**. These should not be collapsed into one authoritative answer. Encourage asking “How do we know?” through one useful source cue or surprising Warburg-style neighbour, not by gating a fun game behind fact-checking worksheets.

### E. Pluralism through performance, not preachiness
**Show It → Spin It → Sell It** is an expressive grammar, not an instruction that every Resident must speak in rap, as a teacher, or as FrizzleBob. Character acting, clay physical comedy, choreography, authored dialogue, a song, a video montage or silence can embody a position. Counter-positions may also be satirized. KFB's worldwide thematic variety is an opportunity to stage meaningful encounters, not to manufacture a single “neutral” voice that secretly always agrees with the creator.

## 3 · How the existing game/media system expresses this

- **KFB Cards / 2×2 comic pages / Decks:** finite, source-preserved statements with specific source neighbours and larger context; not objective moral points.
- **Open World:** the player may discover a Card, see it covered by a HyperNorm facade, deliver it to another worldview, cover it in-fiction, or simply roam. Existing `PULL, DON'T GATE` remains the global rule.
- **Resident Life / ChatterBox / Lean Memory:** characters begin with distinctive authored knowledge, remember new contacts and disagreements, react according to their own history rather than a generic pedagogy model. Changing mind is possible, not mandatory; disagreement can coexist with friendship.
- **Black Fluff / Censorship / HyperNorm:** offer physical, satirical ways to stage visibility, exclusion, omission, persuasion and contradictory official narratives. Black vs colour is NOT true vs false or good vs evil. Repair or containment may have different meanings to different people.
- **Wrestling / Cave / Kayfabulation:** theatrical Face/Heel performances make rival frames tangible, but winning the staged match is not proof of an assertion. The player connects two authentic Cards (e.g. BUT, BECAUSE, MEANWHILE) without an ordained interpretation.
- **FrizzleBob songs, videos and other performers:** provocative, emotionally engaging perspectives on themes and Decks. Different genres, voices and artistic media remain valid. Media can invite players toward real Cards or game places; no referral or conversion obligation.
- **Fractal Almanac:** records what genuinely happened and what the player optionally connected, without fusing source fact, NPC belief, and player conjecture.

This is the **same distributed KFB product intent**, not another gameplay engine or required all-at-once feature list.

## 4 · Product integrity: failure modes to avoid

| Risk | Editorial response |
|---|---|
| False equivalence: repeating several assertions makes them factually equal to corroborated evidence | Identify source independence and confidence without telling the player what to believe |
| Hollow neutrality: the game pretends all framing is neutral | Attribute the speaker, institution and editorial angle; every performer has a point of view |
| Preachy correction / ideological funnel | Let the player leave, laugh, explore another Card or challenge the narrator |
| NPC stereotyping / guilt by association | Preserve personhood, biography, real social context and independently witnessed actions |
| Provocation without humanity or stakes | Show concrete consequences and affected Residents; do not excuse abuse with “it's just Kayfabe” |
| Source/link laundering | Link to original credible evidence where possible; distinguish media art and claims from verified source material |
| Thematic sprawl | Reuse real Decks/characters/songs; prioritize depth of authored relationships over thousands of shallow connections |

### North Star versus impact claim
Georg's “2–4% further” is an **aspiration/metaphor for modest perspective shifts**, not an empirical effect-size guarantee or proposed telemetry target. We cannot certify that players become more tolerant or critical from enjoying a scene. Future user feedback can instead ask whether a player found a surprising perspective, could identify a relevant source, and felt free to disagree without losing the game.

## 5 · Review questions before accepting future content

Does the scene make someone or something **concrete**? Can a player recognize the actual source of a claim? Can the same Card be interpreted differently by characters who have reasons? Could a player disagree or opt out? Does the satire challenge a behavior, frame or institutional practice without dehumanizing everyone involved? Does the moment play well as a comic/game even if the player ignores its philosophical implications? If a journalist or domain specialist checks a material factual claim, does the product distinguish evidence from rhetoric?

Pass the creative test when the scene remains **fun and intelligible without requiring the player to learn or agree with a lesson**.
