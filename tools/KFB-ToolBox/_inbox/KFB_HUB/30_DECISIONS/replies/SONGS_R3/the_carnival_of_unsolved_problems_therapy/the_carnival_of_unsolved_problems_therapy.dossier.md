## Purpose and thesis
The add-on "Artifact Practice: The Therapy Carnival" (booklet 08) is a travelling fairground. Emotional patterns, roles and inner conflicts become attractions you can play, not diagnoses. It is explicitly *not therapy*. It is symbolic play that makes therapy possible, because performance creates distance, distance creates safety, and safety creates movement. Its audience: therapists who want symbolic distance, couples who have had the same fight 47 times, families stuck in roles, solo anti-journalers and workshop facilitators.
**Thesis for the song:** nothing gets fixed, but everything gets better stage lighting, and sometimes that is enough. The satire is aimed at patterns, false cures and performative enlightenment. It never targets the people who seek help, and the therapist card is played with full respect ("I hold the frame, you keep the answers", after the booklet line about holding the frame, not the answers).

## Arc
- **Build-up:** You pay admission with a problem and are promised better lighting, not a solution. The visitor walks the midway, lost and found, the loop rides, the overworked inner staff and the fortune booth. Each card sells its attraction with full conviction.
- **Turn (middle reset, page 7):** "Whistle!" cashes in the Safety Whistle ("someone can always stop the spiral"). Meaning collapses, the guru dissolves, the body demands a taco, and the cosmic slide deck crashes. After that the sideshow gets louder (tonic, clown shoe), then darker (mask that stuck, moving exit).
- **Payoff:** "Quieter now." Real practice takes over. The Grey Stone comes *out of the rucksack* (callback to card 4, using the booklet's example session line). The refund desk stamps the ticket void: no neat endings (callbacks to "Keep your ticket. Refunds at the exit."). Outro: problem not solved, rucksack lighter, same mirrors, different you (from the booklet line "you'll see them differently").
- FrizzleBob does not appear in the intro or outro at all. He appears only where the cards name him (card 2, card 44). There is no "Welcome to", no Meta-Message and no Comic Card Karaoke line. The karaoke element is folded in freely as "Sing the attractions as they pass."
