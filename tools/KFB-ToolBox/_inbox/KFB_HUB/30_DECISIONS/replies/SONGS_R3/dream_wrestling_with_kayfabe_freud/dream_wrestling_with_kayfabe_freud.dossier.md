## Purpose and thesis
- **Printed title:** "Dream-Wrestling with Kayfabe-Freud: Where the Id Always Kicks Out at Two!" A psychoanalytic expansion pack (56 cards, 15 pages) for the Kayfabizarro improv engine. Cover (from the PDF JSON coverQA, not seen): vintage horror-comic poster, a brain luchador against a masked FrizzleBob rabbit, "MADNESS ARENA", "Contains 100% Repressed Content", "Do not eat the cards".
- **What the deck says:** Freud's *Interpretation of Dreams* (1900) works like a wrestling card. Every dream is a scripted match: the Wish books it, the Censor referees, the dream work (Condensation, Displacement, Secondary Revision) does the gimmicks, and the drives never stay pinned. You don't cure the Id. It kicks out at two. The cards are the concepts acting themselves out ("if it makes no sense, it makes PERFECT sense").
- **Audience:** improv and story tables, Comic Seminar players, anyone who knows "Freudian slip" and "sometimes a cigar...".
- **Song thesis:** you lie down and the bell rings; the whole wrestling card is your own dream, booked by you. Every card is a wrestler in your head selling its claim. By morning you forget, and Freud is still in the ring.

## Arc (build-up, turn, payoff)
1. **Intro (bell):** "Lie down. Close your eyes. That was the bell." Madness Arena behind your eyelids. "Who booked it? / You did. / You just don't remember." Plants three setups: lie down (pays off at The Couch), you booked it (pays off at Oedipus), do not eat the cards (pays off at Infantile Material).
2. **P2–P4 build-up:** the roster in the basement (Unconscious, Preconscious, Censor as referee at the door, Wish), the dream-work tag team, then the night's material. **Reset 1 (false finish):** the Alarm Clock counts "One! Two!" and the next line is its own stanza: "Snooze. Kick-out. Back to sleep." (Who kicked out stays open.)
3. **P5–P8 escalation:** the symbols (planted "(Aha!)" gang shouts after Hat, Tie, Flying Machine and later the Sphinx's cigarette, unexplained), bad dreams (the Wish's "lose your teeth" pays off at the Tooth Extractor), the family match (**planted slip:** "Please welcome... Mommy! Sorry. The Queen"), the classic nightmares.
4. **P9 turn (the middle, stanza 8 of 14):** "Masks off!" Oedipus Rex: "You were the detective and the murderer all along!" The unmasking: every wrestler is you (pays off the intro's "You did").
5. **P10–P11 second turn:** dream grammar, then "Ring's gone. Just a rug and a couch." The arena was a couch; The Couch says "lie down" again (intro callback); Transference bills by the hour.
6. **P12 main event:** The Id: "Pin me? I kick out at two!" (title pays off and answers who kicked out of the alarm clock). Ego begs for a tag, Super-Ego watches you shower, Libido explodes.
7. **P13–P14 waking and encore:** "Snooze over." Gap, Navel, Morning Amnesia, the Notebook (callback to the Censor deleting the file). Deja Vu repeats "hear that? That was the bell" (performs itself). Lucidity's backflip as the top-rope finish.
8. **P15 payoff (the fine print):** How to Use ("No sense? Perfect sense."), The Slip catches the announcer ("Mommy? Draw two."), The Symbol finally explains the (Aha!) shouts, Credits & Blame (FrizzleBob's only appearance, natural, as printed). **Outro:** "Here lies Siggy, nineteen hundred to forever." (card 56 tombstone motif) / One. / Two. / Kick-out. / Stay Fluffy! (card 53 rule 4 is "Stay Fluffy.")

POWER is used only as meta-satire where it lands as a punchline: Regression ("Goo-goo, ga-ga!"), The Slip and The Symbol (page 15 cards are rules by nature; sung as payoff, not instructions). No line recites a game mechanic.
