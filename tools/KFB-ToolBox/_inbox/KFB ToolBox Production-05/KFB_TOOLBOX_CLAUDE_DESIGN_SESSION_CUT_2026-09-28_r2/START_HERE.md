## The flow in eight clicks

1. Pick an actor in the top bar (FrizzleBob · Driver Graft is Rig_Medium, Orc Brute is Rig_Large).
2. Type `Reden` in the search. Seven talk clips appear, each with its own id.
3. Click one. It plays on the actor. The badge over the stage says PROVEN / UNVERIFIED / INCOMPATIBLE / SOURCE REQUIRED.
4. **Details** opens the inspector. Change the display name and add tags. The id stays.
5. **Terrain** in the dock puts the same clip into a Resident scene. **Studio** takes it back to the clay floor.
6. Under Resident roles, choose a role and press **Assign**. The role stores the id, not the name.
7. Set Export to **Excluded** on a clip you do not want shipped.
8. **Export JSON** saves the patch. **Reset**, then **Import**, and everything is back.

New clip: drag an FBX onto **Animation ausprobieren**. It stays in the browser. Check the compatibility, switch A/B under Fit & Motion, then press **Export intake receipt**.

Self-test: the **…** button, then **Run LIBRARY self-test** (24 checks, about a minute). It restores your patch, your URL and your workspace afterwards.
