## Actual construction change

Eight separate, grounded tread/riser volumes replace the continuous visible tread profile. Their tops stay planar. Existing rise/run rhythm and 4-lab landing are retained. Deliberately different broad front outlines are geometry, not a texture.

Each cheek uses three broad foundation stones, four bearing spans, two divided at an intentional horizontal bedding joint, five substantial crown stones and one upper termination. The wall still follows the stair incline; its new course, closed segment bodies and overhanging crowns expose construction. No simulated brick pattern or noise layer is used. Two lower pillars each have an overhanging foot, squat shaft and rounded cap. The pillars are a single design family with three anatomical zones.

Five repeatable form families produce 45 mesh objects. This is not a mesh-count acceptance proxy. The massing uses a few long stones rather than a small-block raster. Grounded riser volumes are intentional hidden support; no hollow hovering slabs.

One direction was built. All R3 pictures are neutral gray QA renders. Orthographic cameras, framing and lighting match the new R2 isolation renders. R2 vs R3 comparisons show the actual geometry change.
