## New visual grammar: faithful and build-light
1. **FORM authority:** real donor (Kenney `rock_largeA.glb`, StreakByte base, bridge CC0 source) in clearly isolated view before trying to preserve its shape. A URL/text label or a mood picture does not prove source-conditioned generation.
2. **STYLE authority:** selected KFB style guide & actual inspected materials. Claymation + optional cardboard/cork/moss/balsa mix only where helpful. Silhouette, major volumes and source color regions stay tied to donor.
3. **Presentation:** one asset **per board**; a board may show one hero 3/4 and other front, side, top, below, closeups, and 1–3 variants, with **view labels**. No game branding, marketing titles/slogans, people, animals, vehicles or boats. The main image must not be a full world/scene composition.
4. **Build-light:** at most **5–6 reusable large shape/element roles** per proposed buildable asset concept, expressed in per-board `PARTS.md` with form, approximate H target (if unmeasured say so), palette, count or variations. Avoid repetitive tiny props, fine textures and photographic depth-of-field; simplify Knet facets to a few strong volumes.
5. **Geo-specific (§7a):** drivable bridges deck slope ≤6 %, no central hump, car-width clearance plus footpath on both sides; 'longer' variant spans two floating boulders with abutment niches. These are constraints for a future G4 part/build assessment, not geometry already tested or a G1 runtime change.
6. **Classifications:** `SOURCE_ISOLATED` = actual source mesh projection; `MOOD_ONLY` = aesthetically useful but donor shape differs; `STYLE_DIRECTION_ONLY` = accepted design-language reference; `CANDIDATE_FOR_REVIEW` = plausible output to show Georg; `GOLDEN_REF` only after explicit actual-variant approval. Feedback `super` does not auto-approve an asset.
7. Per generated PNG: add original bytes to incremental ZIP, `SOURCE.json`, SHA256/size, `PROMPT.md` and `PARTS.md` where applicable. Keep ZIP copies available to Work/WSA by attaching directly or storing in user-controlled persistent file location. The binary GH push stays WSA's explicit later batch, not a prerequisite for creative continuation.
