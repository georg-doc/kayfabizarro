- Axis Y-up for exported GLB source contracts; Blender is Z-up internally, document conversion and preserve naming/pivot/orientation.

- Shader: KFB Clay is owner; Blender viewport proxy roughness/microtexture is only a look proxy and must be labeled `PREVIEW_ONLY` unless same material equivalence is measured.
