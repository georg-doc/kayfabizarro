## R2 north star: the library is a world of readable scenelets
**No opaque library block with a roof that hides the library.** The island itself is a continuous terrain/support mass, but the *library* is a living, semi-open assemblage of book walls, staggered platforms, desks, ladders, walkways, reading nooks, balconies and partial rear façades. The eye sees reading, searching, exchanging cards and preserving records directly from the ordinary 3/4 gameplay/hero view. The visual composition resembles a playable, inhabited dollhouse / open theatre scenery, not an exterior postcard or four-wall building with a cinematic cutaway effect.

Architecture can use a fragment of arched masonry, an occasional roof canopy, an open staircase and a single accent tower, **only** where each visibly frames a scenelet instead of blocking it. Do not introduce observatory/dome/vault as mandatory lore/landmarks. Bookshelves should be structural-looking *stage edges* at different heights, not only tiny decals seen through glass. Outer walls may stop at the side or have a soft stage-edge completion; if built later their collisions/supports belong to the existing World/Cell owner.

### Scene grammar safeguards
- `SemanticRoomBoundary`: scene role, activity, privacy and routine semantics, even with missing physical walls.
- `PhysicalBoundary`: only tested support/collision; may be absent or partial.
- `VisualBoundary`: bookcases, floor trim, textile/cardboard, short wall, lighting and material shift.
- Use existing `SceneCell` vocabulary **as an authoring concept**: `sceneRole`, `boundaryProfile`, `floorProfile`, `propAnchors[]`, `residentAnchors[]`, `entryAnchors[]` and optional light/audio/privacy roles. Do not implement a second World store or claim this schema is shipped.
- Spatial story: visitor → accessible study → Lorekeeper/Tome consultation → searchable shelves → personal reading niche. Every object has an activity and a clear route.
- Camera must see the central activity and at least three tableaux in one view; raised elements/tower must not occlude; scale retains Rig_Medium and correct Lectern. Do not solve occlusion by shrinking characters.
- Source donor isolation and the KFB Clay Surface Canon still apply. `TWO_WALL` KayKit promo grammar is a useful tested donor; never falsely call an unrendered source mesh a visual match.
- **One continuous island support mass**, never an assembly of pasted boulder blocks or disconnected stage plates. On-island navigation is important; Cosmic/Joyride track only if an authentic, user-approved road purpose demands it. No mandatory external race bridge or giant track circling the archive.
- No new Hex/Hex tile construction; don't turn decorative façade fragments into fake four-sided closed castles.
