## Design task
Derive four KFB families:
1. **Minigun chip / micro-impact**
2. **Rocket wall breach**
3. **Delayed structural collapse**
4. **Settled rubble + residual smoke**

For each define:
- silhouette;
- large/medium/small shape hierarchy;
- directionality;
- debris family;
- smoke/dust volume;
- timing/choreography;
- clay deformation language;
- what remains visible after the event;
- what to avoid.

The existing POC already owns damage mechanics, support/collapse logic, projectile behavior and persistence. Do not redesign them.

## Visual north star
Tactile, chunky, readable KFB clay destruction. Not photoreal. Not generic game-engine sparks/particles. A single frame should read clearly at gameplay distance.
