## Preserved user requirements

1. **Three** deep, inhabitable and playable prison cells with visible depth and room for interaction; not thin bars on a stage flat. Their individual floors/interiors are visibly located *behind* the barred fronts.
2. A continuous walkable **second-level gallery** in front of those three cells, with plausible clear access and support; a usable stair/vertical circulation route.
3. One corner lookout/watchtower with substantial flat turret top, battlements and a robust mounted searchlight. **No toy soldier or other guard figure on the tower.**
4. Strong open-front, asymmetric theatrical **cutaway composition**, not a closed U-shaped courtyard/fortress. Floor and cells are scenic spaces for characters; allow stage access and camera views.
5. Hand-built cartoon clay material, intentionally swollen/chunky/irregular silhouette, visibly worn, abandoned and lived-in. Less conventional medieval fantasy; more surreal, playful dystopian crossover with limited chunky retro-sci-fi machinery. Patina, moss, cracks, wet patches, lost belongings must have credible position/use/water flow — avoid uniform random clutter and uniformly spotless slabs.
6. Tally scratches belong at believable prisoner reach in **interior cell wall surfaces**, never on banners, logos or freestanding propaganda posters. Restrained occult eye/spiral traces only where causally placed.
7. No monitor/billboard yet (deferred per user), no advertising slogans or invented pseudo-KFB text/branding, no typographic overlays.
8. Geometry remains Meshy-friendly: roughly <=6 macro form families, restrained separate props, strong volumes and readable support. Preserve existing integral props when later using image-to-3D; never globally delete figures/statues from other source-specific jobs.
