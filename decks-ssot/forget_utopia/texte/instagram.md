## caption
Somebody at your table owns a gadget that was supposed to change their life. Ask when it did. Watch them start the answer twice.

FORGET UTOPIA is a free Cut & Play comic: 56 cards naming the moves people use to sell you a finished tomorrow. The Signature Line. The Treadmill, "movement without arrival, billed monthly." Then the counter-move, The Shine Test: the shinier it is, the harder you look for the floor.

Print it, cut it, shuffle. One person sells the future out loud, the next one counts what has to be true. Nobody wins. Somebody will cancel a subscription afterwards.

## alt
1: Comic cover. Uncle FrizzleBob, a scruffy yellow cartoon rabbit, swings in on a rope and splashes paint over a glossy billboard of a perfect future city. Title: FORGET UTOPIA.
2: Card The Glossy Horizon. A chameleon stares at its own rainbow reflection inside a glass dome. Power: Hold up a future so polished the visitor sees their own face in it. Sell the reflection.
3: Card The Signature Line. An octopus, eyes wide, holds a glowing pen over a long contract. Power: Offer the dream. Ask only that they stop looking at the floor and sign.
4: Card The Treadmill. An exhausted hamster sprints on a shiny treadmill and gets nowhere. Power: Movement without arrival, billed monthly.
5: Card The Shine Test. A crow circles a blinding iridescent sphere, tilting its head to find where it stands. Power: The shinier it is, the harder you should look for the floor.
6: Card The Core Question. FrizzleBob pulls back a billboard curtain and shows the unfinished scaffold city behind the render. Power: Hold the brochure up to the light. Count the things that have to be true. Notice how many are not.
