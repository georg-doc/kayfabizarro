## thread
Ask someone at your table when the gadget that was going to change their life actually did. Watch them start the answer twice.

FORGET UTOPIA is a free print-and-cut comic card game about that pause.
---
You take turns selling the future out loud. The Signature Line: "Offer the dream. Ask only that they stop looking at the floor and sign."

The person next to you rules whether the pitch holds.
---
The card people will quote later, probably while cancelling something: The Treadmill.

"Movement without arrival, billed monthly."
---
The counter-move is The Core Question: hold the brochure up to the light, count the things that have to be true, notice how many are not.
