## post
Uncle FrizzleBob here. Next time a relative explains why the future will be fine because of an app, hand them a card instead of an argument.

FORGET UTOPIA is a free comic you print, cut into 56 cards and play at the kitchen table. One person sells tomorrow out loud with a card like The Glossy Horizon ("Sell the reflection"). The next person judges whether it holds. The closing card, The Core Question, has you hold the brochure up to the light and count what has to be true.

Nobody wins. Somebody defends a pitch they hated ten minutes ago. That is the game.
