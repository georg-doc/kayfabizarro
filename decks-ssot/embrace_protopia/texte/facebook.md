Uncle FrizzleBob here. Small question for your next family dinner: what is broken within reach of your arm right now? Not in the world. In this room.

EMBRACE PROTOPIA is a free comic you print, cut into 56 cards and play at the table. You take turns proposing moves you could actually make this week, The Ugly First Draft, The Awkward Start, The Nearest Lever, and the next person judges whether the move would hold up off the page. Nobody wins. Expect the quiet one at the end of the table to propose the smallest idea, and everybody to remember it.
