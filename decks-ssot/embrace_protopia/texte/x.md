Ask the person next to you what is broken within reach of their arm. Not in the world. In the room. Most people find something in ten seconds.

EMBRACE PROTOPIA is a free print-and-cut comic card game about that thing.
---
The Ugly First Draft: "Make the first version bad on purpose. Bad is how you know it is real."

Play it as a dare: describe the worst first version of something you want to do. Everyone else improves it once.
---
The Compounding: "One repair a week is fifty-two a year. Nobody clapped, and the place is different."
---
