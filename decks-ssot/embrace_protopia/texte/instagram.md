## caption
Ask the person next to you what is broken within reach of their arm right now. Not in the world. In the room.

EMBRACE PROTOPIA is a free Cut & Play comic: 56 cards sized to fit inside one ordinary week. The Ugly First Draft, "bad is how you know it is real." The Nearest Lever. The Compounding: one repair a week is fifty-two a year, and nobody clapped.

Print it, cut it, shuffle. Somebody proposes the smallest move of the evening, and everybody remembers it.

Forget the brochure. Ignore the loop. Pick up the pencil.
