## thread
Somebody at your table read the news before breakfast and has been tired since. Ask what they did about it. Most of us: nothing, but with feeling.

IGNORE DYSTOPIA is a free print-and-cut comic card game about that.
---
The Doomsday Clock: "Set a public timer near midnight and move the hands by announcement, never by event."

You play it out loud. Someone else rules whether the alarm holds.
---
The Missing Denominator: "Show the scary number; hide what it is out of."

Try a house rule: nobody reacts to a statistic until someone asks "out of how many?"
---
The last card is THE CORE QUESTION: "Ask this of every alarm before you forward it: who benefits when you stop acting?"
