## post
Uncle FrizzleBob here. If someone in your family forwards a fresh alarm every morning, you do not need to fight about it at dinner. Play a card.

IGNORE DYSTOPIA is a free comic you print and cut into 56 cards. You take turns reading the moves out loud, The Doomsday Clock, The Cortisol Economy, The Missing Denominator, and the next person judges whether the alarm in front of them is a live danger or just upholstery. The last card asks the one question worth asking before anyone hits forward: who benefits when you stop acting?

No villains with names, no theory board. Just the mechanics, played at a kitchen table.
