## caption
Somebody at your table read the news before breakfast and has been tired ever since. Ask what they did about it.

IGNORE DYSTOPIA is a free Cut & Play comic: 56 cards naming the moves that sell you the end of the world as a subscription. The Doomsday Clock, moved "by announcement, never by event." The Cortisol Economy: sell the spike, then sell the relief. The Missing Denominator.

Print it, cut it, shuffle. One person reads the alarm out loud. The next one asks THE CORE QUESTION: who benefits when you stop acting?
