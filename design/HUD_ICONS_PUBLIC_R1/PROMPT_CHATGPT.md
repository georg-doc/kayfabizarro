You are designing the HUD icon system for KFB (Kayfabizarro), a cartoon open-world road-trip game in claymation style. The world is made of soft clay with hand-made dents; the palette is warm and sunny, like the Krazy Kat Sunday pages (turquoise sky, warm orange sun, violet shadows); the tone is satirical comic, not sci-fi.

**The attached prop sheets are fixed.** They show the real 3D models from the game (weapons from the KayKit packs, backpack items, chest, radio, gear), each rendered the same way. Do not redraw, restyle or replace these objects. Your job is everything around them, so that they sit in one coherent icon family.

Make **three different style directions**. For each direction, one full sheet on a plain light background, with:

1. **Item slot:** how one of the attached props sits in an icon (frame or no frame, thick white sticker cut-out border, soft drop shadow, background plate). Show it with four of the attached props: a sword, a gun, a donut and the chest.
2. **Slot states:** empty, filled, selected/active, cooldown (with a radial sweep), locked, new item.
3. **Abstract action icons**, drawn as small clay objects in the same light and view as the props, readable at 48 px, no text:
   - tricks: barrel roll, two wheels, turbo/boost, drift, take off, air brake;
   - panel actions: settings, map, almanac (card fan), backpack/chest, interact, chat bubble;
   - radio: play, pause, next track, sound on, sound off.
4. **Fluff counter:** six clay orbs, one per kind, each with a small clay numeral (tragic `#3e6a83`, comic `#7a8d4a`, absurd `#e9c14a`, heroic `#d97c3a`, mystical `#6b4e7d`, forbidden `#a64670`); an empty orb shows a grey 0.
5. **Target arrow:** a free-floating clay arrow with a short distance label like "120 m".
6. **Action bar:** six slots in a row, three filled with attached weapons, one in cooldown, two empty, with small key hints (1–6).

Rules: everything must look like it belongs to the same clay world as the attached props: same oblique view from above, same soft daylight, same matte clay surface. No flat vector glyphs, no glossy plastic, no neon, no sci-fi panels. Text only for numerals and key hints. Label the three directions A, B, C.
