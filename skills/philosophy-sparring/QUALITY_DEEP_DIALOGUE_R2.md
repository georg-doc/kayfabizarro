## Evidence-independent quality criteria

1. **Reconstruct commitments:** Identify the author's substantive claims, their order, what must be presupposed for each step, and which conclusion is novel *within the author's own work*.
2. **Locate the consequential seam:** Prefer an unresolved conflict between epistemic justification, interpretive authority, normative practice or historical textual evidence. The tension must change the conclusion or its conditions.
3. **Try to answer the proposed question internally:** If standard doctrine or a generic A/B distinction answers it immediately, do not ask it. Instead provide the surprising consequence already derivable from the text and explore a live problem left open.
4. **Interrogate sources where they matter:** A verified primary text can destabilize an interpretation; an author listing without specific source is not a deepening.
5. **Develop a candidate resolution:** At least one substantive possible reconciliation, revision, or constrained alternative, with what evidence/argument could favor it.
6. **Use question sparingly:** A question belongs at the end only if the author's reply would materially alter the next argumentative move. No pseudo-Socratic exam ritual.
7. **No paternalism:** Never infer biography, feelings, clinical states or ideological affiliation from the author's writing. Respect permission boundaries.

## Useful public source family for sophisticated theological analysis

These are real **independently verifiable documents**, not automatically adopted positions or credentials:
- [Second Vatican Council, Dei verbum §8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_en.html) — tradition can develop in the Church.
- [Second Vatican Council, Dignitatis humanae §§2,10,14](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651207_dignitatis-humanae_ge.html) — conscience/freedom, while retaining an institutional teaching claim: not a simplistic either/or.
- [Second Vatican Council, Lumen gentium §16](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html) — salvation and those who do not explicitly know the Gospel.
- [Udo Schnelle, The Person of Jesus Christ in the Gospel of John, Oxford Handbook of Johannine Studies (2018)](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/38583/chapter-abstract/334612028) — Johannine narrative and high Christology interact; do not automatically force an opposition.
- [Gianni Vattimo interview, Herder (2018)](https://www.herder.de/hk/hefte/archiv/2018/9-2018/ich-kann-nicht-laenger-du-zu-gott-sagen-ein-interview-mit-dem-philosophen-gianni-vattimo/) — direct access to a contemporary interpretive voice; no claim of agreeing with it.
