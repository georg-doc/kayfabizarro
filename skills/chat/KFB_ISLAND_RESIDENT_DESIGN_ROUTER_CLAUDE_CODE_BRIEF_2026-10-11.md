## 1. World composition principle
**The bigger-picture:** one historical **KFB Town XL** as a culturally/world-historically central, lived-in origin mass, with Dystopia, Utopia, Protopia as three current narrative worlds and optional future islands around them. This is a **hierarchy of places** (silhouette, density, story/scars, not guaranteed meter values), not a mandatory perfectly symmetric planetary diagram. Optional satellite constellations add surprising Warburg-style neighbors, travel, new residents and theatrical POVs. Fracturing during/after Fluff Incident remains an OPEN story hypothesis; do not turn it into a fixed cinematic timeline.
**Each island test:** unique source-inspired outline and underbody; landscape material hierarchy; one source-correct visual landmark; coherent residents and their *relationships/actions*; meaningful walking/travel arrival; real Deck/Card mapping; an emotionally readable scene. Semi-open cutaway L- or C-stage constructions useful for Tavern/Library/Cantina/Vault/Tomb but NOT universally identical prefabs, and not all floating green disks.
**Do not build a giant theme-park layout**. Town deserves historical ecology, social life and cross-connection. A/B compares *same source story* against two arrangements and demands Georg's human visual choice; separate post-MVP boards may be preserved without becoming an MVP fifth world.
