## Text & sound contract
Triplet `T=(subject, connector, reframe)`, connector has meaningful semantic/verb contribution. One short NPC bubble in interaction, up to two NPC bubbles visible in proximity (candidate budget). Player selects existing BINGO / BOGGLE / BONGO; BLÖDSINN! exits; player may answer with character-profile-selected short Triplet bubble (UX candidate). Text is always authoritative; avoid tags appearing visibly in bubble. Content and source identity come from ChatterBox/Triplet source, never generated by Voice Layer.
One voice profile -> voiceId/preset + optional curated clips indexed by exact text hash, resident, locale, acting tag/emotion, version, allowed playback scope/provenance. Render decision: exact whole-triplet curated audio preferred for quality; dynamic TTS for unseen variants; fragment stitching only when the actual seams sound good. Preserve voice identity, loudness, pauses, codec consistency; never arbitrarily concatenate tagged emotion fragments and claim fluent speech.

## Timing
For pre-rendered ElevenLabs audio, API endpoint `POST /v1/text-to-speech/{voice_id}/with-timestamps` returns audio plus character alignments; align displayed exact text to audible language and store timing map with clip. Tag text may require normalized alignment mapping; manually check visible text vs rendered alignment. For Piper fallback obtain onset/duration/forced alignment or use measured word segmentation; do not assume ElevenLabs timings transfer to Piper, and do not confuse simulated typewriter duration with synchronized speech. Audio player start clock is the authoritative timeline. Full bubble readable muted, interrupt/exit handles stop/duck restore; KFB Audio owner stays mixer.

## Thorsten/Piper facts & cloning gate
Thorsten Piper official emotional model includes drunk, angry etc., but ordinary `de_DE-thorsten*` ONNX models are German (`de_DE`) and should not be assumed adequate for English outputs. Thorsten recorded datasets describe CC0 audio. Crucial distinction: audio dataset CC0 is not itself proof of permission to clone the identifiable speaker using ElevenLabs. ElevenLabs IVC requires confirmed right/consent; PVC is verified own voice only, not someone else's even with consent. Route: use licensed stock/Voice Design English voices; or obtain explicit compatible rights/consent and independently verify current IVC terms; never upload someone else's voice sample solely based on open model weights. Do not assert pre-cloned Thorsten has been approved.
