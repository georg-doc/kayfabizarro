## C. Explicit unresolved uncertainties
1. **Quigley originals**: 1981 full pages and quoted 1891 Rhodes/Milner documents not inspected. Do not cite Corbett as proof of every Quigley paraphrase.
2. **CFR–RIIA 1919–21 governance**: origin kinship yes; claim of formal CFR branch remains unsubstantiated. Comparative charters, board membership lists and succession agreements still absent.
3. **CFR War/Peace source original**: Bowman confidentiality letter and contemporary memorandum registers not read; 670-vs-682 count discrepant across CFR summaries.
4. **Camps causal chain**: named archives CHA/TNA identified by Seidel; direct primary file scans uninspected, governmental adoption/legislation cannot be equated.
5. **2025/26 donor economics**: financial supporters listed, not amounts or editorial instructions; regulator's most recently checked aggregate is FY2025, not same roster period.
6. **Gold/Cyber claims**: Gold taskforce directly reviewed in targeted text, cyber policy report not audited: do not expand claims of a specific state surveillance program.
7. **Other networks**: Bilderberg **uses** the rule but shared ownership with WEF/Trilateral not established; separate governance/personnel evidence required.
8. **Page integrity**: only four **unique** page-image verifications this investigation (CH-S01 1, CH-S03 2, CH-S09 1); PDF screenshot attempts for Gold and Seidel failed, not counted. No full 50/102p scans reviewed.
