## 02 — Binding Research Contract: Emic-First and Non-Exclusion

1. **Represent the worldview as the author understands it.** Write “According to [Author]…” or an equivalently unambiguous author-centered heading. Allow each narrative to explain reality in its own terms.
2. **Preserve complete thematic scope.** Do not discard claims because they concern extraordinary, controversial, occult, political, religious, paranormal, conspiratorial or unusual subject matter. Do not pre-select a safe or plausible subset.
3. **No researcher-invented speculation or fact labels.** Do not independently mark an author’s statement “speculative”, “debunked”, “unfalsifiable”, “alleged”, “misinformation”, “conspiracy theory”, “false” or “verified” as a content judgment in the author-reconstruction layer. If an author uses such descriptions, preserve *their* wording with attribution.
4. **Preserve source-native epistemic posture.** Record confidence, doubt, qualification, imagination, humor, hypothetical reasoning and reported testimony **only where the source expresses them**. Do not turn “someone told me” into “I witnessed”, “I suspect” into “I know”, or an obviously comedic setup into an endorsed cosmological commitment. If no degree of certainty is indicated, leave that field unspecified.
5. **Source fidelity first.** Link meaningful assertions to the appropriate author, specific work, episode or passage and timestamp/page where available. Include verbatim excerpts only when the precise words are recoverable. Never silently attribute a host's follow-up commentary to the guest, conflate speakers, fill a broken transcript with invented quote text or invent causal links.
6. **Mapping, not premature editing.** When authors explicitly link Saturn, Apollo, nonhuman agency, creation, consciousness, Tartaria, Atlantis or historical events, record that relation as part of their model, including source passage. Do not demand independent proof as a gate for **capturing what they say**. For comparisons we construct, retain the originator and the supporting source nodes; never misrepresent them as an author's own sentence.
7. **Researcher judgments are a separate downstream editorial pass, not an intake requirement.** Critical appraisal, validation, truth testing, public-facing editorial choices and any artistic Deck adaptation may be conducted later under separately authorized workflows. They must never retroactively erase the original author-centered corpus.
