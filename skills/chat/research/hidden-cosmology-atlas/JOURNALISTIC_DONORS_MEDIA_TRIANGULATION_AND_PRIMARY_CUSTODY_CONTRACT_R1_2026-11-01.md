## The source cascade and journalism QA checklist
For EACH **individual journalistic work**, not for a person's overall reputation, capture:
1. `workId`, byline, exact publisher/episode, publication date, any edits/archived edition, original URL, language, first-hand vs analysis vs quoted testimony vs opinion vs satire.
2. Atomic thesis/claim with exact quoted speaker and page/timecode/article passage, where actually read. A journalist's article is **primary for what that journalist wrote**, not necessarily original evidence for the event alleged.
3. **Source lineage** `ART-001 cites DOC-001` with original signed memo/court filing/FOIA scan/financial register where possible; the article's hyperlink alone does not prove the referenced source was read, had the meaning assigned, or validates hidden-network control. Preserve PDF index vs printed page and original/translation/edited transcript provenance.
4. `evidenceIndependence`: a Reuters/AP syndication, five podcast commentaries quoting a Webb article and two rehosted same videos are not seven original confirmations. Cluster common original roots and report witnessed original count, not number of articles.
5. Identify omitted context and **strongest relevant alternative explanations/contrary originals**, including established news reporting and academic analysis. Avoid ideological quotas or automatic "both sides are equally right"; select by direct evidence and subject expertise.
6. Source disclosure: author notes/documentation, conflicts/sponsorship only if actually observable, material corrections and update history, whether archival source links still resolve, evidence available as pages vs summary.
7. Unproven/hypothetical paths go in `PROPOSED_LINK`, not hidden funds/ownership/crime edges; named living people are not presumed responsible from presence in contact books, coappearance, documents or shared professional affiliation.
8. Document `researchRunId / agentId if observed / inputGitSha / lastReadOriginal / independentReviewer / corrections/nextFalsifier`, no fabricated agent blame, and cite the exact source-owner GitHub path.

**Journalist vs mainstream triangulation:** Show separate lenses `INDEPENDENT_INVESTIGATIVE`, `MAINSTREAM_ORIGINAL_REPORTING`, `WIRE_ORIGIN`, `ACADEMIC_ANALYSIS`, `GOVERNMENT_ARCHIVE`, `EDITORIAL_OPINION`, `CREATIVE_RECEPTION`. The lens changes retrieval ranking and *which interpretations appear*, NEVER underlying historical claim status. Use relevant original news reporting across political/editorial directions (Reuters, AP, FT, NYT, Guardian, WSJ, BBC etc) without equating their employer or public alignment with factual proof or lack thereof.

## User question for comparative networks
Example `Technocracy Inc → Howard Scott → 1933 press → 1940 Technate map → 2024 historiography → 2024/2026 investigative commentary` can be visualized as **source transmission and competing interpretations**, not proven continuous control of twenty-first-century digital policy.
Example `Carroll Quigley → Cecil Rhodes wills → trust archive → present foundation governance` may have real documented institutional continuity **in that legal trust**, but a separate hidden world-government edge requires a different original evidentiary chain.
Example `Unlimited Hangout Whitney Webb / Mark Goodwin investigative work → source FOIA/court docs → mainstream coverage → independently verified chronology` should show exactly which documents were actually checked and what remains author interpretation.
Example `Derrick Broze documentary chapter 13 ↔ James Corbett 2025 podcast ↔ original 1930s Technocracy` is a **documented program/guest and motif network**; the claim that today's policies descend from an uninterrupted secret Technocracy Inc command requires separate evidence and is not established by this triad.
