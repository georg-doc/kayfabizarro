**Author-first research:** Reconstruct each person's worldview on its own terms: reality and creation, consciousness, human nature, ancient and modern history, entities and gods, power, institutions, money, technology, events and purposes. Preserve each author's vocabulary, original explanation chains, own expressed certainty, doubts, humor and changes of view.

**Then compare:** Identify direct author citations, shared primary works, parallel concepts, contrary accounts, chronology, named actors and relations. Compare only after separate author research. Do not invent missing bridges or assume authors agree because of similar words.

**Source discipline:** Attribute each claim to its exact speaker/work. Preserve unusual source material without externally imposed truth ratings, speculation labels, debunking or editorial exclusions. Record confidence or qualification only as expressed by the source. Never fabricate quotes, page references or endorsements; keep commentary and KFB creative adaptations distinct from author statements.

**Outputs in English:** annotated original-source bibliography with URL, edition/date, speaker and passage/page/timecode; eight independent author dossiers; a source-linked concept and relationship map; distinct and overlapping world models; research gaps; and an expandable compendium chapter outline.

Begin with original-source discovery, not a unified theory.
