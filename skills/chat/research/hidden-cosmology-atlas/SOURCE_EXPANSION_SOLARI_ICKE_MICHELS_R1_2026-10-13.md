## 1. Source intake decisions

**David Icke** joins the core "Worldview According To" author pool as a seventh distinct primary worldview. Do not predeclare his reality model an encompassing theory of the other six. His publications can motivate cross-author comparisons while preserving differences in premises, chronology and meaning.

**The Solari Report's Substack** is added as a publication corpus for **Catherine Austin Fitts**, alongside the already indexed `solari.com` site. It is not a second Catherine persona. Preserve original publication date and republication/update date separately.

**Jesse Michels / American Alchemy** is added primarily as an **interview, documentary and guest-discovery corpus**. The host's own assertions can be indexed as Michels statements when they actually occur. Guests' theses are sourced to the guests, not to Michels, even when the episode framing is his. The user's "alternative mainstream" designation is a media-positioning / discovery lens, not a claim that every guest shares a single worldview.

These additions keep the emic source-first research contract. No source excluded on the basis of subject; no externally assigned speculation labels, debunking or credibility filter in worldview capture. Authorship, exact utterance, source object and the author's own certainty are tracked independently.
