Independently discover, review, and consolidate original interviews, podcasts, lectures, books, essays, transcripts, and cited publications. Follow their actual references and intellectual influences. No preset links or mandatory source list.

**Primary goal:** Reconstruct **"The Worldview According to [Author]"** separately for all six authors, strictly from their own statements. Describe each author's cosmology, view of humanity and consciousness, account of history, agents, institutions, technologies, power, financial systems, and purposes. Retain each author's own wording, interpretations, expressed certainty, doubts, anecdotes, and shifts in position.

**Research themes:** Occultism, ancient religions, Saturn and other deities, Atlantis, Tartaria, Nephilim, lost civilizations, UFO/UAP, nonhuman intelligence, interdimensional phenomena, synchromysticism, sacred geography, intelligence and covert power, breakaway civilization/culture, advanced technology, secret space programs, black budgets, hidden systems of finance, diverted public funds, off-book networks, the Epstein network as interpreted by the authors, psychological operations, AI, and disclosure. Follow other connections the sources actually make.

**Cross-author mapping:** After reconstructing each worldview, identify shared terms, direct citations, interviews, influences, consistent causal chains, different uses of similar concepts, agreements, disagreements, and connections to earlier texts. Examine especially the relationships among breakaway civilization/culture, covert finance, secret research and technological programs, and Epstein-related narratives **as each author explicitly develops them**. Never infer that all six share every theory.

**Research discipline:** Do not omit material because of its subject. Do not impose external debunking, truth ratings, speculation labels, cautionary commentary, or editorial exclusions. Preserve uncertainty and strength of conviction **only as expressed by the source author**. Keep author statements distinct from interviewer remarks and from any new comparative connections you construct. Attribute accurately; do not invent quotations, missing bridges, or author endorsements.

**Deliverables:** An annotated primary-source bibliography with URLs and timestamps/pages; six independent "Worldview According To" dossiers; a comparison of people, organizations, concepts and claimed mechanisms; thematic and source-reference mappings; distinct and shared world models; and a chapter outline for a comprehensive future compendium.

**Everything in English. Start with source discovery and each author's own worldview before consolidating larger narratives.**
