## 3. Two philosophical inputs, with methodological distinction

**Warburg.** The "law of good neighborliness" relates to his library's ordering of books so a researcher encounters not only the requested book but the needed nearby book. The *Mnemosyne Atlas* uses experimental montage and changing image constellations to study how motifs survive and transform. These are related but not literally identical artifacts. See Cornell University Library, `https://warburg.library.cornell.edu/about/aby-warburg/`.

**Gabriel.** A field of sense is not merely an arbitrary personal perspective: in Gabriel's realist ontology, to exist is to appear in a field of sense; appearance is not necessarily mental or observer-dependent. Avoid the straw man "every internally consistent story is equally true". The distinctions between fictional, historical, empirical and theological fields concern different objective structures and truth conditions, not blanket factual validation of a modern narrator's historical speculation. See Gabriel, *Sinn und Existenz* (2016), `https://markus-gabriel.com/sinn-und-existenz-2016`, and *Fields of Sense: A New Realist Ontology* (2015), `https://ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/fields-of-sense-a-new-realist-ontology/`.

**KFB synthesis proposed:** Semantically arrange six neighboring units and carry each through a named field while preserving: (1) exact original usage, (2) sense/conditions of reference, (3) field, (4) admissible inferences, (5) edge type to neighbor, (6) explicit author/editor boundary.

## 4. Six-position research view, without a rigid taxonomy

A constellation is identified by `constellation_id` and a central question, with six **positions** `p1 … p6`; these are six selected neighbors, not six pre-given universal dimensions. Positions can rotate, exchange places or overlap across constellations. The layout choice is for semantic navigation; distance by itself has no evidentiary meaning.

For each position: `concept_id`, `label`, `source_claim_ids`, `field_of_sense`, `definition_in_field`, `source_confidence`, `unresolved`.

For each meaningful edge: `from`, `to`, `relation_type`, `source_ids`, `interpretive_role`, `certainty`, `directionality`. Relations:
- `SOURCE_ASSERTS_LINK`: an identifiable speaker makes the connection; no independent proof implied.
- `TEXTUAL_TRANSMISSION`: historically sourced line of reception, requires exact origin and chronology.
- `HISTORICAL_CORRELATION`: independently documented co-occurrence; not automatically causation.
- `SYMBOLIC_RESEMBLANCE`: analogy / structural echo; no implied descent.
- `FIELD_TRANSLATION`: explicitly construed migration of meaning across fields.
- `CONTRADICTION`: incompatible identity, timeline, causality or metaphysical premise.
- `CREATIVE_BRIDGE`: KFB author's original association, for narrative design, never falsely attributed to a source.

Validation question for every link: **What changed? How did we get from A to B? Is this link direct, historical, analogical, reported, or wholly creative?** A visually adjacent object is not a semantic bridge by itself.
