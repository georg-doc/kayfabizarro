## 03 · Research architecture / method

**Four separate layers:**

1. **Primary corpus:** videos/podcasts, exact speaker, timestamp, quote/excerpt, provenance and transcript certainty.
2. **Claim register:** atomic attributed claims, modality, rhetorical function, support status, dependencies and counterclaims; no creative paraphrase as author quotation.
3. **Comparative knowledge graph:** motifs/entities/texts/events with typed edges and conflict edges; author-specific world versions remain available.
4. **Almanac text:** exploratory synthesis, clearly labeled author reconstructions versus editorial hypothesis; every substantial chapter claim links backward into 1–3.

**Claim entry schema (proposed, editorial, not a new runtime):**
`claim_id | source_id | timestamp_start/end | speaker | literal_excerpt | faithful_paraphrase | stated_certainty | claim_kind | evidence_status | textual_ancestry | motifs | related_claim_ids | contradictions | editorial_inference | reviewed`.

**Claim-kind vocabulary:** asserted fact; claimed anecdote/hearsay; textual interpretation; metaphor/symbol; prediction; joke/hyperbole; interviewer suggestion; editorial hypothesis.  
**Evidence-status vocabulary:** source verified; source attribution uncertain; independent corroboration pending; independently supported; contested; contradicted; unfalsifiable metaphysical proposition. The evidence status applies to an assertion, not to a person's overall credibility.

**Transcripts:** Preserve original ASR alongside any corrected, audio-verified reading. Avoid invented verbatim text and forced source-wording. Original errors include phonetic name spellings. Mark unclear words as uncertain instead of silently repairing.

**Mapping ancient texts:** identify original passage, manuscript/textual tradition, probable date/genre, earliest traceable version, later reception, then modern reinterpretation. When linking Saturn ↔ Baal ↔ Moloch ↔ other deity names, trace the exact author's asserted equivalence AND independently the historical-textual basis; do not fuse them into a single historical personage by analogy.

**Synthesis approach:** use a *polyphonic* model: competing hypotheses are maintained as branches. A maximal-coherence version is allowed as an explicitly editorial *reconstructed cosmology* with an assumption ledger and named incompatible forks, not as an asserted discovery.
