## Research questions and falsifiers
- **JWG-Q01 — Financing scope:** Did this note financing fund O.N.E. Amazon activity? Seek signed note instruments and traced disbursements. A broad company financing announcement cannot supply the missing use-of-funds chain.
- **JWG-Q02 — Partnership and deployment:** Which listed organizations signed which agreement, and which sites were operating at what date? Seek counterpart confirmations, contracts, field audit and procurement records. Proposal language could be narrowed by later cancellation or nondeployment.
- **JWG-Q03 — Rights and consent:** What land/data rights do the exact instruments convey, and what do participating communities consent to? Compare dated executed contracts and independent community testimony. No contract clauses or consent findings are certified in this packet.
- **JWG-Q04 — Instrument and outcomes:** Compare archived 2024 terms with current OAC documents, regulatory status, ecological baselines and independently measured results. One cannot settle benefit or coercion using a promotional page alone.
