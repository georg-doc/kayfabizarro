## 0. Decision: equal graph discoverability, not equal proof
One named person can have multiple distinct documented roles: original researcher, primary-source author, interviewer/host, comedian and creative donor; one talk/podcast may provide a *real episode citation* and several individually attributed claims/riffs. Any religious/apocalyptic, historical-institutional, occult, psychic, synchromystic and artistic worldview is valid to **index as a studied narrative**, with its original context and distinct sources. This **does not make a joke, a vision narrative or an author's assertion equivalent to an official ledger, first-person historical statement or dated signed instrument**. Preserve all categories and allow cross-navigation without collapsing their truth conditions.

Do NOT create two parallel master datasets, one historical and one satire. Create *role tags and typed references on the same canonical research entity/source IDs*, with independently owned derivative editorial/performance packets. The Site is a **read-only viewer + task/export workbench** over GitHub, not the owner of historical truth, editor of real-time gameplay, or owner of private NIE.

### Role identities
- `RESEARCH_AUTHOR`: published work, time-indexed statement, methodological claims and own citations; worldview profile distinct from validation of its external claims.
- `HOST_PUBLISHER`: interviewer and distribution source; question/provocation separate from guest answer.
- `COMEDIAN_CREATIVE_DONOR`: artistic technique, performance and narrative framing; can also be research author but must have exact original passage to claim an asserted research position.
- `HISTORICAL_PERSON`, `LEGAL_ENTITY`, `RELIGIOUS_ORGANIZATION`, `ANCIENT_TEXT`, `MYTHIC_NARRATIVE`, `CONTEMPORARY_CLAIM`: distinct ontology IDs, dates and source kinds.
- `KFB_FICTIONAL_RESIDENT`: existing game character ID **not** an author identity and not a free-standing historical source. Authored resident may receive an *approved lens reference*, not fabricated real-author speech.

## 3. Twelve analysis lenses with source-meaning preservation
Lenses are **switchable read-only graph queries**; they change questions, evidence filtering and display, not rewrite underlying nodes/edges.
- `DOCUMENTARY`: which original documents corroborate narrow exact propositions?
- `CUI_BONO`: who could gain, lose or pay? Incentive question only, not guilt inference.
- `FOLLOW_THE_MONEY`: actual ledgers, transfers, owners, beneficial owners, dates, origin/destination, currency, legal identity; no implied transaction from overlapping memberships.
- `CONVERGING_INTERESTS`: shared board membership/incentives/social context; Carlin-style non-centralized coordination heuristic; actual financial or directional tie requires separate evidence.
- `INFLUENCE_SPHERES`: source-grounded personnel, institutional competence and dated governance relations, no invented centralized control.
- `CONTRADICTION`: opposing reports and precise falsifier for either side.
- `TEMPORAL`: what predates what, when does a person actually serve, publish, die? No causation from succession.
- `SOURCE_TRANSMISSION`: which guest, book, host, interviewer or creative performer passed along a claim? First use date vs later retelling and scope drift.
- `SYNCHROMYSTIC`: symbol recurrence, coincidences, historical transformations, subjective synchronicities; explicitly noncausal.
- `MYTHIC_THEOLOGICAL`: 1 Enoch / Nephilim, Atlantis, Tartaria, Loosh, symbolic/philosophical and literal cosmological variants, with work/version-specific fields of sense.
- `RHETORICAL_STANDUP`: framing, contradiction, status games, satire, voice, performance; attributable real speech vs fictional adaptation.
- `KFB_NIE_ADAPTATION`: request a source-grounded draft for existing authorized output owners; not an automatic card, NPC or unapproved author impersonation.

**Triangulation is NOT three mentions.** Independence must be at the document/underlying-witness level, not three podcasts repeating one person's story. Distinguish source corroboration, actor cross-reporting and lens comparison. Two interpretations of the same source stay one evidentiary root.
