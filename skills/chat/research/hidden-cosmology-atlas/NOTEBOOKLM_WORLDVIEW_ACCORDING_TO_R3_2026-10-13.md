# The Worldview According To — Deep Research

Research and reconstruct the worldviews of **Kurt Metzger, Daniel Liszt (Dark Journalist), Michael Wann (Susquehanna Alchemy)** and other closely related thinkers.

Independently discover and consolidate their interviews, podcasts, lectures, books, articles, transcripts, and referenced publications. Trace their sources, intellectual influences, recurring concepts, and connections.

**Research scope:** Occultism, esotericism, ancient religions, Gnosticism, Hermeticism, Kabbalah, mystery schools, Saturn, Baal, Moloch, Apollo, Yahweh, the Demiurge, Atlantis, Tartaria, Nephilim, lost civilizations, catastrophism, UFO/UAP, The Phenomenon, nonhuman intelligence, interdimensional entities, consciousness, synchromysticism, sacred geography, secret societies, intelligence operations, psychological manipulation, disclosure, and alternative histories.

**Primary objective: Reconstruct each worldview on its own terms.**

Describe reality as each author understands it: cosmology, creation, human nature, consciousness, history, hidden agents, power structures, recurring patterns, and ultimate purposes.

Preserve the author's own reasoning, terminology, causal explanations, interpretations, and expressed degrees of certainty or doubt.

Do not impose external truth assessments, skepticism, debunking, speculation labels, or editorial judgments. Do not filter out unusual claims or introduce generic conspiracy disclaimers.

**Comparative research:**

Identify shared concepts, complementary explanations, influences, disagreements, and contradictions between authors. Trace references to ancient texts, historical traditions, and other researchers.

Reconstruct the most coherent overarching models emerging from these sources while preserving distinct author perspectives and unresolved differences.

**Deliverables:**

1. Comprehensive annotated bibliography with original sources and references.
2. Individual reports titled "The Worldview According to [Author]".
3. Comparative mapping of concepts, entities, events, historical narratives, and causal relationships.
4. Thematic clusters connecting related authors and traditions.
5. Consolidated cosmological models derived from the researched worldviews.
6. An extensive chapter outline for a future research compendium.

Prioritize **source fidelity, completeness, conceptual coherence, and traceability**. Attribute statements accurately without replacing an author's worldview with the researcher's interpretation.

Write all findings in **English**. Retain original quotations and precise citations wherever available.

**Begin with independent source discovery and individual worldview reconstruction before attempting a unified synthesis.**
