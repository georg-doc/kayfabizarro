Behind the HandshakeClaims, evidence and fictional mechanisms.

← Research Atlas

The game borrows questions from research, not scientifically validated control powers. Its institutions, justifications and scores are fiction.

Three distinctions that matter

  • Yurchak describes an unplanned shift toward authoritative forms; Curtis supplies a later cinematic interpretation. Neither gives a universal mastermind rule.
  • Overton concerns acceptable positions on an issue. The pyramid is a Discourse Focus metaphor; the market window is a pricing experiment.
  • An author's framework, a measured effect and an allegation are different kinds of evidence. Northwoods records proposals; it does not establish their execution.

Scope: bounded first research pass, 38 source/findmittel records. No Hughes video corpus, no full Desmet book or complete Curtis film review; no invented timecodes. See each record's scope and open questions.
