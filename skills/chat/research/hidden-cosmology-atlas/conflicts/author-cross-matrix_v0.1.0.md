## 4. Relation-type vocabulary and graph-ready contract

`AUTHOR_ASSERTED_RELATION`: source-level author A links X and Y explicitly; must include `originalSourceId` and passage.  
`DIRECT_AUTHOR_CITATION`: A cites B or B's specific work in identifiable source.  
`DOCUMENTED_INTERVIEW`: A and B participated in an original identifiable interview; agreement not implied.  
`SHARED_PRIMARY_WORK`: both cite the same precisely identified historical/publication source.  
`DOC_RELATION`: Gemini dossier's current editorial inter-author mapping; not a direct speaker-source assertion.  
`KFB_EDITORIAL_COMPARISON`: a separately authored cross-dossier relationship, with both source fields and originator recorded.  
`FIELD_TRANSLATION`: a named word/motif moves between ontological or narrative registers; explicitly explain changed meaning.  
`CONTRADICTION_CANDIDATE`: two propositions appear incompatible under a chosen interpretive lens, **until their original passages/context actually establish this**.  
`KFB_PERFORMANCE_BRIDGE`: an intentionally authored funny link for a fictional Resident/Triplet; never attributed to a real person.
