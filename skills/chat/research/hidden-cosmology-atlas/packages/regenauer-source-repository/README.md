# Tom-Oliver Regenauer · source-link repository

The public Regenauer blog was inventoried on 1 November 2026. Begin with [191 publications](essays.csv). Search by title, stable essay ID or publisher date. For each work, [the edition register](editions.csv) lists the HTML article and/or the author's numbered PDF, and gives the PDF's location or archive member name. For the PDF-only *Nexus Neurotech* (#175, date printed 31 October 2026), open the [publisher's download index](https://www.regenauer.press/blog) and select its title.

## Finding a source

1. Find the essay ID in the publication list and search the CSVs in [links](links) for that ID. Every row retains the edition, source URL and a locator. In HTML, `a108` means the 108th anchor in the article body, including non-HTTP anchors in the position count. In the PDFs, `p3-a4` means the fourth annotation on PDF page 3. For PDF sources the page is the reading location; anchor text is available from the HTML edition when present. HTML anchor labels are normalized and abbreviated to 100 characters.
2. For convergence, search [recurring URLs](recurrences/shared-001.csv). **552 URLs occur in at least two distinct publications.** Two editions of the same article count as one publication. The second count (“edition occurrences”) retains repeated references and split PDF annotations. A high count can indicate self-citation, ordinary site links or a frequently cited document. It does not establish independent corroboration. [Hosts](domains.csv) are ranked by number of distinct publications.
3. Inspect the actual source at the cited passage before making a claim. The link table records what the author pointed to. It encompasses primary material, reporting, author commentary, multimedia and illustrative links. Destination bodies and quotations were not systematically verified.

## Completeness and editions

The [sitemap](https://www.regenauer.press/sitemap.xml) and [RSS feed](https://www.regenauer.press/feed/rss2) each contained the same 190 article URLs. All 190 had a readable article-body container. The five ZIP downloads for 2021–25 supplied 160 PDF members and the 2026 download list added 13 PDFs. Archive #072 appears twice with different filenames and identical file bytes. Of the resulting 172 different PDF editions, 171 matched a published HTML article by title/content and/or overlapping links. The additional PDF #175 brings the count to 191 distinct publications. The first item is the launch notice dated 18 December 2020. [Annual archive hashes and member counts](yearly-archives.csv) allow later snapshots to be compared; the archive and individual download URLs on the host expire. Use the stable blog index plus year, member filename or PDF number.

The 190 HTML bodies yielded 9,045 eligible anchors. The PDF editions yielded 9,564 clickable HTTP(S) link annotations. Together these give 18,609 occurrences, 8,144 distinct URL strings and 2,782 hostnames. URL identity preserves query strings, paths and fragments. The 41 non-HTTP/unsupported candidates and 10 signed or credential-shaped candidates are excluded. Split annotation rectangles on the same page can point to the same target. 22 distinct works had zero eligible embedded links across all editions. Citation references without a clickable target, sources inside embedded audio, dead or redirected targets, and withdrawn or unlisted publications are outside these counts. The essay order uses the HTML publisher's structured date; a date extracted from the PDF's opening lines is separately provided where available. [Coverage data](coverage.json).

The blog's two previously indexed *Monopole, Macht und Mord* essays were used as regression cases. All 108 old links for part 1 and all 170 for part 2 are present in their previous order. The wider article-body selector also captures the inter-part link in part 2's introductory line. Text and PDF originals are retained by the publisher. The repository contains metadata and link evidence.

## Starting points for later journalist collections

Looking only at exact outbound hostnames after treating `www.` and the bare host alike, **45 essays link to 62 distinct corbettreport.com URL strings** and **31 essays link to 40 distinct unlimitedhangout.com URL strings**. Domain membership identifies a publication outlet. Check the individual byline before attributing a particular piece to James Corbett or Whitney Webb. In *Gov-Corp* (PDF #149, p. 3), both targets in the two-part *Dark MAGA Gov-Corp Technate* series appear as wrapped printed links and as clickable PDF annotations. HTML/PDF plain-text URL scanning surfaced the two line fragments; page inspection reconciled both with the existing complete annotations.

## Atlas starting points

[Keyword finder for 190 HTML editions](topic-finder.csv) contains 72 essay/topic matches across 55 articles. These are literal search cues in the article body, not editorial tags or claims. Queries cover explicit COG terminology, stay-behind/Gladio, 9/11 and named people, institutions and publishers. Missing synonyms, names used without their first names, audio and the PDF-only edition limit recall. For example, [Kollektive Selbstzerstörung](https://www.regenauer.press/kollektive-selbstzerstoerung) links its COG phrasing to an encyclopedia. [Flagge meiden](https://www.regenauer.press/flagge-meiden) links its Swedish stay-behind and Palme passages to WikiSpooks. Both are starting points for checking source custody, not evidence about the Toepke handover. The [two-essay source audit](../../topics/gehlen-german-stay-behind/RESEARCH_NEIGHBOURS.md) already follows selected links farther.

For a future publisher inventory, 45 works point to 62 exact Corbett Report URLs, and 31 point to 40 Unlimited Hangout URLs (www and bare host grouped, distinct works counted once). Start by resolving these targets to the publisher's own work IDs. Neither publisher's full corpus is included in this edition.
