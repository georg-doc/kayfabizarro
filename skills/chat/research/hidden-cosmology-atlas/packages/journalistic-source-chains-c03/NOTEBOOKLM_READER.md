# Patrik Baab — source-lead profile

Baab’s methodological argument is kept distinct from an individual war-reporting finding. This profile samples his own published answers about research, a bibliography and an independently issued professional standard. It does not certify his descriptions of any conflict, and it avoids placing a reporting method in the same category as a legal or historical conclusion.

Read selected question/answer passages on research, power, newsroom incentives and comparing media. The page introduces *Recherchieren — Ein Werkzeugkasten zur Kritik der herrschenden Meinung*. No audio played; introductory commentary belongs to Klöckner, not automatically Baab.
- **JT3-S07**, [Baab’s publications page](https://www.patrikbaab.de/bucher). Read entries for *Recherchieren*, *Auf beiden Seiten der Front — meine Reisen in die Ukraine*, and *Propaganda-Presse*. These identify three works and source leads; no book edition, chapter or footnote was audited. The page presents first-hand travel and hearing different sides as methods, which remain author/publisher claims about his work.
- **JT3-S08**, [German Press Council’s Pressekodex](https://www.presserat.de/pressekodex.html), version 19 March 2025. Read preamble and sections 1–3, particularly 2 (diligence) and guideline 2.4 (interviews). This is an original normative countercheck selected by the researcher, not a cited Baab source or a ruling on Baab.

## Author position and independent check
**JT3-C05:** Baab argues that research should scrutinize power, probe beyond press-office answers, consider who benefits and compare outlets. His description of work and newsroom incentives is an explanatory position; it does not establish each alleged incentive in a specific newsroom. **JT3-C06:** The Press Council requires diligent truth-checking, non-distortion, visible uncertainty and correction. The standard supplies a concrete test for any investigation, including a power-critical one; it does not demand that evidence for competing views be equally strong.

The useful tension is methodological: an opposition-oriented question can uncover material omitted elsewhere, but it does not predetermine the answer. Hearing a witness is not the same as confirming that witness’s account. Personal access, representative sampling, independent documentary support and treatment of contrary testimony must be assessed separately. No professional-misconduct finding follows from this comparison.

## Fields of sense
**SOURCE_LENS:** Baab’s account of critical research. **REPORTING_METHOD:** access, observation and witness selection. **MEDIA_ECONOMICS:** testable incentives and ownership. **CONTRADICTION:** records or testimony that challenge a claim. These fields support discovery while preserving differences between interview, promotional book description and institutional norm.

## Open questions / falsifier
**JT3-Q03:** Select one dated field-report claim and compare contemporaneous notes, exact interlocutor role, document, travel/access conditions and strongest contrary source. **JT3-Q04:** Inspect the actual book’s methodological passage before treating promotional description as demonstrated practice. The linked publisher book endpoint and author-hosted referendum PDF did not load; their contents were not used. A contradictory record or unrepresentative witness set would narrow a particular claim, not invalidate every work. Next bounded gate is a complete claim-specific reporting chain. No book chapters, video or audio inspected.
