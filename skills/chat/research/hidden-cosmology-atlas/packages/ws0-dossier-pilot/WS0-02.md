## 3. Interpretation

The existing card turns a philosophical scenario into a game penalty. Preserve that fiction, but separate it from a prediction about an existing AI. Bensinger contests the popular fundraising/obedience retelling. The underlying2010 post has not been independently inspected here.

An emotionally forceful conditional does not establish its premises, the agent’s existence or an obligation to obey.
