## 3. Interpretation

Roko considers conditional punishment of informed potential donors by a hypothetical future singleton. This is one possibility in an argument about altruism. The printed card turns it into a game penalty.

An emotionally forceful conditional does not establish its premises, the agent’s existence or an obligation to obey.
