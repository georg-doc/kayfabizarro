## 3. Interpretation

The card compresses different surveillance mechanisms into an all-seeing backdoor. That is a dramatic premise; the inspected report supports a narrower collection process. The NB01 infographic is useful as a question map, not proof of blanket access. Neither this report nor a product label establishes that every encrypted message is readable.

Avoid both total-immunity promises and the claim that nothing helps. Protection depends on the data, adversary and access mechanism.
