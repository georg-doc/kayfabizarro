## 2. Epistemic Core & Ontology — source-intake scaffold

Based on the author's available book description and passage-level text leads, Bramley's overarching problem is the recurrence of human conflict and the possibility that a hidden nonhuman group was involved in shaping human history and institutions. He calls the posited governing group the **Custodial society / Custodians** in the text. This is his terminology within his historical reconstruction, not a claim verified by the research editor.

**Author-specific questions requiring passage-level intake:**
1. What scope, purpose and historical duration does Bramley actually assign to the Custodians?
2. How does he narrate the transition from ancient gods and human rulers to political and religious hierarchies?
3. Which events and original religious/historical texts does he cite for human warfare, factionalism and conflict?
4. How does he distinguish personal inference, secondhand report, translation, ancient source and strong conclusion in his own text?
5. How does he define possible human liberation or the status of the alleged nonhuman administrators in later chapters?

Do not infer that Bramley's use of `Custodial` proves the same entity-model as another author's archons, parasites, the `Loosh` construct or UFO-disclosure narratives.
