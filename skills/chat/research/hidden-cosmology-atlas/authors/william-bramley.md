- **The Base Reality:** The Earth is not a paradise or a natural learning ground, but a managed colony and "Prison Planet" operated by extraterrestrial "Custodians" who treat humanity as property and slave labor.

- **The Custodial Management:** Figures historically worshipped as gods (such as Jehovah operating as a series of custodial management teams) actively intervened in early human civilizations like Sumer to establish and police a captive workforce.

- **Spiritual Amnesia & Gnosis:** Custodians intentionally induce spiritual amnesia, forcing human focus onto material survival and dogma, while Gnosis (direct spiritual knowledge) serves as the ultimate exit from the physical trap.


### Actors, Hierarchies & Proxies


- **The Top Tier / The Custodians:** A non-human intelligence faction maintaining containment and resource harvesting over humanity.

- **The Brotherhood Network:** The Brotherhood of the Snake (originally founded to disseminate truth, later infiltrated and corrupted) along with secret societies, mystery schools, and orthodox religious hierarchies acting as management proxies.

- **Custodial Prison Planet:** The structural model of Earth as a confined slave colony monitored by non-human wardens.

- **Self-Policing & Guilt:** Psychological mechanisms (sin, fear, social ostracism) internalized by populations to enforce compliance without requiring overt external force.
