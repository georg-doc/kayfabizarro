## Author perspective, before comparison

In [Game Theory #6: The World’s Bank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbamEcNuDXo), published 22 January 2026, Jiang presents financial institutions through competition between states and incentives to protect capital. His narrative moves from Dutch vulnerability to England, parliamentary continuity and government borrowing, then to property protection and financial centres. His closing answer distinguishes private values of family and community from competitive pressures on states.

This is an explanatory and normative framework. Its historical dates, empirical magnitudes, proposed causal links and moral judgments require different kinds of evidence. The title refers to the lecture’s thesis, not automatically to the modern World Bank institution.
