# Nikola Tesla — source-lead dossier

Tesla’s documented electrical inventions, his own explanatory theories and later extraordinary interpretations must be separate objects. This dossier starts with two dated patents and the archive custodian’s finding aid. It neither inventories his complete career nor treats every modern claim using his name as a position he held.

## Works and archive leads
1. **US645576A, System of transmission of electrical energy**, filed 2 September 1897, published 20 March 1900. [Patent reproduction](https://patents.google.com/patent/US645576A/en), **BPS-S08**. Inspected metadata and opening OCR specification. The title and record identify the electrical-transmission proposal; no full technical analysis or original figure inspection performed.
2. **US685957A, Apparatus for the utilization of radiant energy**, filed 21 March 1901, published 5 November 1901. [Patent reproduction](https://patents.google.com/patent/US685957A/en), **BPS-S09**. Inspected OCR specification discussing a radiation-exposed conductor, capacitor and discharge circuit. This is an apparatus claim using incident radiation, not by itself evidence of energy generated without input. Historical particle/ether language must be retained as Tesla’s period explanation rather than silently translated into a modern mechanism.
3. **Nikola Tesla Museum, Activity archive finding aid**, [institutional archive](https://tesla-museum.org/en/legacy/archive/activity/), **BPS-S10**. Inspected collection description and category list. The custodian separates scientific works/patent documentation from plans and drawings. This identifies where originals may be sought; no individual manuscript, contract or drawing was retrieved or read.
