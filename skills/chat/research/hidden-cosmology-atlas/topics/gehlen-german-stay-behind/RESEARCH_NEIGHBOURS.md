# Research neighbors: political violence, financial institutions and continuity planning

This is an index of specific claims to investigate alongside the [Holstein case](HOLSTEIN_TRANSFER.md). Favor the author's own publication for what the author says; use case records for what happened. Similar political context does not identify a shared operator.

## Interviews and competing accounts

- **Dirk Pohlmann / Olof Palme.** In his [2024 Zeit-Fragen interview](https://www.zeit-fragen.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/zeit-fragen/eins/ganze_Ausgaben/2024/ZF_20240820_17_web.pdf), response to the question about milestones, Pohlmann describes the Swedish submarine affair as psychological warfare against Palme's détente policy and names his 2005 and 2014 films. The [production record](https://februarfilm.de/filme/taeuschung-die-methode-reagan/) establishes his authorship of the Reagan documentary. His interpretation concerns pressure on a government. To test the separate murder claim use the [Swedish prosecution review, 18 December 2025](https://www.aklagare.se/globalassets/dokument/ovriga-dokument/amr-8123-25-18dec-2025-translation-of-a-decision-in-a-review-case.pdf), sections reviewing Engström and giving reasons. It maintains closure and revises the grounds: the available evidence cannot identify the perpetrator. No operational link to German stay-behind follows. No film playback performed.
- **Wolfgang Effenberger / German reunification.** His [25 January 2024 commentary](https://apolut.net/vier-attentate-begleiten-deutsche-wiedervereinigung-von-wolfgang-effenberger/) takes as its opening question whether the attacks on Herrhausen (1989), Lafontaine and Schäuble (1990), and Rohwedder (1991) were connected. Index four events separately, including the distinction between killings and survivors. His references 7, 8 and 10 point to the same Lafontaine interview; repeated citations do not make three independent sources. For Herrhausen compare the contemporary [SDR broadcast preserved by SWR](https://www.swr.de/swrkultur/wissen/archivradio/raf-mord-an-alfred-herrhausen-1989-104.html) and the [account of the RAF claim of responsibility](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/196955/vor-30-jahren-ermordung-von-alfred-herrhausen/). Distinguish a group's communiqué, an individual's identification and evidence of commissioning. No common command is established here.
- **Effenberger / military doctrine.** His [August 2026 essay](https://wolfgangeffenberger.substack.com/p/von-win-in-a-complex-world-zu-warfighting) interprets a planning continuity from US Army doctrine to the NATO 2040 horizon. Compare his account of potential opponents with [TRADOC Pamphlet 525-3-1](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/367967.pdf), cover dated 7 October 2014 and section 2-4. The planning document is a source for institutional thinking and scenarios, not an operational order for a past attack.
- **Ulrich Chaussy, Tobias von Heymann / Munich, 1980.** Compare [Chaussy's BR investigation](https://www.br.de/br-fernsehen/inhalt/film-und-serie/oktoberfest-attentat-spurensuche-ulrich-chaussy-webdokumentation100.html) with the [Bundesarchiv's contextualized MfS documents](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/themen-entdecken/online-entdecken/themenbeitraege/das-oktoberfestattentat-1980/). The East German files include reports on West German investigations; who collected a report matters. The [federal prosecutor's 2020 closure statement](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/4645998) identifies Köhler's right-wing extremist motive and reports no concrete indications supporting another perpetrator, instigator or accessory. Distinguish the search for accomplices from an attribution to stay-behind.
- **Peteano, Italy, 1972.** The parliamentary archive preserves [the Gladio inquiry report and separately attributed supplementary notes](https://www.senato.it/legislature/10/leggi-e-documenti/attivita-non-legislative/documenti-non-legislativi?documentoId=30539). The [Peteano-bis discussion](https://www.senato.it/service/PDF/PDFServer/BGT/83505.pdf), passage on Casson's 3 January 1989 request and the Aurisina caches, treats possible protection of the organization and the source of explosives as investigative hypotheses. Record each witness, prosecution request, verdict and appeal separately. The investigation into a killing can expose an organization without establishing that it ordered the killing.
