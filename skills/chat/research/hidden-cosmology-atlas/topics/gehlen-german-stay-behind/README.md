# The German stay-behind handover

**Finding, 1 November 2026.** Elements of Allied intelligence collection and escape networks passed to the BND beginning in 1956. A November 1952 US policy instruction assigning the German service the larger burden predates that transfer. The available documents identify its American author, the receiving German institution and political oversight. The name of the person who first proposed or signed an individual 1956 network transfer remains open.

## What the dated records establish

| 9 June 1951 | The Pullach dispatch reports 21 trained wireless operators and 27 in training for the F-Net. It credits UTILITY's visit to OSMOSIS and the work of his staff with helping advance the base station. UTILITY was Reinhard Gehlen. | [GS-02, MGLA-6815, document 102, §§8, 11–13, pp. 646–648](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/docs/05/b7/a2/05b7a2bdccf54db08e68a130b4d5c00f/cia-and-the-origins-of-the-bnd-1949-56-vol-2_0002.pdf). The dispatch reports a specific personal intervention. [GS-03, NARA cryptonym guide, entries UTILITY, SATURN and KIBITZ](https://www.archives.gov/files/iwg/declassified-records/rg-263-cia-records/second-release-lexicon.pdf). SATURN spans the Gehlen organization and BND. Karlsruhe's KIBITZ was a separately identified project. |
| 8 October 1951 | Allen Dulles told Gehlen that he expected Gehlen's organization to become the German intelligence service and valued its Eastern European reporting. CIA Director Walter Bedell Smith then offered continued assistance. | [GS-04, “DCI's Meeting with General Gehlen,” §§1–3, p. 411](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/docs/fa/d1/79/fad179a9c3fd4cb79488732c0c88566f/cia-and-the-origins-of-the-bnd-1949-56-vol-2_0001.pdf). The memo distinguishes the two American speakers. Its subject is the future service and intelligence cooperation. |
| 12 November 1952 | Smith told Gehlen's deputy, General von Mellenthin, that German government acquiescence would be required for stay-behind and similar activities and the German service must carry the majority of the burden. | [GS-05, Smith–Mellenthin memorandum, §2, p. 413](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/docs/fa/d1/79/fad179a9c3fd4cb79488732c0c88566f/cia-and-the-origins-of-the-bnd-1949-56-vol-2_0001.pdf). This records US policy as stated to the German side. The German government's dated assent and the names of the nets to be accepted are absent from this paragraph. |
| 1955–56 | Bross reported that the federal cabinet's July 1955 decision concerned the creation of the intelligence service under the Chancellery. His 15 May 1956 information memo to the DCI dates the main financial transfer for the organization to 1 February 1956. Gehlen agreed to continue local liaison. | [GS-06, Bross memoranda of 19 April and 15 May, docs. 96 and 98, pp. 583 and 586](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/docs/fa/d1/79/fad179a9c3fd4cb79488732c0c88566f/cia-and-the-origins-of-the-bnd-1949-56-vol-2_0001.pdf). These concern the service as a whole. The formal BND start was 1 April. [Institutional chronology](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/576795/april-1956-gruendung-des-bundesnachrichtendienstes/). A memo sent to the DCI for information does not establish that its recipient ordered a transfer. |
| 10 May 1956 | An internal CIA memorandum earmarked continued American support for activities of particular interest, explicitly including stay-behind. | [GS-07, doc. 97, §2(d), p. 584](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/docs/fa/d1/79/fad179a9c3fd4cb79488732c0c88566f/cia-and-the-origins-of-the-bnd-1949-56-vol-2_0001.pdf). The quoted budget covers several activities and cannot be assigned exclusively to stay-behind. |
| From 1956, as reported in 1991 | The federal government identifies Allied services as builders through 1955 and the BND as recipient from 1956. Asked specifically who initiated the organization, it refers back to this account. It assigns political responsibility to the head of the Chancellery for the period at issue. | [GS-08, Bundestag 12/890, answers 1, 4–5 and 9–11, printed pp. 1–3](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/12/008/1200890.pdf). Original parliamentary reply, retrospective account of the event. It withholds public command details and distinguishes presumption from a record of each briefing. |
| From 1959 | BND participation in the Allied Clandestine Committee is documented in the government's later account, which denies that the committee held command authority over the German SBO. | [GS-09, Bundestag 17/13615, passage on ACC membership and authority](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/17/136/1713615.pdf). Coordination, finance and command are separate relationships. |

## How to use the graph

Documentary edges include Gehlen → visited planned radio-base location (GS-02/03), Dulles → expressed support for Gehlen's future service (GS-04), Smith → stated burden-sharing policy to Mellenthin (GS-05), Allied services → built network elements and BND → assumed elements from 1956 (GS-08), CIA → planned continued financial support (GS-07). Each arrow describes the verb actually supported. Keep the later attribution of intellectual authorship in the [Ganser author record](../../authors/daniele-ganser.md). His country comparisons give research questions about coordination and control. They do not identify the signer of a German acceptance instrument.

**What would resolve personal authorization?** Locate a contemporary German acceptance memorandum or a bilateral handover protocol identifying the specific net, effective date and signatories. The targeted secondary finding aid is Schmidt-Eenboom and Stoll, *Die Partisanen der NATO*, section “Die Übernahme von ausländisch geführten Netzen,” starting p. 142 in [this edition](https://d-nb.info/1071212737/04). Follow its archival notes to individual BND or Chancellery files and compare the name and date with a sending-service counterpart. The contents page was inspected. This is an archival lead.

Coverage: targeted text inspection of declassified CIA record reproductions, parliamentary answers, the NARA research aid and the linked Ganser material. Printed compilation pages are locators. No manuscript signatures were authenticated.
