# Toepke: did the transfer take place?

Archival follow-up, 1 November 2026. [Previous Holstein case](HOLSTEIN_TRANSFER.md).

## What changed

Thomas Wegener Friis reports a completed handover in his review of John V. Jensen's biography, *Sønderjyske Årbøger*, 2017 volume. On **printed p. 209** (PDF sheet 8, right half), he writes: “Dette netværk overgav danskerne i 1956 til BND”. In English: the Danes handed this network to the BND in 1956. Friis attributes this to recently declassified West German records. He separately says Lunding urged Gehlen to have **Toepke himself** admitted to the Bundeswehr. The printed passage contains no signature, effective day, roster or citation to a separate acceptance instrument. [Review PDF](https://tidsskrift.dk/soenderjydskeaarboeger/article/download/119736/167503#page=8) · [University of Southern Denmark publication record](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/jensen-john-v-danmark-en-god-mand-hitler-officer-menneskesmugler-/)

The [Friis/Schmidt-Eenboom archival study](https://tidsskrift.dk/frakrigogfred/article/download/163931/205347/364145), printed pp. 46–48, nn. 32 and 36–37, describes an **offer** to the West German side. It identifies Lunding as initiator and Heusinger as the contact whose assistance he sought. Note 36 cites **file p. 2** of a memorandum dated **13 March 1956**, concerning a meeting with KAMMGARN on **8 March 1956 in Hamburg**; note 32 cites file p. 3. These are the dates of the recorded conversation and the memo. Neither the date nor the meeting title alone establishes Lunding's personal attendance, identifies KAMMGARN with Lunding, or dates acceptance. The two publications share an author and cannot be counted as two independent confirmations.

| Question | Best current answer | What sustains it |
|---|---|---|
| Who initiated it? | Lunding offered the Toepke network; he sought Heusinger's assistance. | Archival study citing a specific German memorandum. |
| Was it completed? | A specialist who used German records explicitly reports transfer to the BND in 1956. | Friis review, p. 209. Record this as attributed completion. No contemporaneous receipt inspected. |
| Who accepted or signed? | Unresolved on both sides. | Neither a signed acceptance nor a directive taking charge of this particular network was recovered. |
| What did Gehlen do personally? | Friis reports a request to Gehlen concerning Toepke's employment. Toepke entered the Bundeswehr. | Personnel and organizational outcomes have different subjects. |
| What about Dulles? | No located source identifies him in this particular transfer. | His documented contact with Gehlen is treated in the [parent case](README.md). |

## Germany: identifiers and follow-up

The two scholarly forms **BND-Archiv Nr. 01292** and **01292.e** point to the retrieval target. Search variants include Kammgarn, Kammkarn, Toepke/Töpke, Mölln, Lunding, Heusinger, the two March dates, and Gegenzeichnung/Übernahme/Unterstellung. The publicly searchable materials inspected yielded the research citations. **BArch B 206/1292** is a different file on economic sites in Görlitz (older identifier 14364/167). The [Bundesarchiv/DDB item](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/item/3WTS6CTBPU6Y3CQWLWD3XEY3O7G54KZB) and the [B 206 finding aid](https://open-data.bundesarchiv.de/apex-ead/DE-1958_B_206.xml) rule out that numerical match. The 2018 export is a finding aid and cannot establish the completeness of holdings today.

Later records cited under 01292 include an explanation dated 25 August 1958, a Mørch item of 8 May 1959, **“Betr. Lunding,” 27 May 1960**, and a 27 July 1962 report on BEGONIE talks of 24–25 July. The footnotes establish these record descriptions in the file. Their cited subject matter does not establish German acceptance of Toepke's network. The German recipient's potential counterpart series include [Heusinger's papers, BArch N 643](https://kalliope-verbund.info/de/findingaid?fa.enum=1&fa.id=DE-611-BF-113744) and [Schleswig-Holstein Interior Ministry, LASH Abt. 611](https://arcinsys.schleswig-holstein.de/arcinsys/showFondsDetails?fondsId=1042). These are fonds-level leads.

## Denmark: register, records creator and independent evidence

- The National Archives' paper name index for **Flensborg, konsulær repræsentation**, lists [packages 157 (A–K) and 158 (K–T)](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/fysiske_enheder_liste?epid=3136179&heid=3136179&faid=11). Start with 158 for Toepke/Thomsen and 157 for Heusinger. The [subject register, package 135](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/fysiske_enheder_liste?epid=3136157&heid=3136157&faid=11) may yield a D number for [the files, packages 135–156](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/fysiske_enheder_liste?epid=433925&heid=433920&faid=11). These are ordering points, not hits inside the files. Flensburg's [separate A1264 record](https://arkiv.dk/vis/1607941) covers 1948–1950.
- **First Danish order:** [archive creator no. 5007](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/arkivskaber_detaljer?ngid=1236073), [KC. Kopibog med bilag (afklassificeret), series 5040899, physical package 4, 1956–1957](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/fysiske_enheder_liste?epid=5040899&heid=5040899&faid=11). This is a declassified copybook with enclosures and an ordering entry, not a digital copy. Search for a dated outgoing confirmation citing the German note and a German reply among the enclosures. The [X-3/Z subject files, packages 1–3](https://daisy.rigsarkivet.dk/fysiske_enheder_liste?epid=5040872&heid=5040872&faid=11) are a second route via journal number. Request March–December 1956 and include early 1957 for delayed confirmation.
- For 1956 use the records creator **Forsvarsstabens Efterretningsafdeling**. The [Danish service's archival notice](https://www.fe-ddis.dk/da/nyheder/2010/fe-afleverer-arkivalier-til-statens-arkiver/) documents transfer of records covering 1937–1967 to the National Archives. It does not identify a specific Toepke file. Where access is restricted use the [Rigsarkivet access procedure](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/ansoeg-bestil/adgang/arkivalier/).
- In [Jensen's Toepke chapter](https://d-nb.info/1370626479/34), pp. 196–198, nn. 26–33, the Danish alien file of 28 June 1951 documents travel and occupation. The newspaper of 6 May 1961 concerns Toepke's military career. His children's interview of 12 June 2016 supports continuing personal contact, and the 1986 DR interview offers retrospective testimony. None of these, as cited, is a Danish operational receipt. Jensen refers the handover offer back to published research. Do not silently merge Thor/Janus or later Dachs/Isegrim with Toepke's people on the strength of a shared region or contact.

## Retrieval brief ready for the archives

**German request text:** Bitte prüfen Sie die Konkordanz der in der Forschung zitierten BND-Altsignatur 01292 beziehungsweise 01292.e. Gesucht wird die vollständige Aktennotiz vom 13. März 1956 zur Hamburger Besprechung mit KAMMGARN am 8. März einschließlich aller Seiten, Anlagen, Randverfügungen und des Verteilers. Bitte prüfen Sie auch das unmittelbar anschließende Schriftgut 1956/57 zu Lunding, Heusinger und dem von Günter/Günther Toepke geführten Netz im Raum Mölln. Gesucht werden die Antwort auf das Angebot, eine Übernahme- oder Auflösungsverfügung sowie Datum, Dienststelle und zeichnende Funktion. Falls der Bestand abgegeben wurde, bitten wir um die heutige Signatur und den Verwahrort. [Documented BND archive application form](https://media.frag-den-staat.de/files/foi/690668/NutzungsantragBND-Archiv.pdf) · [Bundesarchiv enquiry route](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/im-archiv-recherchieren/hilfe/anfragen-an-das-bundesarchiv/)

**Danish request text:** Vi søger journalindførsler, udgående og indgående korrespondance samt eventuelle kvitteringer fra Forsvarsstabens Efterretningsafdeling i 1956–57 om Hans Mathiesen Lundings tilbud om at overdrage Günter Toepkes netværk i Holsten til vesttysk ledelse. En tysk notits af 13. marts omtaler en samtale i Hamborg den 8. marts 1956. Kan I oplyse arkivsignatur, adgangsforhold og eventuel modtagende tjeneste, dato og underskrivende funktion? Sondr venligst mellem Toepkes net og Thor samt mellem overdragelsen og Toepkes senere ansættelse i Bundeswehr.

**Coverage:** Compared the published wording directly in the Danish review PDF with archival citations and follow-up references. Neither the complete 1956 memorandum nor an operational acceptance or rejection letter was accessible in the public records searched. The two requests are prepared for human submission.
