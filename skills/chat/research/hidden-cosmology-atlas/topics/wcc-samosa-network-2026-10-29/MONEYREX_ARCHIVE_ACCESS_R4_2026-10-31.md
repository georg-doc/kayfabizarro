### Claims from the newly retrieved *primary published notices*

- **WC-C45** `MONEYREX_FORMAL_REGISTER_ID_PUBLIC_1990_1995`: a named legal entity `Moneyrex – Euro Market Money Brokers S.p.A.` filed public company notices, shown **in liquidation by 1990**. Supported by WC-S41–43 as original published announcements.
- **WC-C46** `MONEYREX_MILAN_REGISTRY_COORDINATES`: `Milano, Corso Monforte 45`; `Tribunale di Milano n.125461/3208/11` in **1990/1994**; `n.125461/3608/11` in **1995**, different middle number — `PRINTED_VARIANT_UNRESOLVED` (possible typo, not proven).
- **WC-C47** `MONEYREX_CAPITAL_AND_LIQUIDATOR_PUBLIC`: notices list 50 million lire paid-in capital and 1990/1995 liquidator Antonio Castelli; current officer/shareholder structure **not** established for 1964–1972.
- **WC-C48** `MONEYREX_FINAL_LIQUIDATION_AGENDA_NOT_OUTCOME`: 1994 and 1995 agenda reference final liquidation accounts, but no examined original shows whether or exactly when company was removed from register, or whether final distribution occurred.
- **WC-C49** `TWO_DIRECT_OFFICIAL_CUSTODIANS`: Parliament archives is the designated holder of 1981 letter catalogue; Milan Chamber of Commerce can search Tribunal/Chamber historical company records, including pre-1996 holdings, and offers official direct email paths. This is **access route proof**, not proof files have been delivered.
- **WC-C50** `BPF_VS_MONEYREX_DISTINCTION_RETAINED`: The R3 academic passage establishing **Hambros 24.5% BPF** does not establish **Hambros equity in Moneyrex**; these *new Gazette notices* do not name 1964–72 shareholders either. The Moneyrex→Hambros edge stays `OPEN_HYPOTHESIS`.
