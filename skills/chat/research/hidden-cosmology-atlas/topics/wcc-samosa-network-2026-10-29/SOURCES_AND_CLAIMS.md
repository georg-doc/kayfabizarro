1. **Samosa → WCC historical lead → Peter Levenda / Joseph P. Farrell (as Samosa says)** is *one narrator reporting earlier influences*, not two independently audited original citations. Retrieve original books before a multi-source consensus count.
2. **Jorjani → Samosa** is a documented cross-author *citation/reception*. Repetition by a supporter adds reception evidence, not independent proof of a corporate-narcotics-UFO chain.
3. **P2 parliamentary investigation** independently supports specific P2–Ambrosiano links. The image's red arrow **WCC → P2** is not validated by that original.
4. **Scott's correction** (WC-C14) defeats treating every repeated list of 'WCC subsidiaries' as equally reliable.
5. **SOFINDUS → Skorzeny → WCC** has no directly inspected transferable corporate asset document in this packet. UfoFeed's provenance critique is a retrieval lead (WC-S13); assess the original Otto papers, Ganis and banking records independently.
6. **1933 Magenta UFO, retrieved exotic materials, 'space program' and classified UAP custody** remain separate claim families; no examined governmental original in this topic ties them to WCC.
