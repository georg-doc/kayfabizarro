## The real three-Hambro identity problem

Do not treat `Hambro` as one graph node. At minimum:

- **Sir Charles Jocelyn Hambro (1897–1963)**: wartime SOE / Bank of England figure; official BoE biography, WC-S19. Samosa's claimed early WCC founder.
- **Jocelyn O. Hambro (active 1969–1974)**: Sindona-linked banker identified in a 1974 newspaper quotation and 1971 Italian parliamentary testimony index; original name/middle initial and company role should be validated from 1969–1974 filings.
- **Charles Eric Alexander Hambro (1930–2002)**: son of the wartime Charles, later Hambros chairman; **another Charles**, evidenced in Oxford DNB biography (https://academic.oup.com/oxford-dictionary-of-national-biography/reference/63486/reference-article-abstract/580535636), abstract read. Not the WWII SOE director; no automatic WCC membership.

All family links and later banking positions are **person-specific edges**, NOT `WCC_FUNDS_SINDONA` unless a surviving WCC company/share ledger establishes an actual transaction.
