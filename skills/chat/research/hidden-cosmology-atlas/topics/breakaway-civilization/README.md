# Breakaway Civilization: who used the term, and what changed?

Research note · 2026-11-01 · Hidden Cosmology Atlas Editorial Research. Read alongside the [Jorjani dossier](../../authors/jason-reza-jorjani.md), [Farrell dossier](../../authors/joseph-farrell.md) and [Fitts dossier](../../authors/catherine-austin-fitts.md).

## Dolan's earliest located publication

**BC-S01 / BC-C01.** Richard M. Dolan, *UFOs and the National Security State*, vol. 2, *The Cover-Up Exposed, 1973–1991* (Keyhole, 2009). The [author's book page](https://richardmdolan.wixsite.com/rdpress/the-cover-up-exposed-1973-1991) reproduces the introduction. Under “The Problem,” the paragraphs addressing terrestrial breakthroughs distinguish human classified research from what Dolan considers the nonhuman core of the phenomenon. The specific term occurs on **pp. 564–565 according to Farrell** (BC-S05). Those two original printed pages were unavailable for visual inspection. Dolan later identified volume 2 as the first publication in a Dark Journalist interview, at [21:47–22:14 in the indexed transcript](https://fight.fudgie.org/search/show/dkj/episode/20130919_Thu_5CGG5gjsUNY). Audio unverified. The publication year, contemporaneous use and retrospective priority claim have separate evidentiary bases.

**BC-S02 / BC-C02.** Los Angeles, 19 September 2009. In the [organizer's transcript of Dolan's talk](https://projectcamelot.org/lang/en/richard_dolan_awake_and_aware_en.html), find the paragraph beginning “Another conclusion that I draw in this book” and the following explanation. Dolan proposes that a classified community could accumulate enough scientific knowledge, funding and autonomy to diverge from wider society. It remains connected to that society. This is the earliest dated first-person passage inspected here that explicitly uses the term. Earlier radio appearances located in the search lacked a verified passage using it. Absolute priority and the precise first publication day remain open.

## Where Farrell changes the hypothesis

**BC-S03 / BC-C03.** On 29 April 2011 Farrell explicitly tied Dolan's idea to a *possible* financing mechanism for covert space operations. Locator: numbered point 3 in the [bearer-bonds article](https://gizadeathstar.com/2011/04/japan-connecting-some-dots-part-five-miscellaneous-anomalies-about-the-bearer-bonds-story/). Coverage: indexed author text, direct page retrieval failed. This is an early lead; it does not document any transfer of funds.

**BC-S04 / BC-C04.** On 7 September 2011, introducing *Saucers, Swastikas and Psyops* while still writing the book, Farrell asks how a proposed Nazi component related to other components. The [author's announcement](https://gizadeathstar.com/2011/09/new-book-saucers-swastikas-and-psyops/), paragraph beginning “As I point out in the preface,” establishes when this particular question was publicly formulated.

**BC-S05 / BC-C05.** The clearest passage is in *Covert Wars and Breakaway Civilizations* (Adventures Unlimited Press, copyright 2012). In [chapter 9, opening epigraph, numbered lists and endnote 1](https://uncorruptedtruth.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/covert_wars_and_breakaway_civilizations_the_secret_space_program.pdf), Farrell reproduces Dolan's conditional formulation and cites vol. 2, pp. 564–565. He then supplies a proposed structure: financial, corporate, intelligence and military sectors intersecting with American, postwar European fascist and Asian elite factions. Chapter 5 places a similar Dolan epigraph beside Farrell's proposed securities and counterfeiting mechanism. Chapter 5 endnote 2 mislabels the volume as “One” despite giving the second volume's subtitle; chapter 9 endnote 1 identifies vol. 2. Targeted PDF text inspection of front matter and these chapters, zero page images inspected. These are claims made by Farrell. His taxonomy adds proposed actors and mechanisms to Dolan's hypothesis.

## Two branches of reception

**BC-S06 / BC-C06.** Catherine Austin Fitts directly recounts reading Dolan's volume in [her 2 September 2010 Solari program note](https://library.solari.com/the-national-security-state-the-clash-of-civilizations/). Read from “Over the years” through the announced discussion and the closing film recommendation. She applies his framework to corporate power, opaque budgets and the distribution of economic benefits, invites Dolan to discuss it and directs readers to the September 2009 lecture. This establishes her direct reception before Farrell's two 2011 texts above.

**BC-S07 / BC-C07.** In [Fitts' 6 September 2012 announcement with Farrell](https://solari.com/the-breakaway-civilization-with-joseph-farrell/), the paragraph asking about the black budget credits Dolan while framing the discussion around postwar technology, private corporations and funding. The related links include the earlier Dolan interview and her review of Farrell's book. Attribution is to Fitts' published editorial text. Neither Solari recording was used as a verbatim source.

**BC-S08 / BC-C08.** Jason Reza Jorjani, [*Breakaway Culture*, 9 May 2025](https://jasonrezajorjani.substack.com/p/breakaway-culture), three paragraphs preceding “Flying Saucers and the Nazi Bell.” He names Dolan as formulator and Farrell as the author who develops a postwar fascist genealogy. He then uses Spengler's distinction between culture and civilization to justify his own term. Jorjani foregrounds one strand of Farrell's multi-faction account. This passage documents his interpretation of both predecessors. A direct Fitts-to-Jorjani transmission is unestablished.

## Relationships supported by the texts

| Later source | Relation to earlier work | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Dolan lecture | Explains his own published hypothesis | BC-S02 → BC-S01 |
| Fitts program note | Reports reading Dolan and links his lecture | BC-S06 → BC-S01, BC-S02 |
| Farrell book | Quotes Dolan and supplies a proposed postwar institutional and financial model | BC-S05 → BC-S01 |
| Fitts program with Farrell | Names Dolan and brings Farrell's work into the same discussion | BC-S07 → BC-S01, BC-S04 (work discussed) |
| Jorjani essay | Credits both authors and redefines their term | BC-S08 → Dolan, Farrell (author-level attribution) |

These relationships establish a publication history. Evaluating the proposed postwar factions, technology or financing requires independent transactional or archival evidence. The most useful remaining textual check is a first-edition inspection of Dolan's pp. 564–565 and the surrounding paragraph for wording, omitted context and pagination.
