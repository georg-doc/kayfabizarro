## 4 — Author-tab blueprint: the five proposed headings plus source integrity

The Gemini-proposed five headings become the **shared author-specific performance-ready dossier format**, initially instantiated on the **Kurt Metzger** tab before other authors are multiplied.

1. **Core Function & Character Archetype** — the author's worldview function *as later adapted for a fictional KFB Resident*. Clearly preserve the author-vs-KFB fictional role boundary; a Clown is not Kurt Metzger himself.
2. **Fundamental Ontology & Cosmology** — structure/origin of reality, humanity, time, consciousness and metaphysical premises as explicitly described in original works.
3. **Actors, Hierarchies & Proxies** — named agencies/entities/institutions, asserted roles, relations and causality; preserve source-native certainty or doubt.
4. **Core Mechanics & Terminology** — author-defined explanatory mechanisms, vocabulary, exact distinctions, argument flow, relationships to other named authors; for terms like `loosh`, `radio station`, `institutional cult`, retrieve the original speaker and usage rather than silently universalize.
5. **Rhetorical Markers & Performance Blueprint** — source-observed argument style and debating moves, then separately named KFB adaptation potential for stand-up, heckles, Triplets and longer comic beats. **Do not claim to emulate a real author's literal voice, invented words or real-time speech; the Resident owns the performance.**

**Required embedded provenance table/appendix (not a sixth metaphysical heading):** `authorId; sourceId; sourceTitle; originalSourceUrl; episode/publicationDate; sourceSpeaker; passage/timecode; directExcerptOrFaithfulParaphrase; authorExpressedConfidenceIfAny; conceptIds; authorAssertedEdges; editorBridgeIfAny; sourceReviewDate`. Each key claim or mechanism must have such anchors. This records authorship and textual coverage **without external speculation/debunking labels**.

**Topic availability:** `DIRECTLY_SOURCED`, `ADJACENT_SOURCE_AVAILABLE`, `NOT_YET_FOUND` indicate corpus retrieval status only, not whether an author never discussed the subject or whether a worldview is true.

**Non-destructive progression:** Author source corpus is preserved in full; the tab is a derived presentation and research index. If a premise or source is edited, keep revision history, timestamps and a before/after source-reference log. No silent deletion because a compressed dossier seems sufficient.
