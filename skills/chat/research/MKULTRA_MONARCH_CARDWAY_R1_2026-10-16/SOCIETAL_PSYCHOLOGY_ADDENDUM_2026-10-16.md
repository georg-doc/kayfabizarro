## Desmet: his theory and different evidence types
Author's original account: https://words.mattiasdesmet.org/p/the-psychology-of-totalitarianism (2022). His surname and first name: **Mattias Desmet**. Book: *The Psychology of Totalitarianism*, 2022.
His four proposed preconditions: (1) social isolation, weak bonds and loneliness; (2) lack of meaning; (3) free-floating anxiety; (4) free-floating frustration and aggression. A public narrative may concentrate the otherwise diffuse affect onto a threat and promise a response, fostering shared identity and conformity. Desmet says such formation can emerge without central design or be enhanced by propaganda and institutions. These are statements **of his model**, not established measurements of a population-wide trance.
Author's reply to direct critics: https://words.mattiasdesmet.org/p/am-i-an-expert-in-mass-formation (2022). Critical academic book-review introduction: https://www.existenz.us/volumes/Vol.15-2Wautischer.html ; another 2025 review: https://www.independent.org/tir/2025-fall/the-psychology-of-totalitarianism/ . ## Neighboring research to connect without conflation
1. **Psychoanalytic and historical crowd theory:** Freud (1921), Le Bon (1895), Fromm (1941), Arendt (1951). These are diverse frameworks rather than one proven mechanism.
2. **Social influence and conformity:** Asch, Sherif, Milgram, modern social identity/SIDE research. Reicher, Spears and Postmes propose that group norms and social identities help explain group behavior without assuming mindlessness: https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/a-social-identity-model-of-deindividuation-phenomena/ .
3. **Fear-based persuasion:** Tannenbaum et al., *Psychological Bulletin* 2015, meta-analysis of 127 articles, 248 independent samples and N=27,372, effects on attitude/intentions/behavior under specific conditions: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5789790/ . Not evidence of a unified population control program.
4. **Propaganda and public narrative:** Bernays, Lippmann, Ellul, media framing and spiral of silence. Separate descriptive mechanisms from deliberate state strategy; intentions need their own source.
5. **Digital influence:** surveillance, recommender systems, profiling and deceptive interface patterns. See Mathur et al. *What Makes a Dark Pattern... Dark?* (2021): https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04843 . Similarity of effect is NOT proof of MKUltra institutional continuation.
