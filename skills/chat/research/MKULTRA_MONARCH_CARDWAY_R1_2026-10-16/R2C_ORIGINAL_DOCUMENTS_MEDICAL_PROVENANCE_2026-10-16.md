Actual rendered original page screenshots read at **PDF indices 8 and 10** (printed pp7 and 9); text searched at **PDF indices 4–14** (not a full 30-page independent review). Exact page locations:

PHR explicitly says the actual observational medical data **were not public**.
- PDF index 9–10 (printed pp8–9, image of page 10 read): cites OMS instructions to record amount/duration/physical response of water application and modifications including saline; contrasts SERE training which had consent procedures with CIA detainee applications. Its "Waterboarding 2.0" phrase is PHR's analytical term, NOT a CIA codeword established here.
- PDF indices 11–13 (printed pp10–12): PHR discusses 'susceptibility' assumptions and legal good-faith defense via medical reviews, cites exact CIA OIG / OLC memos; the full original CIA OMS patient measurement series remained missing.
**PHR's conclusion is an argued inference from documents; their acknowledged missing individual data and absence of a formally exposed protocol remain central.** This is more serious and specific than a vague 'test camp' conjecture, but the claim that all detainee monitoring was illegal systematic experimentation is still something to adjudicate against actual definitions/record and competing interpretations.
