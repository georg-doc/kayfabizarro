# R2A · Direct Original-Document Deep Dive · CIA / Congress / FOIA
Date: 2026-10-16 · Research-only checkpoint. Branch `research/kfb-mkultra-monarch-cardway-r1-2026-10-16`, Draft PR #408.
**Scope:** A selected, traceable subset of publicly accessible primary-source records. **Not** a complete audit of the 1,200-record collection, the 48-page IG report, the 2026 hearing video, or the original O'Brien/Phillips or Höfer books.

## Can ChatGPT read original government files?
**YES**, when the material is public and technically accessible. This R2A located actual scanned CIA memoranda, a declassified CIA Inspector-General report and official 2026 congressional **written testimony PDFs**. The retrieval pipeline can: search original catalog, open original scan PDF, inspect targeted pages as **rendered images**, read/search extracted text, compare with archival summaries, record document date, original title, project identifier, OCR/scan discrepancy and exact PDF page. Web searches alone do not constitute original-document audit. Some CIA legacy Reading Room document/PDF links **redirect to the CIA FOIA landing page** on direct opening even when their texts appear in search snippets. Use National Security Archive's hosted **original scans** as a legitimate alternative when public and attributed. Some content in the 1,200-doc collection is licensed behind the ProQuest scholarly collection, though important samples are public.

**Archival gap:** CIA destruction of much of the MKUltra record in 1973; surviving financial documents, direct reports, testimony and private archival collections permit reconstruction but not an exhaustive account. A FOIA search returning no Monarch file is not by itself evidence the alleged project existed or did not exist. Contemporary records' internal assertions of success must be distinguished from independent measurement, just as denials by participants are not comprehensive proof of nonexistence.

## R2A original-source reading matrix (PDF page 0-indexed)
### R2A-01 · ARTICHOKE narco-hypnosis, 14 July 1952
**Archival original:** https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/1952-07-14%20JM%20Box%206%20F13%20Artichoke-ocr.pdf
**Source:** CIA Security Office memorandum to DCI, *Successful Application of Narco-Hypnotic Interrogation (ARTICHOKE)*, 3-page declassified scan. First page **PDF index 0 viewed as original image**; OCR/text of first and following pages accessible. The memorandum claims two interrogations of suspected double agents, under psychiatric-medical cover, employing drugs and hypnosis; the memo *reports* regression and amnesia of the interrogation. Source: scan first page PDF index 0, especially paragraphs 1–2 (a)–(f).
**Finding:** This is direct evidence of CIA-run narco-hypnosis interrogations and of what its officers **reported** as results. It does not experimentally establish reliably induced controllable independent personalities, mass behavioral control, or a Project Monarch chain. Follow-up: compare participants' case files and independent testimony, if available.

### R2A-02 · Subproject 35: university hospital cover, 15 Nov 1954
**Archival original:** https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/1954-11-15-AWD%20Mind%20Control%201953-61%20H-2-ocr.pdf
**Source:** CIA TSS memo *Project MKULTRA, Subproject 35*, number **T.S. 101077**, 13-page scanned original, with attachments.
**Original images inspected:** PDF index **0** and **6**. The opening memorandum requests **$125,000 from CIA plus equivalent matching funds** ($250,000 combined) for a medical research-building cover, explains why the hospital/university should not learn CIA's role; attached planning (PDF index 6, printed annex page -5-) assigns **one sixth** of the new wing for use supporting the Chemical Division and spells out deniability and financial routing. Library contextual identification connects the scheme to Georgetown's Gorman Annex and the **Geschickter Fund for Medical Research**: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/32726-document-11-memorandum-director-central-intelligence-project-mkultra-subproject-35
**Precision:** $125k is the CIA contribution requested in this 1954 memo, **not** the final lifetime price of the building or a proven expenditure on specific subjects. The 1977 Senate hearing elsewhere discusses a **$375,000** hospital building-fund contribution (verify whether one or several stages and what same money is being described). Institutions hosting activity ≠ proof every staff member was aware.
**Finding:** A documented *funding, medical-facility and deniable sponsor mechanism*.

### R2A-03 · TSS list of 17 desired capabilities, 5 May 1955
**Archival original:** https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/1955-05-05%20JM%20Box%206%20F3-ocr.pdf
**Source:** TSS Chemical Division draft, *Materials and Methods Under Research and Development at TSS/CD*, 7-page original.
**Original images inspected:** PDF indices **0 and 1**, items **1–10** and **11–17** respectively. The listed ambitions include inducing irrational thinking; making hypnosis easier; sustaining coercion under interrogation; inducing amnesia, shock/confusion; altering 'personality structure' toward dependency; and surreptitious administration of drugs. First item explicitly envisages discrediting the recipient **in public** (individual reputational sabotage, NOT a demonstration of population-scale Mass Formation). Item 12 speaks of dependence on *another person*, not the creation of separately programmable alters.
**Finding:** This list documents **agency ambitions and research directions**, not 17 techniques proven to work. Its breadth reaches medical, behavioral and reputation-disruption objectives, but this is not sufficient evidence of modern platform continuities.

### R2A-04 · CIA Inspector General: MKUltra/MKDELTA, 26 July 1963
**Archival original:** https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/1963-07-26%20JM%20Box%208%20F2%20MKUltra-IG_Report-ocr.pdf
**Source:** CIA Inspector General John S. Earman, *Report of Inspection of MKULTRA/TSD*, **48 pages**, marking / release line **2019/04/03 C06767515**. **Pages visually verified as scan:** indices **0, 2, 8, 11, 18**; relevant other passages inspected as PDF text, but full 48-page substantive review has **not** been done.
**A. MKDELTA is a genuinely documented adjacent codename.** Cover p.0 and introduction p.2 distinguish MKULTRA (research/development) from **MKDELTA** (policy/procedure for operational employment of biochemicals); the latter predated MKULTRA as notice **DD/P No. 220-1, 20 Oct 1952** (p.2). It is **NOT** evidence of Project Monarch.
**B. Human experimentation & concealed financing:** Scan PDF index 8 describes covert experiments on **unwitting U.S. citizens from 1955** and foundation-mediated grants that hid CIA interest from institutions. PDF index 11, section II, paragraph 10 describes testing on **unwitting subjects in normal life settings**.
**C. Outcomes/limits:** PDF index 18, section III, paragraph 23 reports no effective knockout/truth/aphrodisiac/recruitment pill known as of 1960; by 1963 largely unchanged; it does allow progress with drugs as interrogation support, but describes MKDELTA as risky/low-yield and the predictive value of drugs as limited. This corrects an oversimplified total success/failure story.
**D. Bureaucratic incentives:** Index 8: full support for conventional academic research was arranged through ostensibly legitimate foundation grants. IG criticisms emphasize agency exposure/risk and deficient oversight as much as participant rights. That is historically more specific than an all-purpose claim of 'mind control'.
**Next research test:** targeted page audit of the 1963 report's paragraph 10–18 on safehouses, 1963 follow-up memo / injunction, and any actual operational inventory. Do not claim operational 'programmed killers' on the basis of this report.

## FOIA research workflow for subsequent passes (not a new app)
1. Begin with **original document identifiers/dates**, not a Wikipedia entry: e.g. `MKULTRA Subproject 35 TS 101077`, `2019/04/03 C06767515`, `ARTICHOKE Successful Application 14 July 1952`, `Subproject 68 D. Ewen Cameron`.
2. Look up the exact identifier at the **CIA Reading Room**, **National Security Archive** and congressional archives. Record link resolution and whether item is image scan, OCR, excerpt, catalog description or someone's testimony.
3. Read **selected actual PDF pages as images**, mark page index and original printed pagination separately; compare extracted text only when legible. No page citation without verifying original page.
4. Record evidence class: **REQUEST/PLAN**, **FUNDING/APPROVAL**, **REPORTED ACTION**, **SUBJECT/WITNESS TESTIMONY**, **EVALUATED OUTCOME**, **INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION**, **ARCHIVAL GAP**.
5. For new allegations (especially the West 1956 original report), hunt the **provenance and unredacted original** before accepting a spectacular conclusion. Compare opposite-position evidence on the same exact question.
6. Maintain separate cards for one document's status versus a narrator's dramatization. Use existing KFB Card/Almanac, Project Gateway and Iceberg as future consumers; do not modify them here.
