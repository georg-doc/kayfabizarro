## Design grammar (the **same grammar**, not identical architecture)

Every nominated candidate answers eight authored questions before mesh construction:

1. **Hero object / silhouette:** what dominates at traversal distance? PC, beer glass + cat, restaurant/clown structure etc. Do not bury it under tower clutter, banners, unrelated props or broad scenery.
2. **Meaning as place:** who built/found it, why is it here, what is its *current* use? Is it a kitchen, shrine, archive, surveillance apparatus, curiosity, repair workshop?
3. **History at six beats:** Origin → First use → Settlement → Institution/power → Accretions/scars/reinterpretation → Present-day life. Use the existing world-biography doctrine; exact biographies are proposed until Georg and the owning world accept them.
4. **Built access:** enter/approach, connected walkable stone stairs, bridge, platform, reliable end landing and exits; actual collision/support/test required later. A 2D image does not establish climbability or geometry.
5. **Social layer:** inhabitants who clean, adapt, interpret, sell, maintain, patrol, contest or misuse the artifact. Residents' animation/voice/state remain with existing Resident/ChatterBox/Lean Memory owners. Genuine lived activity before player arrival, not decor NPCs posed as statues.
6. **Diegetic affordance:** readable focus point, interaction invitation and small game action; if it opens a real card viewer, Almanac or analyzer, **the existing tool remains the tool**. Preserve deep links, keyboard/touch/2D fallback and focus/exit/restore.
7. **Meta-narrative lens:** official reading vs lived reading vs another witness/player's reading; a clue, contradictory card or question, not mandatory moral lesson or universal answer. The history can differ between inhabitants without inventing KFB canon.
8. **Source kit + rollback:** real source GLB/asset IDs, rights, exact donor-isolation image, anchor/scale/connectors, deterministic build recipe + original source preserved; negative concept images are not source donors.

### Recommended *visual* hierarchy

`Dominant hero object (60–75% of meaningful visual mass; heuristic only) → actual stairs/key access → a small meaningful service/social platform → limited story props → optional supporting backdrop`. This is a design aim, **not a technical measured KFB constraint**. For a Meshy image: one isolated isometric three-quarter scene, neutral studio background, no unrelated surrounding jungle/island, no captions, no fake brand. New kits should use no more than ~5–6 major buildable form families, though actual meshes may exceed that; preserve necessary gameplay contacts.

Architecture is not interchangeable: retro PC = keyboard-access steps; surveillance PC = dark angular control enclosure; jungle PC = old stone stair to keyboard; restaurant = real dining/serving/circulation plan, cat-in-glass = actual readable glass silhouette and support. Avoid applying a PC shrine to every landmark.

## Named concepts: production candidates (not new canon)

### P1. **Computer-oracle family / mythic terminal monuments** · core family

3–4 initially imagined stockwerk-sized computers/CRTs, individually self-contained:
- **Retro CRT / archival oracle:** recognisable large beige CRT with real keyboard + short walkable podium; low maintenance decks, strips of archaeology, readable screen. Could stage a Deck Browser/Almanac/Analyzer from the existing owner.
- **Black surveillance / dystopian mainframe:** Georg rejected repeated CRT-stair architecture and four watchtowers; selected the stronger reference of a **clearly readable black, rectangular central computer** with minimal ring/camera control routes and occult/surveillance sentiment. Not a giant red-eyed sphere or four towers; no projected eye by default. Existing Dystopia setting is a possible receiving context, **not a location decision**.
- **Jungle / time-ruined tribal terminal:** bright, colorful stop-motion clay, **isolated computer**, aged sandstone, roots, exuberant leafy clay volumes, primitive repairs and pots; **an old visibly continuous stone staircase leads to the keyboard**. No banners, no decorative heraldry, no eye faces, no ocean/distant canyon/jungle panorama, no extra tower. Georg reports the resulting Meshy model is generated. This is **a user-reported model**, no model bytes/GLB URL/version and no game proof in GitHub.
- **DIY / sci-fi / multimonitor archive** (fourth *candidate*, not approved look): alternative epistemic temperament, distinct silhouette and structure. Do not merge into one giant multi-PC scene without a new brief.

A computer can *later* have an optional EyeRig/speaker avatar, or its screen can remain completely non-anthropomorphic. **Current jungle and dark concept directions explicitly choose NO EYES on the device geometry.** Any future face/rig on the screen is a separately approved presentation layer using existing EyeRig/ChatterBox, not a generator-default. Build core source first.

**Diegetic interaction beats (proposal):**
`notice from afar → approach through meaningful stairs/space → resident reaction → select/insert a source-backed Card → existing Analyzer/Deck Viewer focuses → optional one pattern + a tension + another source + an open question → leave/return to world without losing position.`
'42' is a satirical/oracular **design reference**, **not** a newly authorized fixed 42-column scoring system or a supposedly solved metaphysical answer. The player closes gaps/reads conflicts. Support refusal/ignore/back away.

### P2. **Cat in a beer glass** · author seed

Georg reasserts **Katze im Bierglas** as an established discussion motif for surreal, oversized diegetic *location/attraction*. Preserve the literal cat **inside a visibly legible beer-glass form** as the central silhouette; source scale, character identity, the glass' contents, agency, location, gameplay and why/how it exists are **NOT yet recovered as binding canon**. Do not invent a named cat, alcohol mechanic, suffering, bartender, mini-game or lore. Candidate spatial readings to *investigate*, not pick silently: oversized glass as climbed/observed translucent artifact; small service deck from which one can view the cat; actor-led event using an existing cat donor if verified. It must behave as a strange encounter and read from afar, not become a generic pub set.

### P3. **Evil clown restaurant** · author seed, distinct from Golden Journey Clown

Georg reasserts **böser Clown für das Restaurant** as a candidate inhabited 3D building/POI. Existing Town Golden Journey has a **juggling Clown at the market**, which does **NOT** prove that resident and the 'evil restaurant clown' are one character. Do not alter the active Resident's personality, retroactively change canon or assume a named restaurant without a Georg decision. A food-serving/dining location needs coherent front threshold, seating, working kitchen or service zone, sightlines and a convincing visible clown presence. The 'evil' nature may be conveyed through design/social encounter; no invented violent behavior or forced combat. Reuse KayKit Restaurant Bits only if their exact current source, license and geometry are confirmed; earlier pack coverage is mixed.

### P4. Expandable 3D-story POI pattern

Later examples need one actual hero artifact and a reason for its built context (e.g., treasure vault, improvised workshop, monument, Temple/Oracle). Do **not** pad a road map with unsupported canon or placeholder institutions. The prototype is a **pattern** with reusable staging and authored local biography, **not** automatic generation of hundreds of elaborate contentless attractions.
