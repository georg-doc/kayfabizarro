## Scope
Georg requests an **English-language or historical English-translated world-poetry corpus** spanning ancient Mediterranean and Asian classics, biblical/apocalyptic texts, esoterica, Latin American indigenous narrative, African oral material, modernist/avant-garde experiment, occult visionary poetry and conceptual references from pop/postmodern lyrics. Emotional, intellectual, provocative, genuinely mysterious and aesthetically surprising works have precedence over "inspirational quote" boilerplate.

**Artistic override:** These are autonomous artistic source materials for the HyperNorm EXPERIENCE plane. A poem can stand alone as film, voice, text-sculpture, a moment of silence or a playful sonic event. No mandatory FrizzleQuestion, Card mapping, data lecture, truth-verdict, quest or moral takeaway. CURATOR editorial fields and existing Quote Pool stay intact.

## What the seed actually contains
- File: `POETIC_OCCULT_ARCHIVE_SEED_R1.json`, 89 unique IDs, 9 research regions/families plus multiple subtraditions, each with title, creator/tradition, mood tags, rights flag and optional individually located historical edition URL.
- **38 records** point to **historical edition/catalog URLs** (some records share an edition); these are not 38 rights-cleared full poems, nor 38 poem-specific verified excerpts.
- No full text, audio sample, popular-song lyric, current translation, confidential material or KFB Card mapping is imported. Rights remain `publicUseApproved:false` for every entry.
- Works include poetic dialogue, ritual, philosophical argument and prose; those are marked as **poetic-adjacent**, not misrepresented as poems.
- Historic editions are often filtered through colonial translation and editorial assumptions. For K'iche' Popol Vuh, Lewis Spence 1908 is an English **retelling/study**, not the definitive full translation. `ǀXam` pieces are recorded oral narratives; name narrators/collectors on the verified individual pages. Rumi and Chinese/Japanese poem titles without pinned English translations are discovery leads only.

## Rights and provenance: five operational bins
`H` HISTORICAL_ENGLISH_EDITION_IDENTIFIED — a named English original or historically dated translation found; per-item public clearance still pending; inspect translator/date/edition/territory (US and EU/Germany) before copying passages.
`T` TRANSLATION_REQUIRED — ancient/language-original work exists; **no English text version selected**. Do not substitute modern celebrated translations.
`E` EXACT_EDITION_REQUIRED — source work known, but actual publication/variant or text extraction still unpinned.
`U` US_ONLY_OR_JURISDICTION_SENSITIVE — US public-domain edition may be viewable, but the English translation/author may remain protected in Germany/EU (e.g. Arthur Waley d.1966; Cyril Scott d.1970; T. S. Eliot d.1965; Ezra Pound d.1972). No cross-border text republication.
`R` MODERN_REFERENCE_ONLY — Bowie, Dylan, Radiohead, Laurie Anderson, Patti Smith, Kate Bush, Gil Scott-Heron, Brion Gysin, Carson, Acker, etc. **metadata/critical aesthetic reference only**; zero song lyrics, text sampling, recordings, album art or modern translation bundled.

A public-safe ingestion must clear separately: original `workRights`, `translationRights`, `editionRights`, `performance/recordingRights`, `imageRights`, distribution territories, provenance page and editorial approval. A historic website's availability is not a right to reproduce a 2026 modern edition. For ancient oral traditions, cultural provenance/custodianship and original narrators remain visible. The KJV has distinctive UK Crown-publication issues: do not simply assume the KJV is globally unrestricted. Project Gutenberg's US flags do not resolve German/EU distribution.

## HyperNorm Experience treatment proposals (NOT engine implementations)
1. **Autonomous poem-film:** 20–60 seconds of curated H13 typography, images, silence and tempo around one exact cleared edition; no forced narrator/explanation.
2. **Polyphonic descent:** Plato / Job / Enoch as optional multi-voice branching chamber; visitors can walk away at any time.
3. **Phonetic architecture:** Hugo Ball / Kurt Schwitters as formal inspiration. For uncleared texts, synthesize *original* independently authored syllables rather than copying protected performance or translations.
4. **Public-domain cut-up:** seed deterministic collage only from separately approved historic passages; store stable source offsets, preserve editorial author attributions, show the result is a remix (never misattribute new cut-ups as ancient original verses).
5. **Optical palindrome:** Blake contraries and Yin/Yang as shared noncausal motifs; historical/cultural origins remain distinct, not assertions of contact/influence.
6. **Star-chamber / night orchard:** ǀXam source poetry with contextual narrator credit; no synthetic "African tribal chant" clichés or scraped current recordings.
7. **Translational tension:** original source language and historic English adaptation can be juxtaposed if both cleared; translation differences become part of the artwork rather than erased.
8. **Modern reference wall:** modern lyric titles/song metadata link out to authorized publishers/performances later; no lyrics imported into project or quoted by text engine.

All future visuals must source-isolate the real H13 asset before adopting it, as PR #450 R3 requires. No design donor accepted by this research alone.
