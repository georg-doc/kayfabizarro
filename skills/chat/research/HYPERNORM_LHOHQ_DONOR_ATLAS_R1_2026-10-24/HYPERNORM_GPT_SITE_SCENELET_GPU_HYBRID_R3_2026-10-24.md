## 2. An authored standalone first scenelet (proposed)
Working experiential premise: **Threshold Observatory / Archive of Unfinished Explanations**. The title is exploratory; do not invent replacement KFB branding or lore. Art direction: irreverent clay-surreal architecture with intentional dark/quiet image gaps; media combines Dada montage, symbolic structures, vintage illustrated public-domain art, H13 abstract kaleidoscopes, clipped research-document quotations and tactile cartoon portals.

**Arrival:** A small authored physically walkable 3D pocket with one striking central moving collage aperture, recognizable relief/space, one visible way back; no generic developer UI visible over art. Source-visible H13 film motifs; camera/lighting/geometry already belonging to a single host. Deliberately staged ambient contrasts: motion → readable image → quiet lull → reappearing motif.

**Three discoverable interactions** (user can ignore any):
1. **Image/CARD**: a fragment or graphic opens a **verified existing Card** by actual `packId+cardNumber` and optional related source; zoom/orbit/back. Same card can change H13 composition or unlock a link to one of its real thematic neighbors, not duplicate Card truth.
2. **Source/ANNOTATION**: visual symbol reveals archive/date/author/original object, source metadata and typed links to original publication, work, FOIA, museum catalog or other verified document; the observer can go deeper with "Do you want to know more?". Source is not a forced full-screen compliance overlay.
3. **Unexplained door / EASTER EGG**: a physically accessible door, image sliver, click-through glyph or silent odd scene transition to another small authored chamber, alternate camera/meaning layer, or one existing Mindspace scene recipe. Return path exact, no maze with mandatory secrets.

**Non-verbal mode:** a player can watch and walk for 10 min without having to click, read or solve anything. Pure visual cinema, music and the art direction should already justify being there. No compulsory quote, explanation, breathing guide, counselor, or reward.

**Voluntary personal closure:** leave the interpretive graph open; let the juxtaposition of a real document and a satirical scene prompt the reader's own thought. No game scoring a "true meaning", no fake solved conspiracies; meaningful surprise over generic mystery tropes.

## 3. Three cooperating rendering/physics media are NOT equivalent
1. **H13 / Canvas/film compositor** = typographic cinema, procedural collage, original art, live beat-driven movement and source overlays on scene surfaces. Existing visual owner remains authoritative; outputs textures/media shot plans to current host, no second rAF or WebGLRenderer.
2. **3D Gaussian Splat scenic layer** = view-dependent captured/curated three-dimensional backdrop, garden/corridor/observatory depth, optionally window to spatial pocket. **Visual representation**; not collision mesh, portal router, mutable relief or physics engine. Use a rights-pinned renderer compatible with existing Three/WebGL host if actual isolate shows it works. Otherwise pre-render or use mesh/2.5D.
3. **WebGL GPGPU particles (WS0 q197 as candidate)** = dynamic destructible/relaxing relief, particles returning to authored anchors, exploded/rippled card glyph/symbol, physically interactive slow morphing terrain. The reported 65,536 simulated spheres are **NOT automatically 65,536 learned 3D Gaussian radiance splats**. Physics positions/velocities may be stored in GPU float textures / ping-pong (cf. three.js GPUComputationRenderer); graphical output could be point sprites/instances/mesh/splats **only after checking actual q197 code**. Keep physics proxies and interaction ownership explicit; no fake Gaussian photogrammetry claim.

**Good first combined effect:** H13 frame depicts original KFB source art, its alpha/depth translated into a procedural *card-relief* only if q197's real implementation supports it. A player touches the relief, its material displaces and reforms, and the movement makes a compositionally meaningful reveal. Behind it sits a pinned shallow Splat/mesh scenic vista. No need to start all three expensive systems at once: validate film and interaction first, then introduce one source-proven q197 effect and optional Splat.

**GPU/cohabitation guard:** a WebGL GPU sim may share the WebGLRenderer, but independently mounted splat viewers commonly come with their own renderers/contexts; adapter/texture compatibility must be shown, not assumed. Budget alpha overdraw, sort/transparency, GPU textures, framebuffer passes, context loss and retained resources. The q197 browser-in-background 30 fps cannot certify target FPS. Choose active simulation counts and pixel budgets only from a measured visible-browser target-device profile; **65,536 is a source-report count, not mandatory game budget**.

## 4. Interactivity architecture and knowledge graph
World host single camera/input/physics/nav owner; curated page/content registry external. Hit test 3D mesh/raycast → UV to **stable authored hotspot surface coordinates** → typed action `CARD_REF | SOURCE_REF | AUTHOR_WORK | OPEN_ARCHIVE | MOTIF_BRANCH | VISUAL_MUTATION | MINDSPACE_ENTRY | RETURN`. Do not execute original third-party HTML, deep-link personal-data list, or infer clickable regions from image pixels alone.

User interactions:
- **PULL DON'T GATE:** inviting images and objects, nonblocking side routes, optional annotation/source peek; source/Back always reachable. Existing content is discoverable, not prerequisite to continue through the room.
- **Visible and hidden media layers:** LHOHQ donor has a measured 21,114-character 311-line ALT payload on an image (one sampled page). In KFB proper concise image ALT for accessibility, separate `longDescription`, optional secret notes and `sourceRef`. The giant ALT itself is not copied or mistaken for proven history.
- **Curated Warburg neighbors:** archive ↔ Card ↔ artist ↔ propaganda parody ↔ dream image via typed `DOCUMENTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, `WARBURG_NEIGHBOR`, `FICTIONAL_ASSOCIATION`, not causal conflation. Existing KFB Research Atlas holds source claims; Experience just references.
- **Record & replay:** deterministic source IDs + authored scene recipe + seed + saved scene-local discoveries (session only for first demo). A run remains surprising because authored permutations/optional links differ across seeds; not indiscriminate everything-shuffles.
- **Lulls:** sound/visual near-silence, sparse corridors, deliberate pauses are as compositional as audiovisual bombardment; not necessarily wellness protocol or mandatory guided meditation.
- **Input/accessibility:** one simple navigation mode with mouse/keyboard, visible focus/return, usable touch option later; opt-in sound, pause, intensity/reduced-motion variation and reliable exit. Flicker may remain an intentional art direction, not filtered out as a generic ban.
- **Source policy:** Commons media individually license tagged, not all public domain; Wikipedia prose is attribution/SA-sensitive; LoC advisories per item; FOIA release does not automatically clear all photographs/books/third-party excerpts. Jacques Vallée works remain author-sourced bibliographic prompts/approved short excerpts until item-level rights are verified. Relevant real official source lead NARA UAP collection `https://www.archives.gov/uap`; source link opening can preserve access without bundling an unlicensed PDF.
