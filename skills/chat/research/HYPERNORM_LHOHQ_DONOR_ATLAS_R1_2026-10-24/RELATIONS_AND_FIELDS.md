## C. Fields of sense (Gabriel-inspired design heuristic; NOT complete ontology)

### Object: `lhohq.info` as a publicly visible creative web object
1. **Net-art field** — existence criterion: an accessible authored/interlinked web artwork or creative practice (L-S02, L-S05, L-S06). Form and interaction can themselves be studied as art; no asserted objective intent beyond source.
2. **Internet-folklore field** — criterion: dated discourse, interpretation and archival reproduction about the URL (L-S11, L-S13, L-S15). The rumors and ARG readings are observable social reception, not proof of mystery.
3. **Information-evidence field** — criterion: identifiable documents with authors/editions and checkable propositions. L-S02 is only evidence of a hosted claim, while primary government documents must be retrieved separately.
4. **Technical-web field** — criterion: observed responses, DOM/HTML, route behavior, scripts, headers and recorded screenshots. Current root/algorithm/live-render data inadequate; do not substitute narrative speculation.
5. **KFB-fiction field** — criterion: a deliberate adaptation in existing KFB lore/MediaSurface, clearly labelled **INSPIRES_KFB / PROPOSAL**. No claim of original-source truth.

### Object: `/container/index.html` as a particular text
- **Archival-text field**: it is a directly read website statement.
- **History-of-intelligence field**: its listed project descriptions require archival cross-check, cannot independently prove historical programs.
- **Graphic-design field**: dense years/acronyms create authority signaling irrespective of truth.
- **Media-literacy field**: useful as an example of the difference between citation and evidence.
- **Fictional-story field**: source for original KFB parody, *not* license to copy its assertions or typography.

## Research vs art boundary
**Documentary graph may say**: “LHOHQ posted this,” “this blog linked it,” “forum X discussed it.” It may **not** say: “the CIA did the techniques LHOHQ alleges” or “LHOHQ is an intelligence operation” without independent historical evidence. **Concept graph may say**: “the aesthetics recall early net art,” “this is an analogy to propaganda,” “this is a useful Warburg pair,” while preserving `causality:false`. A provocative relationship is neither a false statement nor a verified one until its proposition and evidence are specified.
