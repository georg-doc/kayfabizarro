## Taxonomy of donor mechanisms (not a style-copy mandate)
1. **Unstable document classification:** documents, staged authority, fragmentary dossiers and absurd imagery share a presentation field; the user must actively determine provenance.
2. **Enormous textual density against sudden sparse images:** alternation of screen-filling directories/catalogue-like blocks and near-empty single-picture portals.
3. **Disorienting but consequential hyperlinks:** apparent thematic links mutate context; re-encounter of an earlier node changes interpretation. Actual infinite/procedural generation still unverified.
4. **Genre collision:** conspiracy archive, web1 genealogy, devotional propaganda, mock-research, pop culture, advertising and experimental film collide without a clean editorial partition.
5. **Psychological interface:** curiosity → recognition → overinterpretation → doubt → retrospective reading. The interface influences the reader's interpretation as much as the text.
6. **Claims presented with quasi-documentary precision:** dates, abbreviations and agency labels can create an evidentiary aura even without independently supporting originals. Treat as *an affordance to critique*, not content to recite.
7. **Deliberate cognitive friction:** typography contrast, competing scales, long scroll, unexpected montage, abrupt link pivots and sensory escalation. Design intensity should be art-directed rather than purely random.
8. **Networked site identity:** related domains, references, cinema and forum reactions generate a wider mediated object beyond one homepage.
9. **Temporal ambiguity:** old material recurs in a 2026 surface; provenance/version metadata and 'when did this appear?' become part of the experience.
10. **False-familiarity engine:** archive/database conventions suggest public institutional credibility, while content undermines genre stability. Distinguish meaningful uncanny transitions from empty 'glitch' décor.

## Design interpretation
The strongest inspiration for KFB is **epistemic montage with navigable consequences**, not copying flashing GIFs, a 'hacker terminal', static CRT overlay, generic redacted files or an unbounded web crawler. Pair directed semiotic sequences with inspectable source seams; keep intentional wrongness legible and distinguish creative fiction from actual archival assertions.
