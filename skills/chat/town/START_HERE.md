Use this space for meta-narrative and world-design work: places, NPCs, Triplet/closure grammar, embodied tools, memory, module relationships and visitor experience.

Do not turn this into a second technical lead or implementation repo. Travel, Combat, ToolBox, Museum/Iceberg donors and other projects keep their own owners and SSOTs.

## Hard boundaries

- Town is currently a hub concept, not a built runtime.
- `LIVING_KFB_TOWN.md` owns Town-specific decisions.
- Reference files beside it preserve derivation and alternatives; they do not become Town decisions merely by being present.
- Keep source namespaces intact: `L-nn` remains a Frankensteining-Lab reference; new Town decisions use the Town decision log only after Georg adopts them.
- The Meta Compendium routes across modules but is not canon by itself.
- Old version labels, asset counts and project statuses in meta documents are snapshots; verify current state in the named project/tool SSOT before acting.
- ChatterBox/Journey/Overworld are donors for encounter grammar and memory. Do not build a second dialogue architecture by default.
- FrankenStein/ToolBox owns reusable actor/rig/talk production. Town consumes actors; it does not fork them.
- Houses/portals may point to independent instances. Entering a house does not transfer runtime ownership to Town.
