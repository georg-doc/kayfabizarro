## Core image, identity and silhouette
**The Akashic Library** = endlessly tall, half-open cartoon archive combining the imaginative Akashic Records, Alexandria, Vatican historical archives and the **Library of Unwritten Books**. The juxtaposition is mythopoetic satire, not a claim that any mystical archive literally knows all history.
- Fractured, unmistakably **vertical cliff** with shelves stepped into rock, L-shaped open reading chamber, suspended book walks, ladders and scattered freestanding shelves. One hero reading desk and staged line of sight to Lorekeeper; NOT a generic green island or a fully enclosed building.
- Room vignettes: Great Reading Chamber, Stacked Shelf Passages, Philosophers' Alcove, FOIA/Church Committee archive drawers, Occult/Forbidden section, and Unwritten Books. Atmospheric differences and asymmetrical support must remain visible in 3D; different shelves should not become cloned library wallpaper.
- Mini **cartoon bookworms** read, misfile, dispute and carry scraps. Delightful signature NPC concept; no actual KFB bookworm mesh/rig yet verified — no claim of implemented asset.
- Warburg's good-neighbor associations, Gabriel's different Sinnfelder, McCloud's Closure and `Show It → Spin It → Sell It`: a book can stage competing knowledge claims and surprising neighboring Card viewpoints. **No final truth terminal.**

## Growing content and Card relationship
A **heuristically growing corpus** keyed to existing Deck/Card provenance, not a new Library mega-database: literature (Goethe, Dostoevsky), philosophy (Plato, Hegel, Marx), psychology (Freud, Jung), scientific and medical literature, mythology/religion/occultism, world history/geography, financial/geopolitical debate (Quigley, Brzezinski), archival investigations, FOIA and Church Committee source documents, banned/cancelled works and wholly fictional unwritten codices.
Each candidate book may refer to a genuine work ID, creator, publication year, provenance/rights, optional actual source URL, short **original** KFB flap copy and relevant existing Deck/Card refs. Don't invent citations, reproduce unlicensed text or imply disputed Card claims are documented facts. Catalog is NOT a credibility score; a public-domain original, contemporary copyrighted work, fictional book, leaked document and official government record have different provenance. For MED facts, existing medical-scientific QA applies.

## KISS interaction and future sorting
1. Existing prop-overlap / pickup / interaction owner handles an actual Book prop; player can inspect its title, a satirical back-cover text and Card relationships. Optional authentic `BINGO / BOGGLE` interaction follows current Social Calls; no invented automatic game rule.
2. The Library is a *spatial Fractal Almanac*: a found book links to known Card refs and remembered encounters; no parallel Card store, mandatory tutorial or universal lore gate.
3. Later voluntary shelf-placement microgame: place several found books among **multiple plausible neighboring meanings** (e.g. Hegel beside Marx, but perhaps also beside an archival report); reward points or **Fluff** only via approved existing economy owner. Classification should provoke thought, not require the player to endorse an ideology.
4. Funny bookworms can rearrange shelves; the "forbidden" ledger is empty, an unpublished book has overdue fines, and the whole ontology changes when a shelf collapses.
