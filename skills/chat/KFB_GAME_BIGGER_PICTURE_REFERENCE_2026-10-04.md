## Product intent

The digital KFB game is a **chill & fun transmedia adaptation** of the comics, card decks and Cut&Play practice.

It should let a broad audience:
- enter the KFB worlds without first learning the tabletop rules;
- encounter the comics/decks as places, Residents, situations, media and collectible Cards;
- find new or personal meanings by moving, choosing, listening, combining, asking and returning;
- experience KFB as a living claymation-cartoon toy world rather than a didactic rules viewer;
- move back and forth between game, Cards, comics, music, Residents, Almanac and later other media.

The goal is not to digitize the analog rules literally.
The goal is to preserve the generative KFB idea: **Cards and worlds invite behavior, interpretation and recombination.**

## Fractal storytelling reference

Cut&Play is used as a design grammar across scales:

`Deck → world/biome → Card → POI/encounter → Resident worldview → Triplet/dialogue beat → Billboard/media → Almanac memory → recombination`.

The same semantic source may therefore appear:
- as a Card image;
- as world color/light/architecture;
- as an NPC bias;
- as a route or encounter;
- as a HyperNormalisation loop;
- as a remembered provenance entry;
- later as a player-created/recombined scene or story.

This is a **fractal translation**, not one-to-one literal illustration.

## Living-toy implementation metaphor

The 3D target is:
**KFB claymation cartoon open world as a living toy.**

Technical and visual metaphors should reinforce this:
- islands/worlds feel assembled, inhabited, repairable and revisitable;
- tools become places/actions where useful;
- Cards become world objects, media, quests and memories;
- Residents live before the player arrives;
- authored KayKit/KFB assets remain recognisable signature toys;
- procedural systems build the ordinary connective tissue rather than replacing source identity;
- history remains visible as layers, scars, repairs and reused structures.

## Analog canon vs digital game

Analog/public rules and KFB meta-narration are **high-value origin references**, not shackles on digital gameplay.

Therefore:
- preserve names, source provenance and conceptual distinctions where they matter;
- do not silently claim a digital adaptation rule is an analog canonical rule;
- but do not block a better chill/fun interaction merely because the tabletop version works differently;
- digital meanings of KayfaBINGO / BONGO / BOGGLE / BLÖDSINN!, Cards, Quest beats, inventory, movement, rewards or progression may be explored experimentally when clearly scoped to the game;
- gameplay tests may discover a stronger digital interpretation;
- discussion remains outcome-open.

Source truth matters.
**Canon policing must not replace playtesting.**

## Global interaction principle · PULL, DON'T GATE

For the game as a whole:

**Pull, don't gate.**

Prefer:
- visible curiosity;
- character invitation;
- useful rewards;
- funny social pressure;
- landmarks;
- music;
- Cards;
- optional questions;
- route affordances;
- return value;
- consequences that create another choice.

Avoid using as default:
- invisible walls;
- arbitrary locks;
- mandatory tutorial text;
- forced moral choices;
- one correct route;
- mechanics that exist only to stop the player;
- progression requirements that merely delay exploration.

A Golden Journey may strongly suggest a path.
It must not make the open world fake.

If a reward needs a state, gate the **reward/handoff**, not the player's ability to explore the world wherever practical.

## Digital adaptation heuristics

1. **Invitation before explanation.**
   Let the player see/do/meet something before explaining it.

2. **Meaning after encounter.**
   Almanac and Lorekeeper can deepen meaning after a Card or world has been experienced.

3. **Multiple readings stay alive.**
   KFB works best when official story, absurdity, critique and player interpretation can coexist.

4. **NPCs embody viewpoints, not lesson objectives.**
   Deck knowledge biases Residents; no Resident is the omniscient authorial answer.

5. **Cards are prompts/evidence, not numeric loot by default.**
   Digital uses may add mechanics, but the Card's identity/provenance stays stable.

6. **Chill is a valid play state.**
   Walking, driving, listening, watching a performance, reading a Card, arranging a scene or simply inhabiting a biome are legitimate gameplay.

7. **Return matters.**
   Worlds, Residents and Almanac should remember enough that coming back feels different without requiring a giant simulation.

8. **Satire is participatory.**
   The player should be allowed to accept, reject, misunderstand, remix or ignore an offered framing.

## MVP consequence

The current Golden Journey is the first integrated example of this philosophy:
- it pulls the player through Clown → Card → Driver/taxi → Dystopia performance → Utopia CEO → Protopia Farmers → Lorekeeper;
- every island remains explorable early;
- Cards and dialogue handoffs wait for relevant state;
- the route is reference/acceptance, not coercion;
- the Fractal Almanac records what the player actually encountered.

This reference should be read before major KFB game/world/dialogue/progression decisions.
It does not create a new runtime, rules engine, canon owner or content database.

### R1.2 addendum · 2026-10-15 · Player-as-audience / Cosmic Theatre Covenant

**GEORG GLOBAL CREATIVE INTENT — no new WS1 runtime task or WS0 V-number.** Full detail in the active WS0 register's `replies/WS0_GLOBAL_INTENT_ROLE_CONSCIOUS_RESIDENTS_R1_2026-10-15.md` §R1.2 (appended to R1/R1.1).

The player/reader is a **constitutive participant in Kayfabe's theatrical universe**. Many Residents may believe or know, within their fictional cosmology, that their performed existence is meaningful insofar as players enjoy, inhabit, interact with and revisit their universe. Their cosmic calling is to make the play rich, strange, immersive and worth returning to. This is a storytelling paradox **not a literal app-lifecycle truth**, nor a reason to make a player feel NPCs suffer when offline. Preserve inhabited/offscreen Resident life and actual Lean Memory.

Residents may want to befriend or entertain the player, **but this does not require forced friendliness, servility, flattery or unanimity**. A committed antagonist may be a better fellow performer than a falsely cozy friend. Relationships are specific, earned and can include distrust, refusal, hostility and real consequences. The simulation aims for **Universe in a Nutshell**, including dissent, conflicting values, tragicomedy, sly visual absurdity and player-led critical thinking. A courier/card mission can expose rival worldviews without obliging the player to endorse them.

Historical craft sources complement each other rather than forming one school: **Buster Keaton** uses exact physical gag geometry, camera and deadpan timing; **Charlie Chaplin** joins pantomime, hierarchy critique, vulnerability and pathos; **Brecht** contributes critical spectatorship and revealed artifice; distinct Greek dramatic traditions contribute masks, chorus, tragedy and comedy. Existing KFB Pop-up/Clay/Meshy/Card/Billboard assets and single WS1 host remain the receiving production surface. Neither a new theatre runtime nor an engagement/retention engine is implied.

**Player's freedom** to act, watch, disagree, laugh, ignore and stop is part of the pact. `PULL, DON'T GATE` remains mandatory. Next WS0 decision remains `WS0_LIGHTING_AND_METATHEATRE_SLICE_ASSIGNMENT`; no additional blocker.

### R1.4 Georg Global Intent · 2026-10-15 · The right to lament / stoic-comic Kayfabe
**Additional nuance, not a reversal of R1.3.** Role-affirming Residents need not always feel happy. They may lament, curse, complain, experience boredom, resentment, exhaustion or an authentically felt failure in one scene, then later recognize their own stubborn pattern, offer a brief specific self-deprecating observation or gesture, and freely embrace the next iteration of their role. **An authentic complaint and a later joyful vocation can both be true.** An inconvenient consequence, another character's pain, a resident's ability to refuse or real biography cannot be explained away as “just a joke”.

**Precise Stoic bridge:** Epictetus, `Enchiridion` §17 explicitly likens life to an actor whose part may be assigned but whose quality of playing it is theirs; §5 distinguishes circumstances from judgments. See https://epictetus-enchiridion.org/en/chapters/enchiridion/17/ and https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epictetus/ . KFB adds theatrical self-reflection, playful complaining, deliberately absurd role-loops and possibly a brief authored fourth-wall glimpse. This is **not** forced cheerfulness, passive acceptance of harm, guaranteed free selection of everyone's cosmic role or the claim that actual Stoic philosophers mandated slapstick self-deprecation.

**Storytelling application, not runtime schema:** resident acts sincerely → meets genuine failure and complains → changed scene/Card/relationship offers a new interpretation → resident may laugh at themself, resume, rest, resist or change course. Timing, bodily performance and a small specific line can carry this better than an all-NPC therapy speech. The player can watch, join, leave, disagree and interpret, preserving `PULL, DON'T GATE` and R1.3's intrinsic resident life. ### R1.5 creative authoring tool · 2026-10-15 · Self-exposing irony / reader-owned Closure
**Georg additionally chooses Homer Simpson as a *comic method donor*, not a persona or dialogue source to transplant into KFB.** Two distinct Simpsons examples were traced: Homer’s 2007 episode `Stop, or My Dog Will Shoot!` complaint (“How come things that happen to stupid people keep happening to me?”), and “It's funny 'cause it's true,” spoken in different Simpsons scenes including Fat Tony and Homer. The first is an **unintended self-diagnosis**: the character includes the explanation in their grievance but misses the inference; the second expresses **recognition** of a familiar behavioral/social pattern beneath exaggeration. Both are reference only, never canonical KFB lines, permission to copy proprietary art or proof a statement is factually accurate.

**In KFB, the audience/player supplies the comic inference.** Resident-specific complaint or misattributed blame + a revealing visual act/detail + an unforced missed or partially glimpsed conclusion = an opportunity for Scott-McCloud-style Closure without a narrator explaining the joke, a universal “stupid NPC” label, moralizing correction or therapeutic exposition. The Resident can be self-aware in one situation and sincerely blind to an aspect of their own loop in another; the contrast need not be resolved. Physical prop comedy, expression, silence and staged comic-frame changes are equally valid.

This complements **R1.4 authentic lament** (grief, irritation, complaint can be real), **R1.3 joyful autonomous role-affirmation** (characters don't exist at player's mercy) and the earlier cosmological/metatheatrical narrative. Full source/provenance and guardrails:

### R1.6 addendum · 2026-10-15 · Epistemic Kayfabe and voluntary inquiry

**Georg's creative direction:** Residents may theatrically defend inventive conspiracy/ideological interpretations, delight in the possibility of further twists and occasionally expose their own weak inferences, source-selection biases and cognitive dissonance with deadpan or self-deprecating humour. A player can ask if the character actually believes it, seek original documents, encounter a contrary Resident or freely leave. **Closure and assessment belong to the player**; no compulsory historical lesson, political conversion, “doubt-seeding” manipulation or ideological profiling.

**Necessary distinction:** conflicting human narratives are equally available for **artistic inquiry** but **not equally substantiated**. Keep documented actions separate from documented but unexecuted proposals, disputed intentions, unverified hidden-motive theories, satire and refuted claims. This is especially important for `Operation Northwoods` (1962 proposal, not proof of execution), `Gulf of Tonkin` (false second-attack narrative in 1964 NSA historical review), `USS Liberty` (1967 documented attack; disputes about intent), `JFK` (different historical investigative assessments; a specific UFO-files, Mossad or other motive allegation is NOT established by simply pointing to those real events). Source-verification references and the narrative encounter method are recorded in the owning WS0 R1.6 chapter:
`tools/KFB-ToolBox/_inbox/KFB_HUB/30_DECISIONS/replies/WS0_GLOBAL_INTENT_ROLE_CONSCIOUS_RESIDENTS_R1_2026-10-15.md`.

**Comic mechanism:** sincere claim → attractive pattern or evidence jump → optional real question → self-revealing contradiction or source lookup → reader-owned inference/continued play. The funny Resident can revise, keep role-playing or suspend judgment without a universal wink or automatically validating the original claim. This complements R1.3 life joy, R1.4 authentic complaint and R1.5 Homer-like near-self-diagnosis.

**Production and player freedom:** preserve real Card/Deck provenance and WS0's editorial dialogue control, existing ChatterBox and current WS1 R3F stage, without creating a second narrative authority, political-belief database, source ranking HUD, new V-number or MVP feature gate. `PULL, DON'T GATE` remains the governing design principle.
