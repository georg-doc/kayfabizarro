## 4. Spatial & social staging — the reason each object is there
### A. Protopia · Fluff Maker Space (inside current Four-Island visual study)
**Spatial image:** open patchwork work yard with repairs across a lived path, communal table, partially built structure, salvaged crates/block elements; working tools differ in orientation/scale, not an indiscriminate upright tool-forest. Repairs and unfinished edges communicate the ecology.
**Causal chain:** damaged rail/bridge/cart → Resident brings salvaged plank/metal → neighbor lends saw/tongs/wrench → another resident fits repaired piece → route/market reopens → mutually recognized result. The actual object and action/animation remain candidate, not implemented.
**Contrast with current Utopia:** stage same type of original workbench/box materials in Utopia as flawlessly aligned packaging showroom under Robot A/B and Monstrosity, with backstage repair/waste; in Protopia irregular purposeful work with peers and communal access. **Source reuse**, DIFFERENT social organization; avoid communist/industrial costume cliches or blanket party symbolism.
**Optional culture:** map/blueprint, expedition kit and hand tools mix freely; personal inventions, improvised signs and repair receipts become in-world traces (future existing memory/quest/Card owners, not a new crafting database).

### B. Tiny Treats · Bakery & Sweets/Candy (POST-MVP candidate)
**Spatial image:** one bakery cutaway/front counter + warm delivery route, connected outdoor sweet-display plaza and edible-looking sculptural terrain only if sourced or separately approved later.
**Causal chain:** delivery to flour/dough workbench → bread oven and mixer → pastry/display shelf → customer chooses/buys/shares → bench/park encounter. Physical oven/mixer motions are future animation/interaction, not proven.
**Sweets:** owned macarons, donuts, cupcakes, cakes and pies allow a candy-ish stage NOW. A giant walkable gumdrop/candy island, chocolate river or conveyor is NEW visual design, not automatically supplied by owned kits. Do NOT claim a separate KayKit candy pack.
**Exterior:** reconstruct only through owned modular Bakery Interior architecture if the visual source test supports the silhouette; official paid `Bakery Building` is a separate candidate, not automatically available or purchased.
**Casting:** Bakery maker, delivery courier and visiting eater are scene roles, NOT automatically three new resident character identities.

### C. Tiny Treats · Homely House ↔ Pretty Park (POST-MVP candidate)
**Spatial image:** readable small house, its actual `mailbox` at a real path, `letter/package`, threshold/fence/gate, street tree, front bench and facing green park/fountain. **One inhabited neighborhood vignette**, not disconnected park tiles in the void.
**Causal chain:** visitor approaches via traversable path → leaves `letter` / `package` at mailbox → resident exits home → reads/reacts → meets neighbor or walks across the park → social continuation. Mail behavior/quests/NPC routes NOT implemented.
**Expansion:** sourced `Pleasant Picnic` anchors ordinary life in the park; House Plants/Charming Kitchen/Living Room etc may extend interiors after ownership/source test. Extra Tiny Treats sets do not force one new island per pack.
**Keep existing Town authority:** candidate cozy house can be in a later satellite neighborhood or Town neighborhood if current Town owner chooses, not a retroactive replacement for the accepted Town architecture or residents.
