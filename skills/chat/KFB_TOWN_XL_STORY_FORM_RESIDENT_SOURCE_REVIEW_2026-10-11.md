## 1. Audited Town identity and dated precedence
**User's new scale/composition direction:** **Town should read as the central, visibly larger/XL ancestral island** in the future fragmented Kayfabe constellation, not one same-sized decorative satellite. **XL is a spatial relationship and storytelling hierarchy, not a measured terrain recipe or a requirement to enlarge/replace current Four-Island visual deliverables.** A/B must still compare two spatial renderings of the *same* Town + three satellite worlds.
**Actual current Town SSOT overrides an old metaphor:**
- Historical `LIVING_KFB_TOWN.md` describes a cutting mat, toy houses and hexagon map; current accepted **S001 r017–r022** explicitly **supersedes cutting-mat Town terrain** with natural stylized **Travel** hills, rock, vegetation and practical flattened pads. Preserve Cut&Play / tabletop ancestry as a cultural storytelling layer, **not** a literal Town floor that trumps current source.
- Town is **a historical settlement/civic market/social place**, not a polished amusement park, 3D menu, generic medieval prefab village or hollow island with road loop. Natural KayKit Forest/Nature, Kenney City outer expansion and other mixed stylistic donors are candidates only after original-source isolation and measured actor/vehicle scale.
- Town is the origin/hub of current four: **Town → Dystopia, Utopia, Protopia**. It is not itself a fourth future-world Deck. Extra islands (Library, Finance, Dungeon, Forest, Prison, Vampire, Gen Z, Sci-Fi, Unity-Japanese Garden) remain **future optional satellites** under their own concept references; do not pull them into current Four-Island MVP by implication.
- The Fluff Incident and a physically fragmented galaxy are potent shared world-story hypotheses, but the exact mechanism/chronology of Town fracturing remains **unfrozen**.

## 2. Six source-authored story layers → spatial composition
Town's source-backed biography from Four-Island Recovery §3:
1. **Genesis/origin:** early Kayfabian settlement, Caveman among the old inhabitants; geology and settlement emerge from a lived story, not arbitrary plot/hex.
2. **Work and exchange:** mine/early resource labor, a true walkable market with work spaces and believable arrival routes.
3. **Town grows:** homes, shops, communal spaces, farm connection, paths used by inhabitants, land-use traces and reused structures.
4. **Authority:** King Kayfabian's civic/royal presence, king tower as recognizable landmark and an institution in the comedy, not fantasy monarchy pasted across every building.
5. **Scars of use:** mine exploitation/industrialization, remembered transformations, Caveman/Eva personal history, older ruins/repair seams and honest physical transition into other regions.
6. **Present-day life:** resident routines, deliveries, dialogue/courier chains, makespace social play, showstage and meaningful routes to Dystopia/Utopia/Protopia.

**Town XL spatial test (author proposal for Claude Code):** source-isolate then compose two A/B variants:
- **A · Market-heart hilltown:** visibly larger asymmetric contiguous terrain mass. Marketplace near a natural distributor, Town/King tower behind/beside it; compact civic stage plus nearby living residences; farm, old cave/mine, ruin and maker/market edges trace historical layering. Three clear outward **destinations**, not ring with arbitrary inward dead ends. Street and walkway rhythm creates real person-to-person encounters.
- **B · Layered accreted hill/terrace:** same actual story and actors, more dramatic rock levels and pre-existing old cave/mine below civic platform. Workbench and small houses distributed along meaningful terraces, king tower still visible, one living market heart, gentle road links and optional roadside media. Do not invent a second Town or turn each ledge into mandatory district.
**XL should be proven by silhouette, meaningful built density, historical legibility and readable resident relationships, not an unverified numeric scale claim or indiscriminate object count.** Island edges/support, collision slopes, roads, greenery and bridges need physically plausible transitions. No universal six-sector Life Tree or forced oval roads.
