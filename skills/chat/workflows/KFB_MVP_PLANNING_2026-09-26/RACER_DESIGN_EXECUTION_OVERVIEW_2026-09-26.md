### Blender MCP · safe prework now

Blender may author **scenery / set-piece shells with route sockets** now, provided they do not define a new drivable centreline or road surface.

Good candidates:
- bridge pylons / cables / portals / support families;
- tunnel / underpass portals and structural shells;
- parking-deck / industrial shells that can later receive a Track-Core route;
- ramp/skyramp underside/support structures **without** bespoke drivable ramp surface;
- guard/fence/light/gantry attachment structures only after checking existing KayKit/Kenney donors first;
- landmark/scenery pieces keyed later by `frameAt(s)`.

Do **not** ask Blender to build standalone driveable straights, curves, hairpins, chicanes, spiral ramps, loop surfaces, kickers or landing decks before their Track-Core sprint. Those are route/core geometry and would recreate the RKIT-11 one-off failure mode.

### Existing geometry donors — do not rebuild

Current RKIT already supplies useful reference/acceptance material including track/profile families, supports, tunnels, funnel, bowl, switch, pit lane, CITY_STREET, `JUMP_HERO_30`, `JUMP_BASE_12`, loop/skyramp candidates and the frozen RKIT-11 bridge/loop/hop fixture. B4/B5 should consume/rebuild these **through the core**, not replace them with new bespoke meshes.

### “Tokyo Drift” direction

Current GitHub/Dropbox recon found **no dedicated current KFB Tokyo-Drift route/module brief**. Keep it as a later R0 design direction, not a new track system.

Useful core-native beat after B2–B4:
- one tight urban 180° hairpin with measured transition law;
- one S-offset/chicane approach;
- optional helical/parking-deck ascent or descent generated by the Track Core;
- industrial/parking shell from Blender around that route;
- optional rooftop/kicker beat only from the B4 core piece law;
- drift-friendly sightline and recoverable outer edge, without copying a film layout.

What Blender can prepare before that gate is the **parking/industrial shell, supports, barriers/attachments and visual dressing sockets**, not the driving ribbon.
