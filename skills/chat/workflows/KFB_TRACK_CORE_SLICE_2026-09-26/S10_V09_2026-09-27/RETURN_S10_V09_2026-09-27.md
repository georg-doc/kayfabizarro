# S10 · core v0.9: bigger loops, longer width tapers, rounded ramp ends, pit lanes · RETURN · 2026-09-27

**Georg (27.09), after trying the Streckenbauer:**

- Loops should be bigger and follow rollercoaster logic. What counts is height and ride length in the loop, not track width.
- Width transitions don't look organic yet. His choice: **longer and softer** (not one-sided).
- Switches and branches become **pit lanes** (Kenney Racing as reference only). Later this is the scenery for the KFB garage, vehicle rigging lab and BYO models.
- Ramps must start and end rounder and more cartoony. They look squashed and cut off.

**Stage / Live / merge:** none.

## Defects and limits first

- **Existing courses now warn.** TD03 / TD05 roof loop 26 m, seed loop 18 m and FS01 mini loop 16 m are under the new loop size. Width steps in TD03 (1:11) and the seed (1:13) are shorter than the new 1:25. These are warnings only; I did not rebuild the courses.
- **Loop shape is unchanged.** It is the same teardrop (vertical curvature sin²), only the default size changed (100 m). Rollercoaster details (a drop into the loop, a separate lead-in climb, heart-line) are not built.
- **The rounded ramp end narrows the road** by 1.6 m per side in the last 1.6 m before the lip, and the AIR span inherits that width (no surface there). Markings stop 3 m before the end.
- **Landing start:** from a low camera the underside reads as a flat sloped face.
- **The pit lane is a fixed recipe** built from tested pieces: FORK, main straight, JOIN, and a branch that narrows to 10.8 m, steps 6 m away, opens the barrier on the box side, has N boxes, then closes, steps back and widens. The main road must be straight over the pit. Box anchors are flat placeholders, not garages.
- **Graph checks are slow in the builder:** about 3 s for the sandbox with its pit lane (worker, so the UI stays responsive).
- The FORK widening (Weiche) is exempt from the width rule; it stays a switch piece.

## What changed (core v0.9)

| Topic | Change |
|---|---|
| Loops | `LOOP` without `height` = **100 m** (`LOOP_DEFAULT_H`). New check `loop_scale` (warn) below **60 m**; it reports height and ride length per loop. |
| Width | `WIDTH_STEP` without `length` = **25 m per metre of width change per side**, at least 40 m (STANDARD→WIDE 45 m, →HERO 90 m). New check `width_taper` (warn) below 1:25. Switches, splits and ramp ends are exempt. |
| Ramp ends | `END_TAPER` replaced by `ROUND_END`: barriers only lower to 50 % over 16 m and keep their thickness and the deck. Over the last 9 m the side extent and deck depth follow a quarter ellipse (a bullnose: plan corners round in, the underside curves up to the road), and the road corners round with 1.6 m. No more thin, squashed sliver. |
| Pit lane | New graph macro `PIT_LANE` {side, length, boxes, gap, laneWidth, mainWidth}. The branch gets drive mode `assist` + fx `pit_limiter`. Box anchors go into `stream.anchors` (`kind: 'pit_box'`, frame, lat, size). |

Tests: `node test.mjs` **282 / 282** expected, 0 unexpected (was 242). New tests cover the default loop height and the 30 m loop warning, the auto width length (90 m) and the 30 m warning, rounded lip and touchdown, and the pit lane (all checks, 4 boxes, NARROW lane, open barrier, limiter).
