## Defects and limits first

1. **The twist is moderate by design.** Banks are nominally −6° to +20°. After the core's 24 m bank smoothing, the driven banks are −5.1° to +17.2°. Bumps are −0.5 to +1.2 m. Bigger values collide with the lap above: the first try (±22°, bumps to 1.8 m, 8 m floors) left 1.48 m headroom and a 0.92 °/m kink. Push further only with more floor spacing.
2. **The balcony now tops out at 33.0 m** (TD04: 27.1 m), because floors are 9 m apart instead of 6. The laps no longer line up with the 6 m wing floors, and the finale landing is longer (136 m for a 29 m drop, 21 % average as before). The jump speed is unchanged at 26.26 m/s.
3. **Off-camber corners (−5°) push the car outwards.** That is intended as physics, but the WSA runtime has to show whether it is fun or annoying. Values are set per corner in `layout/td05.mjs` (`TWIST`).
4. **The rounding is Blender modifiers only** (non-destructive Bevel). The scenery builder geometry and the TD02 files are unchanged. A runtime export needs these modifiers applied, or the same rounding rebuilt in the three.js scenery.
5. **Blender clearance:**

   | Obstacle | Clearance |
   |---|---|
   | Column, classed as "obstacle" | 0.86 m |
   | Column, support | 1.52 m |
   | Core | 2.80 m |
   | Wing deck | 3.92 m |

   The 0.86 m is the same known spot as before: the track deck sits inside the roof slab and the column stands under that slab. Rounding the columns moved the value from 0.73 to 0.86 m.
6. **The head-on corner shots (`d04`, `d05`) read less clearly** than the overviews. The tilt is best seen in `d02` / `d03` and in the height plot.
