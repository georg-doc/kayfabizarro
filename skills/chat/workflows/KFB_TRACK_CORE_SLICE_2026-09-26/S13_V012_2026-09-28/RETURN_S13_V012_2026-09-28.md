## Open points (defects and decisions first)

1. **Splitter islands are painted, not raised.** They are hatched deck areas with a bollard at the ring end. A raised clay island would need its own narrow kerb section (the kit section is 4.32 m per side and does not fit a 5 m island). Georg decides if that is wanted.
2. **Nature biome:** the splitter hatch and yield lines are also drawn on unpainted nature roads. Other lines follow the biome style (city = street lines, track = edge lines, nature = none).
3. **Contact at kerbs** stays at full barrier height, like the arm routes (same open decision as the city curb from v0.11).
4. **Centre scenery is a placeholder** (park trees, statue, pond, stage with figures). The real sets come from Design / Blender via the anchor.
5. **Builder only:** in the headless test browser, the ground grid shows faintly through all surfaces. This is a renderer depth artefact of the test browser, seen on the old routes as well.
6. **Arm spacing:** arms that are too close are refused with the angle they need. Before that, fillets shrink automatically down to 5.82 m.

## What it is

A roundabout is a flat **junction node** for world roads, not a race-track piece. It has:

- **Plate:** the ring plus arm mouths. Each mouth has flared edges, fillets tangent to the ring, and lanes of constant width around the splitter.
- **Kerb runs:** the kit's side section along every outline. At the mouth corners they hand over to the arm routes with no seam: same point, same tangent, same section.
- **Centre island:** fill plus an anchor `roundabout_centre` with `{p, T facing the arriving traffic, r, scenery: {kind: park|statue|pond|resident, set, seed}}`.
- **Painted splitter islands** with a round cap and nose, and a bollard.
- **Yield lines** across each entry lane.
- **NPC drive paths:** one per entry → exit pair, U-turns included, on lane centres. Right-hand traffic runs anticlockwise; `hand: 'left'` mirrors it.
- **One socket per arm:** `node/armK` at the arm end.

The arms are ordinary routes with the kit profile and the node biome. The node biome is inherited from the arriving route.

### Recipe use (builder)

A `ROUNDABOUT` piece in a route has these parameters: `island`, `ringWidth`, `arms: [0, 90, 180, 270]` (angles like a turn: 0 = arrival, 90 = first right, 180 = straight on), `exit`, `splitter`, `fillet`, `armLength`, `hand`, `scenery`.

The route ends at the arrival mouth. It continues as `M+<node>` from the exit mouth. The other arms become stub routes. `graph.chains.M` gives the drive order with continuous `s`.

### Graph use (world)

`graph.nodes: [{type: 'ROUNDABOUT', id, center, headingDeg, ...}]` places a free-standing node. Routes attach in two ways:

- `start: {socket: 'hub/arm1'}`
- `CONNECT to: 'hub/arm2'`

### New checks

| Check | What it tests |
|---|---|
| `node_fit` | The arms fit; names any shrunk fillets |
| `node_mouth` | Arm end section is on the mouth corners |
| `node_paths` | Every NPC lane centre stays at least 2.05 m from every kerb or island |
| `node_path_radius` | Warns below 8 m |
| `node_clear` | No other route crosses the plate |
| `node_sockets` | Each arm has a level socket |

`cross_clearance` ignores routes that meet end to end at a socket.
