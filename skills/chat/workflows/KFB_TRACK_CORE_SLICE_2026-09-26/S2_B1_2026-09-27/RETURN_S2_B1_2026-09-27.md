## Defects and limits first

1. **Blender is float32.** The oracle checks `s` against chord length at 1e-4 m; the JS core checks it at 1e-6 m.
2. **The seed loop is small** (H 18 m, top radius about 5.6 m). With the full kit barriers (4.32 m per side) it reads as a tube.
   - The seed therefore slims the profile over the loop (side scale 0.45, deck depth 1.0) and restores it afterwards.
   - Whether loops always get a slim profile is a look decision for Georg / Claude Design.
3. **The split gore** is two inner edge lines meeting at a point, followed by a barrier that rises from flush. The "dashed centre line turns solid" step of Georg's rule is not yet a marking blend; it is a v0.3 marking item.
4. **After a split, each lane keeps the STREET centre dash** (10.8 m lanes with a centre dash). Which marking style a single-lane branch gets is a style decision.
5. **The Tokyo-Drift seed is still a piece proof,** not the Uni-Center layout (S3).
6. **The preview GLB is 6 MB** and uncompressed.

## Found and fixed during the run (the checks did their job)

1. **OFFSET_S sign bug.** A −14 m (left) shift actually veered right into the through lane. Found by `cross_clearance` 0.50 m; fixed. The W0 seed had the same inversion, so its fixture now states −8 to keep the approved layout.
2. **Kicker and landing ramps** (cubic) and the crest bell (sin²) had vertical-curvature steps at their ends. `tangent_kink` 0.77 and 0.92 °/m caught them; both are now C2 (quintic Hermite and a 64·u³(1−u)³ bell).
3. **Splits used a lateral offset from a shared axis,** so lanes banked and looped about the wrong pivot. `cross_clearance` caught it.
   - **Now:** SPLIT_HALF and MERGE_HALF move the centre line itself onto the lane centre (smootherstep S), so every route re-bases on its own lane.
   - Deck-level split lanes are flat.
4. **The split was drawn as one stretched face strip** across the topology change, which showed as a dark fin in Blender.
   - **Now:** the core emits a zero-length **break sample** at the split start and at the merge end (`brk: true`).
   - Consumers start a new strip there, and `s_monotonic` allows zero length only at breaks.
5. **Loop frame.** The rotation-minimising frame with twist closure leaned the section sideways by about 25° mid-loop when the loop drifts. That brought the loop's legs to 0.54 m of each other.
   - **Now:** a `loop` law (RKIT-08 donor): world up pitched by the in-plane angle about the loop axis, then orthogonalised. It returns exactly to world up and needs no twist.
   - The default drift is 1.35 × (full section width + 3 m).
6. **Open body ends at the lip and touchdown:** the importer now caps every drawn run.

## New in the core (v0.2)

- **Horizontal parametrisation** for plan pieces. The height schedule is `y(u) = [dy, dy/dx]`.
- **CREST / DIP:** a C2 bell.
- **KICKER → AIR → LANDING:**
  - KICKER: quintic ramp to the lip height and angle.
  - AIR: designed parabola at g 15. It solves the design speed from gap and drop, and has no driving surface.
  - LANDING: quintic from the arrival slope to flat.
- **`jump_speed`:** a warning check that flags a designed speed above 27 m/s (boost needed). Georg: boost is not mandatory.
- **CONNECT:** a quintic Hermite connector from the current frame to a target frame, with zero curvature at both ends. This is the flexible connector piece, used by branches to rejoin.
- **SPLIT_HALF / MERGE_HALF** (Rhein-Run D1 "halve"):
  - HERO 21.6 → 10.8 | 10.8;
  - lanes separate until each has its own barriers plus a 1.0 m gore;
  - the inner barrier rises late, at 30–100 % of the split (taper rule);
  - per-side profile scale `sideL/sideR` lets lanes share a deck edge.
- **Route graph `compileGraph`:**
  - branches declare `from` a SPLIT_HALF and `to` a MERGE_HALF;
  - the core adds the mirrored split, the CONNECT and the mirrored merge.
- **`runGraphChecks`:** per-route checks plus `cross_clearance` (split, merge and connect zones excluded), `split_edge` (inner road edges coincide at the split start) and `merge_end` (branch ends on the main frame).
- **Markings** now carry their placement rule. `markingLat(band, prm)` evaluates the band per sample, so lines follow width and offset changes.
