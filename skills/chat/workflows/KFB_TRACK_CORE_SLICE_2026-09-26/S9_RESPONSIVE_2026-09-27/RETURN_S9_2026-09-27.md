## Defects and limits first

2. **A junction hall is a straight, constant cavern.** Its section is fitted once to both lanes over the whole fork / join zone.
   - Forks in curves are not handled yet: the hall assumes the split pieces are straight, which FORK / JOIN always are.
   - The hall ends are flat walls. In Blender, the tube mouths are cut into them with the tubes' own rings.
   - The hall roof is flat and dark in the shots.
3. **The fork needs a wider gore inside tunnels.** The two tubes leaving a hall must not touch: with the default 1 m gore the alien and toy tubes overlapped by 3.4 m. TN02 uses `gore: 10`. There is no automatic rule yet.
4. **Round tubes stay big.** A round tube is as tall as it is wide, so a HERO road gives a 33 m tube. For low tunnels use rect, oval or arch.

## What is new in the core (v0.8)

- **Vehicle envelope:** height 4.0 m + reserve **3.0 m** = **7.0 m** clear over the whole road width. It is one constant; tunnels and the `vehicle_headroom` check derive from it.
- **Responsive sections.** `w` / `h` default to `auto`: the smallest section of the shape's proportion (`aspect`) that keeps barriers, shoulders and the vehicle envelope `clear` m away, sideways and upwards (the floor sits under the road).
  - Width steps and fork widenings make the tube grow and shrink along with the road.
  - Fixed numbers still win. For example, the hangar has `h: 70`; its width is then chosen so that the floor stays under the road.
  - `aspect: 'fit'` (rect) takes the smallest cross-section area.
- **New shape `arch`:** horseshoe, with a flat floor, straight walls and a round vault.
- **Presets** (`tunnel: {preset}`, any field overridable):

  | Preset | Shape | Host | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `gotthard` | arch | mountain | |
  | `dumb` | rect e 6 | bunker | wall 2.5 m |
  | `alien` | octagon | alien | |
  | `hangar` | rect e 14 | hangar | h 70, 32 m clear to the sides |
  | `toy` | round | toy | thin wall; goes with the new **skin `toy`**, orange plastic |

- **Weichen inside tunnels = junction halls.**
  - A FORK or JOIN on a tunnel route makes the main route carry ONE hall for both lanes. It is fitted to both lanes in the frame of the shared axis.
  - The branch lane has no tube of its own inside the hall.
  - Tubes start and end at the hall walls.
  - Halls are their own tunnel segments (`kind: 'hall'`).
- **`new: true` on a tunnel spec** starts a new tube without a morph, for example bunker → hangar or hall → alien tube. Each tube has a serial (`tunnel.tube`); segments split on it.
- **Checks:**
  - `tunnel_morph` skips pairs across tube or hall borders.
  - `portal_match` skips hall walls and tube ends that open into a hall. There the fork's widening may flare the tube; there is no collar.
- **Other set pieces are already width-responsive** through the profile:
  - road, shoulders and barriers scale with the width class;
  - loops size their drift from the full section width;
  - forks and joins work from the width they start with;
  - kickers, landings and air spans are width-independent.

  The tunnels were the only fixed-size pieces; now they follow too.
