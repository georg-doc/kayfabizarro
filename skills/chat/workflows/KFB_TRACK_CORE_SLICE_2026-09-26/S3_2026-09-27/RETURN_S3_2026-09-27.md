## Defects and limits first

1. **The Uni-Center scenery was adapted to the track,** not the other way round. It is the approved TD02 builder with unchanged functions, but four parameters changed:
   - `hub_r` 44 → 68, so the WIDE drift ring fits on the podium roof;
   - `hole_r` 28 → 36, so the STANDARD 14.4 helix fits around the 13 m core;
   - columns are skipped along the route corridor (102 of 369), the TD02 v2 open item "no stray columns in the track";
   - the TD02 route shells (helix / balcony decks, parapets) are no longer built, because the route now comes only from the core stream.
2. **Only one wing (yellow) exists,** as in TD02 v1. The core is still the 60 m cylinder; the 134 m tower is a TD02 v2 open item.
3. **The balcony climb around the yellow wing (TD02 K3) is not in TD03 v1.**
   - The route is: street, three-floor helix, roof drift ring, 26 m roof loop on the orange arm (hanging out over the plaza), plaza hairpin, elevated east run, jump down to the street.
   - The balcony climb would be the next beat.
4. **Clearance in Blender:**
   - The minimum clearance is 0.73 m, to a column **under** the roof slab where the drift ring sits on the roof. That is a support, not a collision.
   - The core tower and the wing decks are clear.
   - The check does not yet separate "support under the deck" from "obstacle", so that number needs this reading.
5. **GLB vertex colours:** the preview GLB does not carry the skin colours (Blender materials do not use the colour attribute yet). The stream carries them; the runtime reads the stream.
6. **The jump needs 26.16 m/s,** just under the D1 27 m/s. That is reachable, but only with a clean run-up; the runout after the east turn is 20 m.
7. **The track sits 0.3 m above the podium roof,** to avoid z-fighting with the slab. The helix floors are therefore 5.1 m, not 5.0 m.

## Georg's feedback applied (27.09)

1. **Magnet stripes start as an arrow tip.**
   - The first 5 bars of every magnet run grow from 8 % to the full 70 % of the lane (smoothstep), and the last 5 shrink again.
   - The bars stay centred, so the run reads as an arrow pointing in the driving direction.
2. **Colour blending as a design tool, spread across the seam.**
   - Skins are colour presets per role (road, shoulder, barrier side, barrier cap, underside). There are three placeholder skins: street, track and mag; the seeded KFB palettes come later.
   - A skin change blends over 32 m, centred on the joint (it starts 16 m before and ends 16 m after).
   - The blend is staggered per role: barrier cap 0–55 %, barrier side 10–70 %, underside 10–90 %, shoulder 25–85 %, road 40–100 %.
   - So the barrier turns first and the road last, with no hard colour edge. Each sample in the stream carries its `paint` set, and Blender shows it as colour per face corner.

## New in the core (v0.3)

- **`skin` per piece,** defaulting from the markings (STREET→street, TRACK→track, MAG→mag), plus `paintSkins`, which does the staggered cross-seam blend (`SKIN_BLEND`).
- **Magnet bar taper** (`BAR_TAPER` 5, `BAR_SPAN_MIN` 0.08).
- **`riseEase: 'ramp'`** (constant grade, C1 blends, `riseBlend`) and **`riseSteps`**: the rise split into flights that each level out, like car-park floors.
- **CONNECT to an inline anchor frame** (`to: {p, headingDeg, grade}`). A layout can now be authored from building or OSM anchors.
- **`crossing_headroom` check:** designed crossings (both parts tagged `crossing_ok`) need 2.2 m car headroom under the upper deck.
