## Core v0.11

- **Zones.** A zone is created by the piece type `TRANSITION` (a straight, default 80 m) or by `transition: {to}` on any piece; the zone then spans that piece.
  - Biomes: `track`, `city`, `nature`, `canyon`, `coast`.
  - Zone kinds: `leave` (from the track), `enter` (back to the track) and `across` (between two biomes).
- **Data only.** A test compiles the same route with and without zones and finds every sample identical: s, frame, slots, profile, curvature and bank.
- **Per sample.** Inside a zone, `q.zone = {id, from, to, u, w: {surface, markings, barrier, pit, curb, nature, props, light, vfx}}`, with each weight running 0 → 1 inside that layer's own window. Outside zones, `q.biome` is set.
- **Windows** (`TRANSITION_WINDOWS`) come from the grammar table.
  - `leave` has the barrier first and props last.
  - `enter` is deliberately not a mirror of `leave`: props and edges clear first, the Knetstrang gathers late and the markings come last (grammar family C).
  - Tested: no 0.1 of u holds the half-way point of more than 3 main layers.
- **Markings** rebuild as real segments:
  - the old rhythm breaks into shrinking, side-staggered pieces;
  - the target rhythm (city = STREET with a dashed centre line; nature, canyon and coast = none) assembles from short pieces;
  - both overlap in the middle, and no segment is shorter than 0.6 m (no slivers, no alpha fade).
  - After a zone, the target biome's rhythm applies; a piece's own `markings` still wins.
- **`stream.transitions`** lists, per zone: from, to, kind, s0, s1, windows, the zone seed and six derived role seeds (track palette, terrain palette, K2 profile, light, VFX, WFC), and the contract sockets. Sockets (centreline, surface, barrier, curb, sidewalk, nature; left and right; in and out) carry positions and forward/up/right frames from the unchanged profile. `pit_in` and `pit_out` are reserved.
- **Check `transition_length`** (warn) flags zones outside the 60–100 m production corridor.
- Tests: 317/317 (was 308).
