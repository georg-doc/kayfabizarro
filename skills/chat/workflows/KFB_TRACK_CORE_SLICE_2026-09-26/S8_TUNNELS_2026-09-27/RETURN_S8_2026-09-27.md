## Defects and limits first

1. **The "labyrinth" is a two-strand braid, not a maze.** Two tubes swap sides twice and cross over and under each other: M is an octagon gallery that dips 9 m, J is a rect gallery that arches 9 m. There are no dead ends, no third strand and no crossing in plan without a height change.
   - The pieces allow more: any FORK / JOIN graph plus `tunnel` specs.
   - A real maze still needs a layout that holds several branches, and more graph routes than one fork per route pair.
2. **The shafts are steep and long.** Each spiral is 4 turns, R 60 m, 207 m drop, about 1.7 km per shaft at 13.7 % grade. The braid peaks at **15.5 %**.
   - The course is 9.8 km; 3.8 km of it is a plain surface return road.
   - Shorter options: fewer turns with a steeper grade, or a lift.
3. **Tunnels are big, because the track is big.** A 14.4 m road plus shoulders and barriers is 23 m wide, and the car envelope asks for 5 m clear over the whole road width. Resulting sections:

   | Section | Size | Floor fill under the road | Roof over the road |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Round | 26 m | 6.5 m | 19.5 m |
   | Rect | 26 × 11 m | 0.6 m | 10.4 m |

   The fill is fitted automatically as the least fill that still clears barriers, shoulders and the car envelope by 0.3 m. A lower tube or a smaller headroom is a `TUNNEL_DEFAULTS` value (proposal: 5 m headroom, 0.3 m margin).
4. **The hollow earth is scenery only.** The core carries it as a `hosts` entry (`kind: cavern`, ellipsoid, floor height, sun) and checks that open road under ground lies inside it (`buried_open`).
   - It does **not** yet check that tubes stay inside the crust, or how thin the crust is: the cavern top is 67 m under ground, and the surface return road runs over it.
   - The loop is **standing free in the cavern** (cartoon rule), `locked` with `fx: zero_g`.
5. **Blender preview limits:**
   - **A heightfield terrain cannot overhang a portal.** I leave out the terrain faces that would climb from the approach cut onto the tube cover (40 faces). A **headwall in the tube's own shape** closes the gap (4 portals). The mountain is a heightfield from clay "blobs" and looks cushion-like, with a flat rock top.
   - The tube shells show a faint seam line along the widest point (smooth-shading seam).
   - The underground is only visible with `S8_SURFACE` hidden; shots `t05`–`t08` do that. The cavern shell is cut away (north half only).
   - The Blender file is 35 MB and the GLB 48.5 MB, so both stay in Dropbox (not GitHub).
6. **The stream is large.** It is 26.8 MB for 20.8 k samples. On GitHub it is stored as `.json.gz` (2.8 MB), and the Blender scripts read the gz directly. Tube rings are stored once (399 rings for 9,040 tube samples; `tunnelRings` palette + `ringId`, `attachRings()` after JSON load).
7. **The oracle had a float32 limit.** Blender `Vector` is float32 and lost 2.4e-4 m on the s-vs-chord check at 2.5 km from the origin, which made the oracle report a false FAIL. The importer now measures that in float64. `b1_import_stream.py` is changed only in that line and in reading `.gz`.

## Core v0.7: tunnel layer (`track-core.mjs`)

**Usage:** `tunnel` on any piece.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `{shape, ...}` | Enter here (portal). If already inside: morph to the new section over the piece (`morphZone`). |
| `undefined` | Keep the current tube (sticky, like the marking). |
| `null` / `false` | Leave: the portal is at the start of this piece. |

**Shapes (`TUNNEL_SHAPES`):**

| Shape | Size | Construction |
|---|---|---|
| round | 26 × 26 m | superellipse, e = 2 |
| oval | 30 × 19 m | superellipse, e = 2 |
| rect | 26 × 11 m | superellipse e = 10, i.e. a rectangle with rounded corners |
| poly | 28 × 22 m | regular n-gon with a flat floor edge (n = 6, 8, …) |

- Every value can be overridden (`w`, `h`, `e`, `n`, `wall`, `host`, `headroom`, `margin`).
- All shapes use the same 48 radial samples, so any shape morphs into any other point by point.

**Stream:**

- `sample.tunnel` = {shape, morph, host, wall, w, h, cy, fill, margin, ringId}.
- `stream.tunnels` = tube segments (host, s0 / s1, shapes).
- `stream.tunnelRings` = the ring palette.
- `hosts` and `ground` are passed through.

**New checks:**

| Check | Rule | TN01 |
|---|---|---|
| `tunnel_clearance` | Barriers, shoulders and the 5 m car envelope stay ≥ 0.3 m inside the ring | 0.30 m (fitted) |
| `tunnel_morph` | A ring point moves ≤ 0.3 m per metre (no step) | 0.045 m/m |
| `portal_match` | The ring is constant over 10 m at each portal, and no portal sits on an air span or a loop, so the collar is the tube's own shape (Georg: no square portal on a round tube) | 0 |
| `tunnel_shell` | No other pass (tube shell or open deck) within 1 m of rock of a tube's outer shell; per route and between routes | 14.27 m self · 2.81 m M/J |
| `buried_open` | Open road more than 0.5 m under ground only inside a cavern host | 0 |

**Also in v0.7:**

- A CONNECT that is already at its target produces no zero-length samples.
- `self_clearance` and `crossing_headroom` use a 60 m grid instead of all pairs. TN01 checks went from 16 s to 1.5 s. Values are exact below 24 m, and above that the check reports "no far pairs closer than 24 m".
- TD03 / TD04 / TD05 / FS01 fingerprints and values are **unchanged**.
