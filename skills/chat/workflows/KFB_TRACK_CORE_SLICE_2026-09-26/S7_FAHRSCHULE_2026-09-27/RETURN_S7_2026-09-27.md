# S7 · FS01 KFB Fahrschule at the Otto-Maigler-See · RETURN · 2026-09-27

**Executor:** Claude Coworker. **Stage / Live / merge:** none.

**Georg's choice (27.09):** the Fahrschule first. It is a small satirical driving school / practice ground on the lake shore, in Hürth. It is **pull, not a gate**: an optional practice course. It teaches every drive mode once and doubles as the test bench for new pieces.

## Defects and limits first

1. **Only a rough fit to the site.** The layout sits on an OSM extract of the Otto-Maigler-See (lake outline, beaches, car parks, Strandbad), but only roughly:
   - the home run cuts the corner of the Strandbad car park;
   - the west beach run passes the lower end of the water-park area;
   - the lifebuoy line crosses the lake's north-east bay (about 430 m).

   This is cartoon placement ("places recognisable, flow first"), not survey work. Terrain is flat at −0.3 m; the lake is cut in, with water at −1.2 m.
2. **The lifebuoys are drawn as bounce pads, not simulated.** Each buoy is a landing → kicker pair that dips (squashes) to 0.14 m before the next hop, with a buoy skin and drive `fx: bounce`. The spring itself belongs to the runtime.
3. **Drive modes are metadata only:**
   - `free` on the pad;
   - `assist` on most of the course;
   - `locked` on the loop and the magnet catch;
   - `fx` values `bounce` and `magnet_catch`.

   The WSA runtime has to implement them.
4. **The Weiche is built from tested pieces** (widen 14.4 → 28.8, then split into two full 14.4 lanes; mirrored to rejoin). It is not a new moving part. The jump lane is marked by its track skin (teal) against the grey street lane.
5. **The magnet catch sits on a 2.8 m berm plus a 1 m bulge.** At 18° bank on flat ground the low road edge would otherwise lie 1.6 m under the ground. There is no core check for "road under the terrain" yet; I measured it by hand (lowest banked road edge now +0.78 m).
6. **The OSM data came through the browser.** The cloud and the local VM both block Overpass. I read the OSM API (`api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map`) in Chrome. The source and licence (ODbL) are in `otto_maigler_see.osm.json`.
7. **Preview only.**
   - The overview shot shows distance striping on the far road and the pad; the close shots are clean.
   - Cones and the sign are placeholders; the sign text is not readable in the shots.
   - The GLB has no vertex colours.

## New in the core (v0.6)

- **Pads:** free practice areas as rounded rectangles with stations (`cones`, `parking_box`, `brake_line`, `sign`) in pad-local coordinates, output in world coordinates.
  - New check `pad_level`: each pad is reached by a route and lies flush with it. It works per route and for graphs.
- **FORK / JOIN macros (Weiche):** built only from tested pieces (WIDTH_STEP + SPLIT_HALF / MERGE_HALF + WIDTH_STEP). They work in recipes and in graphs.
- **Drive modes per piece:** `drive: {mode: free|assist|locked, fx: [...]}` → `sample.drive`. Graphs can switch auto-bank off (a flat driving school); banks are then explicit.
- **Skin `buoy`:** red/white placeholder.
- **New check `landing_dip`:** a landing ramp may not dip more than 0.1 m below its run-out height. It found a dip of 1.18 m in my first jump-lane draft (fixed). TD04 (0.06 m) and TD05 (0.045 m) pass; buoys are exempt, because their squash is intended.
