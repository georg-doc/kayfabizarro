## New in the core (v0.4)

- **`riseEase: 'rampIn' | 'rampOut'`** (with `riseBlend`): half ramps. One climb can span many pieces at a constant grade, eased only at its outer ends; the grade has no step at the joints (max 1e-3).
- **`closed: true`** on a recipe marks a circuit:
  - the stream carries `closed`;
  - clearance pairs are measured cyclically, so the start and end samples are not "far apart";
  - the new **`closure`** check requires the last sample to equal the first. Position, T, U, all profile values, skin and marking must match.
