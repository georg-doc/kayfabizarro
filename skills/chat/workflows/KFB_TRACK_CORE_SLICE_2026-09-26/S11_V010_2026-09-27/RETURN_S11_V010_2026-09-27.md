## What changed in the core (v0.10)

- **PIT_LANE rebuilt as one module on one deck:**
  - The main road keeps its width and line.
  - On the pit side the main kerb and barrier step off with a hard break, exactly where the pit lane starts 0.05 m wide, flush with the main edge. The pit lane's outer kerb and barrier continue the main ones with no seam (0.000 m in the test), and hand them back at the exit.
  - Between the lanes the deck is filled: a hatched gore up to 5 m wide.
  - In the gore stands a slim pit wall (1.0 × 1.2 m) with a crash cushion at each end.
  - Pit lane 7.2 m. Box apron 7.2 m with box marks. Garages 12 m deep and 5 m high, one bay per box, with doors facing the lane.
  - The outer barrier ends at the building corners with round caps.
  - A deck plinth rounds out of the barrier line into the building's deck over 12 m on each side.
  - `side: 1` mirrors the whole module to the right.
- **WEICHE (new macro, replaces widen-and-halve):**
  - The branch leaves the main edge in one S-curve (80 m) with the main width.
  - The gore is hatched until the nose.
  - At the nose one barrier wraps the split as a U, with a crash cushion in front.
  - The main lane stays straight and keeps its width. The branch continues with `then` pieces or `branchLength`.
  - FORK/JOIN stay available.
- **Ramp ends:**
  - Barriers no longer sag to half height before the lip (`ROUND_END.vis = 1`).
  - The deck nose is taut (radius = deck depth) instead of a 9 m wedge.
  - The plan rounding of the sides is unchanged.
- **No sunk barriers:** a new test checks that barrier visibility is 0 only where that side is bare.
- **Graph output `g.deck = { zones, furniture }`:**
  - zones: gore, apron, plinth;
  - furniture: wall, cushion, cap, garage, boxmark, uturn.
  - Pure data in world coordinates.
- **Piece flag `brk: true`:** a hard profile break at the piece start, with no blend.

## Loader (stream-to-three v3)

- Renders the deck layer.
- End faces are now triangulated as real polygons and wound outward. The old fan made a sail across the road at mid-track breaks, and the landing's front face was open.
- At an internal break, only the side that changes is closed.

## Tests

- 308/308 (was 280): v0.10 pit spec, barrier hand-over, deck layer, mirrored pit, Weiche with its checks.
- Nothing is rebuilt in existing courses. The sandbox preset uses the new pit; its old `gap: 6` now means a 6 m gore.

## Known limits (honest)

- In the Weiche the kerbs start hard at the nose; the concept drawing tapers them in. The profile cannot move a kerb without moving its barrier.
- There is a faint shading seam in the barrier where the pit lane takes over the main barrier (two meshes, same colour).
- The branch edge lines start once the lane is 2 m wide.
- Look and colours are preview only; the design pass comes after Georg's review.
- Pit in curves: not built. Modules stand on straights, by rule.
