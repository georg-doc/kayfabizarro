# Hex World / God Mode · architecture outlook

Status: **PROPOSAL ONLY · NOT PART OF F1.5**

## Direction

Build a later walkable/drivable world-authoring slice from the two indexed KayKit Hex sets: terrain, paths, rivers and bends remain real catalog parts; W6 can consume their directional edges/orientations. A compact responsive search/filter surface selects Hex tiles, water/river pieces, props and residences. God Mode places/removes them; the existing in-scene editor owns transforms and scene-patch export.

Placed groups can be saved as reusable island/zone/Card-Zone recipes. Asset Registry/Librarian remains asset identity/search owner; Hex/World Atlas owns grid and terrain rules; Travel/Race keep navigation, vehicle and macro-world ownership. Card Zone F1.5 remains a visual/source donor, not the world runtime.

## Visual contract to test later

Evaluate the Voxel Zone S2 chunky/cozy material language against real Hex tiles, characters, vehicles and OSM/landmark surfaces as one rounded cartoon-clay cohesion study. Do not apply a global material rewrite before measured source-isolation comparisons.

## First bounded gate after the current roadmap

Pin the exact two Hex-pack catalogs and prove a small 12–24-tile walkable patch with one road, one river bend and one reusable zone recipe. No F1.5 code or acceptance claim depends on this outlook.
