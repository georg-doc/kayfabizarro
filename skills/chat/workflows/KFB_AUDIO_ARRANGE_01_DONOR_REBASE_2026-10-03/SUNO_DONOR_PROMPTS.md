# AUDIO-ARRANGE-01 · Suno Donor Prompt Pack v1

**Purpose:** optional authoring-time donor generation for KFB procedural arrangement.  
**Rule:** choose musical masters first; extract stems only from winners.  
**Runtime:** never depends on Suno.

## Shared negative constraints

Append these ideas where Suno tends to overdecorate:

- instrumental only;
- no vocals;
- no xylophone, marimba, vibraphone, bells or bright mallet lead;
- no cute/cartoon plucks;
- no busy lead melody;
- no constant 16th-note arpeggiator;
- no EDM build/drop;
- no cinematic riser;
- no glossy wall-of-pad;
- preserve negative space;
- bass/drums remain the center.

## P1 · Warm Motorik Funk Bed · 76 BPM

76 BPM instrumental psychedelic groove, relaxed and hypnotic rather than cinematic. Warm dry drum pocket with only kick, soft cross-stick and restrained shaker/hand percussion; rounded electric bass repeats a strong two-bar syncopated phrase; warm Wurlitzer/Rhodes plays low-mid extended chord voicings with long gaps; clipped clavinet replies only on occasional offbeats; mellow organ sustains softly behind the groove; muted electric guitar adds sparse dry upstrokes and one-note replies. Very little melody: one short D-minor-pentatonic hook appears only every 8–16 bars, then silence. Human microtiming, subtle timbral evolution every 8 bars, occasional 1-bar dropout, warm tape saturation, intimate close mix, controlled spring/tape space, soft stereo width. Groove and repetition first; variation by subtraction, re-entry and small register shifts. No bells, mallets, bright plucks, lead solo, risers or EDM drop. Seamless long-form world-music bed.

**Best use:** direct alternative to Cyclical Warmth; likely full stem pull if the master wins.

## P2 · Cinematic Weird Electro-Funk · 80 BPM

80 BPM instrumental minimal electro-funk soundscape with a hypnotic human rhythm-section core. Deep rounded bass and dry kick/cross-stick establish a sparse repeating pocket; warm analog drum-machine detail blends with live hand percussion. Low Wurlitzer, drawbar organ and occasional muted clavinet stabs provide harmonic color, not continuous accompaniment. Sparse muted/twang guitar appears as short percussive answers. Treat sound design as an instrument: rare dry mechanical hit, reversed percussion tail, spring-reverb splash, tape-stop fragment or stereo orbit effect every few bars, always surrounded by silence. Dark-warm, playful-surreal, intimate, physical, cinematic but not epic. Keep one modal/pentatonic tonal center, few chord changes, 8/16-bar evolution, strong negative space, warm tape saturation and dub-like delay throws. No bright mallets, arpeggiators, synth lead, risers or wall-of-sound pads.

**Best use:** Yello-like production-economy donor without copying any song; likely FX/Other + drums/bass/keys stems.

## P3 · Organic Human Loop Engine · 78 BPM

78 BPM instrumental hypnotic groove built from human repetition and subtle drift. Dry live drums and three-part percussion vocabulary only: low kick, soft mid hand-drum/cross-stick, high shaker. Warm electric bass repeats a memorable two-bar motif for long stretches, with only two phrase variations across the piece. Electric piano and mellow organ hold a simple modal vamp; muted guitar provides occasional offbeat scratches. Almost no lead melody; a short minor-pentatonic phrase returns after long absences, sometimes transposed by a fourth/fifth or step while staying scale-safe. Structure through repetition, silence, dropouts and layer swaps rather than chord complexity. Slightly loose human timing, tape saturation, modest room/spring ambience, intimate stereo field. No high chimes, no vibraphone, no orchestral sweep, no EDM energy curve.

**Best use:** CAN-like human-repeat donor; excellent for testing long-session non-fatigue.

## P4 · Dub Pocket Mirage · 76 BPM

76 BPM instrumental dub-inflected psychedelic funk bed. Heavy but soft rounded bass, minimal dry drum pocket, sparse shaker and hand percussion, warm electric piano and low organ chords with large holes between them. Muted guitar upstrokes appear only occasionally. Use delay and spring reverb compositionally: isolated chord or guitar hits throw into space, then the dry groove returns. Long negative-space passages, one near-silent bar every 16–32 bars, subtle tape wobble and filtering, rare reversed or metallic sound-design accent. Only one small pentatonic hook, very infrequent. Cozy, strange, warm, hypnotic, non-fatiguing. No bright lead synth, bells, mallets, arpeggiator, cinematic riser, vocal chop or crowded high end.

**Best use:** SPACE/PSYCHEDELIC section donor; stems useful even if full master is too dub-heavy.

## P5 · Night Circuit Slow Funk · 84 BPM

84 BPM instrumental nocturnal electro-funk groove, restrained and cozy rather than aggressive. Tight dry drums with soft electronic percussion, warm syncopated electric/synth bass, low Rhodes chords, subdued organ, very sparse twang guitar and muted clavinet. Analog synth is used only as low atmospheric color and slow filter movement. One simple minor-pentatonic motif appears rarely in a low register. Build interest through bass articulation, drum ghost notes, short silences, filtered section transitions and subtle phaser/Leslie movement rather than additional notes. Dark amber night mood, tape warmth, close mix, controlled stereo width, occasional weird dry sound-design event. No mallet instruments, no sparkling plucks, no lead solo, no EDM build/drop.

**Best use:** darker WORLD alternative / later night transform donor.

## P6 · Road Translation Donor · 100 BPM

100 BPM instrumental road groove that feels faster through subdivision and articulation rather than frantic tempo. Same warm psychedelic/electro-funk language: punchy electric bass, dry live/electronic drum hybrid, restrained hats/shaker, clavinet and Wurlitzer/Rhodes punctuation, sparse tremolo/twang guitar, low organ and subtle analog texture. Bass/drum pocket remains hypnotic and repeatable; add double-time perception in selected sections without changing the core pulse. Include short 1-beat and 1-bar gaps suitable for drift/impact/jump SFX, then return cleanly to the groove. Evolve every 8/16 bars through layer density, articulation and timbre, not new melodies. One tiny pentatonic hook maximum. No EDM riser/drop, no bright mallets, no busy soloing, no wall of synth.

**Best use:** later ROAD comparison only; not needed for first One Good Island gate.

## Generation / stem strategy

Recommended:
1. generate 2 variants of P1, P2 and P3 first;
2. listen to the masters only;
3. keep at most 2 winners;
4. pull stems only for those winners;
5. P4/P5/P6 only if a concrete gap remains.

Stem priority:
- Drums;
- Bass;
- Keyboard / electric piano / clavinet / organ;
- Guitar;
- Percussion;
- Synth / Other / FX.

Strings and brass are optional; do not request or retain them merely because the splitter offers them.

For every winning donor, preserve:
- prompt;
- reported BPM;
- master;
- exact stem folder;
- stem categories;
- date/version;
- any Suno source ID/export metadata available.
