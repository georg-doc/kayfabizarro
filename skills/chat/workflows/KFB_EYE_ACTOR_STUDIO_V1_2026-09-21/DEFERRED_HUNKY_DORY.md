## Eye stalk concept

Proposed transform chain:

`measured stalk root → secondary/dangle stalk → Eye Slot anchor → Eye Actor`

The Eye Slot still owns:
- size/shape;
- Pitch/Yaw/Roll;
- lids;
- pupil/gaze.

The stalk owns only secondary motion of the anchor.

## Multi-eye possibility

Hunky/Dory are not required to stay at exactly two eyes.

- 1 eye;
- 2 unequal eyes;
- 3 eyes;
- 4 eyes.

Do not decide their final eye count in the Studio build slice.
