## What the benchmark means

### KEEP
- coherent deformation across neighbouring buildings;
- bowed / leaning / tapering walls;
- non-parallel silhouettes and rooflines;
- rounded, chunky clay/model masses;
- sparse narrow windows;
- one readable door plus two or three visible facade windows;
- soft matte model-lighting;
- smooth curved street/curb language.

### TUNE NEXT
- stronger cartoon palette family across walls / roofs / doors / windows / ground / curb / road;
- reproduce the accepted shape language with actual 3D geometry, not a 2D render trick.

### DO NOT INFER
The benchmark does not change OSM source truth, collision geometry, Travel/Race movement ownership, or the current Landmark owner.
