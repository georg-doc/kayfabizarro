- readEnvelope(analysis,sourceId,timeSec) interpolates validated source frames by playback time;
- modeToGeometry(frame,{narrativeField,reducedMotion}) maps 8 measured *synthetic-source* amplitudes and RMS into specific arc radii, stroke widths, field strength, vault displacement, ground echoes;
- sourceIntegrity(data,id,sha256) checks manifest identity.
