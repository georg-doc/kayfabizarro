# KFB Bell Resonance / The Impossible Campanile
## Concept-slide storyboard R1 — 14 frames
Date 2026-10-28 · Source-grounded concept design · Not an existing slide deck, renderer or product
Slide format suggestion 16:9. Narration/visuals in English for eventual presentation. Each slide specifies what is *seen*, *heard*, and *proven*; no substitute generic UI mockups.

### 01 — A Town Keeps Time With Its Ears
**On-screen:** THE TOWN THAT RANG ITSELF.
**Image:** Tilted KFB clay city at predawn. An immense historic bell tower above roof lines, still streets below; faint hand-drawn isochrones through windows and market stalls. One hearing listener; no dashboard.
**Audio:** Distant single bronze toll with long airy tail; birds/river remain quiet.
**Narrative:** Before the clock was personal, an elevated bell distributed communal time across a town.
**Evidence:** UNESCO manual bell-ringing inventory; place-specific social rituals must be researched before copying.

### 02 — The Bell Is a Social Machine
**On-screen:** ONE OBJECT. A HUNDRED REASONS TO RING.
**Image:** Same bell casts different shadow-tableaux: worship, curfew, harvest, celebration, fire, death, uprising. The city moves to the same hammer.
**Audio:** Six *distinct ring-pattern envelopes* played as creative sketches, NOT claims of one universal real bell code.
**Narrative:** Sound determines movement and meaning, not only atmosphere.

### 03 — Anatomy of a Toll
**On-screen:** THERE IS NO SINGLE 'BELL FREQUENCY.'
**Image:** Source bell seen isolated, rotation revealing five translucent rings: HUM / PRIME / TIERCE / QUINT / NOMINAL. Bands stretch at different speeds; audible clapper visibly excites one part of the bell.
**Audio:** Authentic dry ring then solo-mode resynthesis as a controlled demonstration, A/B with original intact.
**Evidence:** Approximate modal ratios (relative to prime): 0.5 : 1 : 1.2 : 1.5 : 2, actual bells need measured data. Single '432 Hz' label is insufficient.
**Source:** https://www.hibberts.co.uk/identifying-bell-partials/

### 04 — The Warble Between Worlds
**On-screen:** EVEN BRONZE CANNOT AGREE WITH ITSELF.
**Image:** Two almost-coincident modal halos drift in/out of phase. The body subtly warps like hand-worked clay, while the tower remains geometrically stable.
**Audio:** A slowly beating pair of nearby physical modes; show exact parameter difference, not fabricated therapeutic effects.
**Transition:** Beating becomes a visual rift in the air.

### 05 — A Bell That Was Taken Away
**On-screen:** YOU CAN STEAL THE METAL. NOT THE SPACE IT FILLED.
**Image:** Tower with a rectangular empty belfry; a negative-volume bronze phantom. Archival photo and provenance plate at the edge, not dominant.
**Audio:** Complete silence at expected toll; after pause, an explicitly reconstructed spectral ghost, imperfect as memory.
**Evidence:** Wartime bell requisitions/loss and local restoration documented; actual prewar audio must not be invented or misrepresented.

### 06 — Hearing Geography
**On-screen:** EVERY BELL DRAWS A MAP THAT YOU CAN'T SEE.
**Image:** Sound-propagation isochrones travel around actual terrain, valley, roofs, rivers, bridges; wind blows and bends their edge. A transparent diagram is subordinate to the physical illustrated town.
**Audio:** Player moves from clear/direct to obstructed/far-field bell; separate room/outdoor decay.
**Evidence:** Propagation approximation is physics-based and modeled, not a guaranteed 1:1 survey.

### 07 — The Second Map
**On-screen:** AND THEN THERE IS THE MAP WE INSIST IS THERE.
**Image:** Ley alignments, old ritual paths, torchlights and stone circles drawn as a second ink/constellation layer over the actual audible area. The maps partly intersect, partly contradict.
**Audio:** Diegetic toll remains; speculative ritual/egregore pulse enters as an identified composition layer.
**Narrative:** Historical sacred geography vs contemporary spiritual interpretations—two stories, not one empirical dataset.

### 08 — Earth Frequency / No Earth Organ
**On-screen:** THE EARTH HAS A PULSE. WE HAVE AN INTERPRETATION.
**Image:** Lightning animates an Earth-ionosphere cavity; next frame explicitly transforms the non-acoustic signal into a visual 7.83-cycle cue and slow/visible subsampling.
**Audio:** Musical tone with a 7.83 Hz artistic modulation or stereo beat difference; never claim audible electromagnetic recording.
**Evidence:** Schumann modes are electromagnetic ELF, not a 7.83 Hz acoustic carrier from a bell.
**Option:** headphone binaural vs loudspeaker isochronic fallback.

### 09 — Bowls / Water / Material Memory
**On-screen:** A SOUND YOU CAN TOUCH.
**Image:** A bowl rubs into motion; water ripples on its rim; a Chladni-inspired clay tabletop explores actual nodal mathematics. Macro lens, wet ceramic, textured gold.
**Audio:** Isolated singing bowl rub/strike vs tower bell clapper; dynamic resonant friction and partial evolution.
**Caution:** true mechanical mode simulation is different from aesthetically similar generated visuals.

### 10 — The Impossible Campanile
**On-screen:** BUILD THE BELLS THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST.
**Image:** Cathedral assembled from irregular suspended bell bodies, hollow spires, folded stairways and otherworldly clay portals. Each bell physically fits one of three distinct silhouettes, rather than an array of generic buttons.
**Audio:** One real attack with synthesized impossible tail; user's relative movement changes the heard spectrum, room and occlusion.
**Interaction:** Strike / rotate / damp / tune partials / move in space / commit to a field. Controls are diegetic levers and bell ropes.

### 11 — The Egregore Choir
**On-screen:** WHAT A CROWD BELIEVES BECOMES WHAT IT HEARS.
**Image:** A public square's inhabitants cast collective aural threads toward the tower. Some thread groups form guardian geometries; others organize a threatening curfew lattice.
**Audio:** Multiple locally spaced tolls merge into a social chord, without masking the listener's critical signals.
**Narrative:** Egregore as KFB's fictional collective memory/attention system, NOT biological or electromagnetic measurement.

## Slide evidence/tone rules
Photographic/archival claims need original location/date and reuse rights, while the imagined KFB clay surreal imagery is explicitly authored artwork. For each later generated slide include source object proof or own illustration, audio cue ID, art direction notes, and a status marker: DOCUMENTED / RECONSTRUCTED / MYTH / KFB FICTION. Do not use placeholder wireframes or generic stock bells as donor proof. No reference images have been licensed, rendered or adopted in R1.

## Creative direction — visual and sonic
Visual: bronze abrasion, oxidized green, wet stone, warped clay cathedral architecture, broken leaded glass, monochrome spectral ink, deliberately theatrical surrealism. Audio: truly recognisable bell attacks, restrained environmental space, individually measured decays, sudden silence, warped ritual time. Slow pace and sparse layering; preserve acoustic grandeur rather than wall-to-wall fake 'magical' pads.
