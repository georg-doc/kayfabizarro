# Claude Design · KFB Track Look T3 · Golden World/Track Seam

## Why this is a restart

T1/T2 is a total visual rejection. Freeze it as failure evidence. Do not patch its colour map, barriers, signs, supports or composition.

The upstream mistake was to ask for a track look as if the track were a standalone object. It is not. The race surface is one route through the same living KlayfaBizarro clay world as its terrain, OSM-derived blocks, buildings, sidewalks, residents and props.

Your task is therefore **not** “design a cool track”. Your task is:

> Prove one visually coherent seam where an ordinary KlayfaBizarro city street becomes a playful stunt/race track without leaving the Hirnwelt clay language.

## Positive north star · colourful, alive, harmoniously weird

The result must be **colourful, living, bent and cartoon-weird**, while still feeling designed rather than random. The benchmark mix is:

- `Claybound` for tactile clay quality, material scale and handmade confidence;
- `KFB Knet-Katalog K1` and `KFB Hirnwelt H0` for the actual KFB colour, building and world grammar;
- the elastic suburban oddness and asymmetrical silhouettes associated with *Rocko's Modern Life*;
- the cheerful underwater colour rhythm and readable shape language associated with *SpongeBob SquarePants*;
- the physical stop-motion construction and fingerprints associated with *Wallace & Gromit*;
- the immediate route readability, toy energy and celebratory accent colours associated with *Super Mario Kart*.

These are mood and design references, not an instruction to copy characters, logos or a specific frame. Translate them into KFB's own forms:

- gently leaning, squeezed or bowed building silhouettes;
- windows and doors with varied but rhythmic sizes and positions;
- curved corners, chunky edges and visible hand-shaped joins;
- saturated accents held together by calmer road, sidewalk and terrain masses;
- small surreal surprises and landmark shapes that remain readable at driving speed;
- a world that feels as if it could subtly breathe, wobble and bounce, without deforming the drivable contact surface.

Reject both extremes: no beige/grey museum maquette, and no uncontrolled rainbow applied independently to every part. Weirdness comes from silhouette, rhythm, scale contrast and a few deliberate colour events.

## Source hierarchy · highest wins

1. **Georg's accepted KFB clay intent, Knet-Katalog K1 and the H0 Hirnwelt images.** These control massing, colour relationships, clay scale, façade softness, cartoon bend and overall charm.
2. **Current World M2A context.** It controls world scale, terrain, OSM route/footprint anchors, player scale, light and camera context. It is the scene in which the result must work.
3. **Real KayKit/Kenney/Tiny Treats source parts.** Buildings, doors, windows, awnings and props are donors. They may be softened, scaled, recoloured and rhythmically repeated; they may not be replaced by invented generic slits or generic graphic shapes.
4. **Existing Track Core streams and road relief.** These control route geometry, width and surface continuity. Keep the accepted road relief. They do not grant freedom to invent a separate visual universe.
5. T1/T2 technical files are donors only for stream reading, role sections, deterministic patches, tunnel rings and measurement views. Their visual composition has no authority.

OSM is a useful anchor for routes, plots and city proportions, not a visual master and not a command to reproduce bad real-world geometry. Chill, fun, readability and cartoon driving may improve the route, but the join back into the world must remain plausible.

## One bounded output

Build exactly one `GOLDEN_WORLD_TRACK_SEAM_T3` scene:

- 60–100 metres of connected route;
- one OSM-derived city street segment;
- clay driving surface with preserved, subtle road relief;
- one readable curb and sidewalk system on both sides;
- one transition into a wider race/stunt section;
- one gentle ramp or playful vertical gesture, not a monumental sculpture;
- a short row of three buildings at different but harmonious heights;
- real donor-derived windows/doors/façade rhythm;
- terrain/grass, two or three restrained props and enough world context to judge scale;
- FrizzleBob or the current player-scale proxy and one vehicle for scale only.

No second option and no three-look chooser. Make one coherent proposal from the established world grammar.

## Visual grammar

- Read the scene as a few large clay masses first: terrain, buildings, street/track, sidewalk/curb, sky.
- Colours belong to those masses and their value hierarchy, not to every technical mesh slot.
- Use the measured K1/H0/Claybound palette family. It may be vivid and playful, but must be harmonised across large world masses; do not invent an unrelated racing palette.
- Fingerprints, dents and tool marks must be scaled to the object: broad and sparse on terrain/buildings, finer on vehicle/player-scale objects. They must not look like giant worms, stickers or noise.
- Buildings retain the low-poly donor silhouette, softened into clay. Windows and doors look pressed into or assembled with the façade, not pasted on as repeated black holes.
- The city street and race section share material, light and clay logic. Race identity comes from width, rhythm, kerb/edge anatomy, elevation and a few controlled accents—not red/white striping everywhere.
- Curbs are pale clay courses with clear joints. Sidewalk and road are related greys with readable separation. Grass/terrain meets the sidewalk cleanly.
- Supports, barriers and tunnel mouths require cartoon anatomy: a believable base, load path and silhouette. No anonymous cylinders, thin caps or floating trim.
- The road remains drivable and visually calm. Never deform the actual contact surface into waves or bulges.

## Explicitly forbidden

- no freestanding track on an empty or black table;
- no continuation of T1/T2's visual tree;
- no red/white pattern duplicated across kerb, wall and cap;
- no independent colour per mesh slot;
- no giant fingerprints, clay ropes or inflated sausages;
- no bright cap glued onto a brown wall;
- no generic arrow stickers, chevrons, billboards or replacement branding;
- no invented window slits when source façade donors exist;
- no new HUD, control panel or explanatory interface;
- no technical PASS claim without the integrated scene being visibly convincing.

## Required proof

Return only these three fixed views of the same scene:

1. **World view:** street, buildings, terrain and track seam together.
2. **Near view:** curb, sidewalk, façade rhythm and material scale.
3. **Ride view:** driving surface, edge readability and forward route silhouette.

Place the H0 Hirnwelt reference beside the World view during review. The first question is: “Does this belong to the same handmade world?” Technical panels and greyscale diagnostics may exist behind an info toggle, never in the main field of view.

Stop after this one scene. If it does not read as the same world, export the result plus three screenshots and a short problem-first Return. Do not patch the rejected direction, produce more options or spend another session polishing details.
