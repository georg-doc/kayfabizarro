## Musical direction

Trait extraction, not imitation:

- **CAN:** hypnotic human repetition, rhythm-section ownership, long-form subtraction/addition, groove that evolves without constant harmonic changes.
- **Yello:** sparse cinematic sound design, powerful low-end, dry foreground vs spacious background, memorable rare events, production as composition.
- **Electro-funk / e-funk:** syncopated bass/drum pocket, clav/Rhodes/organ punctuation, restrained 16th-note energy, natural future ROAD translation.

Target:
**hypnotic human groove + cinematic weird sound design + electro-funk pocket.**

## Reddit constraints adopted for the first proof

Keep it deliberately simple:
- rhythm first;
- only **3 core rhythm sound classes**;
- only **3 rhythm patterns** for the whole first piece;
- melodic material restricted to a **pentatonic core**;
- one short hook/motif only;
- repetition is a feature;
- variations primarily by subtraction, re-entry and scale-safe transposition around ±7 / ±2 semitone classes;
- explicit silence/dropouts;
- production/reverb/space matter as much as notes.
