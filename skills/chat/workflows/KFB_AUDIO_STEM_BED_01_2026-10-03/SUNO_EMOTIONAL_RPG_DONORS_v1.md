**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Purpose:** Fill missing emotional / biome / event coverage for the KFB adaptive stem pool.  
**Use:** Generate masters first. Pull stems only for winners.

## Shared technical constraints

Prefer these properties across all four donors:
- instrumental only;
- clear separation between drums, bass, keys/harmony, guitar/color, percussion and synth/other;
- avoid dense orchestral wall-of-sound;
- avoid xylophone, marimba, vibraphone, bells and cute/cartoon mallet leads;
- avoid constant arpeggiators;
- avoid vocals / chants unless explicitly requested later;
- avoid trailer-boom cliché, EDM riser/drop and overcompressed mastering;
- preserve negative space for gameplay SFX / voice;
- clean 8/16-bar structure and obvious section boundaries;
- end with a loop-compatible or cleanly tailing final section if possible.

Where musically natural, keep a **D tonal center** to make later compatibility analysis easier. Do not force cross-song pitched-stem mixing without key/chord verification.

## E1 · DEMON LORD CHASM · dark boss combat

**Suggested BPM:** 108  
**Suggested tonal center:** D minor / D Phrygian color

108 BPM instrumental dark psychedelic electro-funk battle groove for a tense boss fight deep in a volcanic cave chasm. The rhythm section must remain physical, funky and playable rather than turning into generic trailer music: heavy dry kick and tom accents, tight snare/cross-stick, restrained metallic hand percussion, syncopated electric/synth bass with a dangerous repeating two-bar riff, distorted low clavinet and Hammond-style organ stabs, sparse tremolo or fuzz guitar answers, deep analog synth pressure and rare brass-like or industrial hits. Build tension through repetition, subtraction, harmonic dissonance and rhythmic displacement rather than constant new melody. Use a D-minor / Phrygian tonal center with a short threatening pentatonic/minor hook that appears only at major structural moments. Include 8/16-bar phases: stalking groove, full confrontation, brief near-silence before impact, heavier return, final escalation. Sound design should include occasional reverse-metal hit, subharmonic swell, tape-stop fragment or cavernous delay throw, but leave space for sword impacts, creature roars and dialogue. Dark, funky, weird, danceable at the core, dramatic without heroic Hollywood orchestration. Warm analog saturation, controlled low end, wide cavern ambience behind a dry rhythm foreground. Instrumental only. No choir, no vocals, no xylophone/mallet leads, no EDM build/drop, no cinematic riser spam.

**Stem priority:** Drums, Bass, Keyboard/Organ/Clav, Guitar, Percussion, Synth/Other. Brass/FX optional.

## E2 · EMPTY CHAIR AT DUSK · grief / NPC loss

**Suggested BPM:** 68–72  
**Suggested tonal center:** D minor / D Dorian

70 BPM instrumental psychedelic soul-funk elegy for a quiet RPG passage after the loss of a beloved NPC. Sad, intimate and warm, never sentimental or melodramatic. Begin with spacious low Rhodes/Wurlitzer voicings and a simple rounded electric-bass phrase with long rests; add very soft brushed drums or muted hand percussion only after the emotional space is established. Mellow organ sustains underneath, sparse clean/tremolo guitar answers with only one or two notes at a time, occasional low analog synth breath or tape texture. Use a D-minor / Dorian tonal center and one tiny minor-pentatonic motif that returns after long absences, sometimes lower or transposed by a scale-safe step/fifth. Structure through silence, held chords, bass pauses and small timbral changes every 8/16 bars. Include one section where almost everything drops out except room/tape ambience and a single harmonic voice, then let the bass and soft groove return gently. The piece should feel reflective and human but still belong to the same funky KFB universe. Warm tape saturation, close mix, soft spring/plate tails, restrained stereo width, mellow dynamics. Instrumental only. No piano-ballad cliché, no sweeping strings, no triumphant resolution, no bright bells/mallets, no vocals.

**Stem priority:** Bass, Keyboard, Organ/Synth, Guitar, minimal Drums/Percussion. Strings only if extremely sparse and low.

## E3 · SUNSHINE COMMONS · happy / festival / disco

**Suggested BPM:** 104  
**Suggested tonal center:** D Mixolydian / D major pentatonic

104 BPM instrumental sunshine electro-funk / psychedelic disco groove for a joyful town festival, successful quest return or lively social biome. Bright and danceable but still relaxed enough for gameplay. Tight dry kick/snare with restrained open/closed hats and shaker, buoyant rounded electric bass with a memorable two-bar groove, warm Rhodes/Wurlitzer and clavinet offbeat stabs, mellow organ support, muted clean guitar or light wah-style rhythmic scratches, occasional analog synth sparkle used as color rather than lead. Use a D Mixolydian / major-pentatonic center, simple harmony and a tiny optimistic hook that appears only every 8–16 bars. Build variety through percussion changes, bass articulation, dropouts, filter openings and layer swaps rather than busy melody. Include a short disco-lift section with stronger four-on-the-floor implication, then return to the looser funk pocket; preserve 1-beat and 1-bar gaps for NPC/SFX moments. Warm tape/console saturation, intimate groove, soft stereo width, playful but not cheesy. Instrumental only. No vocals, no cheesy horn section, no bright mallet lead, no glossy EDM drop, no festival-clap cliché, no constant synth arpeggio.

**Stem priority:** Drums, Bass, Keyboard/Clav, Guitar, Percussion, Organ/Synth. Brass only if subtle and rhythmic.

## E4 · ANCIENT MACHINE AWAKENS · mystery / awe / revelation

**Suggested BPM:** 82  
**Suggested tonal center:** D Dorian with Lydian color

82 BPM instrumental cosmic psychedelic funk soundscape for discovering an ancient machine, sacred ruin, alien portal or impossible vista. Awe and mystery first, but with a slow hypnotic body pulse underneath so the piece still belongs to KFB. Sparse dry kick and low percussion establish a patient repeating pattern; rounded bass holds a modal two-bar figure with long gaps; warm electric piano and organ use suspended/extended voicings; muted guitar or processed plucked-string fragments appear rarely; analog synth layers move slowly in the background with phaser/Leslie/filter motion. Use a D Dorian tonal center with occasional raised-fourth/Lydian color for wonder, without turning into fantasy-orchestral music. One tiny motif should emerge gradually, disappear for 16 bars, then return in a different register. Build the form by revealing timbres: bed → pulse → bass → harmony → strange color → near-silence → full awe state → recovery. Treat sound design as a fifth instrument: rare resonant metal/stone hit, reversed texture, distant radio-like fragment, spectral swell or dub-delay orbit, each surrounded by space. Warm analog/tape texture, deep but controlled reverb, dry foreground pulse, immersive stereo depth. Instrumental only. No choir, no vocals, no cinematic boom/riser, no bright bells/mallets, no constant arpeggiator, no heroic melody.

**Stem priority:** Bass, Drums/Percussion, Keyboard/Organ, Synth/Other/FX, Guitar/Color. Woodwinds/Brass only if sparse and atmospheric.

## Generation strategy

Generate **2 variants per prompt** first.

Listen to the masters only and score each on:
1. musicality / would I leave it playing?
2. clear emotional identity;
3. enough negative space for game SFX / voice;
4. useful 8/16-bar evolution;
5. stem-separable instrumentation;
6. compatibility with KFB's funky/psychedelic identity.

Pull stems only from the winning version(s).

Recommended total:
- 8 new masters;
- likely 4 winning masters;
- stems for 2–4 winners, not all 8.

When exporting stems, preserve the BPM-normalized folder name and master side-by-side.
