Welcome, dear Fluffheads!

Ever heard of The Utopian Mirage? Skip a turn staring at the clouds and discard a resource into Dystopia; your fictional default rests on somebody else's dystopia.

The Dystopian Apathy freezes the board for a round while you collect three profit tokens from a burning bank: a cautionary tale turned escapism and a disaster capitalism roadmap.

Do you know The Extractive Status-Quo? Steal 1 resource from everyone else to mitigate damage, then call the taking sustainability while the damage continues.

The Protopian Process says repair one broken board tile and draw a card: proactive prototyping instead of waiting, with reciprocity as the way to tackle inequities.

Ever heard of The Pop Futurist? Instantly silence all critical inquiry cards, then evangelize a singular technology as a magic pill; critical inquiry would only spoil the sales pitch.

The Academic Gatekeeper charges an opponent from the Global South 70k tokens just to play, then says you are not a futurist unless you buy their models.

The Tech Singularity Savior destroys 3 worker-ants for one Tech Ego point, selling the very tech-optimism that, in this card's lore, brought us to the absolute brink.

Do you know The Doom-Monger? Blast the megaphone until all players lose their next action phase; a binary collapse story does not mobilize society, it produces total inaction.

The Disaster Capitalist gains +5 Profit whenever a fire or flood costs another player resources; the world is burning, and the card asks how much profit that suffering makes.

Ever heard of The Tokenizing Crypto-Bro? Convert someone else's free action into a paid micro-transaction, because apparently humans need a financial incentive even to act.

The Philanthro-Capitalist manufactures 10 Toxic Waste tokens, donates one to repair a reputation score, and calls fossil fuels plus charity corporate social responsibility.

The Air Purifier Economy says privatize the commons: pay one token every turn just to draw a card, until only the wealthy can afford to breathe.

Do you know The Long-Termist? Ignore present board damage while banking victory points for imaginary games a billion years away; present climate impacts can wait for imaginary lives.

The Effective Altruist sacrifices a low-tier opponent card to fund AI Armageddon research, insisting that is better charity than feeding a starving child.
