```text
My grandfather took this kingdom with a sword. My father kept it with an army. I inherited a throne and three empty Scene slots.

Yesterday a farmer walked into court with a card.

"The throne is for sale."
"Treason," I said.

Second card.
"It's market day."
"Still treason. Now with customers."

Third card.
"The King is the auctioneer."

The Stage was full. So I played a replacement: "The throne is NOT for sale."
Nobody objected. They all looked relieved.

Then the butcher asked me to open the bidding on the palace.

"That's not for sale either."
He tapped the second card.
"Market day, Your Majesty."

First offer: two turnips.
I refused.
He offered three.

I banged the royal scepter on the table.
"Sold!"

They took the palace. They let me keep the throne.

I asked why.

"You're the auctioneer," said the butcher. "You'll need somewhere to stand."
```
