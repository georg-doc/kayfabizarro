### Critical incompatibilities / hard clamps
1. `South Park Logic`, `Wordacrobat` and `Auditor` sometimes demand **THEREFORE/BUT on every sequence**. For KFB the relation-type gate applies: `CUT`, `PAUSE`, `UPTAKE`, `THEMATIC_RHYME` or `CAUSAL_BRIDGE`. Only the last asserts a source-proven causal state transition. Source Card beats are interchangeable and must not acquire mandatory edge dependencies.
2. NIE `story_beats.md` says a scene without a turn is not a beat. This is a **scene-design heuristic**, incompatible as a strict fail condition with an independently understandable absurd KFB Card fact or silence. Preserve it as optional diagnostic, not runtime acceptance.
3. NIE `Auditor` prefers syllable targets and automatic "insert connectors"/"cut 20–30%" rewrites; KFB's hard acceptance is **original audible title + source POWER/Lore representation + truthful provenance**. Do not let elegance of meter overwrite Card truth or the user's 90%-content-preservation goal. Exact original-token retention and semantic-fact coverage are distinct measurements.
4. NIE `Roastmaster` requires kicker and escalation; Georg explicitly made final punchline/closure **optional**. Leave original authored absurd outcome, maybe pause.
5. NIE `Banterbuddy` proposes mandatory insults and "AI superior" jokes; user expects NO AI QUIRK / NO MEME LOGIC / NO FILLER, and most person-focused insult/memetic devices are unsuitable. Keep it OFF by default; don't route it as global KFB dialog persona.
6. NIE research-to-satire pipeline legitimately generates new creative interpretations during authoring, but **not source facts**. A real researcher's original worldview is kept in PR #394 as an attributed source, not a KFB NPC factual authority or newly invented quote. A KFB Resident may perform a worldview lens as fiction only after provenance is ready.

On a real player utterance/other Resident claim, select separate `TURN_UPTAKE` with `previousUtteranceRef`, exact quoted term and `SAID/MEANT/WANTED` metadata; choose reply speech act (answer, refuse, mirror, repair, counter-question, silence). NIE Authentic Dialogue QA spots unnatural symmetry, compulsory cleverness and over-exposition.

**C · Solo monologue**
NIE `Sprechendes Denken`, cadence and irony are optional **delivery masks**. A short thought pause or cut may replace full cause→payoff; first-person claims require approved fictional speaker biography or explicit stage fiction. No duplicate speech synthesizer or invented voice clip duration.

Only if before-state, triggering action and changed stakes are grounded may a `CAUSAL_BRIDGE` be emitted. Long-set callback and narrative-debt tracking may add continuity but never makes the individual Beat dependent on prior set context. Every Card ends as its own clip/cut unit when actual audio is rendered.
