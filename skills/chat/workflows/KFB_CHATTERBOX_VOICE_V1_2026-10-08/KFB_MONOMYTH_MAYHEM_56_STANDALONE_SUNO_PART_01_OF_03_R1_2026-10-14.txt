[Spoken Word]

Welcome, dear Fluffheads. Monomyth Mayhem: The Journey.

The Ordinary World cancels all Quest progress for a turn and demands an explanation of why nothing can change; dysfunction has become such a habit that the extraordinary seems impossible.

Ever heard of The Call to Adventure? Raise the Quest Die by one and make another player introduce a conflict that wrecks their plans; the universe doesn't knock, it throws a brick.

Refusal of the Call pauses the Quest for one turn so everyone can explain why they can't go; fear calls that self-preservation, and the familiar self is terrible at math.

Meeting the Mentor lets you draw two cards and give one away; wisdom only works when shared, because the Mentor gives you the map, not the legs.

Do you know Crossing the Threshold? Move the Quest Die two forward, discard your hand and draw three; the old world is gone, because commitment makes fear into movement and you cannot un-take the step.

Belly of the Whale makes everybody discard a card and suspends Quest progress for a turn before doubling the Quest Die; you're no longer who you were and not yet who you'll become.

Road of Trials asks for three small problems, adding one to your next roll for every fictional solution; recovery is practice, and the survivor isn't the hero who started.

Meeting the Goddess asks you to narrate acceptance and adds one to the Quest Die for every player who agrees you deserve it; being fully seen means accepting the opposites within.

Ever heard of Woman as Temptress? Tempt another player to discard a Safety card for a reward, but take two off the Quest Die; the familiar cage feels safer than the unknown field.

Atonement with the Father confronts an Authority card: destroy it or integrate it, with different results; to find the authentic 'want,' you must face the internal 'should.'

Apotheosis discards your Actor Marker and lets you intervene in any scene as a nameless force; the caterpillar is liquid, and the butterfly is inevitable.

The Ultimate Boon gives you a permanent Treasure token for one automatic Quest success; the Elixir isn't the object but the internal shift it represents.

Do you know Refusal of the Return? Your Actor may end the game without coming back; after touching the transcendent, the ordinary world looks terrible by comparison.

The Magic Flight ends the Quest and rolls a die: even keeps the Boon, odd loses it in transit; old patterns won't release you, and the ego follows to the threshold.

Rescue from Without supplies a stuck player with the next card they need automatically; you cannot do it alone, because community matters and independence can be a trauma response.

Crossing the Return Threshold gives one drawn card to you and another to a neighbor, with the gift explained in character; the hard part is staying changed back in the old context.

Master of Two Worlds plays cards from the Discard Pile as though they're in your hand; spiritual and mundane no longer split you, because you're both.

Freedom to Live turns your Actor into a Mentor who cannot lose and can just vibe; free of the tyranny of fear, you're alive and that's enough.

Stay Fluffy!
