## Usage / no fake quality claim

These 56 cuts are deterministic **full-source recital beats** with a short original-title opener. Every line contains verbatim original POWER and LORE, and the title is spoken. They can be cut/reordered independently without a semantic Bridge, but they are not yet 56 smoothly rewritten *semantic* Triplets or 56 artistically approved comedy routines. User-requested ~90% original content retention is achieved at 100% literal original fields in THIS archival voice draft; that does not guarantee speakability, scientific validity of attribution, or humor.

Each Card Beat is **one physical text line**, separated from the next by exactly **one blank line**. The final spoken line in each autonomous Suno part is `Stay Fluffy!`. To create ONE complete full-deck master, concatenate both parts and remove the first part's `[End]` and `Stay Fluffy!` plus second part's `[Spoken Word]` and greeting; then only one finale remains. Exact beat ID/order data is pinned separately in the accompanying JSON; no audio timing or individual clip generated.

Solo English spoken-word stand-up set; characterful, conversational and clear natural enunciation. Dry a cappella voice, no singing, no music, no instruments, no backing track. FRANS DE WAAL. Reconciliation: Restore peace after a conflict scene. "Empathy is biological, not a choice. The Age of Empathy."

DIGITAL DISTRACTION. Scroll Doom: Skip your turn to draw a card. We are alone together. Connection without demand. (Ref: Turkle)
