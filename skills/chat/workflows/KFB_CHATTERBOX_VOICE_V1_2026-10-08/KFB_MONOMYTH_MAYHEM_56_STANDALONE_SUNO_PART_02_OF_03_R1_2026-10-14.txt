Welcome back, dear Fluffheads. The Characters and the Loop.

The Anti-Journey cancels a Call to Adventure so nothing happens and the story ends before it begins; refusing the call until death isn't a moral failure, just a statistical reality.

Ever heard of The Circular Journey? Reset the Quest Die to three and start exactly the same story with the knowledge you kept; the spiral goes up or down, but it definitely goes around.

The Hero rerolls the Quest Die once each turn; the transforming ego vehicle is often confused, usually earnest and always sure it's the main character.

The Shadow makes another player face the negative consequence meant for you; the antagonist is the part of yourself you haven't accepted yet.

The Shapeshifter changes the Suit or Type of a card on Stage; with loyalty this ambiguous, is it love or strategy?

Do you know The Herald? It automatically starts a fresh Quest when the last one ends; this Wake Up Call is annoying, but structurally necessary.

Threshold Guardian blocks another player's Stage card until they justify it in character; if you can't get past the doorman, you don't get in.

The Trickster swaps the hands of two players and laughs; chaos upsets the status quo, reveals truth through absurdity and gets very annoying at parties.

The Ally adds one to someone else's die roll; everyone needs a Samwise to carry the load when they can't.

The Failed Journey collapses the Quest so nobody gets a Boon; an ordeal can break you, trauma can win and not every story gets a happy ending.

Ever heard of Quest Die? Set its limits to one and six; if the die tips, reality breaks, because chaos is a valid game state.

BLÖDSINN! stops the narrator to clarify and fix a logic failure, then laugh; it's a reset button, not an accusation, and laughter breaks nothing.

Kayfabe treats a lie as truth for the rest of the game; staged events become real when everybody works the gimmick.

Uncle FrizzleBob can change one game rule for a single turn; he didn't finish this game, because that part's yours.

Mood — Fearful stops you from starting a Quest and leaves you only reacting; the cave you fear holds the treasure, but the cave is very dark.

Mood — Reckless forces you to play the first card you touch without thinking; sometimes that's bravery, sometimes it's a wall.

Mood — Grieving means you cannot win a Boon this turn; loss is the price of love, and the only way out is through.

Do you know Mood — Manic? Play two cards in one turn and speak twice as fast; that's energy without direction, a brilliant disaster.

Stay Fluffy!
