## Cut-up contract: three speaking positions, at least four source roles
The current `subject / connector / reframe` tuple survives **as an output container**, not as a mandate to fill it with abstract principles.

1. **CARD ATOM:** original deck ID, title, deckFunction, source file/blob, original card number when present OR source ordinal, card title, complete power, lore, artworkPrompt; preserve each text independently with distinct attribution. No new static copy of all 4,119 cards.
2. **SOURCE SLICE:** a *contiguous exact excerpt* of original `cardName/name`, `power`, or `lore`. Store source field, exact starting offset and original text. No general-purpose LLM paraphrasing, no untraceable catchphrase.
3. **CARD-TEXT/IMAGE SHEAR:** keep actual card visual as a fourth, **nonspoken** semantic rail. Record the possible contradiction between what the written Power makes the player do, Lore implies, and the image appears to show. Human review must see real artwork; artworkPrompt alone may differ from printed result.
4. **CUT-UP / COLLISION:** intentionally combine distinct card slices across decks. Keep source identities of all contributing cards; stage/reader performs closure (KayfabeTips: `but/therefore`, yes-and, specific people and actions). Do **not** glue three random excerpts with an invented explanatory conjunction and call it coherent.
5. **NPC BIAS:** existing resident world view and actual witnessed Card/event may select eligible source atoms and appropriate register. No omniscient political narrator; a speaker may be biased, wrong or personally implicated. Do not automatically assign all deck lore as a character's knowledge.
6. **QUALITY GATES:** source exactness, contextual performability, RULE1 (No AI Quirk. No Meme Logic. No Filler.), then actual human/visible audio review. A joke is not guaranteed by three valid strings or by an internal card `grade=1`.
