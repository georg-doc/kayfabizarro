Welcome, dear Fluffheads. Monomyth Mayhem — full original card text for editorial comparison.

The Ordinary World. Stasis Loop. Play this to cancel any Quest progression this turn. Narrate why nothing can change. Dysfunction isn't just a state; it's a habit. We normalize the abnormal until the extraordinary seems impossible.

The Call to Adventure. Cognitive Dissonance. Increase Quest Die by +1. Force another player to introduce a conflict that disrupts their current plans. The universe doesn't knock. It throws a brick. The disruption of normalcy is always the beginning.

Refusal of the Call. Rational Panic. Pause the Quest for one turn. All players must explain why they can't possibly go on the adventure. Fear is realistic self-preservation. Change means death of the familiar self. The familiar self is terrible at math.

Meeting the Mentor. External Hard Drive. Draw 2 cards. Give 1 to another player. Wisdom is useless until it's shared. The Mentor cannot go with you. They provide the map, not the legs.

Crossing the Threshold. Point of No Return. Move Quest Die +2. Discard your hand and draw 3 new cards. The old world is gone. Commitment is the moment fear becomes movement. You can't un-take the step.

Belly of the Whale. Digestion. All players discard 1 card. No Quest progress this turn. Next turn, the Quest Die doubles. Liminal space. No longer who you were, not yet who you'll become.

Road of Trials. Grindset. Narrate 3 small problems. For each one you 'solve' in the fiction, gain +1 to your next roll. Recovery is practice. The hero who survives the trials is not the hero who entered them.

Meeting the Goddess. Self-Love Bomb. Narrate a moment of acceptance. Quest Die +1 for every player who agrees you deserve it. The experience of being fully seen. The internal integration of opposites into unconditional love.

Woman as Temptress. Regression Pull. Tempt another player to discard a 'Safety' card. If they do, they gain a reward but lose 2 on the Quest Die. The siren song of comfort. The familiar cage feels safer than the unknown field.

Atonement with the Father. Daddy Issues. Identify an Authority Card on the Stage. Destroy it or Integrate it. Both options work differently. Confront the source of authority. Kill the internal 'should' to find the authentic 'want.'

Apotheosis. Ego Death. Discard your Actor Marker. You are now a Formlessness. You may intervene in any scene as a nameless force. The caterpillar is liquid. The butterfly is inevitable.

The Ultimate Boon. The Elixir. Gain a permanent 'Treasure' token. Use it once to auto-succeed on any Quest roll. Integration of the lesson. It's not the object, it's the internal shift the object represents.

Refusal of the Return. Stay in Narnia. You may choose to end the game for your Actor here. They do not return. They transcend. Why go back to the mundane when you've touched the transcendent? The ordinary world is terrible by comparison.

The Magic Flight. Escape Velocity. End the Quest immediately. Roll a D6. Even: You keep the Boon. Odd: The Boon is lost in transit. Old patterns don't release easily. The ego will chase you to the threshold.

Rescue from Without. Intervention. Play this on another player who is stuck. You automatically provide the next card they need. You can't do it all alone. Community is necessary. Independence is a trauma response.

Crossing the Return Threshold. Re-Entry Shock. Draw 1 card for yourself, give 1 to a neighbor. The gift must be explained in character. The hardest stage. Can you maintain your transformation while re-entering the old context?

Master of Two Worlds. Code Switch. You may play cards from the Discard Pile as if they were in your hand for this turn. No split between 'spiritual' and 'mundane.' You are whole. You are both.

Freedom to Live. Zen Mode. Your Actor becomes a Mentor. You cannot lose. You just vibe. Freedom from the tyranny of fear. You're alive. That's enough.

The Anti-Journey. Tragedy of Stagnation. Cancel a 'Call to Adventure.' Nothing happens. The story ends before it begins. Many refuse the call until they die. This is not a moral failure, it is a statistical reality.

The Circular Journey. Eternal Recurrence. Reset the Quest Die to [3]. Start the exact same story over, but you know it now. The spiral goes up. Or down. But it definitely goes around.

The Hero. Main Character Energy. You may re-roll the Quest Die once per turn. The one who transforms. The ego vehicle. Often confused, usually earnest, always the protagonist.

The Shadow. Projection. Force another player to face a negative consequence you were supposed to face. The dark mirror. The antagonist is just the part of you that you haven't accepted yet.

The Shapeshifter. Trust Issues. Change the 'Suit' or 'Type' of any card on the Stage. Loyalty is ambiguous. Keeps the hero off-balance. Is it love or is it strategy?

The Herald. Wake Up Call. Play immediately when a Quest ends to start a new one automatically. Brings the Call. The Inciting Incident personified. Annoying, but structurally necessary.

Threshold Guardian. Gatekeeping. Block a player from playing a card on the Stage unless they justify it in character. Tests the hero's resolve. If you can't get past the doorman, you don't get in.

The Trickster. Chaos Agent. Swap the hands of two players. Laugh. Disrupts the status quo. Reveals truth through absurdity. Also, very annoying at parties.

The Ally. Emotional Support. Add +1 to any die roll made by another player. You need a Samwise. Someone to carry the load when you can't.

The Failed Journey. The Bad Ending. Collapse the Quest immediately. No one gets a Boon. The Ordeal can break you. Trauma can win. Not all stories have happy endings.

Quest Die. Pressure Cooker. Use this card to set the limits [1] and [6]. If it tips, the reality breaks. Chaos is a valid game state. If the die tips, the reality breaks.

BLÖDSINN!. Nonsense Check. Play anytime. Force the narrator to pause, clarify, and fix a logic failure. Then laugh. 'It is not an accusation. It is a reset button. Laughter breaks nothing.'

Kayfabe. Suspension of Disbelief. Treat a lie as truth for the rest of the game. The portrayal of staged events as real. We are all working the gimmick.

Uncle FrizzleBob. The Author. Change one rule of the game for this turn only. 'I didn't finish this game. That part's yours. Stay fluffy.'

Mood — Fearful. Paralysis. You cannot initiate a Quest. You can only react. The cave you fear to enter holds the treasure. But the cave is very dark.

Mood — Reckless. Leeroy Jenkins. You must play the first card you touch. No thinking allowed. Action without thought. Sometimes it's bravery, sometimes it's a wall.

Mood — Grieving. Heavy Heart. You cannot win a Boon this turn. Loss is the price of love. The only way out is through.

Mood — Manic. Zoomies. Play 2 cards in one turn. Speak twice as fast. Energy without direction. A brilliant disaster.

Engineering. Structure. You may rearrange the order of the Scenes on the Stage. 'If it's stupid but it works, it's still stupid, but at least it works.'

Swordplay. Cut. Remove an unwanted card from the Stage. 'Sometimes the Gordian Knot just needs a sharp edge.'

Diplomacy. Chill. Prevent a 'Kayfa-BOOM' (Quest Collapse) by negotiating the Die back into safe range. 'The art of telling someone to go to hell in such a way that they look forward to the trip.'

Thievery. Yoink. Steal a card from another player's hand. 'Property is theft. Theft is also theft. It's complicated.'

Timeline Shift. Retcon. Undo the last turn completely. It never happened. The past is fluid. Memory is a creative act.

The Tenth Art. Edit. Change the text on a Quest card. Reality is a draft. Feel free to mark it up.

Multiverse. Parallel Play. Start a second Quest alongside the first one. Run them simultaneously. Why settle for one disaster when you can have infinite disasters?

Fractal Narrative. Zoom In. Play a card *inside* another card (place it underneath). It is a story within the story. The spiral goes down. Every story contains a smaller story waiting to unspool.

The Sword. Decisive Action. +2 to Quest Die. Resolves conflict violently. The symbol of power. Use it to cut the cord.

The Mirror. Reflection. Copy the effect of the last card played. Self-knowledge is the most dangerous weapon of all.

The Key. Access. Bypass a 'Threshold Guardian' without discarding. Opens doors. Also opens wounds. Be careful.

The Compass. Direction. Look at the top 3 cards of the deck. Arrange them. It doesn't tell you where to go, only where you are.
