# KFB Elisa 18 · Claymation Birthday Film v1 · 55-second Shot Plan

Target duration: **55 s**  
Hard ceiling: **60 s**  
Format: **16:9, 1920×1080** unless the final delivery surface requires another master  
Animation: browser JavaScript / Three.js + Canvas/CSS only as presentation layers; stop-motion pose stepping over deterministic capture  
Spoken language: **German** by default for this personal birthday film  
On-screen copy: minimal German; avoid subtitle walls

## Rhythm

The film is not eight unrelated sketches. It is one physical transformation:

**birthday card → Cologne paper map → SAE/snack block → commission desk → treasure map → dungeon plan → dungeon → loot → car fantasy → sunset party**

Paper folds, tears, hinges and map geometry carry the continuity. Clay objects punch through the paper world and cast real shadows onto it.

## Camera grammar

- paper-map sections: top/three-quarter camera, small deliberate parallax;
- character scenes: 35–55 mm equivalent, no floaty camera;
- dungeon: wider lens for entrance, tighter lens at treasure reveal;
- loot: object-centred turntable-like hero light but within the scene;
- stunt: one fast tracking move + one clean hero freeze;
- finale: modest pull-back that reveals band + sunset + rainbow.

Avoid:
- continuous orbit camera;
- random rack-focus spam;
- fake handheld noise;
- gratuitous speed ramps;
- auto-generated “cinematic” letterboxing unless the delivery master actually needs it.

## Claymation timing

Suggested:
- deterministic render/capture at 30 fps;
- actor body poses quantised around 10–15 fps depending on action;
- camera can remain smooth where needed;
- selective 1–2 frame holds on impacts / reveals;
- tiny non-looping clay settlement after contacts;
- do not quantise speech mouth animation so harshly that it becomes unreadable.

## Text policy

Text is physical whenever possible:
- printed on cards/maps;
- embossed/extruded clay for the “18” and perhaps the final name;
- no generic lower-thirds;
- no app-style panels;
- no animated gradient titles.

## Final emotional read

The last impression should be:
**“A new adult chapter can contain art, friends, practical independence and increasingly strange adventures.”**

Not:
- “believe in yourself” motivational copy;
- a résumé of Elisa;
- a KFB product demo;
- a game trailer disconnected from the birthday.
