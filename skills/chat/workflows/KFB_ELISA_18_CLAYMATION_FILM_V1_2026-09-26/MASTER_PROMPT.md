## CLAYMATION LOOK

All 3D donor assets should read as if rebuilt from hand-worked clay **without changing their authored identity**.

Material:
- matte to satin-matte;
- high roughness;
- soft broad speculars;
- subtle fingerprint / hand-smoothed microdetail;
- small non-repeating surface variation;
- restrained AO/contact darkening;
- slightly imperfect colour fields;
- no glossy injection-moulded plastic;
- no universal black outline unless an existing donor specifically calls for it.

Geometry:
- preserve source silhouette;
- preserve pivots;
- preserve bones/rig;
- preserve material-zone meaning;
- do not remesh or round everything into blobs merely to signal “clay”.

Lighting:
- tactile key + large soft fill;
- contact shadows matter;
- shallow warm bounce in cosy shots;
- dungeon can be darker but must retain readable colour;
- finale golden hour, not orange wash.

Clay motion:
- visible pose holds;
- tiny settling after impact;
- squash/stretch only where semantically useful;
- no permanent idle wobble on every object.

## PAPER ART / COLLAGE LOOK

Paper is an actual world layer:
- torn/cut masks;
- printed-map fragments;
- folds/hinges;
- slight thickness;
- cast shadows;
- visible fibres/grain if available;
- paper objects can lift into 3D or collapse back into flat plan view.

Avoid:
- generic scrapbook stickers;
- random washi tape;
- fake masking-tape corners on every asset;
- children’s craft clichés;
- “vintage paper” overlay across the entire render.

## OSM / CARTOON-ELASTIC-GROTESQUE MOTION
