## Reference sound scene
Scene `egregore-threshold` / `card-dream-transition`:
- 0–25s **REAL**: recognizable non-musical location/physical acoustic source, spatial foreground object/voice anchor, optional near-silence; KFB C music low or muted.
- 25–65s **VEIL**: coherent transition triggered by threshold/event; micro-grains/reversed/filtered echoes or harmonically related slowly detuned partials and spectral shift. Genuine sample remains detectable; perceivable change in distance/room shape. No random 'psychedelic preset'. 
- 65–95s **DREAM**: constrained non-repetitive slow movement and one meaning-bearing event (Card, quote or Resident fragment sourced from real content); optional harmonic hint from Sonus Bed, musical transition respects existing phrase clock.
- 95–120s **RETURN**: transformed sounds recede into original acoustic environment; narration/VOICE ducks score/ambience as current mixer prescribes while music timeline continues.
Times are an editable artistic reference not mandatory game-loop truth; adaptive triggers from current World authoritative events take precedence where later integrated.
