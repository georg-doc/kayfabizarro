### Triplet compression

`Empty darkness / still under observation / good patrol`

- visibility/surveillance field;
- short clauses;
- no metaphor required;
- reaction can become evidence later.

**PLAYER:** Why?

The line is a diegetic institutional belief, not world truth.

### What makes it his

Repair remains available; he is mocking performance, not destroying the wearer.
