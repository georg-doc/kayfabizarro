## Purpose
Provide the **mental model missing from isolated floating-island assembly**: a coherent comic galaxy of exploded, hovering land fragments with semantic clustering, individual silhouette, landmark recognition and meaningful travel. This is **not** a requirement to depict every world as a rounded stereotypical grassy floating island, nor approval to rewrite active four-island MVP geometry.

## World-origin story hypothesis — keep open rather than canon-lock
KFB Town, around King Kayfabian, is the central ancestral/origin island. Other fragments may once have shared a contiguous world before the Fluff Incident / a hypernormalization rupture fragmented social reality into a hovering asteroid-like debris constellation. This is **visual/metanarrative proposal**, not a settled chronology/cause; reconcile with current Fluff Incident / Four-Island Story Recovery before elevating to canon. A claymation Big Bang / cracking surface / separated chunks can narrate social division and ideological split without making every island look alike.

Protopian inhabitants may reconnect fragments with individually made clay-built race/stunt tracks instead of waiting for central governmental connection. Such bridges can symbolize **comic closure / gutter** between panels, decks, thinking fields, story beats and game moments. The track is a semantic transition and an actual traversable route, not generic decoration. Existing Track Core / Joyride remain route/geometry/collision owners, never add a parallel road writer. Loops, ramps and rails are optional authored variants, not mandatory everywhere.

## Silhouette-first assembly model
Each fragment has:
1. **mass**: unique outer profile (slab, wedge, broken cliff, shard, crescent, knuckle, split-shelf, crater, drooping overhang, asymmetric cluster), visibly fractured underside;
2. **surface**: walkable/habitable contour and region-specific palette/material/atmospheric behavior, without universally applying card watercolor to 3D effects;
3. **signature landmark**: from distance reads the identity (tower, mausoleum, vampire castle, prison panopticon, etc.);
4. **resident/action signature**: a reason people inhabit or visit the island, not a prop on a floating tile;
5. **connection**: meaningful proximity and travel edges to semantic neighbors, including option to leave track gaps for flight/portal;
6. **story/deck relation**: real source-linked themes, Card/Deck refs where genuinely available; never invent canonical cards or force 1 island per 1 deck.

Visual QA question: **silhouette + landmark + motion/color at long distance must distinguish an island before label text**. A loaded island mesh or randomly scattered buildings is not evidence of successful world composition. Close view additionally needs clear micro-story resident/POI relationships and traversal.
