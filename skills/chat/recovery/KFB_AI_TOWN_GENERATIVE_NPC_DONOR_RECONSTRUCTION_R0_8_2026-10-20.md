## Proposed bounded architecture for KFB (not accepted APIs)
WORLD EVENT (WS1 validated) → PERCEPTION (line-of-sight/distance, resident POV) → INTERPRETATION (character authored role, motivation, false beliefs, relations, signature deck) → INTENT (choose one eligible action) → WORLD ACTION (WS1 nav/interactable/combat/save) → CHOREOGRAPHY (R0.7 eight channels) → SALIENT RECEIPT (only meaningful result, source-qualified) → RELATIONSHIP/MEMORY PROJECTION. No shared mind; residents hold limited asymmetric knowledge. A witnessed attack may affect the witness and later rumor, not instant omniscient global aggro. Audio/ChatterBox/LLM do not write gameplay state.

**Memory layers**: fixed kfb.character/1 bio & authored biases; ephemeral observations / intent; lean receipts recording actor, source event, participants, witnesses, outcome, salience and provenance; projected relation/opinion; very rare reflection/summary under a time/token budget. NPC Memory View only an indexed/derived projection of receipts, not duplicate transcript or universal vector DB. Durable persistence requires WS1 kfb.save/1 versioned owner contract. Conversation director bounded by proximity, invite/approach, exclusivity and interruption; Triplet English from accepted pool; allow silence. LLM off by default during motion, optional at social beats with cache/timeout/fallback and explicit author gate.
