## 1 · Georg's authored premise (proposed, not approved)
The existing Quaternius *MonsterCuteCubes* enemies become a recurring dark-goo / black-fluff **meta-plague**. They cover parts of KFB's landscape with censorship ink, turn nearby hanging six-mode Fluff balls into darkened fall-fruit, and interfere with NPC life. Most residents dislike or fear them; brave, heroic, militant or morally questionable residents may pursue them or temporarily cooperate across normally hostile factions, akin to a common enemy overriding faction conflict. Regular maintenance/hunts, group expeditions, trophies and colorful triplet battle stories become a visible self-renewing world activity.
Origin is multiple disputed accounts, **not canonical fact**: Men in Black coverup; Lord Hunky's low-vibrational agit-prop / censorship-cell experiment; Dory unintentionally hatching it in a Cheese Moon omelet. Treat as author candidate, rumor versus actual historical world event; do not assert every NPC knows or believes it.
Potential later area: the **Narrative Wastelands**, especially frequent fluffer spawns, dangerous patrols, chase and racer/stunt kayfabulations. Large PsyOp/Mass-Formation legendary dragons/blobs and Quaternius flyers are campaign/minigame families, not Phase A assets.

**Naming to WS0:** `Dark Fluffer` is a provisional neutral production term (IDs `dark_fluffer`); `Black Fluffer` is Georg's provisional in-world alternative, not a canon override. `Dark Goo` / `Censor Ink` / `Fluff Blight` are distinct candidate labels; don't introduce a seventh canonical Story-Mode `fluff` type as shorthand.
