## 2 · Additional critique of Perplexity TypeScript and NotebookLM
`ResidentProfile`, `ResidentIdentity`, `SocialStyle`, `DailySchedule` as saved mutable copies can fork WS0 `kfb.character/1`. `SaveGameResident` duplicates source profile/schedule AND `eventLog` duplicates memory receipts; risk inconsistent edits/large save. `ConversationOutcome` union contains `trade`/Fluff/Card and `custom`: those are requests that need authoritative Resource/Card validators, never direct changes. `MemoryReceipt.facts: string[]` is not typed source proof; distinguish world event from subjective interpretation. `GameTime` untyped number requires authoritative clock/tick definition. `memorySequence` not globally unique across reload/instances without a stable composed key. `Rumor.confidence` and trust should not cause third-party omniscience or reset past directly observed facts. Do not write these source snippets unchanged into WS1.
NotebookLM's claims of 20-agent frame threshold, 1–5s optimal offscreen cadence, navcat automatically faster than WASM, bit-packing required, instant 60 FPS and GPT Site arbitrary direct API-key availability were not proven by a WS1 benchmark and must remain hypotheses. Confirm first with actual owner runtime, metrics and platform capabilities.
