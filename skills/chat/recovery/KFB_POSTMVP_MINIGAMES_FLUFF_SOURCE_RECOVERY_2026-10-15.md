## 2. Reconstructed game concept in one usable short brief

### Creative contract
KFB is a playful, lived-in, claymation-cartoon **world**, not a list of compulsory grind mechanics. **Chill & Fun is default; PULL, DON'T GATE**. Side activities should be intrinsically delightful with source-authentic animation, humorous context and visual polish. Later Standard/Hard use **parametric rule profiles on the same systems**, not another World/Combat/PlayerSave. All new islands/biomes/resources derive from source-backed residents and places: no anonymous loot furnaces, no generic UI dashboards, no extra hunger meter in Chill.

### Single ResourceActivity / Economy / Save core (proposal, not code)
Existing World config owns authoring-time `worldId`, typed `anchors`, `nodes[]`, stable resident/asset refs and 0…n `deckId` refs. **WorldRecipe ≠ PlayerSave**. A future **single PlayerSave + Lean Event Ledger** owns player collection, Fluff wallet, journey Card/provenance, and challenge profile. One idempotent `ActivityRequest(sourceNodeId, method, actionId)` checks owner-verified node/budget, produces **one** authored `ActivityResult`, and one transaction credits item/wallet/score/Card with provenance; the rendering/VFX method never rolls an additional prize or creates terrain truth. Receipts are replay-safe, saves export/import/reload without duplication.
- **Spendable D6 Fluff = 6 story-color families × HIGH/LOW**. Tragic blue `#3e6a83`, Comic olive `#5e6f33`, Absurd GOLD `#8a6d12` (NOT magenta), Heroic ochre `#b75f23`, Mystical violet `#6b4e7d`, Forbidden wine `#8f3a5f`. The two polarities are within *every* mode, neither mode nor island is inherently “good/bad”; D6 accent role must respect current Environment/Material `ENV_ROLES`. The raw/neutral mixture is an **intermediate**, not a new collectible currency.
- **Lifetime Fluff Score ≠ spendable wallet ≠ historical Fluff HP/POP ≠ Almanac**. Earned score can be enormous and comically celebrated; do not credit the same fish again on processing, trade and crafting. Global Hall of Fame much later needs explicit opt-in, trustworthy server receipts, anti-cheat, privacy/moderation, not untrusted save uploads.
- **Real Cards** always use canonical `deckId + cardNumber`, current approved PDF/Card crop and authentic semantic JSON; Card acquisitions go to one Almanac/collection with Origin Memory, not a second backpack copy or invented deck art. Owner supports existing courier custody.
- Backpack candidate 20 slots for ordinary props, D6 High/Low material quick-wells as **view on same inventory**, not second store. Avoid inventory overflow consuming resources; unknown recipes are harmless refundable/no-op. Later `CraftRequest` resolves same recipe across Backpack hand-knead, Witch cauldron (authentic source), Maker workshop conveyor/factory, UFO replicator/barmixer and future Terraforming Foundry as visual skins of **one craft contract**.
- Typical cinematic transformation: `CLAY FLUFF BALLS → WOBBLE/KNEAD/FLOW/STIR → RAW MASS → SOURCE-APPROVED PROP or TRUE CARD REVEAL`. Reuse actual Clay/Build/Destroy/Repair/UFO source presentations only after visual isolation; VFX ≠ World mutation.
