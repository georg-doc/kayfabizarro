North star

Big shape first. Round by default. Detail last.
Toy basis + slight clay infusion. The result should read at icon/map scale.

DO

Few large masses.
Visible corner radius.
Toy thickness.
Capsule/tube supports.
2–4 color zones.
Matte/satin material.

DON'T

Hard 90° default.
Invisible micro-bevel.
Thin realistic rods.
Every screw / seam / lattice.
Grime before silhouette.
Miniature engineering model.
SOURCE DONOR

Review checklist

  1. Readable as silhouette?
  2. Rounding visible at target distance?
  3. Enough thickness?
  4. Can a part be deleted?
  5. Only 2–4 color zones?
  6. Material supporting form, not hiding it?

Repair order

1 · delete secondary parts
2 · enlarge macro masses
3 · increase rounding
4 · thicken supports
5 · restore one iconic feature
6 · material last
