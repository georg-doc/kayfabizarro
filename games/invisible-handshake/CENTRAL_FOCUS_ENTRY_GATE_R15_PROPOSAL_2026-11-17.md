# THE INVISIBLE HANDSHAKE · R15 concept candidates · Mandatory entry & shared Focus Narrative
**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** UNTESTED DESIGN PROPOSAL / ADDITIVE ONLY · **owner:** `games/invisible-handshake/` · **branch:** `design/invisible-handshake-paper-r0-2026-11-17` · Draft PR #475. **Current tested core remains R14**; no merged rules, site update, official R15 revision, game art or new runtime.

## User correction: an empty own public narrative is not a voluntary contribution
Georg explicitly states the own public narrative is the **ticket to participate in the arena**. Previous "why voluntarily play another own card?" economic framing ignored that intent.

**Proposed precise entry gate:** If **your own** faction's one public narrative slot is empty, your *entire legal action menu* becomes:
- PLAY one card from your hand into that empty slot (costs your one action); or
- if no playable hand card, DRAW one card from stock (costs your one action, max hand 6).
You **cannot** adjust/reroll/move/swap any of the three communal Fear/Hope/Hate dice, gain a trophy or attack a narrative until your own slot is occupied again. Other people's cards remain public and open to manipulation and harvest as soon as you re-enter. This is a play eligibility requirement, **NOT** ownership-only scoring, a protective shield for your own cards, an automatic refill or an extra "pay to enter" currency.

**Emergency exhaustion case:** If your own slot is empty, your hand is empty and stock exhausted, there is no legal re-entry move. Record as an **unresolved endgame/elimination case**, not invent a free replacement from discard. A full-game end condition has not been selected for R14.

**Strategic consequences / open risk:** Winning or burning another player's public narrative now costs that contributor at least one future action to re-enter (potentially two when hand empty). This makes attacking a slot **stronger**, not weaker; test for leader suppression, seat-order snowball and whether six seats get locked out. The earlier R14 automated policy replenished voluntarily; it did NOT enforce the gate. All R14 telemetry remains baseline, not gate-tested evidence.

## Shared arena: six fictional factions outside, one common issue in the centre
- The physical **six-position ring / hexagonal layout** shows Technocracy™, Round Table, Fourth Reich, Illuminati, Holy Empire, Alien Management, but only **2–6 participating player slots** are active. Inactive factions are visual framing only; **no permanent NPCs and no seventh faction**.
- The central **FOCUS NARRATIVE** is **one ordinary card from the existing stock of 50 narrative cards**, laid publicly in the middle at setup. It is the common event / shared question everyone frames through their own card. No extra 57th card, no new card stat, no special faction power, no dedicated centre-card deck.
- Opening conservation: six faction cards are outside the narrative pool, active n players each receive 3 hand cards and occupy 1 public narrative slot; centre gets 1 additional card, so remaining stock is `50 - 4*n - 1 = 49 - 4*n` (41 at 2 players; 25 at 6 players). Max hand stays 6.
- **Same 3 communal Fear/Hope/Hate D6**. Do not import earlier R/A/F face-value cost rules, new Overton currency or an additional die; R/A/F resonance/amplitude/frequency may guide music/presentation, not current R14 mechanics.
- An art/layout concept: handmade KFB Power Pyramid with a central eye on the common FOCUS card, surrounding six faction/narrative cells, three genuine colored dice; hexagonal *board placement*, **not** a procedural hex-tiled world or proof that a real KFB dice art donor was isolated. Placement itself cannot claim art acceptance.
- **Show / Spin / Sell** stays **voluntary and non-scoring**; quiet players may use their printed card description. A card can be contextualized against the FOCUS issue in one short line. No voting that rewards better acting or knowledge of real conspiracy history.
- **No private goals or secret missions** at this gate.

## Two deliberately small center-card variants, mutually exclusive
Both apply the proposed **mandatory own-slot entry gate**. Both start with the centre topic card taken from stock and **unchanged R14 shared dice starting allocation** `die i → player slot (i mod n)`, all face values3. Thus no die is automatically on the centre at opening. These variants are not the original R0.5/R0.6 economic Overton prototypes.

### Variant A · FOCUS AS CONTEXT (control)
The centre card is a **public, permanently displayed topic anchor**. It is not won, burnt, replenished, owned, voted on or assigned a die. Existing F/H/H dice may be moved, swapped and adjusted only on **occupied active player narrative slots**, exactly as R14. The centre affects **interpretation** and the optional one-sentence Show/Spin/Sell, not score or turn eligibility. Taking a normal narrative card across6→7 still awards the actor; crossing1→0 still burns it.

**Why test A:** Is a common reference alone enough to make six factions' narratives about one shared crisis feel like a political/social satire rather than an arbitrary card collection? Adding this card costs only one source card and no new move options. If it fails to create thematic cohesion, avoid piling mechanical bonuses on it.

### Variant B · FOCUS AS OPEN PUBLIC PRIZE (one gameplay experiment)
The centre card is a **seventh public slot (not a seventh faction)** which may hold any of the three existing dice. A die may be **moved or swapped** to/from the centre via the normal R14 single-action movement/swap, retaining its face. The centre counts as an **occupied narrative target** for those two action types, for ±1 adjustments and for reroll.
- If a die at centre is adjusted from **6→7**, the acting player wins the FOCUS card as a trophy, regardless of faction. If from **1→0**, the centre card goes to discard without a trophy. A reroll NEVER triggers0/7, same as R14.
- **Only centre exception to no auto-refill:** If stock has a card, immediately replace removed centre card by drawing **one from stock**, keeping any remaining dice where they are; set triggering die face to3 **on the new centre card**. The centre card was always in the 50-card pool and never enters any player's hand. If stock is empty, the centre becomes unoccupied; redistribute all dice on it to currently occupied player narratives as for R14. If no occupied player narrative exists (possible only under an off-model setup), dice wait centrally per R14 fallback. Do not invent extra copies.
- Capturing/burning an outer player narrative still empties that player's own slot and enforces mandatory re-entry. A player with an empty own slot may not harvest the centre: they must first PLAY (or DRAW when empty-handed).
- Unlike R0.4, there is no FAVOR token, PLOT, or NEED tag. The center is an open *third-party contested claim*, not "ownership" for any faction. It is a natural venue for collective Fear/Hope/Hate influence.

**Why test B:** The shared issue becomes a tangible opportunity and can also lure opponents away from the outer cards. **Unresolved:** center may become an overfarmed free trophy fountain, favor early players/seat order or make contributions to player slots strategically futile. Automatic centre replenishment is one carefully isolated exception, not a stealth reversal of all R14 no-refill rules.

## Two manual turn traces for paper use, not simulations
### Trace 1 · 2 players / The Bell / admission gate (both variants)
- Technocracy's own narrative is `THE BELL`; Round Table's own narrative is `THE COMMITTEE`; centre is `THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY`. Both players had three hand cards at opening and a card in their own slot; all opening dice3 on player slots per R14 (2-person co-location of F and H).
- In a reachable later state, **Fear6** rests on THE BELL. Round Table **adjusts Fear 6→7** as one action and takes THE BELL as trophy. Technocracy slot becomes **empty**, Fear resets3 and is relocated onto another occupied player slot by the actor, as R14.
- On Technocracy's next turn they may **not** manipulate Hope6 or a public centre die, even if an easy trophy awaits. They **PLAY** one hand card into their own empty slot, costing that turn. If hand were empty, they would instead DRAW one card, then PLAY on a later turn. After play they can compete normally again.
- **Consequence:** the previous public-supply "optional gift" critique does not apply. Still test whether attackers can repeatedly force a particular seat to spend every second turn re-entering.

### Trace 2 · 6 players / The Bell / central capture (B only)
- Six active faction slots are occupied, including one displaying `THE BELL`; central FOCUS `THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY`. At setup **25 cards remain in stock** (6 occupied+18 hand+1 centre =25 placed/drawn). The six factions do not become autonomous NPCs.
- In a reachable later state Fear6 is on the central FOCUS (someone previously used MOVE or SWAP), Hope3 and Hate3 are on outer narratives. The acting player, who has their own slot occupied, adjusts Fear6→7 and claims THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY as **one trophy**. Fear resets3; if stock>0 the top stock card becomes the new central FOCUS and Fear3 stays there. All outer cards remain as they were. At 1→0 instead the old focus is discarded, still refilled if stock>0.
- If stock was0, no focus refill occurs. The centre becomes empty and displaced dice relocate onto remaining occupied player narratives; no card created or recycled.
- **Consequence:** new central prize adds an opportunity to the same actor-wins-harvest mechanics, but it does **not** solve the old 5→6 immediate-next-player gift by itself.

## Comparisons and stop criteria
**Single A/B test:** same 12 physical text proxy cards where possible, **same active own-slot gate**, center anchor A vs capturable center B. First do manual turn traces; then instrument same R14 deterministic seeds and player policies for n=2..6, preserving conservation `stock + hands + outer board + (centre occupied?1:0) + trophies + discard =50`. Needed telemetry:
- share of actions spent on re-entry DRAW/PLAY, percentage of players with an empty slot at each round, total trophies and how many come from centre, 5→6 next-actor gift, seat order, voluntary non-scoring movement loops, centre refill count, stock exhaustion and action availability.
- **Do not** compare R14 published 6,000 simulations as if they already covered the gate or center.
- **Stop / simplify** if persistent empty-seat locks, center farming, free extra trophies swamp outer narratives, six-player turns collapse into meaningless draws, or adding the center merely increases visual complexity.

**Human gate:** Georg physically tests A vs B using 2 people first, then a six-seat qualitative stress setup. Select **A / B / FAIL** on clarity and decision quality. The separate risky same-turn preparation-fix remains a future controlled test, not part of this single variable comparison. No full 56-card art, solo NPCs or secret missions before fun is established.
