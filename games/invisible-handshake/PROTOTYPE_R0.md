## A/B comparison — same three decisions

| Decision | A · personal three-card networks | B · shared three-slot stage |
|---|---|---|
| Build a connection | RECRUIT one public-market institution whose SUPPLIES matches a NEED in your network | Overwrite a common Stage slot with an institution |
| Redirect a rival's intention | POACH an EXPOSED rival institution; owner may spend one Favor to veto | Overwrite the shared Stage card supporting the rival's implied aim |
| Recover from setback | RECRUIT from market or play a PLOT; no loss of role or turn | Rebuild the shared Stage, but attribution/control of the rebuild is unclear |

**Selected:** A. Ownership, vulnerability, correction and comic incentive are physically visible. B is simpler spatially, but offers weaker player-specific consequences and more kingmaking risk. No third architecture for R0.

## English Quickstart · one page

### Components
The **planned** common starter has exactly **56 cards**: six ROLES, thirty INSTITUTIONS, twelve PLOTS and eight EVENTS. Use Favor tokens (0–3 per person) and one round marker. The twelve separately listed proof cards are **not** the complete six-player deck. One mechanical resource: FAVOR.

### Setup
1. Shuffle the six Roles. Give each player one face-up Role; set unused Roles aside.
2. Give each player **1 FAVOR** and **1 PLOT** face-down from the twelve-card Plot pile. Keep remaining Plots as draw pile. A player never holds more than one Plot.
3. Shuffle thirty Institutions. Reveal the first **four** as the MARKET; keep the rest as its supply pile.
4. Shuffle eight Events (four PUBLIC HEARING and four EXPEDITED FUNDING). They are the **eight-round clock**. Start with an empty personal NETWORK, maximum **three** Institutions. Everyone sees all networks.
5. Choose first player. Move first player clockwise after each full round.

### One round
1. Stand all sideways **SUSPENDED** institutions upright again.
2. Reveal exactly one Event and apply it. Do not replace the Event.
3. Each player takes exactly one turn, clockwise.
4. After the eighth round, score **before** clearing suspensions. The deck clock, not elimination, ends the game.

### Your turn
First **gain 1 FAVOR (maximum 3)**. Choose just one main action:

- **RECRUIT**: Take one face-up MARKET Institution into your NETWORK. If you already have three, return one you own to the **bottom** of the Institution pile first. Refill the Market immediately.
- **POACH**: Pay **1 FAVOR**. Choose an **EXPOSED** Institution from another player's network. A card is EXPOSED when its NEED is not met **or** its owner's network has three cards. The owner gets **one** reaction: pay **1 FAVOR** to VETO, or allow the transfer. If vetoed, the attacker may RECRUIT a Market card instead (no additional action). If successful, transfer the Institution to the attacker, replacing one of their own first if at capacity.
- **PLOT**: Play your one held Plot, apply its effect, discard it, and draw another Plot if the pile is not empty. PLOTS without explicit VETO text do not allow a counteraction.

No other player may react. No negotiations, dice rolls or storytelling are required for legal moves. A voluntary one-sentence explanation is welcome.

### Institution logic
Each Institution has exactly one **SUPPLIES** and one **NEEDS** tag, chosen from MONEY / RULES / VOICE. A NEED is met if **another active** card in the same network SUPPLIES the required tag. One source can satisfy multiple needs. A sideways **SUSPENDED** card scores **zero**, supplies nothing, and cannot fulfill its own NEED. All suspensions clear only at the beginning of the next round.

### Event templates
- **PUBLIC HEARING (4 copies)**: Each player with **three Institutions** must suspend **one of their own** until the start of the next round.
- **EXPEDITED FUNDING (4 copies)**: Each player with fewer Institutions than the largest network gains **1 FAVOR** (maximum 3).

### Score and winner
At the end of round eight: **+1 per ACTIVE Institution; +2 for each ACTIVE Institution with a satisfied NEED; +2 if the Role AGENDA is fulfilled.** Highest total wins. Tie-break: more FAVOR. Still tied: shared win. Everybody plays every round.

### Solo: the System
Use the same eight rounds, four-card Market and Institutions/Plots/Events. After the round Event but **before** your turn, the System discards one Market Institution and immediately refills its slot. It chooses the **leftmost** Market card that could satisfy one of your **currently unmet** network NEEDS. If none qualifies, it chooses the Market's leftmost card. The System does not hold a hand, acquire an agenda or make subjective choices. **Win with at least 8 points at the end of round eight**, otherwise the System wins. The normal Event effect still applies to the solo player.

### Refill / conservation
Removed Market Institutions are replaced immediately. RECRUIT replacements return to the bottom of the Institution pile; System removals are kept in an Institution discard pile and reshuffled if the Institution pile is depleted. POACH simply transfers control, so does not alter the Market. Used Plots are discarded; their draw pile may empty safely. Never produce a second 56-card deck.

**Example C — Solo System pressure**: Midgame solo R01 has N01/N02, Favor 1, Market from left [N05,N03,N04,N06]. The new Event is PUBLIC HEARING: no effect with only two Institutions. N01/N02/N03 now form a three-card cycle: provisional base **9**; R01 has no unmet NEED, so agenda 0.
