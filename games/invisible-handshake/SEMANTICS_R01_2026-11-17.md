# THE INVISIBLE HANDSHAKE · R0.1 Machtmittel & Satire
**Datum:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** TUNE / DESIGN CANDIDATE, not playtested · **Owner:** `games/invisible-handshake` · **Branch:** `design/invisible-handshake-paper-r0-2026-11-17`.
**Source:** Georg's feedback 2026-11-17 on the R0 paper prototype, read with R0 SSOT and Atlas v29 mechanics brief. **No new ruleset, runtime, art, deck count or promotion**. This is an additive semantic supersession to the R0 labels and candidate card text, not a rewrite of R0 evidence.

## Main result
The three SUPPLIES / NEEDS categories describe distinct **means of exercising power** in a network, not separate quantitative currencies. **INFLUENCE replaces VOICE** in this candidate. **RESOURCES replaces MONEY provisionally**, to express more than cash and to avoid confusing an in-fiction currency with a game token. **RULES stays**, but denotes law, institutional procedures, enforcement, exceptions and weaponized legality, not justice.

| Type on proposed R0.1 cards | Supply means | Need means | Includes | Excludes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RESOURCES** | Control over capital, credit, liquidity, supplies, energy, production, transport or procurement | Requires material backing, supply access or financing | Fictional near-collapse monetary system; monopoly on fuel or logistics | Not a second counted money resource; not automatically identical with KFB's in-fiction currency |
| **RULES** | Ability to certify, regulate, grant exemptions, adjudicate, classify or legally obstruct | Requires a recognized rule, protection or administratively valid pretext | Lawfare, emergency powers, selective enforcement, procedural delay | Not necessarily legitimate, just, democratic or lawful in moral sense |
| **INFLUENCE** | Access to audiences, intelligence, personal leverage, intermediaries, reputations and clandestine commitments | Requires compliance, public consent, secrecy, pressure or a broker | Media, propaganda, manipulation, kompromat/blackmail, backchannels, diplomacy and geopolitical maneuvering | Not the all-purpose word for *total* game power, and not the spendable FAVOR token |

**Design invariant:** Network control/influence is the **outcome**. RESOURCES / RULES / INFLUENCE are *channels*, never interchangeable scores. FAVOR remains the **only spendable token** (0–3, gained by the existing R0 turn rule): its function is limited tactical bandwidth to POACH, VETO or pay costs, not a currency simulation. The suggested economic lore may later name an in-universe currency, but that does not create a second numerical economy.

### Source → tactic → consequence
- The **INSTITUTION** provides one channel and needs one other channel. The two labels explain why two otherwise unrelated actors support each other.
- A **PLOT** uses the network's position as a concrete tactic: juridical obstruction, kompromat, infiltration/backroom deals, buyouts, diversion of supplies. One action, at most the existing one-target reaction; do not introduce a generic free-form conspiracy modifier.
- The **EVENT** shifts the environment (e.g. liquidity crisis, public hearing), not a fourth resource or a new action phase.
- The satirical micro-engine is: **PUBLIC CLAIM → PRIVATE INCENTIVE → VISIBLE CONSEQUENCE**. The card's image must physically reveal the contradiction without an explanatory joke. Fictional actions are not factual allegations.

**Existing test mechanics are unchanged:** RECRUIT, POACH, one VETO, SUSPEND, FAVOR and eight rounds. The number of tag pairs and 56-slot budget remain 6 ROLES + 30 INSTITUTIONS + 12 PLOTS + 8 EVENTS. All six directed type pairs still have five planned Institution slots; this is a *distribution target*, not finished copy.

### Plot directions without increasing complexity
P01 PUBLIC AUDIT (R0) can be re-themed as **EMERGENCY INJUNCTION** (LAWFARE): 'Suspend one institution in any network until the next round. It supplies nothing and scores 0.' Same mechanical effect.
P02 BACKROOM BROKER (R0) can be re-themed as **THE COMPROMISING FILE** (BLACKMAIL / KOMPROMAT): 'POACH an EXPOSED rival institution for 0 Favor. Its owner may VETO for 1 Favor; if vetoed, RECRUIT instead.' Same effect, existing one-reaction window.
P03 BAILOUT remains the RESOURCES response: 'RECRUIT one market institution, then gain 1 Favor (max 3). Replace one of yours if full.' Same effect.
The examples are **fictional rule text candidates**, not approved source quotations or real-world accusations. Distinct tactics should be felt in *what changes on the table*, not just in their title.

### Quick pressure example (constructed, not played)
Player A: Emergency Lender (RESOURCES→RULES) + Press Office (INFLUENCE→RESOURCES). The Lender lacks RULES, the Press Office's RESOURCES need is met.
Player B: Compliance Bureau (RULES→INFLUENCE).
A could RECRUIT a RULES supplier to complete a lucrative cycle; B can instead seek influence/POACH exposure. Playing THE COMPROMISING FILE against an EXPOSED B institution costs A **0 FAVOR**; B may pay **1 FAVOR** to VETO. If vetoed, A may RECRUIT instead. This is the existing R0 action resolution, not a new blackmail mini-game.

### Not yet accepted / what to test
- **Label certainty:** INFLUENCE is Georg's explicit preferred direction; RESOURCES is a reasonable provisional replacement for MONEY, subject to physical-table wording test.
- **Still open:** Is the in-fiction crisis currency a concrete named subcase of RESOURCES? If the game later needs to model the financial collapse, prefer a fixed EVENT/PLOT rather than a new treasury subsystem.
- **Avoid:** INFLUENCE silently meaning both tag, spendable currency and victory points; one tag doing the work of all three; more than one reaction; arbitrary 'blackmail beats anything' power.
- **Gate:** Keep the current R0 paper-game human KEEP/TUNE/FAIL gate, but use **R0.1 semantics** during the test. Observe whether the three channels explain dependencies without a lecture and whether LAWFARE/BLACKMAIL feel meaningfully different through the existing effects.
