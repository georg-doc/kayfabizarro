## Change from R22 and precise intent

Georg now proposes a **round-table narrative ritual**: six power factions publicly present one ordinary narrative card each, clockwise and alternating across human presenters including NPC revelations, using a short **SHOW IT → SPIN IT → SELL IT** opening. Then a human puts HATE/PROBLEM at **1** on the public card they blame, the next puts FEAR/REACTION at **1** on another card and responds, the next puts HOPE/SOLUTION at **1** on a third card and sells a purported rescue. Humans continue clockwise, including A→B→A for two humans. All three reference one shared central **QUEST/FOCUS** card, without placing a die there.

**Georg's proposed new continuous loop differs from R22 A and R22 B:** on each subsequent human beat, the next active role die (HATE → FEAR → HOPE → ...) is **TURNED up exactly one and RELOCATED to a different occupied outer narrative card with no die** in one indivisible narrated move. The next speaker must inherit, contradict or repurpose the previous statement while pointing at the **actual card and faction**. One die per card, no die on center. In the first stripped-down candidate, no SWAP, inversion, separate numeric stat, fifth/sixth free action or NPC speech.

This is a design exploration, not a replacement of R19's working HTML or acceptance of R14's obsolete scoring. Original KFB Finger Rule, KayfabeTips' therefore/but causality, R15C six-slot geometry and R22's **assertive accusation-as-physical-action** are sources reused, not reinvented. Original R/A/F dice and optional six-mode Story D6 are distinct donors; neither is silently inserted here.

## Physical table & public entrance

- Six fixed faction seats at the vertices of a hexagon. With n=2..6 humans, the remaining 6−n seats represent visible NPC factions. One ordinary narrative card face-up on **each** exterior seat. One different ordinary **FOCUS** card at the center (the public case/end panel). Six faction identity cards remain six, and the ordinary deck budget remains **50**, not seven extra Quest cards.
- R16 opening-budget donor (candidate, not retroactive rule): deal each human four ordinary narratives, choose one to publish on their faction seat, retain three in hand; reveal one ordinary card for each NPC; reveal center FOCUS. Stock thereafter is 43−3n. For a simple speech test, a human presenter may reveal each NPC card clockwise; **NPC seats do not gain human turns**.
- SHOW IT: physically display the real card, say its title. SPIN IT: state what this faction claims. SELL IT: state why this claim serves its own interest. Ten seconds is a suggested timing target, not a penalty or scored performance. Each card is exposed once to all players before the first accusation. Reuse the actual printed PUBLIC CLAIM and PRIVATE INCENTIVE where present; don't invent numeric card powers.
- Open with center FOCUS clearly named as the **shared contested case**, not a seventh faction, seventh die target or automatically awarded trophy.

## Proposed R23 baseline: one action = one Kayfabe line

| Beat | Question / speech move | Physical gesture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 PROBLEM / HATE | “Which faction/card is responsible, and what precisely did it do?” One actor-led accusation. | **SET** HATE on one occupied die-free outer card at 1. |
| 2 REACTION / FEAR | “Who escalated it, and how?” Explicit response to the accusation. | **SET** FEAR on another occupied die-free outer card at 1. |
| 3 SOLUTION / HOPE | “Whose claimed cure benefits whom?” Retort or appropriation of previous two claims. | **SET** HOPE on a third occupied die-free outer card at 1. |
| 4 onward, repeating | Next HATE, FEAR, HOPE speaker builds on the immediately previous claim and brings it back to FOCUS. | **MOVE+TURN as one move**: take the active die to another occupied **die-free** outer card and increment its face by 1, maximum displayed 6. |

A real target is an **occupied narrative card with no die**, NOT an empty/vacant board position. As long as all six narrative cards stay and three dice are down, every active die has exactly three valid destinations. No stacking, no die on center, no free SWAP, no extra automatic hand play. The dice' narrative roles are temporary and change with placement; **the printed cards do not have permanent HATE/FEAR/HOPE labels**.

**Speech contract**: specific subject + definite act or consequence + reference to previous claim; point to the actual card being recast, then link back to the FOCUS. This is hostile, performative Kayfabe, not a generic “hate because a document leaked” category explanation. The KFB Finger Rule and therefore/but are tools for memory and causality, **not a tribunal**. No points for joke quality or historical accuracy. A novice may read a card title and make one concrete accusation; no performance penalty. Do not make a full three-sentence recital mandatory each turn: the three successive people jointly tell the chain.

**“Ich packe meinen Koffer”** is an *incremental memory* metaphor: the next speaker acknowledges/reframes the previous claim and shared FOCUS. They are **not** required to recite all previous rounds, which would grow without bound and crush UNO-speed tempo. After each three beats the three dice form a visible new public Problem–Reaction–Solution constellation.

### Example (original proxy names, not completed final deck)
**Focus:** THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY. Human Round Table points to **THE EMERGENCY LICENSE OFFICE**, sets HATE=1: “The License Office sold the only shelter permits to its own donors. They're responsible for the evacuation failure.”
Human Technocracy points to **THE MAINSTREAM SYNDICATE**, sets FEAR=1: “But the Syndicate announced that everyone without a permit was a security threat. Now the shelter gates are closing.”
Next human (or first human in 2H) points to **THE SAFETY ADVISORY**, sets HOPE=1: “Our advisory will end the panic: one approved security contractor controls every gate, with the contract renewed for as long as threats are broadcast.”
Next PROBLEM beat points to THE MAINSTREAM SYNDICATE, moves HATE from the License Office to another die-free occupied card (target must be legal at that moment; here **THE MAINSTREAM SYNDICATE carries FEAR so this direct move is illegal in R23 baseline**). They must instead accuse a presently die-free **third-party card** or wait until another turn frees the Syndicate. This explicit example is a **deliberate test of the no-SWAP rule**, not an invented bypass.

## The mechanical catch exposed by R23 static enumeration

R23 **solves R22 A's infinite unchanged-face relocation loop** because every post-setup beat advances one die. But with mandatory +1 and no other action, it introduces a strictly determined countdown:
- Beats 1,2,3 SET faces 1/1/1. At beats 16,17,18 respectively HATE, FEAR, HOPE arrive at face6.
- The next attempt to go beyond a real D6 face6 occurs on **beat 19**, always **HATE/PROBLEM**, regardless of which card was chosen. A real D6 never visually shows face 7. This is a **TILT event**, whose payout and reset are still unapproved.
- The visible sum after setup equals the beat index (e.g. after beat12 sum12). Thus **the numbers alone are a public clock, not yet risk-reward strategy**; the meaningful baseline choice is *which current public narrative takes the blame and how it changes the next story*.
- Awarding the case to the TILT speaker or the final SOLUTION speaker would produce predictable **role/seat advantages**. Merely rotating the case starter can distribute seat exposure over repeated cases; it does not prove balance.
- Early removing/capturing exterior cards can eliminate all three legal die-free occupied targets. **First physical trial should keep six outer cards in place until a case transition**, with any claim/capture treated separately from this movement-only baseline.

**Baseline A · “Karaoke clock” (recommended first comprehension trial):** opening SET, mandatory MOVE+TURN per beat, central FOCUS always referenced, first natural TILT ends the current case with **no automatic personal trophy**. To continue a new case, reset/replace via a deliberately separate next-case ritual using the same 50-card stock. Opening/turn tempo and quality of real-card continuity are the acceptance question, *not* balance or a finished victory condition.

**Challenger B · “Claim under pressure” (NOT accepted / do not stack yet):** after the action-as-speech baseline works physically, test **one optional declaration of a card claim** once pressure is high, with a real cost for failure. A provisional numeric comparator is a separate resolution D6 rolled at active face 4,5,6, winning on 1,2,3 respectively (1/6, 2/6, 3/6); a failed challenge escalates the case or sacrifices a hand card. This is **only a test hypothesis**, not a rule: the claim's exact target (outer vs center), replacement/re-entry, cost, timing, extra die availability and seat bias must be decided **before implementation**. Never roll the three fixed-position pressure dice as the randomizer.

**No subjective judge or “best story wins”**. Tactical risk must become a real optional choice with an explicit possible failure, not simply a higher dice face awarded by rehearsed eloquence. No 50 unique card powers needed.

## Geometry: the triangle of three dice and the Panopticon eye

The three outer dice physically create a moving triangle, with central FOCUS as the eye. This is excellent **graphic/comic staging** and a causal reading aid. It is not automatically a good capture criterion. For three distinct vertices of a regular six-vertex hexagon, **20** unordered configurations exist: center strictly inside **2**, on a triangle edge **12**, outside **6**. A naive “center inside triangle” rule means either **2/20 strict** or **14/20 inclusive**, depending on whether edge counts; the majority of boundary ties make the criterion easy to misunderstand. Keep triangle/FOCUS linework as a visual, non-payout semantic motif until a separately designed objective *claim gate* proves useful. Not a hex terrain grid and not a 4th die.
