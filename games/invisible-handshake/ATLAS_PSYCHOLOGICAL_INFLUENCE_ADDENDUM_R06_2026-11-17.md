## WP4 · Nudging, NLP, behavioral advertising and AI-mediated persuasion
**Clarify acronym:** `NLP` can mean **Neuro-Linguistic Programming** or **Natural Language Processing**; research both but NEVER conflate the scientific and computational domains.
- **Neuro-Linguistic Programming:** identify historical promoters, specific testable hypotheses, systematic reviews and independent reproducibility; do not attribute general persuasion results specifically to NLP without valid comparison.
- **Natural Language Processing:** study large-scale platform recommendation, targeted messaging, personalization, LLM-generated persuasion, advertising attribution and algorithmically controlled feed visibility using reproducible contemporary studies. No assumption that these tools produce unlimited or guaranteed coercion.
- **Nudging:** distinguish descriptive choice architecture from explicit deceptive dark patterns and coercion. Clarify sampling and outcome sensitivity; neither research result alone resolves all settings.
- **Dark patterns:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04843 . Critically separate platform feature, exposure and measurable behavior change from assumed command of a population.
**Deliverables:** 8–10 papers/case rows, one "stronger/weaker evidence" grid; 3 game-effect candidates as existing PLOT / EVENT / FRAME operations (not new stats).
