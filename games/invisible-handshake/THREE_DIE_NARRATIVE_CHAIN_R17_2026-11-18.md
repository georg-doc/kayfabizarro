## Persistent layout before the new narrative mechanic
- Six OUTER faction narrative positions all occupied at beginning: n human players (n=2..6) + 6−n randomly revealed NPC faction narratives. A cleared seat may be temporarily empty. Die count 3 and **at most one D6 per outer card**. FOCUS is one ordinary center narrative card, NEVER hosts a D6.
- Humans begin with 4 random narrative cards: publish 1 to own faction position, retain 3 in hand. Six public outer + one focus +3n hands + `43−3n` stock = 50 ordinary narrative cards. NPCs do not start with a hand.
- Faces on the three shared colored dice = 1..6, distinct physical dice `HATE`, `FEAR`, `HOPE`; **the number measures tactical proximity to 0/7**, the color/identity determines dramatic narrative function. Do not reorder 1..6 against the causal order. Old R/A/F Resonanz/Amplitude/Frequenz was a historical KFB donor, not a new track here.

## One light story sequence: HATE → FEAR → HOPE
Every normal HUMAN dice-manipulating turn, **point in fixed order to exactly the three public cards currently carrying the three dice**:
1. **HATE = PROBLEM**: "Who or what must be blamed?" Point to that physical card; use its actual TITLE or illustrated subject as the named villain/problem.
2. **FEAR = REACTION**: "What public reaction follows?" Point to that physical card and say **THEREFORE** (or `also/deshalb` in informal German explanation) connecting the first to the second.
3. **HOPE = SOLUTION**: "Who claims to offer the solution, and who benefits?" Point to the physical card and say **BUT** (or another causal reversal) connecting the second to the third; connect the proposed remedy to the central FOCUS issue.

**Minimal valid example spoken in a human turn:** “THE LEAK causes the crisis, THEREFORE THE BELL demands emergency powers, BUT THE SAFETY ADVISORY sells permanent surveillance as relief.” FOCUS card: THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY. The named physical cards matter because each is the SOURCE of its spoken subject and current die role. An arbitrary football sticker cannot satisfy the actual named political referents without replacing the story.
**Player-level context:** each HUMAN speaks from their chosen faction's public worldview; they may sincerely sell, hypocritically manipulate or openly invert it, but no ideological jury or historical-claim verdict is involved.
**KISS accessibility:** pointing to the three physical cards and merely reading their TITLES through the printed “X → therefore Y → but Z” template counts as fully valid. Optional richer improvised sentence 5–15s, no public vote, King's judgment, humor points or penalty for limited improvisation. The three-card finger path is an **action presentation requirement** for a narrative game, not a *second gameplay action*.
**If fewer than three legally placed dice remain:** point only to available dice-bearing cards and use the shorter version; any reserve-die reactivation is separately undefined in R15C and not silently fixed here. On a mandatory own-slot PLAY/DRAW re-entry turn, simply name or read the new card; full chain resumes on the next dice-action turn.
**Turn order:** perform short chain *before* the single chosen normal R14/R15C-adjusted action; then resolve card movement/harvest/collapse; the next player reads the *new* causal configuration. The names can change because MOVE/SWAP changes which public narrative is HATE/FEAR/HOPE, or because a player PUBLISHES/REWRITES their own public card. This makes card choice and the spatial die assignment part of the fiction.
**Caution:** card identity still creates no *objectively audited* unique ability in this control version; storytelling and co-occurrence are meaningful play experiences, not mathematical proof that a title changes the score. If titles still feel like stickers in human A, do not manufacture subjective skill penalties; test B below.

## Two non-stacking alternatives (same story chain, different center payout)
**A · STORY ONLY / BASELINE:** use the three-beat finger narrative above and the established single-action R14 outer capture/burn rules; center Focus is the public subject/context, not won via a new narrative rule. All dice moves legal under R15C, and NPC turn rules still pending. Tests whether a short, obligatory but low-pressure causal story makes cards salient without changing numerical outcomes.

**B · THE SOLUTION CLAIM / FIRST TACTICAL CHALLENGER:**
- Same three-beat finger narrative. The narrative card currently beneath the **HOPE** D6 is the **publicly proposed SOLUTION** of the dispute over FOCUS. This can be any human/NPC card; die location (not printed card category) determines the role. HUMAN player can MOVE or SWAP dice under existing legal one-per-outer rules to shift who controls the solution.
- When any legally placed outer die is **deliberately ADJUSTED 6→7**, the acting HUMAN wins the outer narrative as in R14, irrespective of whose faction contributed it. **Separately**, if the central Focus exists, check the HOPE die's *pre-collapse* outer position:
   * If HOPE rests on an OUTER HUMAN faction **other than the acting human**, that faction's human wins the CENTER FOCUS as a second, distinct trophy. (The focus has no D6 and is not a seventh faction.)
   * If HOPE rests on an NPC outer faction, the centre is **REFRAMED** instead: the existing Focus goes to DISCARD without any NPC trophy.
   * If HOPE rests on the ACTING HUMAN's own outer faction, leave FOCUS unchanged; no double jackpot. If an actor's own narrative is removed, they must re-enter after this action under the already-proposed gate.
- When any legally placed outer die is deliberately **ADJUSTED 1→0**, discard the removed outer narrative and the existing FOCUS (if any), score no trophies from that negative collapse, and allow the center to be renewed later. This is public narrative destruction/reframing rather than automatic scoring.
- **FOCUS is re-revealed from common stock at the beginning of the NEXT human round if absent**, NOT instantly mid-round, and only if stock has a card. This limits the center to **at most one focus release per human round** without adding a used/unused token. The FOCUS thus has enough continuity for players to remember it; multiple outer cards may still be won/burned within a round. If stock0, keep center empty; do not create a card.
- The center's owner follows **HOPE's location** not the contributor of the collapsed outer card. This is a deliberate replacement candidate for the older R15B contributor-reward, not an additional cumulative payout. The other two dice (HATE and FEAR) still determine the current spoken Problem and Reaction roles, and may trigger the outer collapse directly, but they do NOT gain extra ability points.
- Outcome: manipulation of HOPE's position to one's public narrative can make a third party's impending collapse a **publicly advertised 'solution' that awards the center to its sponsor**. A rival could harvest the outer card while you benefit from controlling the solution. NPC HOPE location can neutralize that award by forcing a reframe. No extra private missions or modifiers.

**Important fairness warning from static enumeration:** With neutral uniformly distributed three-die placements and every human actor sampled, the chance of a HOPE-center trophy to a human other than the actor is `(n−1)/6` per *positive outer collapse* in this model; n=2 => 1/6, n=6 => 5/6. This is a **large player-count dependence** inherent in the center's HUMAN/NPC eligibility. It is not an empirically observed player rate; B might need a different NPC 'proxy' or equalizing payoff before it is balanced. Do not compensate invisibly now.

## Card interchangeability: why this has a chance, and what it does not solve
- Unlike R16 fixed **Fear/Hope/Hate printed lens icons**, R17 assigns the card's *temporary narrative role* by **which D6 physically occupies it**, so the same illustrated card can be tomorrow's villain or supposed cure. This uses actual **cards, title and satirical illustration** as lines in the causal script, and gives MOVE/SWAP a political reframing sense. Prefer **A/B test against R16 C1**, not stacking icons and dynamic roles.
- The cards' **individual identity** is the named proposition, concrete illustration, short PUBLIC CLAIM / PRIVATE INCENTIVE pair and public faction position. That affects what story the player constructs and why they choose PLAY/REWRITE, even if no value is printed. If that alone is insufficient, research one *printed relationship/contradiction keyword* or motif on a later slice rather than inventing 50 attack/defense values.
- This is not proof every card has a unique strategy; no amount of required speech alone makes identical cards mechanically distinct. B adds mechanically distinct **narrative positions/ownership paths**, not individual numerical powers. That tradeoff must be evaluated honestly at the table.
- Keep total 56 product slots: 6 faction +50 ordinary narrative. No extra objective cards or fourth Quest die. Previous KFB Quest die remains a future alternate, NOT added to this test.

NPC outer vacancy awaits the separately proposed NPC editorial PULSE, not automatic refill.
**6H:** FOCUS = THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY; HATE on Technocracy narrative, FEAR on Round Table narrative, HOPE on Illuminati narrative, all three on different cards. A player who moves HOPE to their own public narrative can do so by one legal MOVE into its die-free occupied slot. Later, a different player's 6→7 outer collapse makes the HOPE-position holder eligible for center Focus. No secret mission needed. If another collapse occurs that same human round, it cannot award a second center card because FOCUS is already absent until next round.
