## Distinguish three different things
1. **The topic / cluster / POI** that the public argument is about: e.g. AI infrastructure, secret research, university accreditation, platform governance. Such topics are external *fictional* cues printed on one shared board or derived from visible institution cards; no new card attributes are required in A.
2. **The institutional channel used to present the topic**: RESOURCES, RULES, INFLUENCE. These are already supplied by each institution in R0.3 and are **not** truth scores or ideological left/right.
3. **R/A/F as rhetorical dynamics**, attached to three distinct physical dice, regardless of where they are moved: **R** = Resonanz, who identifies with the framing; **A** = Amplitude, how loudly amplified; **F** = Frequenz, how often reappearing. This is source-language reinterpretation for this *separate* game. R/A/F are **not three extra spendable resources**, combat attributes, or 3 separate score counters.

## Option A · KISS physical triangle, six-faced dice and a moving public focus (FIRST TEST)
**Components:** A small triangular playing aid, three original colored D6 marked R, A, F and one central eye symbol. The triangle's three **corner cells** are labeled by existing SUPPLIES types: RESOURCES / RULES / INFLUENCE. Each die occupies a different corner; the colored die identity and the value visible on its top face (1…6) are independent of the corner occupied. The *topic* can be hand-labeled at the center of the board, such as `AI RESEARCH`, or represented by a current POI/card beside the board.

**Starting state:** R at RESOURCES, A at RULES, F at INFLUENCE; show **3/3/3** for a contested opening. For a strictly non-neutral diagnostic start, show 4/3/2; document which is used. The board is shared by all players; no player owns a die.

**Define FOCUS exactly:** if **one** die is strictly larger than both others, its **current corner is the public foreground**; e.g. R=4 on RESOURCES, A=3 on RULES, F=3 on INFLUENCE → foreground RESOURCES. If the highest values tie between 2 or 3 dice, the centre **EYE = CONTESTED FRAME**, with no favored corner. This is a public *spotlight*, not objective reality. All dice are always visible. A swap of positions reassigns whose rhetoric foregrounds which channel without changing die faces.

**Candidate RECRUIT cost (and only mechanical effect in first test):**
- When unique foreground exists, one Market Institution whose `SUPPLIES` matches that foreground costs **0 FAVOR** to RECRUIT; all other Market Institutions cost **1 FAVOR**.
- When frame is contested, any Market Institution costs **1 FAVOR**.
- Existing +1 FAVOR at turn start (max 3) makes any ordinary Market RECRUIT affordable, so **no hard ban** on alternative discourse. POACH, VETO, NEED links, points and seven other Event rules unchanged.
- R0.5's old two-of-four MARKET window is **disabled** during this variant. Do not double-charge or double-discount.

**One existing PLOT slot — optional `P04 MOVE THE FRAME` replacing a duplicate:**
"Choose one: rotate the **top face of one die by exactly ±1** (stopping at 1 or 6), **or swap the corners of two dice**. Then RECRUIT one Market Institution at its new Frame cost." It is one entire **PLOT** main action; replace a planned duplicate PLOT in the existing 12-card product budget, do not add a 57th card. If the market is empty, discard Plot after change and do not invent an institution. If Plot hand refill pile empties, base rules apply. Face rotation is deliberate agency, **not a random dice roll**. At boundaries, invalid turns are not allowed; swap is always valid. A contested frame permits RECRUIT for 1 FAVOR following the normal +1 FAVOR turn gain.
- Rationale: Rotation changes **intensity**. A **swap changes topic/channel direction**. Both expose which visible dependency becomes cheap to recruit. R/A/F qualitative reading helps `SHOW IT → SPIN IT → SELL IT` optional 10-second narration; no narration improves the discount or wins a subjective vote.
- **Scope warning:** With only one or three copies of P04 in the planned 12 PLOTS, face shifts may happen too seldom. Test with one **P03→P04 paper swap**, including a prearranged opening Plot hand; if effect is scarce in an eight-round game, consider three P04 copies within 12 (not final-approved). Never silently grant a free die rotation every turn.

**Distinctive example (constructed, no playtest):** `AI RESEARCH` is the issue, and the three corners frame it as supply/compute (RESOURCES), licensing (RULES), or strategic communications (INFLUENCE). Dice show R4 at RESOURCES, A3 at RULES, F3 at INFLUENCE: `THE COMPUTE CHOKEPOINT` (supplies RESOURCES) costs 0 FAVOR, a RULES supplier costs 1. A rival plays MOVE THE FRAME and swaps R/F positions. R4 now stands at INFLUENCE: a media/intelligence supplier costs 0, the compute supplier costs 1. The material state **has not changed truth**, but the sanctioned/recommended recruitment path did. An opportunistic Player may still pay 1 FAVOR for the unfavored supplier.

**2–6 / Solo:** Only one shared visible board. PLOT can move dice once in its own turn, no interrupts and no new voting phase. At 5–6, watch whether inspecting 3 faces each turn costs too much. Solo THE SYSTEM from R0.4 reads the same Market costs only for its deterministic RECRUIT: if it has FAVOR, pay 0/1; if short, action is skipped; it never rotates dice by itself in initial Solo A, so human PLOT access can bias difficulty. **No solo fairness claims until measured.**

## Option B · an actual issue/POI triangle with movable dice (DEFER UNTIL A IS ENJOYABLE)
Use **three named topic / claim clusters** around the same triangular board (e.g. PUBLIC AI, CLASSIFIED AI, CORPORATE AI) and place at most one die per corner; swap or rotate to shift which specific frame is acceptable. However the card inventory currently carries **only supply/need**, not topic labels. To make B playable, define **one handwritten public ISSUE strip** for a test and place current cards beside topic corners; do not silently add six new faction tags or extra cards. A genuine policy-acceptability Overton model is about competing positions **on a single issue**, not topic popularity. The issue triangle should not falsely imply a one-dimensional Overton spectrum. If tested, choose a **specific ordered five-position policy continuum** embedded beside/inside the pyramid to separate legitimacy from volume; that is a *second* conditional test, not part of A.

## Design alternatives and rejection criteria
- **A** public focus chosen by top face (first test): 3 dice, one compact public discursive change, one cost consequence; top-tie = contested. Distinct dice identities are narrative for now, not three different numeric multipliers.
- **B** issue-specific triangle with POI assignments (later): richer satire but needs topic-to-card membership and explicit political acceptability positions. High onboarding risk.
- **C** normalize 3 dice into barycentric X/Y/Z coordinates and calculate exact internal hex/triangle cells: mathematically interesting, **reject for current paper prototype** because players must calculate a 3D coordinate from 216 face triples or consult a lookup table. Could be considered only in a digital visual experiment if A/B pass.
- **Stop or simplify** if board only produces arbitrary FAVOR tax, causes all recruitment to cost 1 most turns, increases 6-player downtime, or requires explaining a complex ratio. Do not infer real-world political causality from game tags.
