Dice faces still govern normal R14/R15C single actions and 0/7 outer collapse. An existing MOVE or SWAP can move the “solution” to another real card without changing the die face. After the quick HATE/FEAR/HOPE title chain, players take exactly one ordinary action; no extra Tarot action. Title-only novice mode remains valid. **Never require a seven-part fortune-telling speech each turn.**

English micro-script: “**[HATE title]** is blamed; therefore **[FEAR title]** demands a response; but **[HOPE title]** sells a solution to **[FOCUS title]**.” If useful, the actor adds just one positional question about the card being moved or captured; no points for wit, no subjective judge. The satirical reversal lies in the concrete public claim, the private incentive and the visible consequence of a choice. Do not claim the tarot labels by themselves change payoff or make the game's balance better.
