## User's scope correction · what the game is actually about
**Not a podcast/media add-on.** The visible media actor is a **small, often replaceable endpoint** of larger networks: capital, infrastructure, ownership, state/security policy, intelligence methods, universities, research funding, culture, space industry and narratives. A corporation may be sponsor, technology bottleneck, regulatory target, covert-operation target and agenda setter at the same time. The game should dramatize how the same organization acts differently depending on who controls what.

**Core experience:** build a 2–3-institution coalition; see why its dependencies make it powerful and vulnerable; use one well-chosen legal, material or clandestine intervention to turn an opponent's alliance against itself. Some networks arise from conspiracy, some from shared incentives, some from accidental self-reinforcing institutions. Do **not** predetermine that every social institution has one secret master.

## One simple mechanical grammar (R0 preserved)
- **Three SUPPLIES / NEEDS channels:** RESOURCES (capital, logistics, energy, compute), RULES (law, accreditation, exemptions, institutional gatekeeping, lawfare), INFLUENCE (narrative authority, strategic communications, psychological leverage, audience access, reputation and secrecy).
- **One spendable token:** FAVOR 0–3. **Not** money, cashflow, a fourth channel, or the in-world economy.
- **One action per turn:** RECRUIT from market, POACH exposed rival institution (one existing FAVOR VETO response), or play one PLOT; 8-event clock. No mini-games for PsyOps, social-media follower counts, ideological gauges, therapy or CIA missions.
- **Physical network:** three controlled institutions maximum per player, one SUPPLIES and one NEEDS each. **The thematic six-sphere network exists across the deck/players/public market**, not as 30 concurrent cards for one player.
- **Six spheres classify narrative content only**, never give extra points by themselves. The existing six role cards may embody agendas across these spheres but are not new faction decks.
- **Cross-network ambiguity:** R0 requirements are satisfied by *your own* active institutions only; a relation to other players is expressed via POACH / PLOT / EVENT, not an invented second global graph. If a real playtest finds this too restrictive to evoke global power, investigate **one** visible public common-node variant separately, rather than changing this baseline silently.

## Kayfabe + Bizarro = two editorial operations, not two extra resources
Each institution's **readable physical card** needs:
1. **PUBLIC CLAIM / KAYFABE:** the respectable official role or public reason for existence.
2. **PRIVATE INCENTIVE:** the concrete reward or dependence that makes the actor behave.
3. **BIZARRO REVERSAL:** the satirical turning point where the stated virtue produces its opposite, or its critics reproduce the original power structure. The reversal must be carried by a visible image/action/consequence, not a text-only punchline.

These are **art direction / fiction fields, not additional turn obligations**. Gameplay validity cannot depend on delivering a joke or storytelling skill. The single intervention is still a RECRUIT/POACH/PLOT action.

**Four tiny scene sketches (unproduced):**
- `THE CRITICAL THEORY OFFICE`: PUBLIC: “Question every authority.” INCENTIVE: department controls accreditation for acceptable dissent. BIZARRO: the rebellion earns tenure and issues a list of approved rebellions.
- `THE ANTI-ORTHODOXY COMMISSION` (optional alternate node): PUBLIC: “End ideological gatekeeping.” INCENTIVE: license the correct anti-gatekeeping reading. BIZARRO: the censorship tribunal calls itself Free Speech Week. Neither of these framings is prescribed as the historical origin of any present ideology.
- `THE COMPUTE CHOKEPOINT`: PUBLIC: “Make intelligence accessible.” INCENTIVE: every alternative needs the same chips, power and licenses. BIZARRO: an Open Model poster is stapled to the closed export gate.
- `THE HOLLYWOOD FORECAST BUREAU`: PUBLIC: “We predict what audiences want.” INCENTIVE: pre-sell the projected future. BIZARRO: a film studio markets its own dystopia as a preventive awareness campaign; the audience buys a ticket to the warning.

## Philosophical lenses as moves rather than factions
- **Foucault / power–knowledge, normalization, governmentality:** institutions can govern through audits, examinations, classifications and apparently neutral criteria. Mechanic: a 'RULES' supplier sustains the 'INFLUENCE' or 'RESOURCES' card that claims to be independent. See [Stanford Encyclopedia: Foucault](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/foucault/).
- **Derrida / deconstruction:** the hierarchy 'normal/deviant', 'truth/propaganda', 'free/controlled' can be inverted to show who benefits from the binary. Mechanic: a PLOT changes ownership or suspends a prestigious node while preserving the same mechanical constraints. See [Stanford Encyclopedia: Derrida](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2008/entries/derrida/).
- **Michael Hartmann / elite recruitment and reproduction:** no omnipotent puppetmaster required; seemingly open selection can repeatedly privilege existing networks. Mechanic: prestigious resource/academic nodes create a self-reinforcing three-card coalition whose core is POACHable. See [Hartmann's 2025 article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11609-025-00557-4).
- **Bernays, Ellul, social identity research, Desmet, Chase Hughes:** distinct claims about public relations, group dynamics, incentives and persuasion, NOT one jointly proven mass-mind-control technique. Existing MKULTRA social-psychology donor documents the status differences. Turn into competing narrative frames or short plot surfaces, not assumed empirical superpowers.
- **Double-sided satire:** the theorist who anatomizes domination may become a campus authority; the anti-ideology entrepreneur may professionalize ideology. Likewise an official fact-checker can gatekeep while an anti-fact-checker runs its own purity tribunal. **Both claims remain contestable frames; the game does not assert that Foucault/Derrida caused 'woke' politics or that their critics are equivalent.**

## Concrete multi-sphere dry run (NOT human playtest)
A controls three different-sector nodes:
- `THE SOVEREIGN FUND` (RESOURCES → INFLUENCE),
- `THE RECOMMENDATION ENGINE` (INFLUENCE → RULES),
- `THE EMERGENCY LICENSE OFFICE` (RULES → RESOURCES).
All NEEDS met: **9 base points**, but with three cards each is **EXPOSED** under R0.
B controls `THE FRONT FOUNDATION` (RESOURCES → INFLUENCE) and `THE PEER REVIEW GATE` (RULES → RESOURCES). B's Foundation still needs INFLUENCE; the Peer Review Gate's RESOURCES need is already satisfied. Both are distinct from A's cards. If B holds 2 FAVOR and spends 1 to POACH A's EXPOSED recommendation engine, A may VETO for 1 FAVOR. If A does not VETO, B gets the INFLUENCE card and forms a 3-card cycle (9 points); A loses that INFLUENCE supplier, so its RESOURCE node's NEED becomes unmet and A has only 4 base points. This **9→4 / 4→9 two-sided swing** is a *balance risk to test*, not proof of a good rule.
The meaningful plot is **infrastructure + legality + platform control**; no podcast is required.
