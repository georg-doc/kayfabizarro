## Core mechanics: use the existing pressure as the target number

Let `S=HATE.face+FEAR.face+HOPE.face`, so `S∈[3,18]` while all three dice are legally placed. **The faces remain on the existing physical cubes** and are not rerolled for a capture. Each human on their turn speaks one actual faction accusation/response/solution, **MOVEs+TURNs** the appropriate role die to a currently occupied die-free exterior narrative (or SETs its die at1 in opening three beats). After all three dice have been SET once, **that human may then elect one CLAIM** against the current central FOCUS, with a wholly separate random roll. The current role speaker is not automatically the winner; the current human actor is the claimant, regardless of whether their own public faction has the HOPE die.

### Compare randomizers, do not stack

**A · Recommended first probability/control candidate: roll one D20, success if `D20≤S`.** This is a **temporary randomizer** only, not a fourth Borromean/Quest pressure die and not one of the colored role dice. Real D20 geometry exists as a KayKit BoardGameBits donor; source/visual isolation is still required before embedding in the scene. The success curve is exactly `S/20` for the legal pressure range: S3=15%, S6=30%, S9=45%, S12=60%, S15=75%, S18=90%. One point of visible pressure = five percentage points. *No automatic 100% capture before a TILT.*

**B · All-W6 challenger:** roll **three additional ordinary W6**, compare their summed result with S; *do not roll or disturb the three role cubes*. Exact CDF: S3=1/216 ≈0.46%, S6=20/216 ≈9.26%, S9=81/216=37.5%, S12=160/216≈74.07%, S15=206/216≈95.37%, S18=216/216=100%. This has a strong late-game nonlinear curve and uses readily available D6, but needs **three physical randomizer dice** or rerolls of a separate die; the existing role dice cannot serve as both persistent pressure and randomizer. B may end almost certainly late and is mechanically noisier. An ordinary D6 or D12 against S alone saturates early and is not a suitable unmodified comparator.

**Why high-pressure tactics are not yet automatic:** In plain R23, S increases by one on each successful post-SET narrated MOVE+TURN. Thus S is a **visible common clock**, not an independent positional scoring matrix. A special modifier based on the triangle's geometric relation to FOCUS must be a separate later hypothesis; R23 counted 20 triangles with 2 strict interior / 12 boundary / 6 outside, an ambiguous immediate capture trigger. Do not pretend die placement currently changes S or the basic odds: it changes **what the public narrative means**.

## What does the player actually capture? Separate scoring from hand

**Recommended first win condition (candidate):** A **successful CLAIM moves the physical FOCUS card into the claiming HUMAN's visible Trophy/Almanac collection**. It is not awarded automatically to the HOPE holder, to the current SOLUTION role or to an NPC faction. New FOCUS is revealed from the **existing shared 50-card supply** if one remains, otherwise the case is closed and final trophies count. **First to collect three FOCUS trophies** is a testable optional short-game target, not a locked global rule; at supply exhaustion, most trophies is the fallback, ties unresolved. One centre trophy per successful claim. The central tower's eye may react to the claim as a visual event, but never computes it.

**The 3 private hand cards are NOT the win tally**. Their job is to offer possible different narratives and a risk stake, not a second hidden scoring game. Starting hands: each human privately holds 3 after publishing exactly 1 of 4 dealt ordinary cards; NPCs have no private hands. Keep a player's public faction seat and identity stable even when the current narrative occupying it changes.

## Handcards should be actions and risks, not a hidden second score

**Baseline candidate with genuine opportunity cost:**
1. On their normal human beat, player narrates the current role and physically SETs / MOVE+TURNs the real colored D6 legally.
2. **Optional PUBLISH**: before narrating the beat, they may take **one hand narrative card** and replace **their own faction's exterior public narrative**; prior narrative goes to the ordinary discard pile. A D6 already on that seat retains its **slot position and face**, it does not attach to the discarded card. This is a concrete card change in the same beat, not an extra free score or die action. The new title/claim must appear in the spoken retort.
3. **Optional CLAIM**: after the die gesture, if all three pressure dice lie legally and they have at least one hand card, the human chooses **one named hand card as an explicit stake/pitch** referring to FOCUS, then rolls the claim randomizer. One roll maximum on that human turn. No additional free dice move.
4. **Success:** keep the staked hand card, move FOCUS to personal public trophy pile, trigger bounded scene-change and fresh case (below).
5. **Failure A (recommended first real-risk control):** **discard the staked hand card**. The pressure dice/FOCUS remain exactly as they were, and next human continues. This costs a meaningful narrative option and makes spam at S3 expensive. No narrative quality judge. If a person has no private hand cards, they may still perform ordinary role moves but cannot CLAIM.
6. **DRAW:** A human with **zero hand cards** may elect to draw **one** from stock **instead of the die MOVE+TURN**; it is a whole turn with no CLAIM. This is a comeback route but makes the fixed R23 pressure clock longer. The next human/role proceeds normally; do not let unlimited stock-free pass turns create an infinite loop. If stock is empty, DRAW is unavailable. This draw action is a proposed explicit exception to the R23 mandatory rising-pressure clock, not silently its original rule.

**Failure B (Georg's suggested softer challenger):** after a failed claim, **discard the staked card and draw one replacement ordinary card into the hand**, if available. The hand count stays constant; that is *card cycling*, not a numerical penalty, though the player loses a chosen tactical identity. The other suggestion “on failure draw an **extra** card without losing one” actually increases options while rewarding failed early claims; call it *consolation/catch-up* rather than a punishment and test separately. If the game instead used “first to empty hand”, extra draws would be a setback, but that is a **different win condition**. Do not claim one action can be simultaneously a penalty under trophy-collection and hand-emptying rules.

**Potential later tactical modifier (NOT baseline):** Spend one extra hand card *before the D20 roll* for +1 to S (exactly +5 percentage points), capped at S18 or D20 roll success≤19, while discarding that card regardless of outcome. This makes hand assets relevant numerically, but risks converting illustrated storytelling cards into fungible +1 coupons. First test the unmodified role-and-title-driven PUBLISH/CLAIM; do not adopt printed numeric stats or card icon powers yet.

## Public-card circulation after center capture

A captured central FOCUS card is **collected**, never silently returned into stock. Six fixed faction identities remain in place; the six displayed **outer narrative cards** are temporary public claims and may be replaced.

**Refresh A · recommend as KISS test:** after a successful CLAIM, replace **exactly ONE** outer narrative: the card beneath the **active role die just moved/turned** is discarded, and a random ordinary card from the same stock takes that faction's outer slot. Keep that role die in the **same slot**. Replace center with exactly one new FOCUS from stock. **Reset the three visible role dice to face1 while keeping their three separate outer positions**, rather than resetting faction seats or introducing 3 separate NPC turns. Previous two die-bearing narrative cards survive as remembered causal context. This creates two new public subjects per trophy (one outside + one center), not four. For a 2H/4NPC round, the replaced outer seat may belong to either human or NPC faction; **card ownership stays with the faction slot**. If shared stock has fewer than 2 cards, no phantom replacement: classify shortage and use an explicit exhausted-deck end rather than leaving an empty die-occupied seat.

**Refresh B · harder challenger:** replace all **THREE** narratives currently under HATE/FEAR/HOPE, then replace FOCUS; four new subjects on each success, with a higher memory/onboarding cost. This is the alternative Georg explicitly mentioned, not automatically combined with Refresh A. Three discarded, four drawn from existing shared stock; dice keep distinct positions, faces reset1. Strong card turnover but much faster deck consumption and story discontinuity. Do not give removed NPC cards to an imaginary NPC hand or move them into human hands without a separate transfer rule.

**An unsuccessful claim alone leaves all outside cards in place** under baseline; optional PUBLISH causes participant-controlled card churn during ordinary turns. An automatic NPC editor pulse from R16 is *separate* and not silently added; can compare later for 2H/4NPC, but no autonomous NPC story action.

**Natural non-CLAIM TILT:** if a later MOVE+TURN attempts to raise an active D6 from6 past6 while FOCUS remains unresolved, the case **collapses publicly**; no acting-speaker trophy, no award to HOPE/NPC. For this test, discard and replace only the center and optionally one outside affected narrative from stock, keep dice position and reset1; if stock insufficient, close the deck. This avoids an automatic winner on HATE's 19th beat but **is still an unaccepted R25 proposal**, requiring game/memory tests.

## Conserving actual fifty ordinary cards

Initial exact setup with n human players:
`50 = 6 outer + 1 center + (3×n) private hands + (43−3n) stock + 0 trophies + 0 discard.`
For n=2 initial stock37; n=6 stock25. Show the **six faction identity cards separately**: they are never consumed or counted as ordinary narrative cards.

On successful Refresh A if ≥2 stock: center→one human trophy (+1), active outer card→discard (+1), draw two new cards from stock (one new Focus, one outer), so ordinary card total is still50. On failure A: one staked hand card→discard; no drawn card. Failure B: one staked hand card→discard, one shared stock card→that hand. PUBLISH: one hand card→own outer slot, old outer→discard. On DRAW: stock→hand. On TILT: center/outer→discard, stock→center/outer; no free trophy. If stock becomes exhausted, the game ends or a separately approved recycle rule is needed; DO NOT reshuffle the trophies, synthesize cards or allow hands to become negative.

**Design concern**: Discaring one staked hand card on failure may end personal CLAIM access until the optional DRAW turn; this makes real tactical choice but also risks slowing the conversational chain. Compare failing-stake A with loss-and-replacement B before accepting either. Early-sum rolling should be an exciting temptation, not a forced optimal action or silent extra automatic winner.
