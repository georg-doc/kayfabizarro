# THE INVISIBLE HANDSHAKE · R0.2 Medien- und Kulturmacht als spielbares Netzwerk
**Status:** TUNE / SOURCE-LED DESIGN CANDIDATE (2026-11-17) · **Owner:** `games/invisible-handshake/` · **Branch:** `design/invisible-handshake-paper-r0-2026-11-17` · **PR:** #475. **No human playtest yet.**
**Chain:** R0 paper mechanics → [R0.1](SEMANTICS_R01_2026-11-17.md) introduces RESOURCES / RULES / INFLUENCE → **this additive R0.2** integrates the modern communications ecosystem into the unchanged R0 mechanical core. Research Atlas remains a source donor, not a second game or runtime owner.

## Product thesis
The game does not ask who is objectively right. It stages **who owns distribution, who grants access, who funds attention, who controls legal framing and who can flip an intermediary**. A nominally independent outlet can be recruited, funded, poached, shielded or exposed; that is a fictional game relationship, not a factual allegation about any real outlet.

**Satire engine for every scene:** Public justification / hidden material incentive / visual evidence of the contradiction / playable transfer of leverage. A fictional editor, host, sponsor, NGO employee or press secretary needs an understandable interest; avoid generic all-powerful shadow entities.

## R0.2 channel taxonomy · unchanged 3 tags
- **RESOURCES** (formerly MONEY): credit, liquidity, funding, supply chains, physical infrastructure, distribution and media-buy budgets. Financial collapse is a possible event, **not a second cash subsystem**.
- **RULES**: law, regulation, accreditation, licenses, platform policies, lawfare, procedural delay, selective enforcement, emergency powers. Rules may be technically valid but self-serving.
- **INFLUENCE** (formerly VOICE): attention, narratives, news framing, reputation, manipulation, kompromat/blackmail, covert agreements, amplification, social algorithms, audience access, psychological operations. Influence is **not** total victory points and is **not** the 0–3 spendable FAVOR token.

No new tag for CULTURE, PSYOPS or MEDIA, no extra resource track. Those are **fictional institution/plot themes**, not mandatory additional mechanics.

## Three layers of the media ecosystem (all use existing card types)
1. **VISIBLE DISTRIBUTION:** mainstream newsroom, local outlet, podcast host, platform feed, streamer, cultural studio. Network cards can SUPPLY INFLUENCE, but may NEED RESOURCES or RULES to operate.
2. **INTERMEDIARIES:** PR agencies, advertisers, civil-society organizations, front organizations, foundation grants, fact-check consortium, accreditation office, reputation-management firms, platform moderation. Their role is defined by SUPPLIES/NEEDS and the particular card text, not by assuming an entire real category is secretly controlled.
3. **OPERATIONS / SHOCKS:** kompromat, sponsorship agreement, manufactured dispute, leaked funding records, lawfare, strategic communications, liquidity crisis, major platform policy changes. These live in the existing PLOT and EVENT budget; they do not become extra permanent statuses or subdecks.

## One mechanism for bought, turned and narrative-setting media
Use **only existing actions**:
- **Buy / sponsor = RECRUIT** the media card from the open market, its printed NEED showing the funding/access dependency.
- **Turn / flip = POACH** an EXPOSED media institution. It physically changes owner and supplies INFLUENCE to its new network. The prior owner gets the existing single **1 FAVOR VETO** (unless a Plot states otherwise).
- **Shape a narrative = PLOT**. A published story changes control/exposure/suspension through the card text; it does **not** create a free-floating invisible persuasion score. R0.2 plot candidates may reuse the three existing effects so the mechanical vocabulary is unchanged:
  - `THE COMPROMISING FILE` (BLACKMAIL) = R0 P02 BACKROOM BROKER effect, **POACH an exposed rival for 0 FAVOR; 1 FAVOR VETO or RECRUIT fallback**.
  - `EMERGENCY INJUNCTION` (LAWFARE) = R0 P01 PUBLIC AUDIT effect, **SUSPEND one institution until next round**, supplies 0, scores 0.
  - `THE SPONSORSHIP PACKAGE` (MEDIA BUY) = R0 P03 BAILOUT effect, **RECRUIT one market institution, then gain 1 FAVOR (max 3)**.
- **Culture conditioning / anticipatory framing = fictional PLOT skin**: `THE CONDITIONING CAMPAIGN` may reuse **P02's POACH/VETO** effect to signify a sponsor-backed entertainment narrative that converts a public figure. It is an **alternative title/fictional premise for an existing Plot slot**, not evidence of a current historical MKULTRA program, not an eleventh test card, and not a new persuasion subsystem.
- **Set a public frame = EVENT** with a one-round global effect. Candidate replacement for a duplicate existing `PUBLIC HEARING`: `THE FEED TURNS` = **This round, all institutions supplying INFLUENCE count as EXPOSED.** At most one normal POACH reaction per attack. No new marker, rating or phase. Do **not** lock this in until the R0 playtest shows that the event doesn't enable runaway flips.

**Important:** "Narrative control" must change a visible network relation or an actionable vulnerability; otherwise it is merely a joke/title, not gameplay. The player must see exactly which card can now be poached or supplied.

## Constructed example: the podcast changes hands
Before: Player A holds `THE SPONSORED PODCAST` (INFLUENCE → RESOURCES), with its RESOURCE need met by `THE EMERGENCY LENDER`; A's network is full (3 Institutions) and thus its podcast is **EXPOSED** under existing R0 rules. Player B has `THE VERIFICATION COUNCIL` (RULES → INFLUENCE), whose NEED will be met by owning a new INFLUENCE supplier, and holds 1 Favor.
- B's turn grants +1 FAVOR: **1 → 2**.
- B declares POACH at podcast: pays **1 FAVOR**, 2 → 1.
- A may VETO by paying **1 FAVOR**. If A does not, podcast moves from A's network to B's (B must replace one if already full).
- The podcast now provides **INFLUENCE** to B's network and no longer contributes to A's needs/score.
- If A VETOs, podcast stays A's and B may RECRUIT another card instead (no new action).
This is not a new “influencer conversion” rule. Its visible story is conversion, its game verb is POACH. Costs and one reaction remain exactly R0.

## Cultural / historical layers and sourcing
- **Current media:** media formats are plausible archetypes, not claims that named journalists, NGOs, platforms or fact-checkers are agents of a conspiracy. Distinct factions in a later game can compete for the same outlets.
- **NATO cognitive warfare:** NATO Allied Command Transformation describes cognitive warfare as an exploratory/conceptual project on how behavior, attitudes and decisions can be influenced/protected/disrupted with military and non-military instruments; Lawfare is discussed as related to cognitive effects. It supplies a **publicly documented conceptual reference**, not proof of any particular secret media operation. See [ACT cognitive warfare page](https://www.act.nato.int/activities/cognitive-warfare/), [ACT concept update, 2024](https://www.act.nato.int/article/cogwar-concept/), [NATO Defense College, Jan 2026](https://www.ndc.nato.int/war-is-a-mind-game-countering-weaponised-information/).
- **MKULTRA** was a historical CIA research program documented in declassified sources, notably the [1963 CIA IG report archived at GWU](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/32731-document-16-john-s-earman-inspector-general-us-central-intelligence-agency-report). A fictional CULTURE STUDIO or conditioning satire may draw on motifs of engineered perceptions, coercion and entertainment, but **“MKULTRA controls today's entire pop culture” is not an established historical finding** and is not attributed as fact.
- **Original Illuminati gameplay:** [publisher's rules](https://www.sjgames.com/illuminati/img/illuminati_rules.pdf) distinguish group control and power/resistance/income. The candidate game draws on visible network transfers rather than importing its whole statistics architecture.
- **Research Atlas provenance:** continue separating original documents, later author interpretations, editorial motif comparisons and outright fictional satire. Source metadata never become attack/defense points.
- **“Pre-Crowd Planning”:** wording appears in Georg's input but is not identified as a verified specialized reference in this pass; treat as an uncommitted *cultural anticipation / audience shaping* design theme, not a named technical or historical doctrine.
