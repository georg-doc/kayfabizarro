## User intent, without reinterpretation

This is a **fast dialogical sequence of accusations, challenges, escalation and defenses** anchored to **actual cards and their power interests**. The actors do not perform an explanation about a category. They **make a new claim that changes the table**. Georg's proposed first question is **“Which party/card is the problem?”**. Player1 might point to the faction's public narrative, place the borromean **HATE** die at face 1 on that card; Player2 responds on **REACTION/FEAR**, pointing to a card they blame for worsening the situation; Player3 proposes **SOLUTION/HOPE**, defending or manipulating a claim. Subsequent beats can move, turn up, swap or invert dice with an in-universe spoken justification. Keep tone bold and absurd (Kayfabe) without punitive judging of whether a line is the “right” Story Mode.

## Test surface: the six cards and the case in the center

Keep **six outer face-up faction narrative cards** around one shared central **FOCUS/QUEST**. The center names the current public argument (e.g. THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY) and never receives a die. Each outer position belongs to a human or NPC faction, independent of which human points to it. The **three existing emotion dice** begin off-board (possible fast setup: face 1 for first placement; initial 3/3/3 is a deliberately separate old R15C baseline). A player's turn may introduce a **new ordinary narrative from hand** into a faction's outer slot as their evidence or may point to the **already visible public card**. If introduced, the replaced ordinary card moves to discard and its die remains with the same outer slot until the active die operation. Card drawing/refill timing and the final exact 50-card inventory **remain open**; do not pretend that a free new card plus automatic draws is already balanced.

One completed story **triplet** consists of three consecutive *human* beats, not necessarily three distinct humans. With 2 humans: A→B→A; 4 humans: A→B→C, then D→A→B; 6 humans: A→B→C then D→E→F. **No automatic personal reward for SOLUTION** (3H/6H would lock that seat). Human turn order stays clockwise. NPC factions are eligible *subjects*, not automatically extra human-like turns.

### The actual playable grammatical contract

**A human takes one physical action and one short line, as one indivisible beat.** The active die is fixed by the current beat:

| Beat | The single substantive question | Speech function | Active die |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROBLEM | “Who is responsible for this?” | **ACCUSE**, name a faction/card and the specific act it committed | HATE |
| REACTION | “Who is making it worse, and how?” | **ESCALATE / COUNTER-ACCUSE**, explicitly build or challenge the preceding claim | FEAR |
| SOLUTION | “Who claims to rescue us, and at whose expense?” | **DEFEND / CO-OPT / PROMISE**, connect solution to preceding problem and reaction | HOPE |

The line must specify **actor + concrete act/consequence + pointed-at card** in character. No abstract filler (“hate because information was leaked”), no 3-line speech; the following player can invert the accusation without a referee. Do **not** judge humor, political correctness, historical correctness, tone adherence or rhetorical quality. Contradiction is an offer for the next beat; if a player freezes, allow a short title-and-visible-detail fallback without a penalty, and the next person takes the story forward.

### Four physical die changes as ONE move (proposal, not settled)

**Candidate A · one-destination automatic grammar (first KISS physical trial):** The speaker chooses the single outer **target card**, so the physical die operation is *determined by the resulting occupancy*, not chosen from a large action menu.

1. Active emotion die has **never been placed** → **SET** it on the named occupied outer card, showing face **1** (Georg's suggested example). Center is illegal.
2. Active die is **already on the named card** → **TURN** the same cube **one face up** (face 1→2→…→6). A separately testable **INVERT** (opposite face `7−n`) would mean “the accusation has been turned against itself”; do **not** stack inverse and turn on a single beat.
3. Active die is on another card and **target has no die** → **MOVE** active die to named card **without changing its face**.
4. Active die is on another card and **target holds another emotional die** → **SWAP** their card positions **without changing either face**. Both dice remain on distinct occupied outer cards.

A TURN from visible face **6** invokes a **TILT / BREAK** event instead of pretending a real D6 displays 7. The same question at face **1** for negative collapse or face inversion is a **different experimental rule**, not simultaneously invented. The original R14 6→7/1→0 scoring must **not** be copied; it creates a next-player preparation problem. This movement grammar has a serious potential **indefinite MOVE/SWAP cycle** because movement does not increase pressure; see proposed B test below.

**Candidate B · pressure always moves (small challenger, do not stack):** Every successfully completed HATE/FEAR/HOPE beat advances **its own active die by one face even if it MOVEs or SWAPs**. At face6 the die tips on the next successful beat. This prevents “shuffle forever” but risks making the story artificially mechanical or forcing a game-ending event regardless of agency. Compare A versus B on real timing and expressive freedom, not merely automated inevitability.

**Card replacement should initially be optional**, because requiring every beat to both draw a card and manage hand depletion would undo the intended short turns. If card choice becomes too arbitrary, later test “play one card from hand OR point one current card” but only after six public-card accusation alone is understood.

## Model first full triplet: a believable, assertive Kayfabe dialogue

**Focus:** THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY, public claim “Extraordinary measures will be temporary.” **Mode** (if chosen) = FORBIDDEN. Human A, human B, and (if 3+ players) human C; for 2 players A also performs the third beat. Six outer public narratives remain face-up and initially no emotional dice are placed.

- **A, HATE / PROBLEM — SET HATE face1** on the existing **THE EMERGENCY LICENSE OFFICE**: “**Das Notfallamt ist schuld.** Es lässt nur die Leute in Schutzräume, die vorher eine Sondergenehmigung gekauft haben. Und es verkauft die Genehmigungen selbst.” One specific accused public card, one concrete profitable action; A does not say “Hate because ...”.
- **B, FEAR / REACTION — SET FEAR face1** on **THE MAINSTREAM SYNDICATE**: “**Und heute Abend erklärt das Syndikat alle ohne Genehmigung zum Sicherheitsrisiko.** Die gleichen Firmen besitzen schon die Zäune um die Schutzräume.” This builds the accusation and redirects the escalating threat.
- **C (or A), HOPE / SOLUTION — SET HOPE face1** on **THE SAFETY ADVISORY**: “**Wir machen Schluss mit der Panik.** Ab morgen darf nur noch unser Sicherheitsdienst die Genehmigungen kontrollieren. Der Vertrag verlängert sich automatisch, solange jemand Angst hat.” This is a straight-faced, coercive “solution” with a private incentive already embedded in the spoken play.
- **QUEST reading:** All three have constructed a machine in which fear is the only renewable resource. The seventh card **does not automatically go to the Solution speaker**; its claim is not resolved until a separate mechanical event/condition is settled.

**Next beat example (different turn, no added action):** The next HATE speaker points to the **MAINSTREAM SYNDICATE** already holding FEAR, declares: “**Das Notfallamt hat niemanden in Angst versetzt. Die Sendung hat das Tor schließen lassen. Die Redaktion bekommt heute die Anklage!**” and **SWAPs** HATE/FEAR. No second free die turn, no retrospective story edit that undoes the already established facts. With physical movement this sentence is itself a reallocation of blame.

## What the center and “win” may mean — keep unresolved, isolate alternatives

The **central seventh card is a CASE / QUEST**, not a random bonus magically paid on every SOLUTION. One possible **A shared-public-verdict** first test: each successful triplet publicly states its three-card case and keeps the center unchanged until any die **TILTs**, at which point center is read **as evidence**, archived/reframed from existing stock, and the implicated outer faction's narrative is temporarily removed or marked implicated. **No automatic personal Quest trophy**: the table can observe closure without fixed seat-win bias. One possible **B individual-win challenger** later: an actual player must be assigned objectively to the successful accusation/defense, without awarding every Solution to its third-position speaker, a subjective judge, or an automatic next-seat capture. R14's 5→6 prep theft and R17 B's `(n−1)/6` small-vs-large player bias remain specific negative donors. No scoring threshold, victory cut-off, punitive “you played the wrong mode” rule or third-party NPC award is approved here.

**Important technical distinction:** `ROLL` randomizes the mode at the beginning of a triplet (if enabled); `TURN` intentionally increases the current emotion die on a named card; `MOVE` changes the target, `SWAP` exchanges accusations. These are different hand actions. Do not treat face inversion, roll, high tip and negative burn as the same untested mechanic.

## Scaling and NPCs (simple, falsifiable test order)

- **2 humans / four NPC factions:** A→B→A, followed by B→A→B. No one has a permanent Solution position. Four NPC cards are public targets, **not four autonomous speeches**. If a complete triplet has ended and a separate NPC pulse is needed, reuse R16's **single** collective editorial update, not one NPC turn per NPC. Test whether the mostly neutral NPC board makes human accusation allocation meaningful.
- **4 humans / two NPC factions:** A→B→C, D→A→B, C→D→A, B→C→D. All players eventually occupy every role without manipulating the turn order. No author receives a trophy merely for being the third.
- **6 humans / zero NPC factions:** A→B→C, D→E→F; the roles repeat in the same seats on each triplet under simple clockwise rotation, so **only objective die/card consequences, never “Solution wins,”** can decide a competitive reward. The next player must reply to an actual assertion, not tell a detached solo monologue.
- **Optional NPC pulse after a complete triplet only:** select next NPC faction deterministically clockwise; when it is empty, draw/republish one card from the same stock; otherwise a separate future challenger could MOVE one die to the next legal occupied free seat. **Do not activate both in the baseline**. No NPC hands, judged speech, hero-stat tracking or additional dice.
