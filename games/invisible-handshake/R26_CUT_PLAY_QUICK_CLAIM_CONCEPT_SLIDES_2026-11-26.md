# The Invisible Handshake · R26 · Cut & Play / Quick Claim
26.11.2026 · **CONCEPT SLIDES / WS0 INTAKE / NOT IMPLEMENTED / NOT ACCEPTED**. User request: an immediately legible, fast 1–6-person/NPC card-centered minigame, with the existing hexagon, tower, three rotated shared dice, standees and effects; same playable paper rules. No account/economy/online infrastructure now. Extend R25; do not replace Freestyle or R19. Working slide label “Quick Claim” is descriptive, not product branding.

## Slide 01 — What do I do?
Win the illustrated card in the middle. On your turn choose a highlighted outside card, move the current shared die there and turn it up one, optionally risk a hand card to claim the center. The cards' printed claims and motifs give the move meaning; a sentence is invited, not a speech exam. A new case starts when the center is won or collapses. Proposed short format: three trophies wins; target 5–10 min is a hypothesis, not measured. Six fixed faction seats, not a terrain hex grid, no running pawns or 52-cell Ludo copy.

## Slide 03 — Table: seven cards, six positions
Six distinct permanent faction/seat identities with one public narrative each; seventh FOCUS in the center (never a die target). Existing R24 standee shows the seat's card; physical die belongs to the seat and may sit beside it. Three shared D6 on three distinct occupied outer seats. No additional printed board required (arrange six cards in a ring plus center); existing printable R24 layout optional. Physical inventory: 50 ordinary cards + 6 identities (IH planning budget), 3 role D6, optional separate D20 (or digital randomizer, same odds); three hand cards per active seat if playing competitive R26; trophy piles/discard/stock separate.

## Slide 04 — 30-second onboarding
1. Choose character/faction and publish one of four dealt narratives; retain three hand cards. Passive NPC seats reveal one card each, no hand. Show center (title and one printed line).
2. First three active beats SET HATE/PROBLEM, FEAR/REACTION, HOPE/SOLUTION at 1 on distinct outer cards. The game demonstrates them immediately; real titles/read-out, optional SHOW/SPIN/SELL, no required full seven-person recital. One first-turn tutorial prompt highlights legal cards. 
3. Thereafter always MOVE+TURN as one gesture. Existing R23 exactly +1, different occupied die-free target, no stacking or center; current role cycles HATE→FEAR→HOPE. Three legal targets when full. One tap digital / one move at table. Optional one specific causal clause or read a printed line, no judge of wit, no LLM needed.

## Slide 05 — One decision, then the temptation
R25 control: after a legal beat when all three dice SET, choose CLAIM (one named hand card as stake, one D20≤sum), or finish turn. Optional PUBLISH before gesture: replace own public narrative from hand, previous goes discard, die remains tied to slot; no auto power/stat. To avoid a button forest first tutorial hides PUBLISH until next case (compare after first capture); physical quick-start omits it in first case. Prefer single claim decision over extra movement menus. If no hand card, normal beat remains possible; R25 zero-hand DRAW instead of move is separate action, stock required, no CLAIM; advance actor and role as R25. No other free draws or moves.
S=3…18; D20 independent. 3→15%, 6→30%, 9→45%, 12→60%, 15→75%, 18→90%. The three board dice are deliberately turned, never rolled for chance. Actual position changes story, not the sum. At 6 the next attempted +1 is TILT (not face7); case collapses without trophy. Baseline 19 beats only with no reset/DRAW. No automatic award to HOPE or HATE.

## Slide 06 — Capture and turnover
On success keep stake, capture Focus into personal trophy pile; discard active die-bearing outer narrative, reveal a new narrative there plus new Focus, reset all three faces to1 keeping distinct locations. Rotate next case starter one participating seat forward relative to previous case starter; maintain role order and record bias. If stake fails, discard it and leave center/dice untouched; compare softer discard+replacement separately, not automatic. TILT: discard focus, replace center only (bounded R26 suggestion), reset faces to1; no trophy; if insufficient stock end after crediting earned trophy, do not generate phantom cards. First 3 trophies or exhausted supply, most trophies; tie shared (proposed). 50-card conservation: outer6+focus1+hands3n+stock(43−3n)+trophy/discard=50. Early end may have vacant center; no ongoing play without populated board. No forced login reward, no permanent unlock in test.

## Slide 08 — Solo to six players
Keep six seats occupied irrespective of active participants. Original R23 human-only speaking circuit with passive NPC subjects is the control. R26 extension for 1–6 humans: solo default 1 human +1 active bot +4 passive NPC narratives; 2–6 humans can play with or without additional active bots filling up to six; at least two active participants for competitive mode. Active actors clockwise over enabled seats, passive seats are targets not extra turns. Active bot has same 3-card stake/hand rules, legal move target chosen with deterministic policy (prefer legal matching context where existing metadata, fallback seeded), no new generated dialogue: show/read actual card lines, use existing X/Y and NPC gestures. Bots can win trophies under same rules (new candidate, original R25 human-only); rotate case starter, test first-mover and fixed role bias at 2/3/6 active; auto move on optional digital timeout, no punishment for reading. Six active actors may be too slow; measure bot beats 1–2s presentation with skip. No full games measured.
