- **360** legal TILT-edge outcomes under A's only-on-own-current-target raise, compared with **2,160** under B's advance-after-MOVE/SWAP/TURN. These are fixture counts, *not probabilities of playing an actual game*.

Same 60-beat sequence under B yielded **9** tips (three per die over twenty turns for each). **This exposes a structural progress failure in A**, and proves only forced progress under *that specific B fixture*, not B's game balance or fun.

## Findings

1. The strongest new feature is not “match a card to Hate”: it is **an adversarial Kayfabe statement directly causing a visible public claim redistribution**. The manual gesture has game consequence and leads into the next sentence. Source slogans without causality are not compliant.
2. An occupancy-determined turn (SET if new, TURN if current already on named card, MOVE to free, SWAP with different die on target) is mechanically concise and preserves the R15C one-die-per-card invariant; **no event should place a D6 on FOCUS**.
3. Candidate A cannot guarantee an eventual tipping/Quest event: legal MOVE/SWAP cycles may persist indefinitely. Candidate B fixes one liveness seam but perhaps imposes an unwanted mechanical countdown and raises separate last-actor/5→6 fairness risk.
4. Neither the current R14 “actor captures a high-tip” rule nor the R17 B “Hope position receives Focus” has been shown fair for this new setting. **Claim winner, loser, central Tarot/Quest resolution, hand/card turnover, Story D6 mode penalties, and NPC actions remain intentionally UNSETTLED**.
5. KFB original R/A/F Resonanz/Amplitude/Frequenz donors do not imply those same dice always meant HATE/FEAR/HOPE. Here HATE/FEAR/HOPE is Georg's *game-adapted* use. Optional Story Mode D6 is distinct from those three and remains tonal only.
