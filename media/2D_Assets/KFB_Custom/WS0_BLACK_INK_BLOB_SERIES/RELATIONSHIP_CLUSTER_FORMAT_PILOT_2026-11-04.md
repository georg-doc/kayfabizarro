Requested by Georg: retain KFB whole footless ink-monster/material/eye canon while experimenting with relationship rather than six isolated occupations. First product ONE portrait1024x1536 sheet,2columns x3rows,6actors=3married couples, pair per row. This is a pilot, not proof of a new superior production method or canonical registry schema.

## Visual contract
Each row belongs together through matched limited palette/cloth detail, small paired rings/marks, reciprocal gaze and counterposed posture. Small physical contradictions convey dysfunctional love-hate: offering/withholding, proud display/resentful admiration, exhausted resistance/protective tenderness. Each actor still readable as individual full footless ink blob; partner contrast in silhouette/mouth/lids. Inward gesture can suggest interaction without contact. Transparent gap separates independent silhouettes and props; no arm/object bridges cell seam, no overlap. No floor/scenery/scenelet. All props fully inside cell. No captions to explain relationship. 3mixed married pairs=3female-read actors; explicit relational-format cast exception to normal1–2per6.
