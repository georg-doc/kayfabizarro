# Story trios · R1 · DESIGN

Georg2026-11-05: three stands for many. Same landscape3-column×2-row sheet, but each ROW contains THREE DISTINCT CHARACTERS belonging to a group/story, not the same identity at three levels or states. Six actors per sheet, exactly two female-read actors in each normal six-cast.

Belonging comes from small shared material/colour/accessory motifs and complementary roles. Differentiate body contour, gaze/lids, mouth, attitude and personality before props. Each actor must also work alone: full upright footless ink blob, no directed handoff, shared prop, touching, overlapping or scenelet. White/ivory sclera, red iris, black pupil; expressive lids and lips, few readable modeled props.

Manifest links each actor to a trio and its two partners; pair/state fields remainnull. These are source-authoring group links, not new game composition rules. Three can later seed a larger mixed mob, but no WFC/spawn/runtime behavior is implemented here. Literary/film sources are loose role associations using original ink characters, no film stills or actor likeness references and no automatic KFB deck/canon assignment.
