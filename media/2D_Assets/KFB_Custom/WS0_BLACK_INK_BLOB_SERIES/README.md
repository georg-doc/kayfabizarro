## One-paragraph family canon
Every actor is visibly **a whole footless black-purple ink splat**, not a person with their lower body cropped away. A small self-supporting pool of goo with rounded uneven tentacles/droplets carries oversized expressive eyes and a huge irregular cartoon mouth. The clay is visibly handmade, sculpted, dark and slightly wet, under soft studio light. Character and theme are communicated through contour, face, posture and at most tier-appropriate attributes. The *body stays the monster* even when a boss wears a hat, costume, glasses or holds a prop.
