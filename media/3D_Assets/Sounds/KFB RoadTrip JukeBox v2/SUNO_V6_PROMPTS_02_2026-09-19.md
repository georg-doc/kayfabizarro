Use one compact musical brief in the prompt box. Do not split the generation brief into the old A5-style Style / Exclude / Race-Modulation pseudo-fields.

Recommended first pass:
- Model: v6
- Instrumental: ON

- Standard generation for quick audition

Official v6 guidance says the model understands complex instructions, instrumentation, structure, mood and references in one request. Variety may rewrite style prompts; setting it to 0 keeps tighter control.

## Prompts

### 1 · Cosmic Surf Noir · 155 BPM

**Prompt · 509 characters**

155 BPM psychedelic cosmic surf noir instrumental for a surreal arcade road race: tremolo surf guitar, baritone guitar, elastic electric bass, dry breakbeat drums, vibraphone, organ and analog synth. Dark desert-night mood with neon warmth. Build a subtle continuous Shepard-Risset ascent from layered synth pulses and guitar harmonics so motion always feels uphill without a literal riser. Strong 8/16-bar evolution, brief stop-time gaps for impacts and jumps, brighter final-lap lift, clean loopable ending.

**Suggested file:** `KFB RoadTrip v2 - Cosmic Surf Noir.mp3`

### 2 · Route 66 Roadtrip · 152 BPM

**Prompt · 499 characters**

152 BPM psychedelic Route 66 roadtrip instrumental: dusty highway funk crossed with electro-jazz, twang guitar, clavinet, Wurlitzer/Rhodes, punchy electric bass, tight live drums and electronic percussion. Sunny weird Americana with hypnotic forward motion. Use cyclical Shepard-style bass and synth-register climbs that reset invisibly. Groove first, new details every 8/16 bars, short rhythmic gaps for race SFX, final-lap lift with open hats, tambourine and guitar answers, clean loopable ending.

**Suggested file:** `KFB RoadTrip v2 - Route 66 Roadtrip.mp3`

### 3 · 70s Synth Pop Soundscape · 158 BPM

**Prompt · 507 characters**

158 BPM psychedelic 70s synth-pop soundscape for night racing: warm analog polysynths, sequenced arpeggios, Moog-like bass, tight acoustic/electronic drums, electric piano, phase guitar and melodic percussion. A Shepard-Risset chord/register cycle creates endless forward lift beneath one short memorable motif. Dreamy but propulsive, evolving every 8/16 bars with filter motion, a lean breakdown and bright octave final-lap return. Keep the mix spacious for gameplay SFX and finish on a clean loopable bar.

**Suggested file:** `KFB RoadTrip v2 - 70s Synth Pop Soundscape.mp3`
